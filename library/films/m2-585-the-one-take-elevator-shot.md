# The One-Take Elevator Shot

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: film crews that keep resetting a perfectly functioning elevator scene*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
DIRECTOR VEX, an orangutan
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
CAMERA MARA, a macaque
FLOOR MANAGER ODO, a gibbon
SOUND NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. STUDIO ELEVATOR - DAY

Pip stands alone in a stationary elevator car set. Mara frames the shot. Vex holds a slate.

VEX: This is one continuous take. Nobody may blink.

ODO: The elevator is a set. It will not move; the background changes behind the doors.

NARI: Sound is ready. The chime is a playback cue.

PIP: What is my line?

VEX: You discover that the elevator goes to the wrong floor.

PIP: The script says floor three.

SCENE 2. INT. STUDIO ELEVATOR - CONTINUOUS

The doors close. A screen shows floor two. Pip looks at the number.

PIP: We are one floor below.

VEX: Cut. That was too accurate.

MARA: The camera is still rolling. We can use that take.

VEX: The audience expects confusion.

ODO: Floor number is an intentional prop. It changed from one to two as planned.

SCENE 2A. INT. STUDIO ELEVATOR - CONTINUOUS

Mara lowers the camera. Odo checks the playback monitor. The first piece of footage is a rehearsal, not a recorded take: the slate never clapped, and Nari has not played the chime.

ODO: Rehearsal image only. No sound cue recorded.

VEX: But the line was excellent.

PIP: It was the written line, delivered before the screen changed to three.

MARA: We can wait for the screen. The elevator car remains stationary throughout the shot.

NARI: The chime will play only after Odo changes the floor graphic. No one needs to ride the real elevator.

ODO: Battery is at twenty percent. One recorded take is available before it needs charging.

VEX: We need twelve takes.

MARA: We need one correct take. The actor will say the written line when the graphic shows three.

PIP: I'll stand on the mark and wait for the chime.

Vex steps behind the monitor. He starts to call a countdown, then Odo raises a hand to silence him.

ODO: Floor graphic is set to one. We will cue two, then three. Camera rolls after the slate.

MARA: The setup is ready. Nobody resets the image after recording begins.

SCENE 3. INT. STUDIO CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Odo places the rehearsal clip on the monitor. Vex watches Pip's early line and calls for a dramatic elevator lurch. Mara mutes the playback before Nari can add an unplanned crash sound.

VEX: A wrong-floor discovery needs danger. Tilt the camera; shake the car.

MARA: The set is stationary. A tilt would show the studio wall, and shaking it could loosen the door track.

ODO: We have a safe graphic for the floor change. The actor can sell the surprise with a look.

PIP: I can wait for the number. I don't need the floor to move beneath me.

NARI: The bell cue is loaded. It sounds once after the graphic settles.

Vex asks for a second bell to suggest panic. Nari plays the sound once through the monitor, then points to the cue sheet: one chime, one reaction. Vex reluctantly crosses out the extra effect.

VEX: One chime. But make it the most consequential chime in cinema.

MARA: It is a lobby bell. We are ready for one recorded take.

Odo swaps the rehearsal card for the slate. Pip returns to the marked position; no one resets after the camera rolls.

SCENE 3A. INT. STUDIO ELEVATOR - CONTINUOUS

The studio holds still for the slate. Vex wants to whisper an extra line through the set speaker, but Mara disconnects the speaker before the take so Pip will hear only the agreed cue.

VEX: What if the elevator voice announces a floor that does not exist?

ODO: We have a printed list of floors one through five. There is no extra graphic loaded.

NARI: Sound has one bell and one cue. I will not add a second voice after the slate.

PIP: Then I can react to the number on the screen, which is what the scene is about.

VEX: You are all defending reality from cinema.

MARA: We are defending the take from surprises that were not rehearsed.

Vex steps away from the speaker cable and lets Odo position the slate. The battery indicator remains at twenty percent. Nobody starts over; this is the setup for the single recorded take.

SCENE 4. INT. STUDIO ELEVATOR - DAY

The doors close, screen changes to three, and the chime sounds. Pip delivers the line. Mara keeps the shot steady.

PIP: We have arrived at the wrong floor.

ODO: That is floor three. The line is wrong.

VEX: Perfect. The performance has found its contradiction.

MARA: We have the take. Stop the camera.

TAG: Vex steps into the real studio elevator and reads the buttons twice before choosing the lobby.
