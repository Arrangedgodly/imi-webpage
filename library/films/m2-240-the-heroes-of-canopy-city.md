# The Heroes of Canopy City

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: superhero origin films, secret identities, team-up montages, and a final battle won by ordinary primate skills*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
CAPTAIN CLIMB, a gibbon with a climbing harness
TINY THUNDER, a macaque with an echoing shout
THE FINDER, a capuchin who locates lost objects
MAYOR FERN, a ring-tailed lemur
PHOTOGRAPHER, a tamarin
REPORTERS AND MAINTENANCE CREW, macaques and capuchins

SCENE 1. EXT. CANOPY CITY - DAY

A shadow sweeps across the rooftops. The city's giant acorn sculpture has disappeared. Mayor Fern calls the heroes to the central plaza. Captain Climb descends from a tower on a rope. Tiny Thunder arrives by shouting, "I am here," and hearing it echo back from three alleys.

MAYOR FERN: The acorn statue is gone.

THE FINDER: I found it.

MAYOR FERN: Where?

THE FINDER: I found the empty place where it was.

The team examines the pavement. A clean circle remains where the statue stood.

SCENE 2. INT. HERO HEADQUARTERS - AFTERNOON

The heroes review their origin montage. Captain Climb recalls learning to climb by reaching a high shelf. Tiny Thunder learned to project his voice by calling across a canyon. The Finder learned to locate lost things after misplacing his own hat.

CAPTAIN CLIMB: We need a clue.

TINY THUNDER: A clue!

His echo returns from the next room, where a teacup falls off a table. The Finder looks through the open headquarters door and spots a trail of acorn-colored paint flakes on the pavement.

SCENE 3. EXT. ROOFTOPS - SUNSET

The heroes follow the trail. Captain Climb moves across the rooftops. Tiny Thunder calls down alleys, creating a citywide echo. The team hears the acorn sculpture rolling somewhere below.

THE FINDER: It is moving toward the river.

CAPTAIN CLIMB: How do you know?

THE FINDER: I found the trail.

The team leaps from roof to roof, but the sculpture rolls through a market square, knocking over empty baskets. Everyone assumes it is a villain attack.

SCENE 4. INT. RIVER TUNNEL - NIGHT

The heroes find the sculpture wedged beneath a low tunnel arch. A flock of pigeons has built a nest inside it. Tiny Thunder shouts, and the echo makes the pigeons flap, shifting the statue deeper into the tunnel.

MAYOR FERN: Can you lift it?

CAPTAIN CLIMB: It is tangled in the nest.

THE FINDER: The pigeons have made it their headquarters.

The team backs away. The Finder offers a handful of seeds. The pigeons exit one by one. Captain Climb ties a rope around the statue, and Mayor Fern helps pull from the other side.

SCENE 5. EXT. CANOPY CITY PLAZA - DAWN

The statue returns to the plaza. The team discovers the original bolts were never fastened; the acorn had rolled away during a maintenance check. The city repairs the base. The pigeons keep the nest, now relocated to a nearby tree.

TINY THUNDER: We saved the city.

MAYOR FERN: You found a loose statue.

THE FINDER: That is one way to save it.

SCENE 6. EXT. CANOPY CITY PLAZA - LATER

The city holds a press conference. Mayor Fern introduces the heroes with a dramatic smoke machine. The smoke drifts into the hollow acorn sculpture and exits through a vent beneath its stem. The reporters ask who moved the statue.

CAPTAIN CLIMB: It was not stolen.

THE FINDER: It rolled.

MAYOR FERN: It has been returned and bolted down.

A reporter asks for the villain's name. The heroes have none. Tiny Thunder repeats the question and hears his echo answer "maintenance." The crowd turns toward the crew fixing the base.

SCENE 7. INT. HERO HEADQUARTERS - EVENING

The team returns to headquarters. Captain Climb places his rope back on the hook. The Finder checks the hat rack and finds three hats that are not his. Mayor Fern brings a box of bolts and asks the heroes to inspect the other city statues.

TINY THUNDER: Are they in danger?

MAYOR FERN: They are standing on uneven bases.

Mayor Fern opens the box. Captain Climb reaches for a bolt as if drawing a sacred weapon. The Finder labels the box "EVIDENCE"; the mayor crosses that out and writes "SUPPLIES."

SCENE 7A. EXT. CITY FOUNTAIN - LATER

The Finder locates a loose plaque. Captain Climb ties a rope around it and strikes a rescue pose. A crew member tightens its screw behind his elbow. Tiny Thunder calls out the next landmark; his echo knocks Captain Climb's cape into the fountain.

MAYOR FERN: The Hardware Heroes have completed their first patrol.

CAPTAIN CLIMB: Do the bolts come with capes?

MAYOR FERN: The capes are on back order.

The Finder retrieves the wet cape. Captain Climb wraps it around the supply box so the photographer will have one heroic object to frame.

SCENE 8. EXT. CANOPY CITY PLAZA - NEXT MORNING

The heroes pose for a team photograph. Captain Climb hangs from a rope, Tiny Thunder shouts the team's name, and the Finder looks for his missing hat. The photographer counts down.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera flash reveals the Finder's hat on his own head, while the acorn statue remains firmly bolted behind them.
