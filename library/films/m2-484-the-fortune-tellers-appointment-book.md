# The Fortune Teller's Appointment Book

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: supernatural mystery films that treat a mundane administrative mix-up as a vision of fate*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A fortune teller's appointments appear to predict a palace disaster, until the clients discover the ink has run across two pages and everyone is due at the wrong hour.

Cast: fortune teller Sela, a macaque; assistant Domi, a lemur; palace messenger Venn, a gibbon; seamstress Oda, an orangutan; visitor Kiri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - INT. FORTUNE TELLING TENT - MORNING

Sela sits before a glass orb and a tall appointment book. Domi keeps the curtain open for the next visitor. The book's pages are damp at one corner.

SELA: I foresee a visitor at eleven, carrying a silver thread.

DOMI: It is ten. Oda is waiting outside with thread.

Oda enters carrying a spool. Sela studies her palm and says she will repair a hem before noon. Oda glances at the appointment book; her time is crossed out and rewritten as eleven.

ODA: The book says eleven. The hem says now.

Sela turns the page. The next entry reads "Messenger, urgent, at 11:00." The ink has bled from the previous line, making the time look like a warning.

SCENE 2 - INT. PALACE HALL - LATER

Venn arrives with a sealed note. He asks for Sela's prediction about the palace clock. Sela sees the entry and says a bell will ring before the hands meet. Venn looks at the wall clock, which is missing its minute hand. He says a bell is already ringing in the courtyard. Sela asks Domi to write down the omen.

DOMI: The bell is the lunch bell.

SELA: It rings before the hands meet.

Venn opens the note. It is a request to repair the clock before the noon ceremony. Domi observes the book has three entries at eleven. The wet ink has spread the first entry across the next line, and the page edge has stuck to the sheet underneath.

SCENE 3 - INT. FORTUNE TELLING TENT - AFTERNOON

Kiri arrives for a palm reading. Sela tells her the cards show a sudden journey. Kiri pulls out a folded bus ticket and says she is going to visit her aunt. Sela adjusts the prediction: the journey will be successful if the bus leaves on time.

KIRI: It leaves in ten minutes.

DOMI: The book has you down for eleven.

Kiri points at the damp page. The one has run into the next entry, turning ten into eleven. Sela takes the orb from its stand and finds a ring of water beneath it. The orb has been sweating onto the paper.

SELA: My visions have become very literal.

Kiri asks for a receipt. Sela writes one and the ink begins to spread. Domi lifts the book and discovers the tent's condensation has soaked the schedule from the back.

Domi lays the wet sheets on the table and tries to separate them with playing cards. Sela insists the order must remain mysterious. One damp page sticks to the back of a card and flips over, showing tomorrow's appointments. Venn is listed twice, once as "messenger" and once as "man with envelope."

VENN: That is the same appointment.

SELA: The cards have confirmed your double appearance.

Domi peels the card away. The ink has transferred a small black mustache onto Venn's portrait in the book. He turns the page around so the mustache faces the wall. Oda returns with the repaired clock hand and asks where she should leave the tool kit. Sela says she foresaw its arrival, but Domi points to the written note she made earlier.

SCENE 4 - INT. PALACE CLOCK ROOM - LATE AFTERNOON

The group carries the appointment book to the clock room. Venn and Oda hold the pages apart while Domi copies the correct times onto dry cards. Sela tries to make a grand prediction about the clock's repair. Venn hands her the minute hand, found in the note envelope.

VENN: The future is in this envelope.

Sela fits the hand back on the clock. Oda tightens the center nut. The clock begins ticking, but its chime is disconnected and sits on a workbench. The group tests the mechanism. The hands meet at twelve; nothing rings.

SELA: The bell has changed its mind.

DOMI: It is unplugged.

Venn reconnects the small chime cable. The clock rings once. Everyone ducks, including Sela, who had predicted a sudden sound but not from the ceiling.

SCENE 5 - INT. FORTUNE TELLING TENT - EVENING

Sela replaces the wet appointment book with a dry calendar. Domi moves the orb onto a cork mat away from the papers. Oda finishes the hem, and Kiri catches her bus. Venn takes the corrected schedule to the palace. Sela turns to the camera as if addressing an unseen audience.

SELA: I foresee fewer appointments at eleven.

Domi writes her down for ten.

FINAL IMAGE: The new calendar hangs far from the orb, while the old appointment book lies open beneath a drying rack with every time blurred into a single dramatic hour.
