# The Mayor Woke Up in the Mascot Suit

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: body-swap comedies that use identity confusion to reveal character through escalating public performances*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A town mayor and the festival mascot trade roles after a costume mishap, and each discovers that the other's day is mostly paperwork and very warm fabric.

Cast: Mayor Leda, a macaque; mascot performer Poro, a gibbon; aide Tavi, a lemur; deputy Beni, a capuchin; town announcer Koro, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. FESTIVAL DRESSING ROOM - MORNING

The mayor arrives to record a welcome message. Poro is already inside the oversized otter mascot suit. A fan in the suit has stopped working.

PORO: I need a minute to cool down.

LEDA: I need the otter head for the parade entrance.

TAVI: The head is on the chair. Please do not put it on the wrong person.

Leda lifts the mascot head. Poro, trying to reach the fan switch, puts on the mayor's ceremonial sash instead. They catch sight of each other in the mirror.

PORO: I look official.

LEDA: I look like a river animal.

The door opens. Koro enters and sees the mayor-sized otter and the sash-wearing performer.

KORO: The mayor is ready for the parade.

SCENE 2 - EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MOMENTS LATER

The mascot suit waddles toward the stage. Inside, Leda cannot see well. Poro follows wearing the sash, carrying the mayor's folder.

TAVI: Mayor, your notes are in the folder.

PORO: I can speak from the heart.

He opens the folder anyway. It contains the parade route, a budget and a list of street closures. He reads a line about the rubbish pickup schedule.

PORO: The town will have its bins collected on Tuesday.

The crowd applauds, thinking it is a joke. Inside the suit, Leda waves too broadly and knocks a paper flower off the stage.

SCENE 2A - EXT. TOWN SQUARE STAGE - CONTINUOUS

The announcer hands the sash-wearing Poro a microphone. Poro sees the prepared speech on the paper and reads it upside down.

PORO: Citizens, thank you for attending the budget review.

KORO: This is the festival welcome.

PORO: I have the wrong page.

He turns the paper over and finds a grocery list for lantern oil, water and bananas. The crowd laughs when he reads the bananas aloud.

PORO: I can promise we have bananas.

Tavi signals him to step aside. Leda, inside the suit, tries to mime "the other folder" with both padded paws. Poro holds up the parade route instead.

LEDA: That is not my speech.

PORO: It is a very clear route.

Koro takes the microphone and announces the parade will begin after the mayor finds her notes. The crowd stays patient, watching the mascot silently point toward the correct folder on the chair.

SCENE 3 - INT. TOWN HALL - LATER

Poro sits at the mayor's desk. He tries to answer a question about the festival budget. The mascot head rests on a chair beside him.

BENI: The budget committee needs your signature on the lantern rental.

PORO: How many lanterns?

BENI: Forty.

PORO: Could we have fewer lanterns and more fans for the mascot suit?

Beni laughs, then realizes Poro is serious. Across the hall, Leda in the suit struggles through the mayor's office door. She bumps the frame and drops the route map.

LEDA: I need someone to hold this door.

TAVI: The otter has a request.

SCENE 4 - EXT. FESTIVAL ROUTE - AFTERNOON

The parade begins. Leda moves carefully in the costume and points out a blocked curb to the crew. Poro, wearing the sash, directs the route from the sidewalk. He lets a delivery cart cross before the band passes.

KORO: The mayor has ordered a pause.

PORO: The cart is carrying the stage's water jugs. Let it through.

The crowd waits. The band leader lowers the baton. Leda helps push the cart over a small pavement ridge, then turns back toward the parade. The mascot head slips sideways, revealing her face.

LEDA: The suit is hotter than I expected.

PORO: The sash is heavier than it looks.

They swap places behind a screen. Leda takes her notes and Poro puts the mascot head on with the fan restored.

SCENE 5 - INT. FESTIVAL STAGE - EVENING

The mayor makes her welcome address. Poro stands beside her as the otter mascot. He bows to the crowd, careful not to knock over the microphone. Beni hands Leda the corrected route plan and the lantern invoice.

LEDA: The parade was delayed for a delivery cart, and the water arrived on time. Thank you to everyone who waited.

Poro waves. The suit's fan whirs audibly. He holds a handwritten sign: PLEASE WAIT WHILE THE OTTER BREATHES.

Koro removes the mayor's sash from the chair and hangs it on its labeled hook. Tavi labels the costume rack with the correct names.

FINAL IMAGE: The mayor's sash and the otter head hang side by side on separate hooks, each with a clear name tag and a small fan between them.
