# Ferry Through the Fog

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: nautical suspense adventures with a lost channel marker, a stubborn captain, and a crew navigating by sound*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: CAPTAIN VELA, an orangutan; NERI, a macaque deckhand; HOB, a baboon cook; LISS, a gibbon signal keeper; PAX, a capuchin passenger.

EXT. HARBOR - NIGHT

Fog rolls across the water. A small ferry waits at the pier. Captain Vela checks the compass. Its needle turns slowly.

CAPTAIN VELA: The west marker is gone.

NERI: The channel is still there.

HOB: The soup is still hot.

LISS: The harbor bell has stopped.

Pax stands on the deck with a folded map.

PAX: The map says to follow the bell.

VELA: The map is old.

PAX: It says "updated last week."

The ferry's horn sounds. The echo comes back from two directions.

ACT ONE

INT. FERRY CABIN - NIGHT

Vela asks the passengers to remain seated. Neri checks the stern rope and finds it snagged around the dock post. The ferry cannot turn until the rope is free.

NERI: The stern is tied to the pier.

HOB: We have not left yet.

Liss climbs the signal ladder and strikes the hand bell. The sound fades into fog. A second bell answers from the north channel.

LISS: That is not our bell.

VELA: It is the old buoy.

They compare the sounds. The old buoy rings twice, then pauses. The harbor bell rings once and has a lower pitch. Neri uses a wooden mallet to tap the ferry's rail. The echo returns from the near seawall.

PAX: We can map the echoes.

VELA: We can also wait for the fog to lift.

PAX: The ferry leaves at midnight.

Vela checks the tide table. The water will shift the channel in twenty minutes. Waiting could push them against the pier. She orders the engine to idle and sends Neri to release the stern rope.

A gust lifts the map from Pax's hands. Hob catches it with a soup ladle. The map lands in a bowl, soaking the corner.

HOB: The route is now seasoned.

Pax wipes the paper. A small blue mark appears beneath the wet ink, showing the old buoy's position.

The ferry drifts toward a sandbar. Vela turns the engine to idle and asks Pax to watch the waterline through the side window. Hob ties the soup pot to the counter so it cannot slide. Neri checks the depth with a long pole.

NERI: We are one boat-width from the shoal.

VELA: Can we turn into the channel?

LISS: The north bell is still answering.

Pax listens. The north bell rings twice, then stops. A second echo comes from the seawall. Vela uses the two sounds to hold the ferry between the rock edge and the channel mouth. The engine shakes as it changes direction.

HOB: The soup has turned with us.

VELA: Keep the pot level.

The ferry clears the sandbar, but the current pulls the stern sideways. Neri releases a spare line and catches it around a deck cleat. Pax steadies the map with both hands. They can now see the lantern of the far pier through the fog.

PAX: The map says the pier is east.

VELA: The sound says it is ahead.

ACT TWO

Liss strikes the hand bell once. The old buoy answers twice from the north. Neri frees the stern rope. Captain Vela turns the ferry toward the seawall, then listens for the echo to return from the channel.

VELA: The seawall echo is close. Keep the bow on that side until the buoy answers.

The ferry moves at idle speed. The old buoy rings twice. Vela turns the wheel, and the ferry clears the rocks. Pax stands beside the window, calling out each marker as it passes.

PAX: Buoy one, left. Buoy two, right.

NERI: There is no second buoy.

PAX: Then the map is two buoys ahead.

Hob serves soup. The bowls slide as the ferry rocks. Liss catches one against the rail and braces the pot with a rope.

They reach the far pier. A harbor worker confirms the channel marker has drifted. Vela ties the ferry to the dock and radios the harbor office to replace the marker before the next crossing.

VELA: We made the crossing. The marker will be fixed before dawn.

LISS: The harbor bell still needs a new clapper.

HOB: I have a soup ladle.

Liss rings the hand bell once to signal safe arrival. The passengers step ashore in a line. Pax folds the map and tucks it into a waterproof sleeve.

FINAL IMAGE: The ferry rests at the far pier while the fog lifts enough to reveal the missing marker drifting nearby.
