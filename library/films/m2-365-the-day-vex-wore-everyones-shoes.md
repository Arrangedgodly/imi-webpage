# The Day Vex Wore Everyone's Shoes

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: body-swap comedies, identity mix-ups, exaggerated walk cycles, and characters who learn their lesson by borrowing each other's footwear*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
VEX, a macaque dance teacher
PIP, a capuchin courier
LUMA, a lemur mechanic
NARI, an orangutan judge
ODO, a gibbon cobbler

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY HALL - MORNING

Vex arrives to teach a dance class. He finds a pair of polished shoes by the door and puts them on. They are several sizes too large. Pip enters with a parcel and mistakes Vex for the cobbler. Luma arrives in work boots, carrying a small toolbox. Nari is preparing for a costume contest.

VEX: These shoes are unusually expressive.

ODO: They are mine.

The shoes belong to Odo, who has left them by the repair bench. Vex tries to return them but steps into a dance pose, sliding across the hall and colliding gently with the coat rack.

SCENE 2. INT. COSTUME ROOM - LATER

A gust blows the labels off the shoe boxes. Everyone puts on the wrong pair while trying to sort them. Pip wears Nari's dress shoes, Nari wears Luma's work boots, and Luma wears Vex's tap shoes. Vex is still in Odo's oversized pair. They all attempt to walk normally and produce different rhythms.

NARI: This is not a dance.

LUMA: It is a diagnostic sound.

Odo checks the soles. A loose tap plate has fallen from Vex's shoes and stuck to one boot. He repairs it, but now the shoe makes a click every time Vex shifts weight. The rhythm becomes a beat. Pip starts tapping his parcel; Nari counts it as a dance step.

SCENE 2A. INT. COMMUNITY HALL - MIDDAY

The class rehearses a simple dance while the wrong shoes remain on. Each character's movement changes. Nari cannot turn in Luma's heavy boots. Luma's tap shoes create a beat that makes Pip step in time. Vex tries to teach a spin, but his oversized shoes carry him across the floor and into a stack of folded costumes. The pile lands neatly on a chair.

ODO: The shoes have found their place.

VEX: I have found the floor.

Odo checks the shoe boxes one by one. The name tags have fallen inside the lids, so he matches each pair to its owner by tread marks and foot shape. Pip's courier shoes have a patch on the left heel. Nari's pair has a small paint mark from the contest stage. Vex recognizes his shoes by the tap plates. The crew returns each pair and checks the size before putting it on.

SCENE 3. INT. COMMUNITY HALL - AFTERNOON

The costume contest begins. Each participant must walk across the stage. Vex tries to lead the class in a dramatic runway walk, but his shoes keep sliding. Nari's boots are too heavy to turn. Luma's tap shoes make a clatter that echoes through the hall. Pip's parcel is delivered to the wrong table because he cannot walk in the oversized shoes.

Odo checks the labels and discovers the shoes were all moved to the costume room by a volunteer. The boxes were organized by color instead of size. He asks everyone to stand by their own shoes and read the name tags. They exchange footwear, one pair at a time.

SCENE 3A. INT. COMMUNITY HALL - LATE AFTERNOON

The contest judge asks the participants to walk once more in their own shoes. Odo repairs the loose heel pad on the bench. The judge waits while each contestant tests their own pair once. Vex walks the runway without sliding, though the repaired tap plate gives one loud click at the wrong end of the music cue. The result is slower but steady, with no one stumbling for the camera.

SCENE 4. INT. WORKSHOP - EVENING

Vex asks Odo why he keeps so many shoes. Odo repairs footwear for dancers, workers, and visitors. Each pair is fit for a different task. Luma says she wore the tap shoes because the label was hidden. Pip says the right fit makes a long delivery easier. Nari says the contest judges should score comfort along with appearance.

The group checks every box and writes the wearer's name and size on each label. Odo installs a shelf for repaired shoes. Vex returns the large pair and discovers his own shoes are still on the dance floor. He walks back to get them without sliding.

SCENE 5. INT. COMMUNITY HALL - NIGHT

The costume contest restarts. Nari walks in her own shoes, Luma in her boots, Pip in courier shoes, and Vex in tap shoes. The repaired tap plate gives a clear beat. The participants turn it into a group dance. Odo does not compete; he watches from the repair bench.

FINAL IMAGE: A row of labeled shoes waits beneath the door as the hall empties, each pair pointed toward its owner's exit.
