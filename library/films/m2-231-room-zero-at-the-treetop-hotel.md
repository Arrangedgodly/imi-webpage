# Room Zero at the Treetop Hotel

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: hotel farces, mistaken room assignments, revolving doors, and a manager who insists every inconvenience is an upgrade*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
MILO, a macaque night manager
RENA, a capuchin bell attendant
BEX, a gibbon guest
PIPER, a ring-tailed lemur concierge

SCENE 1. INT. TREETOP HOTEL LOBBY - EVENING

A storm sends guests into the Treetop Hotel. Night manager Milo checks the room board. Every room is occupied except Room Zero, a tiny maintenance closet beneath the stairs.

BEX: I reserved a room with a view.

MILO: Room Zero has a view of the stairs.

PIPER: The stairs are historic.

Rena carries three suitcases at once, one balanced on her head. A fourth suitcase rolls down the steps, opens, and releases a dozen socks. The guests applaud her attempt to catch them.

SCENE 2. INT. ROOM ZERO - LATER

Bex enters Room Zero. The closet contains a mop, a bucket, and a small round window looking directly at the tree roots. Milo calls it a private nature view. Bex asks for a larger room. Piper shows him a map of the hotel, which has more rooms than the building.

PIPER: The map includes future expansions.

BEX: I need a bed tonight.

Rena suggests moving Bex into the ballroom. Milo agrees, but another guest is already asleep in the coat-check booth, using a hat as a pillow.

SCENE 3. INT. BALLROOM - NIGHT

The lobby's revolving door jams. Guests enter the ballroom and leave through the same doorway, each time carrying someone else's luggage. Rena sorts the bags by sound: one suitcase rattles, one squeaks, and one contains a snoring cat.

MILO: The hotel is experiencing a room shuffle.

BEX: I am experiencing no room.

Piper sees a guest holding a pillow and assumes the ballroom has become a suite. He sells the guest a room-service menu, but the chef arrives with a broom and a pot of soup.

SCENE 4. INT. STAIRWELL - LATER

Rena finds a door behind the linen cart. It opens onto a quiet attic room with a real window and a bed. The room is not on the map. Milo says it was sealed after a wallpaper dispute.

BEX: Is there a view?

RENA: Of the tree canopy.

BEX: Is the wallpaper historic?

RENA: It is peeling.

Milo reaches for a key. Piper objects that the room is unlisted. Bex pulls the door open and discovers a small balcony. The whole group crowds onto it, then remembers Bex needs the bed and retreats.

SCENE 5. INT. HOTEL LOBBY - DAWN

The storm stops. Milo gives Bex the attic room. The guests have sorted the suitcases and returned the socks. The cat has woken and claimed the ballroom stage. Piper adds Room Zero to the hotel map as a "private stair view suite."

BEX: Please call it a closet.

PIPER: That is a very short suite name.

SCENE 6. INT. ATTIC ROOM - MORNING

Rena delivers a tray of tea. Bex opens the window and sees the whole canopy below. The hotel is finally quiet, except for the revolving door, which continues to turn by itself.

MILO: We should repair it.

PIPER: It is our new lobby attraction.

SCENE 7. INT. HOTEL LOBBY - LATER THAT MORNING

A new guest arrives carrying a suitcase and asks for the private nature-view suite. Piper offers Room Zero. The guest opens the door, sees the mop, and closes it. Milo brings the attic key and leads the guest upstairs. Rena demonstrates how to open the window without knocking loose the peeling wallpaper.

PIPER: We can sell both rooms.

MILO: We can call one a room and one a closet.

The guest unpacks. A sock falls from the suitcase and rolls down the stairs, through the revolving door, and into the lobby. The hotel's new attraction begins spinning it around. Rena catches the sock and returns it to the guest, who requests a view of the lobby.

MILO: The lobby has a sock.

PIPER: A rotating sock.

The guest takes a seat by the door. Piper puts a small sign beside him explaining the carousel schedule. Milo removes the sign.

SCENE 8. INT. ATTIC ROOM - AFTERNOON

Milo updates the room board. He writes ROOM ZERO beside the closet, then ROOM ONE beside the attic. Piper adds a tiny note: "private roots view." Rena asks whether the hotel has a room with a view of the canopy. Milo points upward, but the ceiling has no window.

BEX: The tree roots were a view.

RENA: They were a very private view.

The new guest arrives from the revolving door, carrying the sock carousel sign. Milo takes it down and turns the sign into a luggage tag. Piper offers a chair beside the window instead of a new room.

FINAL IMAGE: The revolving door slowly spins a single sock around the lobby while Milo adds "sock carousel" to the guest guide.
