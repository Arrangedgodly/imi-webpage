# The Winter Festival with No Snow

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: holiday specials, artificial snow, last-minute miracles, and festivals that proceed because someone updates the program*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
FESTIVAL CHAIR LUMA, a lemur
WEATHER KEEPER NARI, an orangutan
DECORATOR PIP, a capuchin
MAYOR VEX, a macaque
STORYTELLER ODO, a gibbon
CHILD, a young macaque festival visitor

SCENE 1. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MORNING

The winter festival is scheduled for the first snowfall. The square is green. Festival chair Luma checks the forecast; it predicts rain. Decorator Pip unpacks paper snowflakes. Mayor Vex asks if they can make artificial snow. Weather keeper Nari says the snow machine is broken and the outdoor power line is wet.

VEX: The festival needs snow.

ODO: The festival needs visitors.

A gust blows the paper snowflakes off the table and onto the mayor's coat.

SCENE 2. INT. TOWN HALL - LATER

The committee debates canceling the festival. Odo suggests moving the story hour indoors. Pip proposes stringing paper snowflakes across the hall. Vex says the event will look like a meeting. Luma checks the schedule and moves the food stalls under the covered arcade. Nari updates the weather notice.

The committee tests the paper decorations. They stick to the wall with tape, then fall when the heater turns on. Pip folds the snowflakes into garlands and hangs them away from the vents. The room looks festive without pretending it is snowing.

SCENE 2A. INT. TOWN HALL - NOON

The committee opens the supply closet. It contains paper lanterns, a box of winter costumes, and an artificial snow machine with a cracked hose. Vex says they can use the machine indoors. Nari checks the label and points out it uses dry powder that should not be inhaled. Luma decides to leave it unplugged. Pip finds a stack of blue fabric and turns it into a backdrop for the story stage.

ODO: It can be a winter sky.

VEX: It is a blue blanket.

ODO: A winter sky can be a blue blanket.

The committee moves the stage away from the door so the food line can pass without crowding. Nari updates the program. The snow machine is listed as unavailable, not as a surprise finale.

SCENE 3. EXT. COVERED ARCADE - AFTERNOON

Rain begins. The stalls open under the roof. Vex asks the band to play a winter march. The brass section gets wet near the edge, so Nari moves them farther under cover. Odo reads a story about a snow fox, pausing while a child points to the actual rain outside.

ODO: In the story, the fox waits for the snow.

CHILD: What does it do in the rain?

ODO: It finds an umbrella.

Pip distributes paper umbrellas folded from leftover programs. The rain keeps falling, but people stay for the music. Luma asks the food stall to move its soup pot away from the wet floor. Vex tries to light the artificial snow machine. Nari unplugs it and hands him a broom to clear the water.

SCENE 3A. EXT. COVERED ARCADE - AFTERNOON

The food stalls serve soup and warm bread. Pip hangs the folded paper snowflakes above the line, where the breeze makes them turn. Vex asks a volunteer to toss shredded paper for a snowfall effect. Luma stops the volunteer and points to the soup pots and the slippery floor. Nari has the floor dried and places a mat near the entrance.

The band plays under the roof. One trombone slide catches on a garland. Odo helps free it and asks the band to move two feet away from the decorations. The song continues. A child offers the musicians a paper umbrella. They set it beside the music stand as a prop for the story.

SCENE 4. INT. TOWN HALL - EVENING

The festival wraps up indoors. The forecast says the rain will continue through the night. Luma announces a new date for the outdoor parade, but the indoor music and story program is complete. Vex gives the volunteers a thank-you speech. It is short enough that no one has to stand near the leaking doorway. He tries to end with a ceremonial snowfall, but the paper flakes stick to his damp sleeve. A child asks if the forecast changed. Vex peels one off and hangs it back on Pip's garland.

Nari files the updated safety plan. Pip collects the paper snowflakes and stores them for next year. Odo signs books for the children. Vex asks whether the event can still be called a winter festival. Luma says it is a winter festival, whether the snow comes or not.

SCENE 5. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - NIGHT

The rain stops briefly. A single real snowflake lands on the mayor's sleeve and melts. Vex holds out his hand for another. None comes. He returns inside and helps stack chairs.

FINAL IMAGE: The square shines with rain beneath paper snowflakes visible through the hall windows.
