# The Riverboat Sails Backward

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: river adventures where the crew mistakes a stern-facing camera for reversed travel*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
CAPTAIN MARA, a macaque
CAMERAPERSON VEX, an orangutan
DECKHAND PIP, a capuchin
NAVIGATOR ODO, a gibbon
GUIDE NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. RIVERBOAT DECK - MORNING

The boat moves downstream. Vex films from the stern, showing the wake behind them.

VEX: We are traveling backward.

MARA: The bow is forward. You're filming the stern.

ODO: Current is moving south. Our route follows it to the landing.

PIP: The mooring line is in the deck box. The rail is clear.

NARI: The landing is three bends ahead.

SCENE 2. INT. RIVERBOAT CABIN - CONTINUOUS

Vex studies his footage. The shoreline appears to move toward the boat.

VEX: The river is pulling us into the past.

MARA: The camera is pointed backward. The boat is moving downstream.

ODO: Compass agrees. Heading south.

PIP: I can switch the camera to the bow. Keep the operator behind the rail.

NARI: The next bend has a marker buoy. We'll pass it on the right.

SCENE 2A. INT. RIVERBOAT CABIN - MORNING

Odo lays the route chart on the table. Mara points to the bend and the landing marker. Vex turns the camera monitor toward himself.

MARA: We travel downstream to the inspection buoy, then turn upstream to dock. The boat's bow remains pointed in the direction of travel.

VEX: The camera makes it look as if the shore is approaching us from behind.

ODO: That is because the camera faces the stern. The compass reading and water markers agree.

PIP: I can move the camera to the forward mount once the boat slows at the buoy.

NARI: Keep the strap attached to the camera. Do not lean over the rail to reach the mount.

Vex adjusts the monitor. The riverbank slides across the screen in the opposite direction.

VEX: The footage is traveling backward.

MARA: The camera is pointed behind the boat. The boat itself is moving forward downstream.

Odo marks the buoy on the chart. Pip checks the rope box and closes its latch.

PIP: Bow line is inside the box. Stern line is coiled separately.

NARI: Landing is clear. No other boat is using the approach.

Mara returns the chart to its sleeve and takes the wheel.

SCENE 2B. EXT. RIVER - LATE MORNING

The marker buoy comes into view. Mara slows the boat before the bend and keeps the bow facing downstream. Vex mounts the camera on the forward rail and narrates the shoreline as if the river were chasing them.

VEX: We have outrun the landscape.

MARA: We have reached the buoy. Pip, confirm the landing is clear before I turn upstream.

PIP: Landing is empty. Bow line is ready; stern line stays coiled until we reach the dock.

ODO: Current is pushing from behind. The engine is steady, and the buoy is on our right as charted.

Vex watches the water move toward the forward camera. He swivels the camera to the stern, then sees the shoreline slide the opposite way again.

VEX: The river changes its mind every time I turn around.

NARI: The river keeps flowing south. You keep pointing the camera in different directions.

MARA: We have one turn to make. Keep both lines aboard until we are alongside the landing.

The boat passes the buoy once. Mara turns upstream toward the landing. The camera stays secured to the bow mount; Vex holds the monitor instead of leaning over the rail.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIVERBOAT DECK - LATER

The boat is headed upstream from the buoy toward the landing. Vex films from the bow this time. The water flows toward the camera.

VEX: The river is coming at us.

MARA: The boat is moving into the current now; I turned upstream for the landing.

ODO: Engine is steady. We are not drifting.

PIP: The rope is ready at the bow cleat.

SCENE 3A. INT. RIVERBOAT CABIN - CONTINUOUS

Mara keeps one hand on the wheel as the landing comes into view. Odo holds the chart flat while the boat rides the bend. Vex narrates the approach into the camera microphone.

VEX: We face the river's wrath.

NARI: We face the landing. The river is flowing past us, and the dock is on the left bank.

PIP: Bow line is ready, stern line still coiled. I will pass the bow line only when Mara says we are alongside.

VEX: The line must be thrown dramatically.

MARA: It must be attached safely. Keep it in your hand until we are close enough.

Vex points the lens at the stern again. The wake appears to chase them.

VEX: The river has caught up.

ODO: The engine is slowing us. The wake is behind the boat, exactly where it should be.

NARI: If you keep filming the wake, it will look as if the landing is following us.

VEX: That is our sequel.

MARA: First we dock once. Then you can film the empty water.

SCENE 4. EXT. LANDING - DAY

Mara eases the boat alongside the landing. Pip secures the bow line; Odo secures the stern line. The engine is neutral.

NARI: Landing reached. The walkway is clear.

VEX: We have traveled both ways through time.

MARA: We traveled downstream, then turned upstream to dock.

TAG: Vex reviews the footage with the bow marker visible. He labels it "river, not reverse."
