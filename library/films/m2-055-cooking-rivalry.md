# The Great Simmer-Off

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: cooking competition films, sabotage reveals, and judges who narrate every bite*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: Two rival macaque cooks face a televised stew challenge whose timed twists keep threatening the meal more than either chef.

Cast: Lalo, a meticulous macaque chef; Bruna, a bold baboon cook; Judge Crumb, a lemur food critic; Host Whisk, a gibbon presenter; and Tess, a capuchin stagehand who controls the kitchen timer.

SCENE 1. INT. STUDIO KITCHEN, DAY

Host Whisk announces a rivalry that has lasted since Lalo borrowed Bruna's ladle three years earlier. Lalo says he returned it. Bruna says he returned it with soup on the handle. The judges unveil the challenge: make a stew using the pantry basket and one mystery ingredient.

WHISK: Your time begins after my dramatic pause.

TESS: The timer started during the pause.

The first ingredient is a jar labeled MYSTERY. Lalo sniffs it carefully. Bruna opens it and finds cinnamon. Judge Crumb calls this a revelation and writes down the word layered before tasting anything.

SCENE 2. INT. STUDIO KITCHEN, LATER

The cooks begin. Lalo measures every spice with a tiny ruler. Bruna roasts vegetables over a portable flame, which sets off a confetti cannon meant for the camera. Whisk calls the explosion a surprise twist. Tess sweeps paper confetti out of the stockpot before anyone can serve it.

Lalo's burner will not light. Bruna offers her flame. Lalo refuses, suspecting sabotage. Bruna lights his burner anyway with the same match she used for her own. The camera zooms on Lalo's suspicious face, then Bruna's, then a close-up of the ordinary match. The audience waits for a villain. None appears.

JUDGE CRUMB: I detect tension.

BRUNA: That's the timer.

SCENE 3. INT. PANTRY SET, MOMENTS LATER

A producer slips a card to Whisk: announce a last-minute twist. He declares the cooks must swap pots. Lalo protests that Bruna's pot is twice as wide. Bruna says it is because she cooks for people, not camera angles. The swap sends Lalo's carefully arranged vegetables into one corner and Bruna's stew across the broad base.

Lalo tastes Bruna's broth and admits it needs salt. She tastes his and says it needs heat. They each reach for the salt cellar at the same time. Their elbows knock over the mystery jar. Cinnamon scatters across the counter. Judge Crumb gasps as if a secret has been exposed.

LALO: It was cinnamon from the beginning.

CRUMB: My notes say cardamom.

Whisk looks at the label. It clearly says CINNAMON. Crumb calls the correction a palate journey.

SCENE 4. INT. STUDIO KITCHEN, FINAL MINUTES

Tess's timer jams. It rings continuously, interrupting Whisk's countdown. He tries to raise the stakes by counting louder. Bruna uses the ringing as a rhythm and stirs in time. Lalo's sauce thickens too quickly, so Bruna adds water. He adds salt to hers. They both plate one pot of stew, each pretending the collaboration was accidental.

The judges taste it. Crumb closes his eyes and describes a childhood memory in which he has never been. He calls the stew courageous. A second judge asks what is in it. He says courage, then quietly asks Tess for the ingredient list.

SCENE 5. INT. STUDIO KITCHEN, AFTER JUDGING

The stage crew sets out bowls for the studio audience. The cameras keep filming, waiting for the cooks to argue over who gets credit. Bruna serves first. Lalo takes a bowl, tastes, and adds a pinch of salt. She adds a spoonful of broth to his. The crew eats standing up because the set has no audience chairs, and they begin rating the stew by whether they want another serving. Everyone does.

Whisk asks for a winner to announce. Tess hands him a clean towel and asks him to wipe the counter. He says that is not his role. Auntie Sal, visiting from the community kitchen, reminds him that the challenge is about cooking. Whisk wipes the counter; Judge Crumb calls the gesture a bold finish.

The cameras turn toward Bruna and Lalo for a handshake. They are busy scraping the pot. Their handshake happens with greasy fingers, leaving a print on the lens hood. Pip wipes it clean while saying the shot is better now.

The winners are announced: the studio crew, who will eat the stew. Lalo and Bruna receive matching aprons that say FRIENDLY RIVALS. They trade the old ladle, now scrubbed clean. Whisk announces a rematch, but Tess removes the confetti cannon and unplugs the timer.

Bruna tells Lalo the borrowed ladle had been hers because he left it in her sink. He says he thought it was communal. She points to her name engraved on the handle. He apologizes and offers to wash it after dinner.

Lalo offers Bruna the old ladle to keep. She hands it back and says it belongs in the kitchen drawer, not a trophy case. The producers put a spotlight on the drawer.

FINAL IMAGE: Judge Crumb asks for the recipe and begins describing it as a secret. The camera pulls back to reveal the full recipe printed on a card taped to his forehead.
