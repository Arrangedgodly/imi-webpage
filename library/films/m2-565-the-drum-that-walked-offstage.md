# The Drum That Walked Offstage

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: backstage capers where a missing instrument is simply being carried to storage*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
BANDLEADER MARA, a macaque
DRUMMER PIP, a capuchin
STAGE MANAGER VEX, an orangutan
RUNNER ODO, a gibbon
USHER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. THEATER BACKSTAGE - EVENING

A marching drum is missing from its stand. Vex shines a flashlight beneath a table.

VEX: The drum has been stolen.

MARA: The concert ended ten minutes ago. We are packing instruments.

PIP: I carried the drum offstage so the crew could roll the riser.

VEX: You have no witness.

ODO: I saw Pip cross through the left wing with the drum and a strap.

NARI: The storage room door is open. I can hear something rolling inside.

SCENE 2. INT. STORAGE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

The team enters. A drum rests on a padded shelf. Another drum rolls gently against a crate.

VEX: There are two drums. One may be the impostor.

PIP: This is the marching drum. That is the bass drum from the orchestra.

MARA: Both have tags. Check the labels before moving them.

ODO: The marching drum's tag says rack three. The bass drum goes on the floor cradle.

VEX: The culprit has left a clue.

NARI: The clue is a storage label.

SCENE 2A. INT. STORAGE ROOM - EVENING

Mara checks the concert inventory. Two drums are on the list; one is marked "strap repair." Pip compares the tags.

PIP: The marching drum has the blue tag. The bass drum has the red tag.

VEX: The missing instrument may have changed colors to hide.

ODO: The tags are tied to the handles. The instruments did not move after I put them on the shelf.

NARI: I have the stage crew's final checklist. The riser was rolled to the left wing at 8:15, then the marching drum went to storage.

MARA: That matches Pip's route. We should look at the strap before we move the drum again.

Pip presses the worn buckle. It slips open. Mara takes the drum's weight while Odo holds the shell steady.

MARA: The buckle is worn through. Don't carry it by this strap.

VEX: The escape mechanism.

PIP: The strap didn't escape. It failed. I'll place it in the repair bin and use the spare sling.

Odo fetches a padded sling from the shelf. Pip threads it through both handles and tightens the buckle. Mara tests it with a small lift.

MARA: It is secure. Keep the drum level and walk; don't swing it.

VEX: The team proceeds with caution.

NARI: The aisle is clear. The storage rack is ready.

SCENE 2B. INT. BACKSTAGE - EVENING

The bandleader lays out the next day's rehearsal schedule. Pip checks the drum's repaired sling against the tag.

MARA: The drum stays on rack three tonight. The strap goes to the repair shop tomorrow.

PIP: Sling is temporary. I'll attach a red note so no one uses the old strap.

VEX: The mystery deepens: one instrument has two carrying systems.

ODO: One is damaged; one is safe. The note makes that clear.

NARI: The stage crew wants the riser moved before morning. We can do that now while the aisle is empty.

MARA: Move the riser first, then close the room. Keep the drum in storage.

The crew rolls the riser to the marked bay. The wheels lock. Pip checks the floor for loose hardware and finds a bolt beside the leg.

PIP: This bolt belongs to the riser wheel. The wheel is still locked, but we should replace it before the next concert.

ODO: I'll put it in the maintenance bag and tag the riser.

VEX: The theater has more clues than I expected.

MARA: It has a checklist. We'll finish that before leaving.

SCENE 3. INT. BACKSTAGE - LATER

Mara finds a loose strap on the marching drum. Pip tests the buckle; it slips.

PIP: This strap needs repair before I carry it again.

VEX: The drum escaped because its restraint failed.

MARA: The strap is worn. Put it in the repair bin and use the spare carry sling.

Odo brings the sling. Pip threads it through the drum's handles and checks the buckle.

ODO: Secure. The drum can move to rack three.

SCENE 4. INT. STORAGE ROOM - NIGHT

Pip places the drum on rack three. Nari checks that the rack is stable. Vex labels the repair bin dramatically.

VEX: Case solved. The missing drum was hiding in plain sight.

MARA: It was carried to storage, where it belonged.

PIP: The concert is over. The instrument is safe.

TAG: Vex turns off the flashlight; the room remains lit. He whispers, "The darkness has been defeated."
