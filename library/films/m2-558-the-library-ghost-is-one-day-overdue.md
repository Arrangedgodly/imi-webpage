# The Library Ghost Is One Day Overdue

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: ghost stories where a catalog error sounds like a haunting*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
LIBRARIAN MARA, a macaque
ARCHIVIST ODO, a gibbon
VISITOR PIP, a capuchin
HOST VEX, an orangutan
PAGE NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. LIBRARY - EVENING

A book slides from a shelf and lands open on the desk. Vex jumps behind Mara.

VEX: The ghost has selected a volume.

MARA: The shelf is tilted. The book was leaning against its neighbor.

ODO: This section is cataloged by subject. The book belongs two shelves over.

PIP: I returned it to the wrong cart earlier. The spine label is smudged.

NARI: The cart wheel is uneven. It rattles when pushed.

VEX: The ghost is moving the cart.

SCENE 2. INT. STACKS - CONTINUOUS

Mara checks the shelf. A book is missing from its place. Odo compares the call number on the label.

ODO: The ghost left an empty space.

MARA: Someone checked the book out yesterday. The space is supposed to be empty.

PIP: The return cart has a stack of books with the same blue cover.

VEX: A message from beyond the shelves.

NARI: It's the new mystery series. Four copies, all due Friday.

SCENE 2A. INT. LIBRARY - EVENING

Mara places a level against the shelf. One foot is shorter than the others. Odo supports the shelf while Pip moves books onto a cart.

MARA: We'll fix the wobble before putting the books back. Nobody should stand under the shelf.

VEX: It is the ghost shifting the floor.

NARI: The building floor is level. The shelf foot is worn.

PIP: The books are in the return cart. I have not scanned them yet.

ODO: This call number is for the history section, not the mystery series. The labels are smudged.

MARA: Separate the two groups and keep their spines visible. We'll update the labels after checking the catalog.

Vex takes a flashlight and shines it across the shelf. A book's shadow lengthens.

VEX: A figure is moving behind the books.

PIP: That's the light you are holding.

Vex lowers the flashlight. The shadow vanishes. Odo places a shim beneath the short shelf foot. Mara tests it with the level.

MARA: Stable. We can reload the shelf from the bottom up so the weight stays low.

NARI: I found the library's repair record. The wheel on the return cart is also due for a washer.

ODO: I'll lock the cart until it is fixed. We shouldn't push it down the aisle with a wobble.

PIP: That explains why it rattled when the book fell. The cart shifted and bumped the shelf.

VEX: A chain of events, not a haunting.

MARA: A shelf, a cart, and one mis-shelved book. Put the mystery series on the correct shelf when the catalog check is done.

SCENE 2B. INT. LIBRARY - EVENING

The books are sorted into two piles. Odo scans the first call number, then compares it with the catalog.

ODO: The label is missing one digit. This title belongs in the history section, not the mystery shelf.

PIP: I'll print a replacement label after I scan the rest.

MARA: Good. The old label goes in the recycling, and the book stays on the return cart until the record matches.

NARI: The cart is locked for repair. We can use the flat table instead of pushing it.

VEX: The ghost has been trapped in the catalog.

MARA: The book has been identified. The catalog is doing its job.

SCENE 2C. INT. LIBRARY - EVENING

The motion sensor lights come back on as Nari enters the aisle. She carries the cart repair tag and points to the wall switch.

NARI: The sensor works. It timed out because nobody moved for fifteen minutes.

VEX: The ghost waits for us to leave.

MARA: It waits for motion. That's what the sensor does.

ODO: The book's due date was yesterday. It was returned today, one day late.

PIP: The scanner beeped because of the late notice, not because the book was cursed.

MARA: We'll put the notice in the book sleeve and shelve it in the correct section after the label is fixed.

VEX: The ghost has a late fee.

NARI: There is no fee. The library stopped charging one.

SCENE 3. INT. LIBRARY - LATER

The lights dim automatically. Vex whispers. Odo reaches for the wall switch.

VEX: The ghost has darkened the room.

MARA: The motion sensor timer expired. We'll reset it after the group leaves.

PIP: The book is in the right section now. I can scan it.

The scanner beeps. Its screen says OVERDUE: 1 DAY.

VEX: The book has returned from the dead.

ODO: It was returned late. The scanner is correct.

SCENE 4. INT. FRONT DESK - NIGHT

Mara updates the catalog record. Nari wheels the cart to the repair bench. Pip checks the due date. Vex holds a flashlight pointed at the floor.

MARA: The shelf is level, the cart wheel is fixed, and the return is recorded.

VEX: Will the ghost come back?

ODO: If a book is shelved wrong, a librarian will move it.

TAG: A book slides from the cart because the repaired wheel turns smoothly. Vex bows to the floor.
