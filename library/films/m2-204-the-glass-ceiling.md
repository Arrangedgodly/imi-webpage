# The Glass Ceiling

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: psychological thrillers, unreliable narration, mirrored rooms, and a protagonist who treats reflections as evidence*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
NARA, a capuchin conservatory guard
VELL, a ring-tailed lemur night curator
SIL, a macaque glass artist

SCENE 1. INT. GLASS CONSERVATORY - NIGHT

Nara walks alone through a greenhouse of glass sculptures. Her reflection follows her from every panel. One reflection appears to move a second late.

NARA (V.O.): The glass had begun to remember me.

She turns. Nothing moves. A warning light blinks over the central dome.

SCENE 2. INT. CENTRAL DOME - CONTINUOUS

The dome is fogged from inside. Fingerprints appear on the glass, one after another. Nara traces them. Vell arrives carrying a bucket and cloth.

VELL: The humidity system is stuck. The glass is fogging and clearing unevenly.

NARA: Who left the prints?

VELL: Visitors. They touch the glass when it fogs.

Nara sees a shadow cross the dome. Vell points to a passing headlight outside. The shape stretches across the curved ceiling.

SCENE 2A. INT. GLASS GALLERY - NIGHT

Nara walks around a mirrored sculpture. Her reflection appears on three sides, then disappears behind a column. She follows the reflected figure to a locked door and finds her own key in the lock.

NARA: Someone has been in here.

VELL: You used the key earlier.

NARA: I would remember.

VELL: You were carrying the bucket and the cloth. You put the key down.

Nara opens the door. Inside is the humidity control panel, cycling air through the dome. A low click repeats every six minutes. She records the interval in her notebook.

NARA: The house has a pulse.

VELL: The system has a timer.

Nara writes the two explanations side by side. She watches the reflection as the humidity changes. This time it moves exactly with her.

SCENE 3. INT. CONSERVATORY OFFICE - LATER

Nara reviews security footage. The images appear to show her entering the dome twice. Vell notices the camera's clock is one minute behind. A reflected image from the corridor has been recorded as a second person.

NARA: I saw myself in the glass.

VELL: You were looking at the mirrored wall.

NARA: One reflection was late.

VELL: The camera is late.

The curator shows her the humidity log. The system has been switching between warm and cool air every six minutes.

SCENE 3A. INT. CONSERVATORY OFFICE - LATER

Nara replays the security footage at half speed. The image of her reflection appears in the corridor camera, then in the dome camera, then again in a polished display case. She tracks the timestamps. Each appearance follows the humidity cycle, but the reflection is always a few frames behind.

NARA: It looks like someone is following me.

VELL: It follows the light.

Nara turns off one lamp. The shadow disappears from the screen. She turns it back on and watches the reflection return. The curator asks whether she wants to leave the night shift.

NARA: I want to finish the inspection. I don't want to chase myself through another hallway.

They walk together to the dome and mark the cracked panel with tape. The camera no longer isolates Nara; both are visible in the same frame.

SCENE 4. INT. CENTRAL DOME - NIGHT

Nara and Vell disable the cycling system. The fingerprints stop appearing as the glass dries. Sil, the glass artist, arrives with a panel to replace the cracked dome. She explains that the delayed reflection comes from a laminated safety layer, not a presence inside the glass.

A crack widens with the temperature change. The trio cordons off the area and guides visitors away. One sculpture shifts as the air cools, making a low tone. Nara hears the note and recognizes it as the vent vibrating.

NARA: The building has been speaking in ordinary sounds.

VELL: That is usually how buildings speak.

SCENE 5. INT. CONSERVATORY - DAWN

The new panel is installed. The glass reflects the morning light evenly. The Conservatory director reviews the inspection notes and signs the maintenance log. Nara asks that the humidity timer be labeled, so the next guard knows when it will cycle. Vell adds a clear tag beside the switch and tests it once while the room is empty. Nara walks past the dome. Her reflection keeps pace with her. She pauses. It pauses too.

NARA (V.O.): The glass did not remember me. I was the one remembering the glass.

Sil laughs from the scaffolding and asks her to hold a panel steady. Nara does. The reflection now shows three figures, each working from a different angle.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty dome catches the sunrise. No fingerprints appear. In the polished floor, the reflected crew keeps working long after the real crew steps out of frame.
