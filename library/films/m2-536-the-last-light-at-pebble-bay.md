# The Last Light at Pebble Bay

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: isolated-island mysteries with a lighthouse blackout, competing alibis, and a final revelation in the storm*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: MARA, a macaque keeper; OREN, a baboon pilot; TIL, a gibbon cook; BESS, an orangutan guest; KOR, a capuchin electrician; INSPECTOR VALE, a mandrill.

EXT. PEBBLE BAY LIGHTHOUSE - NIGHT

A storm sweeps across a rocky island. The lighthouse beam goes dark. Mara runs up the stairs with a lantern. Oren enters, soaked, carrying a wet map.

OREN: The beacon failed.

MARA: It is off, not broken.

TIL: The kitchen stove also went out.

Bess holds a candle beside the window. A small boat sounds its horn beyond the rocks.

BESS: Someone is still outside.

Inspector Vale arrives from the harbor, carrying a dry notebook.

VALE: No boat leaves until we know what happened.

KOR: The generator is running. The lamp circuit is not.

ACT ONE

INT. LIGHTHOUSE LAMP ROOM - NIGHT

Kor checks the switches. The beacon lens is intact, but one fuse is missing. Vale asks who had access. Mara, Til, Oren, and Bess all hold keys.

BESS: I was in the kitchen.

TIL: The stove went out when the generator dipped.

OREN: I was at the harbor, bringing back the map.

MARA: I was on the stairs.

Vale looks at the floor. Wet footprints lead to the lamp room and stop at the fuse cabinet. One print is smaller than the others.

VALE: Someone came here barefoot.

Kor opens the cabinet. The fuse is not stolen; it has fallen into a tin cup beneath the shelf. Mara recognizes the cup as the one used to collect rainwater from a ceiling leak.

MARA: The drip hit the cabinet.

TIL: The fuse is dry.

KOR: It is loose. The cup is not the cause.

Oren says the boat outside is waiting for the signal. Mara checks the lamp but does not touch the circuit. Kor reseats the fuse, then tests the backup lamp. It flickers.

KOR: The wire is wet.

The storm leaks through a cracked pane. A drop falls on the switch panel. Vale moves the cup under it. Mara closes the shutter, but the wooden latch will not catch.

Inspector Vale checks the lower stair and finds a second wet trail. The prints end beneath the lamp housing. Oren says he came upstairs only after the beacon went dark, but his coat is dry below the knees and wet above them.

VALE: You reached the upper landing before the others.

OREN: I climbed the outside stairs to see the boat.

MARA: Those stairs are locked.

Oren shows a broken latch on the exterior door. He says the wind pushed it open. Kor inspects the latch and finds no fresh tool marks. Bess admits she opened the interior shutter to check the storm. She did not enter the lamp room.

A boat horn sounds again, three short bursts. Liss recognizes the pattern as a request for direction, not an emergency. She sends a steady hand-lantern signal from the lower window. The boat responds with one long horn.

TIL: It has seen the light.

VALE: Not the lighthouse light. The hand lantern.

Mara asks everyone to stay clear while Kor replaces the wire. The signal gives them time, but the beacon still needs to return before the boat enters the rocks.

ACT TWO

The boat sounds its horn again. Oren recognizes the pattern as a distress call from a fishing crew. He grabs a hand lantern and signals back from the lower window. Mara and Kor work together to seal the cracked pane with a rubber strip. Til brings a towel from the kitchen and blocks the leak beneath the fuse cabinet.

Kor replaces the damp wire with a spare from the emergency kit. The lighthouse lamp comes on, and the beam sweeps across the bay. The boat turns toward the safe inlet.

VALE: The beacon did not go dark because anyone took the fuse.

MARA: It went dark because the storm reached the wiring.

BESS: Then why are we still here?

The inspector closes the notebook and tells Oren to guide the boat to shore. The storm eases. Mara opens the shutter and watches the beacon make one full turn.

TIL: The stove is still out.

The harbor boat ties up at the pier, and the crew carries the sealed lamp parts inside. Oren checks each one against the repair log. No part is missing; the wet wire is the only failed piece. Vale crosses out the line marked "possible sabotage" and writes "storm damage."

KOR: I can repair the stove after we get the crew inside.

TIL: That was the mystery.

They all look at the dark kitchen window.

FINAL IMAGE: The lighthouse beam turns steadily over the bay as Til serves cold bread by lantern light.
