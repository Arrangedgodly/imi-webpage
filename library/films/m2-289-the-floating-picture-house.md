# The Floating Picture House

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: flood-disaster movies, frantic command centers, waterline close-ups, and a community saved by the people who know the building*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: When a sudden flood traps the town's last movie audience inside a low-lying theater, a gibbon projectionist and a crew of primates must keep the building afloat long enough to guide everyone to the roof.

Cast
- LIO, a gibbon projectionist who knows every passage in the theater.
- MIRA, a macaque manager who keeps the evacuation keys on a wrist loop.
- TESS, a capuchin ticket taker who climbs the balcony rail with ease.
- BOK, a mandrill carpenter who built the stage.
- MAYOR OMI, an orangutan who coordinates emergency teams from a nearby bridge.
- PIP, a ring-tailed lemur child who refuses to leave before the movie ends.

## Scene 1: The Screening

INT. RIVERBANK CINEMA - NIGHT

A storm strikes during the evening film. Rain runs down the lobby windows. The audience watches a comedy while water begins pooling beneath the seats.

MIRA: We are closing the theater.

PIP: The hero is about to open the door.

LIO: Then we will pause the movie.

The power flickers. A loud boom shakes the building. Water pushes through the loading entrance, lifting a row of folding chairs. The audience panics. Lio climbs the booth stairs and shuts off the projector.

## Scene 2: The Evacuation

Mira opens the main exit, but the street outside has flooded. Mayor Omi speaks through a portable radio from the bridge.

OMI: Move everyone to the roof. The upper footbridge is accessible from your balcony.

BOK: The balcony stairs are blocked by the scenery flats.

TESS: I can climb the side rail and open the upper hatch.

MIRA: Take the safety line.

Tess ties a rope around her waist and climbs. Her tail steadies her against the rail. She reaches the hatch and opens it, revealing the roof stair. The crowd moves upward in pairs.

PIP: Can I bring the movie ticket?

LIO: Keep it in your pocket. We are not losing anyone over a ticket.

## Scene 3: The Floating Floor

Water rises to ankle height in the auditorium. A section of the wooden stage begins to float. Bok identifies the cause: the theater's old stage rests on sealed air chambers from a previous flood retrofit.

BOK: The stage will lift if we keep the side vents clear.

MIRA: Will it hold everyone?

BOK: It was designed to hold props. It was not designed to hold a crowd.

The audience climbs onto the balcony instead. Lio retrieves the emergency lamp from the projection booth. A rack of film reels shifts toward the water. He saves one reel, then remembers the archive is backed up in the city library.

LIO: The film can stay. People cannot.

## Scene 4: The Emergency Plan

From the roof, the group sees the flood moving through the street. Mayor Omi directs a rescue boat toward the cinema. The boat cannot reach the front steps because a floating sign blocks the approach. Tessa spots it from above and signals the boat around the alley.

The audience forms a line to pass life vests down. Mira checks each vest. Pip is last in line and insists the adult behind her go first. Mira holds her hand while they cross a narrow roof plank to the rescue ladder.

MIRA: One step, then pause.

PIP: I know. I am watching the water.

BOK: Watch the rope too. The roof edge is slick.

The first group boards safely. The rescue boat returns for the rest.

## Scene 5: The Final Seat

Lio realizes one ticket taker, Tess, has stayed behind to retrieve the keys. She is on the balcony above the flooded lobby. The water has reached the lower stair.

TESS: I can climb to the roof hatch.

LIO: The rail is wet.

BOK: I will brace the ladder from the stage. The wood is still floating.

Lio and Bok secure the ladder to a balcony post. Tess climbs, her tail wrapped around the side rail. She reaches the roof with the keys. The last rescue boat arrives. No one is left inside.

## Scene 6: The Morning After

At dawn, the theater remains standing. The flood recedes. The mayor meets the crew at the bridge. The stage has floated loose but stayed inside the auditorium. Mira locks the roof hatch and hands the keys to the emergency team.

OMI: The building held.

BOK: The retrofit held. The stage did what it was built to do.

LIO: We can repair the screen and reopen when it is safe.

PIP: Will the movie still be there?

LIO: The movie is at the library. We can screen it when the power returns.

## Final Image

The theater sign hangs over the quiet street. One marquee letter has fallen into a puddle, spelling a new title: "O". The rescue boat passes beneath it as the water drains away.
