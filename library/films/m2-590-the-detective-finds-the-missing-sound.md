# The Detective Finds the Missing Sound

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: noir mysteries where a missing sound is treated like a vanished witness*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
DETECTIVE MARA, a macaque
CLIENT VEX, an orangutan
SOUND TECH PIP, a capuchin
ARCHIVIST NARI, a lemur
CUSTODIAN ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - NIGHT

A silent film plays on the screen. Vex sits in the front row, clutching a notebook.

VEX: The piano has vanished.

MARA: This screening is silent. It never had piano accompaniment.

PIP: The sound system is switched off. The speakers are working.

NARI: The archive card says silent print, no soundtrack.

VEX: I heard music here last week.

ODO: Last week was a different film with live accompaniment.

Mara examines the empty piano bench. A folded program lies on it, listing tonight's screening as SILENT CLASSIC.

VEX: A clue planted in plain sight.

MARA: A program telling us what we are watching.

SCENE 2. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - CONTINUOUS

Pip opens the equipment cabinet. The amplifier is off and the cable is coiled neatly. Odo holds the event schedule; Nari checks the film can label.

VEX: Someone cut the music.

PIP: No cable was connected to the amplifier.

NARI: The film can has no audio track. It is an original silent print.

MARA: That leaves your memory of last week's piano.

VEX: Memory is an unreliable witness.

ODO: Last week, Pip played at the opening. I signed his payment card.

Vex circles the line in his notebook, then asks Pip to recreate the missing music. Pip declines until the film ends, because the theater is showing the print as it was made.

PIP: I can play afterward. During the screening, the silence is part of the presentation.

VEX: The silence is becoming suspiciously organized.

MARA: The archive label, event card and equipment all agree.

SCENE 3. INT. THEATER LOBBY - LATER

The screening ends. The audience applauds. Pip wheels a small keyboard toward the stage, while Nari returns the film can to its padded case. Vex examines the applause, searching for the missing piano among the claps.

VEX: The sound has returned in fragments.

NARI: Those are the audience clapping.

ODO: The print is back in its case. The booth is locked.

MARA: We can reconstruct the event from the schedule. Silent screening first, live piano after the credits.

PIP: I was booked for the lobby set, not the film.

VEX: Then there was no missing accompaniment.

MARA: Correct. You remembered a different part of the evening.

Vex looks at his notes. He has drawn a suspect portrait of a piano with a dark hat.

VEX: It had motive and opportunity.

PIP: It was on stage last week.

VEX: A classic alibi.

Pip places the keyboard on the lobby stand and checks the volume without playing. Vex writes "instrument ready" in his notes as if it were evidence.

SCENE 3A. INT. THEATER LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Before Pip begins, Vex asks for an encore of the imagined missing accompaniment. Pip refuses to play over the guests leaving the screening. Odo moves the sign so the exit remains clear.

VEX: A detective needs one last performance to verify the truth.

PIP: I have not played the set yet. The keyboard is ready now.

MARA: Let the audience settle. The lobby set starts after they have passed the doorway.

NARI: The sign points toward the seats, away from the exit. No one will mistake it for the film program.

Vex steps to the side, but his notebook blocks the path. Mara gently lowers it to his lap.

VEX: I was guarding the scene.

ODO: You were standing in the doorway.

The last guests leave. Pip checks the stand and starts the planned melody. Vex listens for a few bars, then shuts the notebook without writing anything down.

MARA: That is the difference between a witness and a soundtrack.

VEX: I have no notes.

SCENE 3B. INT. THEATER LOBBY - MOMENTS LATER

The melody ends. No one has spoken over it. Vex looks at his case notes, then closes the notebook and slides it into his coat pocket.

VEX: I will amend the report. There was no theft.

MARA: Write that the screening was silent and the piano played afterward.

ODO: The schedule and the archive card can stay with the film record.

NARI: I'll return the program to the lobby rack. It should not be filed with the audio equipment.

PIP: The keyboard can stay on its stand until the audience leaves.

Vex offers to label the keyboard as a suspect. Mara hands him the CLOSED stamp instead. He hesitates, then stamps his own notebook.

VEX: Case closed, sound present.

MARA: Better.

SCENE 4. INT. THEATER LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Nari displays a sign: LIVE PIANO, AFTER THE SCREENING. Odo unlocks the lobby doors for departing guests. Mara tells Vex he can stay for Pip's set, but the detective must stop questioning the instrument.

VEX: I will observe the suspect from a respectful distance.

MARA: Sit in the audience and listen.

Pip plays. The melody fills the lobby. Vex closes his notebook and listens without interruption.

PIP: Is the case closed?

VEX: The sound has been found, but I cannot prove it was missing.

TAG: Mara stamps Vex's notebook CLOSED. Vex asks whether the stamp has a sound; Odo answers by snapping it down once.
