# The Costume Change in the Wrong Room

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: backstage farces where a rapid costume change becomes the engine of mistaken identity and missed entrances*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: Two actors swap dressing rooms before a double costume change, then spend the final act pursuing one another's hats, capes, and cues.

Cast: actor Kessa, a macaque; actor Vali, a capuchin; dresser Omi, a gibbon; stage manager Rena, a lemur; understudy Pora, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. THEATER BACKSTAGE - EVENING

The stage manager posts the running order. Kessa plays a queen in Act One and a gardener in Act Two. Vali plays the gardener first, then the queen's messenger. Their dressing-room labels have fallen off the doors.

RENA: Room one is queen. Room two is garden.

OMI: The signs are on the floor.

Kessa and Vali enter opposite rooms. Each finds the other's costume laid out. They assume the wardrobe team has changed the order. Omi returns with a basket of hats and sees both actors dressed incorrectly.

SCENE 2 - INT. DRESSING ROOMS - MOMENTS LATER

The first act begins. Kessa enters wearing Vali's gardener coat and a messenger's hat. Vali appears as the queen, holding a pruning shears prop. The audience applauds the bold reinterpretation. Rena signals a hold. The actors retreat through opposite doors and switch hats, but not coats.

KESSA: I am still the gardener.

VALI: I am still the queen.

Omi checks the labels on the costume racks. The rack itself has been turned around, so the labels now face the wall. Pora, the understudy, tries to help by carrying costumes through the hallway. She delivers the queen's cape to the orchestra pit.

SCENE 3 - INT. BACKSTAGE CORRIDOR - LATER

Rena calls the next entrance. Vali rushes out wearing the gardener's apron over the queen's coat. Kessa has the messenger's hat and cape, plus the garden gloves. The actors pass each other at the narrow corridor and exchange costumes again by mistake. Omi uses a mirror to show them the labels on their backs. Both have the wrong name pinned to them.

PORA: The audience thinks this is the plot.

RENA: It is not the plot.

A prop bell rings. Kessa answers it, believing it is her cue. Vali follows, believing Kessa is the queen. The two enter the scene together, one carrying pruning shears and the other a cape.

SCENE 3A - INT. COSTUME STORAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Omi sorts the clothes by character. The labels have fallen from the costume hangers, leaving the queen's cape beside the gardener's apron. Pora wears the messenger's hat and asks whether she is expected onstage. Rena tells her to stay ready as understudy. Pora steps behind a screen to change and emerges in the gardener coat, which is also the queen's scene-two disguise.

PORA: Which entrance is mine?

RENA: The one after the bell.

Omi rings the bell to test it. Pora dashes through the stage door and appears in the middle of the current scene. The audience assumes she is a new character. Rena pulls her back and moves the bell to the proper cue table. Vali offers Pora the messenger hat, but Rena holds up the cast list first.

SCENE 4 - INT. STAGE - CONTINUOUS

The queen's speech begins. Kessa starts the gardener's lines. Vali responds with the messenger's cue. The actors improvise a negotiation about a hedge, a missing letter, and royal gardening rights. The audience laughs. Rena lets the scene continue while Omi rotates the costume rack so the labels face out.

The actors realize they have reached the correct ending despite the swapped roles. Kessa hands Vali the letter prop. Vali signs it with a gloved hand, leaving a smudge of garden soil on the royal seal.

When the bell sounds, Rena checks the cast list before sending anyone out. Kessa enters as the gardener, and Vali remains the messenger. The costume changes are done in the labeled rooms. Pora stands by the correct door wearing the spare queen's cape, then removes it before the cue. Omi retrieves the cape from the orchestra pit and hangs it on the queen's rack. The hanger has its label facing out. Rena leaves the spare bell on the cue table.

SCENE 5 - INT. DRESSING ROOMS - LATER

The cast bows. Rena relabels the doors and tapes the costume list to the mirror. Omi returns the cape from the orchestra pit. Kessa and Vali agree to check the room numbers before the next show. Pora puts on the spare messenger hat and is accidentally announced as the new queen.

FINAL IMAGE: The room labels are firmly taped to the doors, while the costume rack still faces the wrong direction and displays every outfit correctly.
