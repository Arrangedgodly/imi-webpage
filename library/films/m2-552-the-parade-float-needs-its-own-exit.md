# The Parade Float Needs Its Own Exit

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: festival films where the farewell finale causes a traffic jam*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
PARADE LEADER NARI, a lemur
BUILDER MARA, a macaque
DRIVER PIP, a capuchin
ANNOUNCER VEX, an orangutan
MARSHAL ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - AFTERNOON

A giant paper sun sits on a float shaped like a garden cart. The parade has ended, but the float blocks the community hall entrance.

VEX: One last farewell pass for the crowd.

ODO: The route is closed. The street crew needs to bring the barricades through.

NARI: We have an exit route behind the hall, but the float's sun catches on the arch.

PIP: The cart can turn if we remove the banner pole.

MARA: The pole is attached with two pins. I'll hold the frame while Pip removes them.

VEX: The crowd is still applauding.

NARI: They're applauding because the parade is over and the band is packing up.

SCENE 2. EXT. ARCHWAY - CONTINUOUS

Pip removes one pin. The banner pole lowers. The second pin is hidden behind a ribbon.

PIP: I can't see the latch.

MARA: Wait. Don't pull. The ribbon is tied through the pin hole.

VEX: A final knot to bind the celebration.

ODO: It is a safety tie. Cut the ribbon only after the frame is supported.

Nari and Mara lift the pole. Pip releases the ribbon and pulls the second pin. The pole lowers onto padded stands.

NARI: The float is clear of the arch now.

PIP: The wheels are straight. Driver's position is ready.

VEX: Before it goes, I should climb aboard for a final wave.

MARA: No. The float is moving to storage; no riders.

SCENE 2A. EXT. REAR LANE - CONTINUOUS

The float reaches the narrow turn. Mara checks the wheels; the left one is angled toward a drain.

MARA: Stop. The wheel is close to the grate.

PIP: Brake engaged. I can turn it by backing half a wheel length, then steering wide.

ODO: I will watch the rear corner. Nari, keep the lane clear.

NARI: The lane is clear. The band has already packed its instruments.

VEX: I can call the turn a daring maneuver.

MARA: Call it a slow turn. Don't make the driver rush.

Pip backs up. The paper sun wobbles. Vex reaches to steady it, then waits for Mara's cue.

MARA: Hold the frame at the reinforced base, not the paper edge.

Vex grips the brace. The cart clears the drain.

PIP: Wheel is straight again. We can continue at walking pace.

VEX: I have never seen a parade float reverse with such suspense.

NARI: The garage is through that open door. No more turns after this one.

SCENE 2B. EXT. GARAGE DOOR - CONTINUOUS

The float is almost inside. The left banner stand catches the door frame.

PIP: Stop. The stand is hitting the trim.

MARA: Brake on. We'll lower the stand six inches.

NARI: I'll hold the frame while Pip loosens the height clamp.

VEX: The parade's last obstacle.

ODO: It is a garage doorway. The clamp is the obstacle.

Mara steadies the frame. Pip lowers the stand and retightens the clamp. The banner clears the trim by a hand's width.

PIP: Height set. The wheels are still straight.

MARA: Continue slowly. Odo, watch the opposite corner.

Vex stays behind the cart rather than stepping onto it. The float rolls into storage. Mara blocks the wheels with two rubber wedges.

NARI: Float secured. The door can close without touching the sun.

VEX: The grand finale has fit through the service entrance.

SCENE 3. EXT. REAR LANE - MINUTES LATER

Pip guides the float at walking pace. Odo walks beside the rear wheel, keeping the lane clear. Vex follows with a handheld microphone.

VEX: The last float leaves the square. The parade ends with a journey into the unknown.

NARI: It is going to the garage, which is on the map.

A paper sun slides sideways. Mara steadies it from the ground while Pip stops the cart.

MARA: Stop. The brace has loosened.

PIP: Brake on. We can tighten the brace before turning.

ODO: The locking pin is still in the tool bag.

Vex opens the bag; the pin is at the bottom under the program cards. He hands it over.

SCENE 4. INT. GARAGE - LATER

The float rolls through the wide door. The sun clears the frame. Nari marks the storage place with tape.

NARI: Float inside, wheels blocked, banner pole stored on padded stands.

VEX: The parade is complete.

MARA: The float is stored. The celebration ended in the square.

PIP: The garage door is clear. No one is behind the cart.

TAG: Vex announces a grand finale to the empty garage. Odo switches off the work light before the echo ends.
