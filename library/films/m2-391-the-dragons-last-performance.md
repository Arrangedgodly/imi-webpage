# The Dragon's Last Performance

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quests, reluctant monsters, prophecies that refuse to retire, and heroes who arrive after the final curtain*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MARA, a macaque dragon
SIR ODO, a gibbon knight
PIP, a capuchin stagehand
LUMA, a lemur town historian
VEX, an orangutan innkeeper

SCENE 1. INT. MOUNTAIN CAVE - MORNING

Mara sits beneath a painted sign reading "DRAGON, AVAILABLE FOR QUESTS." She is packing a leather trunk. Sir Odo enters, armor clanking against the cave walls.

ODO: I have come to defeat the dragon.

MARA: We closed at dawn.

ODO: The prophecy says a hero will arrive when the red moon rises.

LUMA: The red moon rose last night. It was an eclipse.

Odo draws a scroll. Only a red ink circle is visible on the folded scroll. He tucks it into his breastplate without opening it further. Pip carries away a cardboard tower.

ODO: You cannot take the tower. It is part of the lair.

PIP: It belongs to the theater company. We have a matinee.

Mara unhooks the "AVAILABLE FOR QUESTS" sign. A second sign beneath it reads "PLEASE RING BELL FOR LEGENDARY ENCOUNTERS." She hands it to Odo.

MARA: The bell goes with the sign.

ODO: I came all this way for a final battle.

MARA: I have a train at six.

SCENE 2. INT. CAVE OFFICE - LATER

Luma opens the town's history book. It records twelve dragon defeats, all staged by the same traveling company. In each portrait, a knight holds a cardboard sword and Mara pretends to fall.

ODO: I was told the dragon's hoard is here.

MARA: There is no hoard.

PIP: The gold is a rented stage replica. We return it after the final performance.

Odo searches behind a rock. He finds a stack of invoices and a tin cup. He shakes the cup. A coin drops out.

ODO: A coin.

VEX: That's my coat-check token.

Vex enters wearing an innkeeper's apron. He says the company left its props in his storage room. Mara reaches for the token, but Odo closes his fist around it.

ODO: I claim this as the spoils.

VEX: You can claim a coat when you return the token.

Odo asks Mara to breathe fire. Mara points to a red lever on the wall.

MARA: The theatrical flame is in the next room.

Pip pulls the lever. A silk ribbon unfurls and gently waves. Odo steps back.

ODO: The fire is weak.

PIP: It's a ribbon.

MARA: The real flame is for the kiln. It costs extra.

SCENE 3. EXT. MOUNTAIN PATH - NOON

Odo insists that the prophecy requires a duel. He places two wooden swords on a flat stone. Mara declines to fight. Odo begins a formal challenge. His helmet visor drops over his eyes, and he swings at a pine tree.

LUMA: That is not the dragon.

ODO: I know. I was testing the reach.

The pine sheds a few needles. Odo calls it a warning. Mara tries to leave. Odo blocks the path with his sword, then realizes he is standing on the track where a small prop cart comes down the hill.

Pip rings the quest bell. Odo turns toward it. The cart rolls past carrying a painted cave mouth. The fabric backdrop catches on his sword and wraps around him.

ODO: The mountain has captured me.

PIP: It's the curtain.

Mara lifts the cloth away. Odo takes the scroll from his breastplate and unfolds it. Its final line has been tucked beneath the wax seal.

ODO: It says, "When the red moon passes, let the dragon go."

LUMA: That was always the last line.

ODO: The scroll was folded.

MARA: The legend was also on a poster.

SCENE 4. INT. INN ROOM - AFTERNOON

Vex unlocks the prop trunk. Odo returns the token and folds his banner. Mara hangs her dragon cloak over a chair. Pip packs the silk fire ribbon beside the rented gold.

ODO: If the dragon is retired, what am I?

VEX: You could be a knight who arrives on time.

ODO: I have practiced for a duel.

MARA: Try carrying boxes.

Odo lifts the trunk. It is heavier than the armor. He sets it down and removes his helmet so he can see the stairs. Pip offers him a job moving props for the matinee.

ODO: Is there a prophecy?

PIP: The schedule says we start at two.

SCENE 5. INT. TOWN THEATER - AFTERNOON

At two, the audience gathers for the final matinee. Mara stands backstage in a plain coat. Odo carries a painted sword onto the stage, then sets it down beside the prop tower.

PIP: You were meant to defeat the dragon.

ODO: The dragon has a train.

Mara enters from the wings and waves to the audience. They applaud. Odo takes his bow beside her. Luma turns the history book to a clean page and writes "Dragon retired after final performance."

FINAL IMAGE: The cardboard cave rolls offstage. The bell rings as the last person leaves, and Odo takes down the sign before anyone can ask for another quest.
