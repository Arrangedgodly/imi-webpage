# The Score Rehearsed the Actors

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prestige biopics about film composers, studio notes, and dramatic music that arrives before the scene*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
NARI, a gibbon composer
SERA, a capuchin actor
VEX, a macaque director
PIP, a lemur editor
ODO, an orangutan studio executive

SCENE 1. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - MORNING

Nari sits at a piano while Sera rehearses a quiet walk across an empty room. Vex asks for a score that makes the walk feel important.

VEX: The character needs to feel he has lost everything.

SERA: She is going to the window.

NARI: The window can be a turning point.

Nari plays a swelling chord. Sera stops walking and turns toward the piano. Vex likes the moment. He asks her to repeat it without music.

Sera walks to the window. Nari hits the chord. She turns again.

SERA: I hear it before I do it.

NARI: That is anticipation.

Pip threads a strip of test film into a projector. The image shows Sera standing still. Nari's music plays over the shot, and Sera raises her hand in time with every note.

ODO: The music is telling her what to do.

VEX: It is helping the performance.

SCENE 2. INT. EDITING ROOM - AFTERNOON

Pip cuts the scene. Nari's score has eight notes. Vex wants the chord to begin before Sera enters. Nari says the cue should follow the door opening. Odo says the audience should know the door matters before it opens.

PIP: If the music starts early, the door will look late.

ODO: Then make the door more urgent.

Sera rehearses the entrance. Nari plays the cue. Sera arrives at the door, waits for the chord, and knocks exactly on the final note.

VEX: Perfect.

SERA: I was waiting for the music.

Nari asks to try once without it. Sera knocks. Nobody answers. Nari does not play. The pause stretches. Odo leans forward.

ODO: Now the door has suspense.

Pip rolls back the film. It has captured the knock, but no one can hear it over the orchestra.

SCENE 3. INT. RECORDING BOOTH - NIGHT

Nari records a new version with fewer notes. The musicians wait for Vex's signal. Vex says he will raise one hand to cue the door, but the conductor mistakes the gesture and starts early. Sera opens the door while the music swells. Behind it is the soundstage crew carrying a flat window.

SERA: That is not the room.

PIP: We moved the wall to make space for the orchestra.

The window flat leans against a music stand. Nari plays a bright note, and the flat rolls away. Odo says the movement feels like the character's past leaving the room.

VEX: We can use it.

Sera tries to follow the flat with her eyes. It rolls behind the orchestra, then circles back because one wheel is loose. Each pass lands on a different musical beat.

NARI: It has found the rhythm.

SCENE 4. INT. PREVIEW THEATER - NEXT DAY

The studio watches the cut. The music begins before the title card. Odo asks if the audience will recognize the composer. Nari says the card is about the actor. Odo asks for the score to announce itself.

Nari plays the first eight notes. The title card appears. Sera's still image on screen raises one hand.

SERA: I didn't do that.

Pip checks the film. The loose frame makes the image jump. Vex calls it a deliberate gesture. Nari repeats the opening notes. The image jumps again.

ODO: There. The film is answering the score.

PIP: It is the projector.

Vex asks the projectionist to stop. The film stops, but Nari's recorded music keeps playing. Everyone looks at the blank screen.

NARI: That is the ending.

ODO: The scene has disappeared.

NARI: The score remains.

Sera steps into the beam of the projector. Her shadow appears on the screen. Nari plays the final chord. Sera turns toward the piano before the chord resolves.

The preview audience begins to applaud. Odo signals for them to stop, saying the film has not announced its ending. The applause fades. Nari holds the chord. Sera keeps her shadow still.

SERA: Are we waiting for the scene?

NARI: We are waiting for the sound.

Pip switches off the projector. Nari releases the chord. The audience applauds again, this time after the silence.

SCENE 5. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - EVENING

The crew removes the window flat. Nari writes a small cue beside the door scene: "Let the knock happen first." Vex says the instruction is too subtle. Odo asks if the music can be louder. Sera takes the cue sheet and puts it over the speaker.

PIP: Now we can hear the knock.

Sera knocks. The sound carries across the empty stage. Nari waits, then plays one quiet note. Vex and Odo both look at the door.

FINAL IMAGE: The door opens on the musicians packing up. Nari lifts her hand to cue them, but they leave before the chord.
