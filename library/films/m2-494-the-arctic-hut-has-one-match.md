# The Arctic Hut Has One Match

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: survival adventures that make a small supply mistake into a heroic ordeal with dramatic lessons about grit*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: Two monkey researchers reach a snow hut with one match, a thermos, and a fully working stove they forgot to open.

Cast: surveyor Toma, a macaque; radio operator Leni, a gibbon; guide Pavi, a lemur; cook Soba, an orangutan; narrator Eno, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - EXT. ARCTIC HUT - DUSK

Wind blows snow across a small wooden hut. Toma holds up a matchbox. Leni checks the radio. Pavi carries a pack. Soba reaches the door and finds it frozen shut.

TOMA: One match. We must save it for the crisis.

PAVI: The stove is inside.

ENO (V.O.): The expedition had reached the most dangerous place on Earth: a hut.

Soba clears snow from the door with a shovel. It opens immediately. The team enters.

SCENE 2 - INT. ARCTIC HUT - CONTINUOUS

The hut is cold but dry. A stove stands beside a neatly stacked pile of kindling. Toma inspects the matchbox. Leni tunes the radio. Pavi checks the stove's instruction card, but Toma declares that firewood is limited.

Leni: We have a full bundle.

TOMA: The match is our final hope.

Soba finds a kettle and pours hot water from the thermos. The team drinks. Eno's narration describes their struggle against the elements while the stove remains untouched.

Pavi tries to measure the wood pile. Toma insists the crew should ration every stick, including the kindling. Soba stacks the pieces beside the stove and notices a laminated instruction card attached to the wall. It lists three steps: open vent, connect power, press starter. Toma studies the card as if it were a map to civilization. Eno narrates the discovery of an ancient technical tablet. Leni reads it aloud and flips it over. The back lists emergency radio frequencies.

SCENE 3 - INT. HUT - LATER

The team wraps themselves in sleeping bags. The radio crackles with a weather update: the wind will ease by morning. Toma hears only static and calls it a warning from the wilderness. Leni adjusts the antenna. The message repeats clearly.

LENI: It says the wind is easing.

TOMA: Nature has sent a coded message.

Pavi reaches to light the stove. Toma stops her, saying the match must be kept for a greater emergency. The room is cold enough that their breath clouds the air. Soba places the kindling beside the stove and asks what emergency could be greater than freezing in a hut.

SCENE 4 - INT. HUT - NIGHT

The matchbox falls from Toma's pocket and slides beneath the stove. Pavi reaches for it. The matchbox opens, and the last match drops onto the floor. The team gasps. Soba picks it up carefully. Leni points to the stove's electric starter, plugged into the wall.

LENI: The hut has power.

TOMA: The expedition has discovered electricity.

Soba presses the starter. The stove lights. The team warms the room, boils water, and places their boots by the vent. Eno's narration shifts to describe the profound resilience of turning a switch.

The crew records a dispatch for the research station. Eno wants to narrate the night as a battle against the polar dark. Leni plays back the audio and the stove clicks loudly under every sentence. Toma asks Soba to turn it off for a cleaner take; the room begins to cool. They record with the stove on and describe the click as the hut's heartbeat. The radio operator sends the report. The station replies with a request for the stove model number.

SCENE 4A - INT. HUT - LATER

Eno asks the team to reenact the crisis for the expedition log. Toma stands by the door and narrates the wind. Pavi opens it for a gust. Snow blows in and covers the stove controls. Soba closes the door and wipes the panel clean. Leni records the scene over the radio, but the transmission captures only the stove clicking and Toma shouting over the wind. Eno listens to the playback and calls it a raw account of hardship.

SCENE 5 - INT. HUT - MORNING

The crew prepares to leave. The stove is off. The one match sits taped to the expedition log as a keepsake. Toma asks Pavi to write down the lessons of the night. She writes: "Check the stove before saving the match." Toma says the expedition should preserve the dramatic version for the report.

Leni transmits the weather update. The radio works perfectly. Soba packs the thermos and the remaining kindling. Eno narrates their departure as a victory over the frozen wilderness.

FINAL IMAGE: The team steps into the bright morning, carrying the unused match in a tiny glass case labeled LAST RESORT.
