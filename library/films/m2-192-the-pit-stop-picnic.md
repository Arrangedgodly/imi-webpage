# The Pit Stop Picnic

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: racing films, rival drivers, precision pit crews, last-lap strategy, and slow-motion finish-line shots*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
DARA, a squirrel monkey driver
KEL, a capuchin mechanic
VOSS, a macaque rival
ANNOUNCER, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. KETTLE CIRCUIT - MORNING

The race cars line up. Dara drives a tiny electric cart with a picnic basket strapped to its hood. Voss's machine is a polished red racer with a spoiler shaped like a wing. The announcer introduces the championship as "the fastest race on the garden path."

DARA: The cart is light. I can handle the turns.

KEL: The brakes are good. The steering is good. The picnic basket is not aerodynamic.

DARA: It's full of sandwiches. We'll need them after the race.

The flag drops. Voss rockets ahead. Dara follows, navigating around a watering can that rolls into the track.

SCENE 2. EXT. CIRCUIT - LAP TWO

Voss's car takes the inside lane and kicks up gravel. Dara keeps to the outer edge. Her cart slows at each bump, but the basket stays steady.

ANNOUNCER: Voss leads by half a lap. Dara is maintaining a sensible speed, which is not usually a racing strategy.

KEL: You can pick up pace on the straight.

DARA: The straight has a sprinkler.

A sprinkler bursts on. Voss skids, loses traction, and swerves into the pit lane. His crew changes all four tires, though only one is wet.

SCENE 2A. EXT. BACK STRAIGHT - MID-RACE

Voss pulls alongside Dara and taps his horn. Dara checks her mirror; it is made from a polished spoon, so it reflects the sky and half a pickle.

VOSS: You're driving a picnic basket.

DARA: I'm driving a cart. The basket is a passenger.

VOSS: Passengers slow you down.

DARA: Then why are you carrying that wing?

Voss glances at the spoiler. A gust catches it, lifting the front of his car just enough to make the steering twitch. He swerves into the pit lane for a second inspection.

KEL: Your rival is losing time because of the wing.

DARA: We can tell him.

KEL: It's a race.

DARA: It's also a shared track.

She signals Voss with a hand wave. He misreads it as a challenge and accelerates past a turn marker.

SCENE 2B. EXT. WINDING TURN - CONTINUOUS

Voss brakes hard for a sharp turn. Dara sees the wet track and reduces speed. Her cart slides a few inches, then grips the gravel. Voss takes the corner too quickly and spins into a straw barrier. His crew rushes forward with tire gauges and a dramatic smoke flare.

DARA: Is he hurt?

KEL: No. The straw stopped him. The spoiler is bent.

Dara parks and offers Voss a spare wrench. Voss hesitates, then accepts. They straighten the spoiler enough to finish the race, though it now points downward like a little shovel.

VOSS: I can still beat you.

DARA: You can still race. Let's get to the line safely.

The announcer calls this "an unexpected alliance." Kel points out that both cars are back on the track.

Dara pulls in. Kel checks the cart and finds a loose wheel nut. The pit crew rushes around her with air guns and hoses. One mechanic removes the picnic basket instead of the wheel.

KEL: Stop! The basket is not part of the drivetrain.

Dara's sandwiches are laid out on a tarp. The crew checks each one for missing ingredients. The race continues around them.

DARA: We have thirty seconds.

KEL: The wheel is fixed. The sandwiches are also fixed.

The team reloads the basket. A pickle rolls beneath Voss's car as it speeds by. Voss taps the brakes. His spoiler catches the wind and pushes the car sideways.

SCENE 4. EXT. FINAL LAP

Dara follows Voss into the final turn. He takes the inner line too fast. She brakes early, letting the cart roll through the turn without sliding. Voss spins once and stops facing backward. He tries to reverse, but the spoiler lifts the front wheels.

Dara passes him. The crowd rises in slow motion. The announcer speaks each word as if it travels through a canyon.

ANNOUNCER: Daaara… approaches… the finish!

Dara crosses the line at a comfortable speed. The basket shifts. A sandwich slides across the hood and lands in her lap.

SCENE 5. EXT. VICTORY PLATFORM - DAY

Dara receives a small brass trophy shaped like a wheel. Voss stands beside her, covered in grass and pickle slices. He admits the spoiler made his car look fast but had never been tested in crosswind.

KEL: The fastest machine did not win. The driver who understood the track did.

DARA: And the picnic survived.

Voss asks for a sandwich. Dara offers one, but the announcer insists the snack be handed over in a dramatic slow-motion sequence. The crew passes the sandwich from one hand to another. A pickle falls again, this time into Voss's jacket pocket.

FINAL IMAGE: The racers sit on the curb, sharing lunch beside the circuit. The sprinkler turns on; everyone moves their sandwiches at once, like a perfectly rehearsed pit crew.
