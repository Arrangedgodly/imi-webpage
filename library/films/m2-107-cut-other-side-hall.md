# Cut to the Other Side of the Hall

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action-chase movies, impossible geography, and cuts that teleport pursuers between doors*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A courier runs through a recreation center to return a lost whistle, while the film crew keeps cutting her pursuer to the wrong side of the building.

Cast: Tali, a macaque courier; Venn, a gorilla coach; Nori, a lemur editor; and Puck, a capuchin camera operator.

SCENE 1. INT. RECREATION CENTER LOBBY, DAY

Tali finds a whistle on the bench. The tag reads COACH VENN. She jogs down the east hall to return it. Puck follows with a camera, shouting that she is in a chase scene. Venn enters from the west hall, already ahead of her.

TALI: How did you get there?

PUCK: We cut.

NORI: We have not cut yet.

Puck asks Tali to start over.

TALI: I already ran the route.

PUCK: The camera was not ready.

TALI: The whistle is still at the end.

Venn waits by the gym door while Puck rewinds. Nori chalks EAST and WEST on two floor arrows. Puck asks if the arrows can be removed for the shot. Nori leaves them; otherwise, the crew will repeat the same false geography. She retraces her route while Venn waits at the same door.

SCENE 2. INT. EAST HALL, MOMENTS LATER

Nori draws the floor plan on a whiteboard. The east hall ends at a stairwell. The west hall is on the other side of the lobby. Puck says the audience understands the geography emotionally.

NORI: The exit is not emotional. It is on the west wall.

TALI: I can run the marked path.

VENN: I can hear you.

Puck directs Venn to run around the building. Venn takes the marked path and arrives two minutes later with a water bottle. The chase music ends before he appears. Puck asks him to move faster. Venn says the path is for walking. Tali asks if he has the whistle. He says no; she still has it. Tali asks if the whistle can simply be handed over.

Venn calls from the west hall.

VENN: I can hear you.

Puck directs him to run around the building. Venn takes the marked public path and arrives two minutes later carrying a water bottle. The chase music ends before he appears. Puck asks him to move faster. Venn says the path is for walking.

Tali follows the map from the east hall to the lobby, around the front desk, and to the west hall. Puck films each section continuously. Nori checks the footage. The route makes sense, but the run is short. Puck asks for a jump over the reception desk. The center manager says the desk is fixed and the public uses it.

SCENE 3. INT. LOBBY AND HALLS, LATER

Puck adds a rolling cart to make the chase more exciting. Tali pushes it down the hall. One wheel turns sideways, and the cart stops at a doorway. Venn is waiting there with the whistle tag. Tali hands him the whistle.

Puck calls cut before the handoff, then asks them to repeat it with a dramatic slide. Nori blocks the slide.

NORI: No sliding in the lobby.

TALI: I can hand it to him here.

VENN: Please do.

A visitor crosses the lobby. Tali waits, then hands over the whistle. Puck says they need a turn in the chase. Tali points toward the gym. Venn blows the whistle once, and the recreation class stops to hear his practice announcement. The whistle was needed there after all. A visitor is walking through the lobby. Tali waits for the visitor to pass, then hands over the whistle at normal speed.

VENN: Thanks. I was looking for that.

TALI: It was on the bench.

Nori plays the shot back. The complete route and direct handoff are clear. Puck says there is no cliffhanger. Venn blows the whistle once. Everyone stops; the recreation class was waiting for his signal to begin.

SCENE 4. INT. EDITING ROOM, EVENING

Nori cuts the chase in order: Tali finds the whistle, follows the hall, reaches the lobby, and returns it. Puck asks to insert Venn at the west door earlier. Nori shows him the continuity sheet; Venn was still in the gym then.

Puck accepts the sequence. Venn watches the final edit and asks why the camera ran past the front desk twice. Tali says the first pass was a pickup. Venn asks if they can label the halls for the next shoot. Nori adds EAST and WEST arrows to the location map. Puck asks if the sequel can have Venn chase the whistle. Venn clips it to his shirt. Tali checks the fastener. Nori marks the location wrapped and shuts down the camera. No one runs out of the building.

FINAL IMAGE: The whistle hangs around Venn's neck. Tali walks out through the lobby at a normal pace while Puck carries the camera behind her without running.
