# The Moon's Last Delivery

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sentimental delivery adventures where a simple parcel is burdened with cosmic destiny*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
COURIER MARA, a macaque
NIGHT HOST VEX, an orangutan
ROOFTOP GUIDE PIP, a capuchin
SCHEDULER ODO, a gibbon
RECIPIENT NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. HILLTOP POST OFFICE - NIGHT

A small sealed parcel sits on the counter beneath a moon-shaped lamp. Vex holds an envelope like an ancient prophecy.

VEX: The moon's final delivery is entrusted to us.

MARA: It is a parcel of night-blooming seeds for Nari's rooftop garden.

ODO: Address and delivery time are printed clearly. It must reach her before midnight.

PIP: The hill path is open, but the west stair is closed for painting.

NARI, over radio: I will be at the rooftop gate. The porch light is on.

VEX: The moon itself chose the route.

MARA: The route is the lit hill path. Keep the parcel level.

SCENE 2. EXT. HILL PATH - NIGHT

Mara carries the parcel in a padded satchel. Pip walks ahead with a lantern; Vex follows, narrating each step as a cosmic trial. Odo stays at the post office and tracks their progress by radio.

VEX: We cross the Valley of Shadows.

PIP: It's the lower path. The lantern shows the stones.

MARA: The satchel is secure. Do not pull at the seal.

VEX: A celestial message may be hidden inside.

MARA: Nari ordered seeds. She can open the parcel herself.

A gust blows Vex's script pages down the slope. Pip catches one page against a fence. Vex reaches for another, but Mara warns him not to leave the lit path. He lets the paper go.

VEX: The prophecy is scattering.

PIP: It's your call sheet. Odo has another copy at the post office.

VEX: Then the moon has backup.

SCENE 3. EXT. ROOFTOP STAIR - LATER

The group reaches the west stair and finds a fresh paint sign. Pip points toward the open east stair, which leads to the rooftop gate. Mara checks the satchel buckle before taking the alternate path.

MARA: The parcel stays level. We can still arrive on time by the east stair.

VEX: The moon's route has been altered.

PIP: The painter needs the west stair dry. The east one is clear.

NARI, over radio: I can see your lantern from the roof. Come to the marked gate.

VEX: The recipient appears on the horizon.

MARA: That's the rooftop. Keep moving.

They climb the east stair. Vex attempts a dramatic pause at each landing, but Pip keeps the lantern pointed at the next step.

PIP: One landing left. No pause; the parcel is due before midnight.

VEX: The moon waits for no courier.

MARA: Neither does a clock.

SCENE 3A. EXT. HILL PATH - CONTINUOUS

Halfway up the east stair, Pip's lantern flickers. He shields it from the wind while Mara keeps the parcel steady. Vex suggests using the moon-shaped lamp as a beacon, but the lamp is at the destination.

VEX: The celestial signal has gone dark.

PIP: The lantern is still lit. The wind is blowing across it.

MARA: We can pause inside the stair alcove until the gust passes. The parcel stays in the satchel.

VEX: A pause in the journey risks the midnight deadline.

ODO, over radio: It is eleven-thirty. You have thirty minutes.

NARI, over radio: I can wait at the gate. The porch light is still on.

Vex holds his script pages against the lantern glass to block the wind. One page sticks there briefly, then slides down. Pip removes it before it catches flame.

PIP: Your prophecy is too close to the lantern.

VEX: The moon rejects paper offerings.

MARA: The lantern is for the path. Keep your pages in your pocket.

They resume climbing once the flame steadies. No one opens the parcel or leaves the marked stairs.

SCENE 3B. EXT. EAST STAIR - LATER

The porch light is visible above them. Mara checks the label without opening the parcel. The address is right-side up, the seal is intact, and Nari's signature card is still clipped to the satchel.

MARA: Everything is still in place. We can finish the climb.

VEX: The mission's final obstacle is a staircase.

PIP: It is the east stair. Six steps to the landing.

ODO, over radio: Time check: eleven-forty-five.

NARI: Gate is open. I'll meet you there, not halfway down the stairs.

Vex asks whether the parcel can be handed over beneath the moon-shaped lamp. Nari says the lamp is attached beside the rooftop gate, so he stops looking for another one on the hill.

VEX: We will deliver under its light.

MARA: We will deliver to the marked address. Keep walking.

SCENE 4. EXT. ROOFTOP GARDEN - NIGHT

Nari opens the gate and receives the satchel directly from Mara. She checks the recipient label and signs the delivery card. The parcel remains sealed.

NARI: Thank you. I will plant the seeds tomorrow at dawn.

VEX: The moon's last wish has been fulfilled.

NARI: The moon is full every month. These seeds are for my garden.

ODO, over radio: Delivery recorded at eleven-forty. Route complete.

Mara gives Nari the card. Vex looks at the moon-shaped lamp on the rooftop wall.

TAG: Nari hangs the lamp beside the gate. Vex whispers that it may be the moon's local representative; Pip hands him the empty satchel to carry back.
