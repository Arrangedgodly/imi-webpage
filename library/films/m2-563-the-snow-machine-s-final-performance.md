# The Snow Machine's Final Performance

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster movies that turn the scheduled shutdown of a snow machine into a blizzard emergency*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
OPERATOR MARA, a macaque
ANNOUNCER VEX, an orangutan
TECHNICIAN ODO, a gibbon
RUNNER PIP, a capuchin
PARK KEEPER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. WINTER PARK - AFTERNOON

A snow machine blows a light flurry across a fenced practice slope. A sign reads FINAL TEST: SHUTDOWN AT 3:00.

VEX: The blizzard is building.

MARA: The machine is on its last test before storage.

ODO: Wind is low. Snow output is set to minimum.

PIP: The flakes are landing inside the practice fence.

NARI: The public path is open and clear.

VEX: Could the mountain be buried?

MARA: The mountain is a painted backdrop. Stay behind the fence.

SCENE 2. EXT. CONTROL SHED - CONTINUOUS

Mara checks the switch. The timer shows 2:58. Odo inspects the power cable.

ODO: Cable is dry and plugged into the marked outlet. The shutdown timer is working.

VEX: Two minutes remain before the snowstorm.

PIP: The timer counts until the machine turns off. It doesn't count down to a disaster.

The wind shifts. A few flakes blow toward the open path. Nari closes the gate while Pip brushes the path with a small broom.

NARI: Gate closed. Path clear again.

SCENE 2A. EXT. WINTER PARK - AFTERNOON

Nari checks the slope and finds several flakes beyond the fence. She raises a broom to sweep them away from the public path.

MARA: Keep the machine off while we clear the path. Odo, confirm the breaker is isolated.

ODO: Breaker off. Indicator dark.

PIP: The flakes are paper, not artificial snow. The machine uses biodegradable confetti for the final test.

VEX: The storm leaves debris.

NARI: It leaves paper in the path. I'll sweep it into the recycling bag.

Pip lifts the chute cover only after Odo confirms the power is off. A small paper wad is caught at the edge.

PIP: That's what caused the last puff after shutdown. I'll remove it with the tool, not my hand.

MARA: Good. Keep fingers outside the chute.

Pip uses a long wooden hook and pulls the wad free. He places it in the bag.

ODO: Chute clear. The machine remains isolated.

VEX: The final flake has escaped.

MARA: The final flake has been collected. We will not run another test today.

The park's closing bell rings in the distance. Nari checks the gate and turns the sign to CLOSED FOR EQUIPMENT STORAGE.

SCENE 2B. EXT. WINTER PARK - AFTERNOON

The machine's operator panel shows a maintenance notice. Mara compares the serial number with the service log.

MARA: This is the last run before storage. The nozzle has to be rinsed and dried after shutdown.

ODO: Manual says disconnect power, wait for the fan to stop, then remove the chute cover.

PIP: Fan has stopped. The breaker is still off.

VEX: The blizzard's machinery is silent.

NARI: The practice area is empty. I'll check the path and sweep the remaining paper flakes.

Mara removes the nozzle with the proper tool. Pip holds the cleaning tray underneath. A few damp pieces drop into it.

MARA: Nozzle is clear. I'll rinse it at the utility sink, then dry it on the rack.

ODO: Storage label is ready. We should note that the machine was tested at minimum output and shut down at three.

VEX: The record will preserve our ordeal.

MARA: The record will preserve the service status. That is what the next operator needs.

Pip carries the tray to the sink. Nari closes the practice gate. Vex rolls up the dramatic storm poster and puts it in the event box.

PIP: Cleaning tray is ready.

MARA: Keep the power isolated until the nozzle is dry and reinstalled.

SCENE 3. EXT. SLOPE - 3:00

The machine slows, then stops. A final handful of paper snow falls from the chute.

VEX: The storm has ended.

MARA: The scheduled test is over. Odo, isolate the power before unplugging.

Odo flips the breaker. The indicator light goes dark. Pip unplugs the cable and coils it.

ODO: Power isolated. Machine is ready for storage.

VEX: Could we restart for a final shot?

MARA: No. The test is complete and the machine is being packed.

SCENE 4. INT. STORAGE SHED - LATER

The snow machine rests on a pallet. Nari labels the cable box. Mara closes the vent cover.

PIP: All paper snow is swept from the path and bagged for recycling.

VEX: The park survived the blizzard.

NARI: The park stayed open during a short snow test.

TAG: A paper flake falls from Vex's hat. He salutes it; Mara drops it into the recycling bag.
