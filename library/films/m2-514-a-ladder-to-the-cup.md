# A Ladder to the Cup

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: underdog sports films with a skeptical coach, a stubborn venue, training montages, and a final play*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: JUNE, a gibbon captain; ROK, a baboon blocker; PIPER, a capuchin rookie; COACH VALE, an orangutan; REFEREE, a macaque; DORO, a mandrill rival captain.

EXT. MUNICIPAL FRUIT COURT - MORNING

A small court sits beneath a banner: CITY TUMBLEBALL FINALS. The trophy, a silver cup, is perched on a shelf high above the court. June's team rolls a padded orange ball toward a chalk circle. It hits a crack and veers into the fence.

JUNE: The court moved.

ROK: The court is bolted down.

COACH VALE: The ball is round. It finds every bump.

Piper picks up the ball and tests the seams.

PIPER: It has a flat side.

ROK: That's where it was patched.

Doro's team arrives in matching red jerseys. Their ball rolls perfectly straight.

DORO: We have a sponsor, a new ball, and a photographer.

JUNE: We have a ball with a history.

COACH VALE: The history is why we keep losing.

ACT ONE

INT. REC CENTER - AFTERNOON

Coach Vale runs the team through a training drill. June rolls the ball along a chalk line. Rok blocks with a foam shield. Piper stands at the target circle, ready to catch. The ball hits the seam and bumps around the room.

COACH VALE: Again.

PIPER: The seam turns it left.

JUNE: We can use that. Aim right.

They try again. The ball curves into the target circle. Piper catches it against their chest. The team cheers, then the ball rolls free and knocks over a stack of cones.

ROK: We scored and lost the cones.

COACH VALE: The cones are practice equipment. The point counts.

A volunteer enters carrying the trophy. The cup wobbles on the shelf.

VOLUNTEER: The shelf is not part of the game.

DORO appears at the open doorway.

DORO: It will be after tonight.

Coach Vale asks the team to run a final play. June calls a spin pass. Rok blocks the route. Piper moves late and stops the ball with one palm.

COACH VALE: Why did you stop the pass?

PIPER: The ball was going toward the shelf.

The team looks up. The trophy stands above them, slightly tilted.

EXT. MUNICIPAL FRUIT COURT - NIGHT

The crowd fills folding chairs. The referee checks the court and places the ball at center. A hand-painted scoreboard reads ZERO-ZERO. Doro's sponsor banner hangs above one sideline. The trophy is moved to a table at court level.

REFEREE: Three rounds. A point for each ball inside the circle. No contact with the shelves or walls.

DORO: Understood.

The whistle blows. Doro's team rolls first. Their ball settles in the circle. June takes the patched ball. She aims right to use the seam's curve. It veers left, strikes the circle edge, and rolls just outside.

JUNE: The seam changed.

PIPER: The floor is wet near the line.

Rok points to a damp patch by the gate. A water bottle has leaked from the cooler.

REFEREE: Pause. We dry the court.

ACT TWO

The referee and teams mop the patch. The scoreboard remains at one-zero for Doro. June asks for a retake, but the referee rules that the first shot happened before the leak was spotted.

REFEREE: The next round starts after the court is dry.

DORO: We have the lead.

COACH VALE: You have one point. The game has two rounds left.

June studies the ball. Piper presses the patched seam, then turns it so the uneven side faces away from the target.

PIPER: The patch is still here. We can choose its direction.

The second round begins. Rok blocks Doro's ball without crossing the line. June rolls; the ball curves into the target. One point each.

In the final round, Doro aims straight. The ball clips the circle rim and rests on its edge. The crowd rises. The referee measures the gap with a card. The ball is outside.

DORO: It is touching the circle.

REFEREE: The center of the ball must be inside.

The scoreboard stays one-one. Piper takes the final shot. June positions the ball with the patch aimed toward the gate. Rok holds the cooler back from the boundary. Piper rolls gently. The patch turns the ball in a clean arc; it stops inside the target.

REFEREE: Point. Final score, two to one.

Doro checks the scoreboard, then shakes June's hand. Coach Vale lifts the trophy from the low table and offers it to Piper.

PIPER: Captain should take it.

JUNE: The rookie read the floor.

June raises the cup with the team. The volunteers sweep the court and cover the damp spot.

FINAL IMAGE: The team carries the trophy through the open gate while the patched ball rolls straight beside them.
