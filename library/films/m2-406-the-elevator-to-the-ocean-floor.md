# The Elevator to the Ocean Floor

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: deep-sea expeditions, countdowns, and equipment that keeps announcing routine pressure changes as disaster*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
CAPTAIN SERA, a gibbon
PIP, a capuchin engineer
NARI, a lemur marine biologist
VEX, a macaque announcer
THE ELEVATOR, a silent performer

SCENE 1. INT. UNDERSEA ELEVATOR - MORNING

All four crew members wear sealed diving suits with helmet voice links. Their research lift is flooded; bubbles rise past its waterproof controls. The elevator descends through a glass shaft toward the ocean floor. Vex narrates the journey.

VEX: We are entering the crushing depths.

NARI: We are twenty meters below the surface.

PIP: The elevator is rated for two hundred.

A warning light flashes. Captain Sera checks the panel. The display reads "PRESSURE CHANGE."

SERA: That happens when we go down.

VEX: The machine is warning us.

PIP: The machine is reporting.

A fish swims past the glass. Nari points it out. Vex calls it a scout. The fish turns and disappears.

SCENE 2. INT. ELEVATOR - LATER

The elevator slows. The pressure gauge rises. Pip checks the seals. One panel rattles. Vex asks whether the cabin is safe.

PIP: The pressure is equalizing.

VEX: The ocean is pushing in.

NARI: The seals are holding.

Sera presses the next floor button. It is labeled "OCEAN FLOOR." The button lights up, then turns off.

SERA: The elevator has refused the destination.

Pip tests the button and points to its broken contact.

PIP: The spare is in the locked service compartment. It needs a key.

Nari finds a maintenance key taped beneath the panel. It opens a small compartment containing a spare button. Pip installs it. The display now reads "OCEAN FLOOR, REQUEST RECEIVED."

VEX: We have been acknowledged.

SCENE 3. INT. ELEVATOR - CONTINUOUS

The cabin shakes. Vex grips the rail. Sera checks the depth. The elevator stops at an intermediate service platform. Nari says a research team installed a sensor there.

VEX: We are stranded.

PIP: We are at a platform.

The lift hatch opens onto a narrow metal landing. A crab walks into the flooded cabin, circles Vex's boot, then walks back through the same hatch onto the platform. The crew remains inside their sealed suits. Vex says the crab has taken the controls.

PIP: There are no controls on the platform.

Sera asks whether they should inspect the sensor. Nari steps out and checks a blinking light. It is a routine status signal. She returns through the open hatch before Sera checks the passenger count. The elevator display asks them to confirm occupancy.

VEX: We have a passenger.

NARI: The crab left.

PIP: The crab is not on the passenger list.

Nari asks Sera to record the crab's visit. Sera opens the logbook. The form asks for the visitor's depth, direction, and purpose. Nari watched the crab cross the elevator but did not see where it came from.

SERA: We can write "unknown."

VEX: A visitor from the deep.

PIP: A crab from the platform.

The elevator beeps. The display says "PASSENGER COUNT MISMATCH." Sera checks the cabin. Four crew members are aboard. The machine's list still includes the crab.

VEX: It has counted us as five.

NARI: We need to correct the manifest.

Pip presses the minus button. The display subtracts one, then asks which passenger has left. Vex points to the empty platform.

PIP: The crab left.

SERA: We don't have its name.

Nari writes "crab, exited to platform" in the waterproof log. Pip points to its retreating claws through the hatch.

PIP: It has completed its visit.

VEX: Without signing out.

Sera taps CONFIRM on the passenger screen. The warning clears. Pip closes the one hatch, checks that all four suit lights are green, and presses DESCEND.

SCENE 4. EXT. OCEAN FLOOR - AFTERNOON

The elevator stops at the survey area. Its hatch opens onto the ocean floor. Nari checks her helmet seal and steps through. The hatch remains open behind her as she finds a field of sea grass. Vex describes it as the edge of the unknown. Sera says it is the survey area.

Pip pays out the measuring cable. Nari records the height of the grass. The marked end disappears in the stalks. Vex says the ocean floor has swallowed the measurement. Nari kneels and finds the cable caught around a small rock.

NARI: The floor has kept the line.

PIP: It has a small knot.

Vex unhooks the cable. A bubble rises from the grass and breaks against his helmet. He ducks.

VEX: The ground has spoken.

NARI: That was air.

Pip reels in the measuring cable, but its free end catches in the open elevator hatch. He asks Vex to hold the loop. Vex backs away, pulling the cable tight. The hatch begins to swing inward.

SERA: Don't pull.

VEX: I am securing the line.

Nari reaches the panel and presses "OPEN." The hatch swings outward again, and the cable slips free. Vex falls backward onto a cushion of sea grass.

NARI: The floor is soft.

VEX: The ocean caught me.

SCENE 5. INT. ELEVATOR - EVENING

The crew returns to the flooded cabin. Pip closes the hatch behind the last diver. The display says "UPWARD JOURNEY." Vex announces the expedition is returning from the abyss. Pip presses the button. The elevator begins to rise.

The warning light flashes again. This time the display reads "PRESSURE CHANGE."

VEX: We are in danger again.

SERA: We are going up.

NARI: The pressure is changing in the other direction.

Pip hands Vex a clipboard with the readings. He narrates each number as it appears. The elevator reaches the surface.

FINAL IMAGE: The doors open. The first thing they see is the same fish, swimming past the shaft in the opposite direction.
