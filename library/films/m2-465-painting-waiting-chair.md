# The Painting of the Waiting Chair

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: arthouse minimalist films that stretch a static image into an event while resisting ordinary explanations*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: An artist films a chair in an empty gallery for an hour, while the curator, cleaner and visitors keep using it for its obvious purpose.

Cast: Director Sora, a macaque; artist Lemi, a gibbon; curator Varo, a capuchin; cleaner Pali, a lemur; visitor Odo, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. GALLERY - MORNING

A wooden chair sits in a white room. Sora sets a camera on a tripod. Lemi stands beside the chair.

SORA: We will film the chair without movement.

LEMI: The chair is already still.

VARO: The gallery opens at ten. Visitors may sit if they need to.

SORA: Sitting would alter the composition.

VARO: The chair is for sitting.

Sora starts the camera. The frame holds the chair, a white wall and a patch of sunlight. Nothing moves for several seconds.

SCENE 2 - INT. GALLERY - LATER

Pali enters with a broom. She sweeps around the chair. The broom touches one leg. The chair shifts half an inch.

SORA: The chair has changed.

PALI: I moved it so I could clean underneath.

LEMI: The shift alters the relationship between the object and the room.

PALI: There was dust under it.

Sora restarts the shot. The sunlight has moved. Lemi asks whether this is a second act.

SCENE 3 - INT. GALLERY - MIDDAY

Odo enters carrying a folded brochure. He sits in the chair. Sora continues filming.

ODO: Is this seat reserved?

VARO: It is available.

ODO: Good. I need to rest.

Lemi watches him. Odo finishes reading the brochure, stands and leaves. The chair is now warm. Sora turns off the camera.

SORA: The chair has experienced a visitor.

VARO: The visitor used a chair.

LEMI: We have a human scale in the frame.

ODO: I am a monkey.

SCENE 3A - INT. GALLERY LOBBY - MOMENTS LATER

Varo shows Sora the exhibit schedule. A children's group arrives early. The guide asks for a place to sit while she explains the room.

GUIDE: Can we use the chair?

SORA: It is part of the shot.

VARO: The gallery can provide stools.

The guide brings three folding stools. One has a loose hinge and squeaks when a child sits. Sora points the camera at the stool.

SORA: This one has a more active history.

LEMI: We are filming the chair, not the stools.

The guide asks the children to keep the walkway clear. They sit on the stools and look at the chair. One child raises a hand.

CHILD: Is that chair in the movie?

SORA: Yes.

CHILD: Why?

Sora considers the question. The camera continues recording. Lemi waits. Varo checks the room schedule.

LEMI: We want people to notice the chair.

CHILD: I noticed it. Can we go to the sculpture room now?

The guide leads the group away. The folding stools squeak in a short, uneven rhythm as they are carried out. Sora does not add music.

SCENE 4 - INT. GALLERY - AFTERNOON

Sora asks the gallery to close the room. Varo points to the posted hours. The room will close at five. Sora wants one continuous shot until then. Lemi leans against the wall. Pali returns with a dustpan.

SORA: The chair has been still for the last twenty minutes.

PALI: It moved when the visitor sat.

SORA: The film is about waiting.

VARO: Waiting for what?

Lemi considers the question. Sora keeps the camera running. A delivery cart passes outside the room, making a soft rolling sound. Dust shakes from the chair's seat.

LEMI: The room is waiting for the dust to fall.

PALI: It is waiting for the next cleaning.

Sora zooms toward the dust. The camera lens loses focus and the chair disappears into a soft blur.

SCENE 5 - INT. GALLERY - CLOSING

Varo unlocks the door for the last visitor. The room is empty again. Sora has filmed the chair in morning light, shade, after cleaning and after it has been used. The artist writes a title on a card: WAITING CHAIR.

SORA: We have a complete film.

LEMI: The chair began still and ended still.

VARO: It was sat in, moved, cleaned and filmed.

PALI: I have to move it again to mop under it.

Sora removes the camera from the tripod. Pali lifts the chair. A clean rectangle on the floor appears where it stood. Odo returns to retrieve his brochure and glances at the empty patch.

ODO: Is the chair gone?

VARO: It is being cleaned.

Odo waits by the wall. Pali replaces the chair after mopping. He sits down again.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera's last frame is the empty rectangle of floor, with the chair just outside the edge of the shot, occupied by Odo reading his brochure.
