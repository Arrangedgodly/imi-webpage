# The Great Cider Chase

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: high-speed chase movies, impossible vehicle stunts, engine close-ups, and a getaway plan interrupted by rolling cider barrels*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
TESS, a macaque scooter mechanic
DUB, a capuchin orchard runner
MACK, a gibbon delivery driver
LENA, a ring-tailed lemur cider maker

SCENE 1. EXT. ORCHARD ROAD - MORNING

A truck loaded with cider barrels is parked beside a steep hill. Mack checks the brakes. Tess, a macaque mechanic, inspects a small scooter. Dub carries a crate of apples.

MACK: The truck is too heavy for the hill.

TESS: Then do not take it down the hill.

LENA: The market is downhill.

A barrel rolls off the truck and gathers speed. Dub chases it on foot. Tess starts the scooter and follows. Mack runs after them holding a brake wrench.

SCENE 2. EXT. ORCHARD ROAD - CONTINUOUS

The barrel bounces over a ridge. Tess leans into each turn. The camera cuts to the scooter's engine, then to the barrel, then to a close-up of a leaf stuck to Tess's helmet.

TESS: The leaf is aerodynamic.

DUB: It is a leaf.

Mack catches up and uses the brake wrench to steer the truck away from the road. The truck stops against a haystack. The barrel continues toward the village, now followed by three loose apples.

SCENE 3. EXT. VILLAGE BRIDGE - DAY

The barrel reaches a narrow bridge. Lena blocks the path with a sign reading "CIDER TASTING." The barrel hits the sign and turns onto a footpath. Tess cannot follow on the scooter because the path is too narrow, so she picks it up and runs.

TESS: I have never carried my vehicle before.

DUB: It is lighter than the barrel.

They reach a fork. Dub takes the path toward the market. Tess follows the barrel's wet trail toward the orchard. The camera shows a suspenseful close-up of a single cider drop on the ground.

SCENE 4. EXT. CIDER MILL - AFTERNOON

The barrel rolls toward the mill's loading ramp. Lena opens the gate. Mack positions the truck beneath the ramp. The barrel bounces once, lands in the truck bed, and knocks over a stack of empty crates.

MACK: We caught it.

LENA: We caught the empty barrel.

The cider inside has leaked through a loose bung. The trail of cider has led every bee in the orchard toward the mill. Dub arrives carrying the last apple, which the bees have chosen as a landing place.

SCENE 5. EXT. MILL YARD - LATER

Tess fixes the barrel's bung. Mack learns to use the hand brake on the slope. Lena diverts the bees toward a bucket of cider-soaked fruit. Dub places the recovered apples in a new crate.

MACK: The truck is ready.

TESS: Take the flat road.

MACK: It is longer.

TESS: It is still a road.

SCENE 6. EXT. MARKET SQUARE - EVENING

The cider reaches the market in an orderly delivery. The villagers cheer. Lena pours a sample for everyone. Tess parks the scooter beside the stall; its leaf has become stuck to the helmet visor.

DUB: You can take it off now.

TESS: It is part of the design.

SCENE 7. EXT. MARKET ROAD - NEXT MORNING

The village hires Tess to teach the delivery crew how to secure barrels. She places one empty barrel on the truck and asks Mack to brake on the slope. He stops smoothly. Dub marks the safe route with apples, setting one at each turn.

MACK: We can deliver twice as fast now.

TESS: You can deliver without a chase.

Lena asks the villagers to move the tasting table away from the road. The bees follow the cider scent to a nearby orchard box. A child rolls an apple toward the old hill, but Dub catches it before it reaches the slope.

DUB: We have learned from yesterday.

TESS: The apple has not.

SCENE 8. EXT. ORCHARD ROAD - AFTERNOON

Mack loads three empty barrels and drives slowly down the hill. Tess walks beside the truck, checking each brake. At the turn, one barrel shifts. The crew stops before it can roll. Dub tightens the strap while Lena moves the bees' fruit box away from the road.

MACK: This would have been a very short chase.

TESS: That is the point.

They test the turn again. The truck follows the flat route to the market. The villagers clap as it arrives with every barrel still on board. Lena pours a sample, and Mack refuses to park on the hill.

MACK: I have seen what happens there.

FINAL IMAGE: The last apple rolls down the market table and stops in a cup of cider, where it bobs like a tiny red boat.
