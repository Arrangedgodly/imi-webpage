# Almost at the Same Tree

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic comedies built on near-meetings, mistaken messages and a final choice at a picturesque location*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: Two monkeys are sent to the same orchard by friends who hope they will meet. A series of misplaced notes brings them repeatedly to the wrong branch until they stop following the plan and speak for themselves.

Cast: Miri, a macaque baker; Odo, a gibbon gardener; Piri, Miri's friend; Venn, Odo's cousin; and a mail-carrying tamarin.

SCENE 1. EXT. ORCHARD PATH, MORNING

Piri hands Miri a folded note.

PIRI: Odo will be at the old fig tree at noon.

MIRI: Which old fig tree?

PIRI: The one by the bend.

The tamarin takes the note before Miri can fold it. It flies toward the ridge.

SCENE 2. EXT. RIDGE TREE, NOON

Odo waits beside a tall tree with a basket of seedlings. Venn hands him a note.

VENN: Miri will meet you at the old fig tree.

ODO: This is an old fig tree.

Venn points down the hill. Miri crosses the distant orchard path. Odo waves. Miri looks behind her, assuming the wave is for someone else.

SCENE 3. EXT. ORCHARD, AFTERNOON

Miri asks Piri to deliver a corrected note. The tamarin delivers it to Odo's basket and pecks at the corner.

ODO: "Meet me where the path turns."

Venn walks Odo to the first turn. Miri reaches the second turn. They both arrive, look around, and leave by opposite paths.

SCENE 4. INT. BAKERY HOLLOW, EVENING

Miri prepares small fruit tarts. Piri reads the delivery log.

PIRI: We have made three attempts to arrange one meeting.

MIRI: The notes keep disappearing.

The tamarin enters carrying a torn corner of paper and drops it beside the flour.

MIRI: It has delivered half a message.

PIRI: The half that says "turn."

Miri writes a new note: "I will be at the orchard. Come if you want." She walks it to the post.

SCENE 5. EXT. ORCHARD FORK, MORNING

Piri stands at the bend, holding a sign that points toward the creek. Venn waits on the ridge with a second sign pointing toward the tall tree. Both friends have agreed to "help" by directing Miri and Odo.

MIRI: I have a note that says the orchard.

PIRI: This way.

Odo arrives at the ridge path.

VENN: The old fig tree is that way.

ODO: I thought we were meeting at the bend.

Venn points downhill. Piri sees Odo and signals for him to wait. Odo thinks she is waving to someone behind him.

Miri follows Piri past the bend. Odo follows Venn down the ridge. They cross in the middle, each looking at a different sign.

PIRI: They have missed each other again.

VENN: We should stop helping.

Miri and Odo reach the fork at the same time from opposite directions. They see one another, then see the two signs.

MIRI: Did you write these?

ODO: I thought you had.

They turn the signs around. Both backs are blank. Piri removes the signs from the posts.

PIRI: We were making the route more complicated.

VENN: We can leave the fork unmarked.

MIRI: Or we can walk together to the orchard.

Odo takes the seedlings from his basket. Miri carries a tray of tarts. They choose the center path, which neither sign indicated.

The four of them walk to the post and pull the misleading signs out of the ground. Piri carries one under each arm. Venn removes the ribbon that marked the ridge route. They leave the fork clear, with no arrows pointing either way.

PIRI: We can let them choose the path.

VENN: We can walk with them.

SCENE 6. EXT. ORCHARD PATH, NEXT MORNING

Odo reads the note at the path entrance. He puts down his seed basket and walks toward the orchard without Venn.

Miri carries a tray of tarts along the same path. They meet beneath the bend tree.

ODO: I thought the old tree was the one on the ridge.

MIRI: I thought the bend tree was the one by the creek.

ODO: We could have asked.

MIRI: We did. We asked our friends.

They laugh. Odo sets the seedlings down. Miri offers a tart.

ODO: I brought a fig cutting.

MIRI: I brought lunch.

They plant the cutting together. The tamarin lands beside them and steals a tart.

SCENE 7. EXT. NEWLY PLANTED TREE, LATER

Piri and Venn arrive with a sign that reads "MEET HERE AT NOON."

MIRI: We have met.

PIRI: We have brought a clearer sign.

Odo turns it around. The back says "NOT THE RIDGE TREE."

The four friends sit beneath the new cutting. The tamarin carries away the sign, leaving the group to finish the tarts without further directions.
