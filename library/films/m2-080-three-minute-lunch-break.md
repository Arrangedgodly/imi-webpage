# The Three-Minute Lunch Break

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: workplace comedies, heist planning montages, and heroic plots built around a stolen lunch*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A theater crew treats a mislabeled lunchbox as a high-security theft, turning the stage, props room, and intercom into an overproduced search operation.

Cast: Dena, a baboon stage manager; Kitt, a macaque lighting operator; Sovo, a gorilla carpenter; and Rue, a capuchin usher.

SCENE 1. INT. MUNICIPAL THEATER, MIDDAY

The crew returns from a rehearsal break. Sovo's lunchbox is missing from the worktable. Dena shuts the doors and asks everyone to stay calm. Kitt dims the house lights for a search. Rue raises a hand; the lunchbox is visible on the prop cart, labeled "KING."

DENA: Nobody touch it. We do not know if the label is part of the case.

SOVO: It is a peanut-butter sandwich.

Dena calls it a possible prop contamination incident. Kitt plays a tense musical sting from the sound board. Rue asks if the audience has arrived. The house is empty except for a janitor asleep in the last row.

SCENE 2. INT. PROP ROOM, MOMENTS LATER

Dena organizes a search grid. Kitt creates a lighting cue for every shelf. Sovo checks the refrigerator and finds a second lunchbox, also labeled KING. The carpenter says it belongs to the actor playing the king. The crew now has two identical boxes and no clear ownership.

Rue proposes asking the actor. Dena says they need chain of custody first. Kitt photographs the boxes beside a ruler. Sovo recognizes his box by the small dent from a falling wrench. He reaches for it. Dena stops him until Rue checks the sign-in sheet. The sheet says the actor left his box in the dressing room.

They carry Sovo's lunch to the break table. The actor's box remains on the prop cart. The search seems finished, but Dena notices the janitor is holding an empty napkin and watching them.

SCENE 3. INT. THEATER LOBBY, AFTERNOON

The janitor says he moved the lunchbox from the worktable so a paint bucket would not drip on it. He placed it on the prop cart under the nearest sign. Dena asks why he did not leave a note. He points to the note taped beneath the cart: "Moved lunch away from wet paint." It was hidden by the wheel.

The janitor demonstrates the route with the cart. Each turn of its bad wheel swings the lunchbox toward the wet paint. Kitt tries to replay the route with a dramatic overhead light. The cue illuminates a ladder, not the cart. Sovo moves the ladder out of the aisle before someone trips. Dena admits her search grid sent the crew past the note three times because it mapped shelves but not the cart's path.

Rue finds a clean tray and puts a paper towel beneath the box. The janitor checks the ceiling for leaks. There is none; the paint bucket was just being moved. Dena removes the "evidence" tag from the lunchbox, and the crew watches it peel away in one satisfying strip. Kitt wants to add a reveal sting. Sovo says the sound effect can wait until after he eats.

Kitt announces a new plan: return the lunchbox to Sovo without disturbing the actor's prop. Dena says they can simply hand it to him. Kitt has already marked a route with spike tape and a spotlight. Rue rolls the cart. It hits the tape, pivots, and brings both boxes back to the worktable.

The actor enters wearing the king's crown and asks why his lunch is in the lobby. Sovo points out the dent on his own box. The actor checks his name on the other one. The crew has made an ordinary mix-up look like a royal succession crisis.

SCENE 4. INT. WORKTABLE, CONTINUOUS

Dena apologizes. The actor says the crown is uncomfortable and asks if anyone wants his extra apple. Sovo takes it in exchange for the sandwich. Kitt tries to provide a musical resolution. The sound board is still set to the tense sting, which plays each time someone opens a lunchbox.

Rue marks both containers with their owners' names. The janitor asks whether the labels should go on the top or side. Dena chooses the side, visible when the boxes are stacked. Kitt restores the regular work lights. No one gets a speech; they have three minutes left in the break.

The crew sits down together. Sovo opens his sandwich, the actor eats the apple, and Dena signs the theater's new rule: label personal food before leaving the table. The janitor adds, "and keep it off wet paint." They agree.

The actor asks if the lunch-search procedure is part of the rehearsal schedule. Dena checks the call sheet and sees she wrote "emergency lunch recovery" in the blocking notes. She crosses it out. Rue asks if they should keep the spike-tape route for the next production. Sovo suggests using it to mark the safe path around the paint bucket. Kitt relights the route in plain white so the crew can see where they are walking.

FINAL IMAGE: The spotlight isolates the two labeled lunchboxes. The tense sting begins, then Kitt cuts the cue and joins the crew for lunch.
