# The Paint That Changed Color

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: art mysteries, secret inks, dramatic restorations, and detectives who confuse wet paint with coded messages*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
RESTORER LUMA, a lemur
DETECTIVE VEX, a macaque
ARTIST NARI, an orangutan
GUARD PIP, a capuchin
CURATOR ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. GALLERY - MORNING

A painting changes from blue to green overnight. Detective Vex studies it under a flashlight. Curator Odo says it was repainted yesterday. Restorer Luma examines the surface and asks everyone to step back. Artist Nari says the paint may still be drying.

VEX: It is a message.

LUMA: It is wet paint.

The gallery lights warm the canvas. The green patch becomes more visible.

SCENE 2. INT. RESTORATION ROOM - LATER

Luma compares the paint sample with the artist's color chart. The pigment shifts under different lighting. Vex asks if the shift could hide a symbol. Nari demonstrates by moving a lamp across the sample. The color changes from blue to green and back. Pip checks the wall lights and finds one bulb was replaced with a warmer one.

ODO: The painting is changing because the light changed.

VEX: A light can be an accomplice.

Luma turns off the bulb and asks for a standard light. The painting returns to its original blue.

SCENE 2A. INT. RESTORATION ROOM - NOON

Nari checks the paint sample against the artist's chart, while Vex films each lamp change as if it were evidence. Luma asks him to stop shining the flashlight at the wet surface. Pip puts a swatch beside the canvas and guards it from the cord tugging at the lamp. Odo checks the temperature log and finds the gallery's new bulb runs warmer than the old one. The conservator approves the cool lamp and leaves the paint to cure. Vex labels his footage by lamp setting; his first take says "secret code" over a close-up of the bulb box. He asks Nari to repeat the paint test for a second take. She declines and hands him the chart, so he films the chart instead. His dramatic narration continues while the camera shows a row of bulb labels. Pip places a cover over the unused warm lamp, and Vex tries to make the cover look like a sealed evidence bag. Odo removes it and returns it to the lighting shelf. Vex records the shelf label without adding ominous music.

SCENE 3. INT. GALLERY - AFTERNOON

The team studies the rest of the painting. A series of brush marks looks like a code. Vex writes down numbers. Nari explains they represent shadows from the garden outside the window. Luma compares the marks with a photograph taken at noon. They match the direction of the sun.

Luma checks the gallery log and finds the warm bulb was borrowed from the portrait display for a camera test. Odo records its return to that display. Vex asks whether the mystery is solved. Luma says the color shift is understood; the artwork still has things to say.

SCENE 3A. INT. GALLERY - AFTERNOON

A visitor asks whether the gallery will change the lamp again. Nari points to the new setting label, and Luma shows the chart beside the painting. Vex prepares to lean over the rope for a close-up; Pip blocks him with the sign-in clipboard. The camera operator tests the view with a gray card. The lens renders the canvas greener than it looks in the room because its automatic color setting is still adjusting. Nari locks the white balance, and the picture matches the visitors' view. Vex asks whether the camera was also in the mystery. Odo points to the camera manual, which Vex has been using as a coaster.

SCENE 4. INT. GALLERY - EVENING

Before visitors return, Vex places a card beside the painting: "THE GREEN LIGHT IS A CLUE." Luma turns on the standard lamp and asks him to remove it. The pigment returns to blue. Vex flips the card over and writes, "THE WARM BULB WAS A CLUE." Odo says that is accurate but not a mystery. Vex adds, "Case closed," in smaller letters.

Visitors return. Nari gives a short explanation of how the colors appear under different light. One visitor asks whether the painting will be green again. Luma says it will if the warm bulb is used. Vex keeps the flashlight in his pocket. Pip puts a note beside the switch to indicate which bulb belongs in each room.

SCENE 5. INT. GALLERY - NIGHT

The gallery closes. Odo checks the lighting log. The painting remains blue. Vex files his notes under "lighting observation." Nari asks him to avoid calling the bulb a suspect. He updates the file title.

FINAL IMAGE: The blue painting rests beneath a cool white gallery light, its green pigment chart displayed beside it.
