# One Road, Three Detours

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: road movies whose destination keeps moving because the travelers refuse to consult the map*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: Three friends set out to reach a fig festival before sunset. Every scenic detour adds an obstacle until the group lets the quiet navigator choose the route.

Cast: Driver Varo, a baboon; navigator Seli, a gibbon; passenger Niko, a macaque; and mechanic Piri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. VILLAGE ROAD, MORNING

Varo ties a map to the front of a cart.

VARO: The festival is one straight road away.

Seli unfolds the map.

SELI: We need to turn left at the stream.

NIKO: The ridge road has a better view.

PIRI: The ridge road is closed for repairs.

Varo starts down the ridge road anyway.

SCENE 2. EXT. RIDGE ROAD, LATER

The cart stops at a barrier. Piri checks a wheel.

PIRI: We have to turn around.

VARO: This is a scenic delay.

NIKO: We can take the upper trail.

Seli points to the map. It shows the upper trail ending at a cliff.

NIKO: The map is old.

SELI: The cliff is still there.

They turn the cart. A basket of fruit rolls out. Niko catches it.

SCENE 3. EXT. STREAM CROSSING, AFTERNOON

The group reaches the stream. A small bridge is blocked by a fallen branch.

VARO: We can drive through.

PIRI: The wheel will sink.

Seli points to stones across the shallow water. The cart cannot fit between them.

NIKO: We can carry the fruit.

Varo starts to lift the basket. Piri stops him and secures the wheel.

PIRI: We will take the cart across one axle at a time.

Seli and Niko push while Varo steers. The cart crosses slowly.

SCENE 4. EXT. FORK IN THE ROAD, LATE AFTERNOON

The festival bell rings in the distance. Two paths lead toward it.

VARO: We take the wide path.

SELI: The map says the narrow path is shorter.

NIKO: The wide path has shade.

PIRI: The sun is already low.

Varo looks at the map, then gives it to Seli.

VARO: You navigate.

Seli chooses the narrow path. They walk the cart by hand, avoiding another detour.

SCENE 4A. EXT. STREAM PATH, LATE AFTERNOON

Piri checks the wheel after the crossing. One spoke is loose.

PIRI: We cannot hurry with this wheel.

Varo looks toward the festival lights.

VARO: We are losing the last hour.

Seli takes a small tool from her bag and tightens the spoke.

SELI: We can repair as we walk, if someone holds the cart.

Niko and Varo steady the cart. Piri works on the wheel. A group of travelers passes them carrying empty baskets.

TRAVELER: The fruit judging is over.

NIKO: Is the festival still open?

TRAVELER: The meal is.

Varo stops looking at the sun and helps Piri hold the axle level.

VARO: We can arrive for the meal.

Seli checks the map.

SELI: There is a level path by the creek.

They take it together, with the cart moving more slowly but steadily.

SCENE 4B. EXT. CREEK PATH, LATE AFTERNOON

The friends reach the narrow creek path. The cart wheel bumps over a stone. Piri stops to check it.

PIRI: The spoke is holding.

Seli tests the wheel and nods. Varo asks a passing cyclist how far the festival is.

CYCLIST: A short ride.

VARO: We are walking.

CYCLIST: Then a longer walk.

Niko laughs and offers the cyclist a fig. The cyclist takes it and points out a smoother turn.

SELI: That path is not on our map.

PIRI: It is still on the ground.

The group follows the turn. They hear music from the festival and continue without rushing.

SCENE 5. EXT. FIG FESTIVAL, SUNSET

The group arrives as the last fruit is being shared. They have missed the judging but not the meal.

NIKO: We came for the festival.

PIRI: We brought our own basket.

Seli offers a fig to the gatekeeper. The gatekeeper gives them a seat.

VARO: The road was longer than I expected.

SELI: It was the same road on the map.

[THEY SIT TO EAT. THE CART WHEEL ROLLS AWAY.]

PIRI: We need to catch the wheel.

Varo runs after it, catches it, and returns.

NIKO: That was the last detour.

PIRI: It was a loose wheel.

They repair it together. The festival lights glow behind them.

SCENE 6. EXT. VILLAGE ROAD, NIGHT

The friends walk the cart home. Varo follows Seli's directions without suggesting a view.

VARO: Turn left?

SELI: At the stream.

NIKO: And the ridge?

PIRI: Closed.

They pass a sign pointing to the scenic overlook. Varo starts to turn, then stops.

VARO: We know what that road looks like.

They continue toward home.
