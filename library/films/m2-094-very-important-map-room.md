# The Very Important Map Room

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: military command-room thrillers, glowing maps, and leaders who confuse pointing with planning*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A town's emergency team must route a detour around a flooded underpass while its commander keeps demanding a dramatic map briefing.

Cast: Commander Palla, a baboon; Sedge, a tortoise road engineer; Nix, a capuchin dispatcher; and Miri, a macaque bus driver.

SCENE 1. INT. TOWN OPERATIONS ROOM, MORNING

A monitor shows the underpass closed after overnight rain. Palla stands before a large map and points to the red line. Sedge says the red line marks the maintenance route, not the detour. Palla asks for a more commanding color.

PALLA: We need a plan that can be seen from space.

NIX: The bus riders need a turn they can make.

Miri arrives with a route log. The eastern bridge is open to buses but not heavy trucks. Palla asks her to confirm the bridge using a ceremonial pointer. Miri says she drove across it ten minutes ago.

SCENE 2. INT. OPERATIONS ROOM, LATER

Sedge checks the water report and road barriers. The underpass will remain closed until the crew inspects the drain. Palla wants to send a convoy through the east route. Miri notes that the bus stop is on the north side of the bridge. Nix moves the route marker on the screen.

Palla asks for a dramatic satellite image. Nix opens the town map, which has a bakery icon covering the bridge label. Sedge zooms in and finds the bridge weight limit. He tells the team to divert delivery trucks and keep buses on the east route. Palla tries to issue the order as a speech. Nix reads it over the dispatch radio in clear language.

Palla adds three colored arrows and a blinking circle to the screen. Nix cannot see street names beneath them. Sedge asks for the unmarked map. Palla says it lacks urgency. Miri points to a bus stop and asks whether a bus can turn there. Sedge checks the vehicle length and says the corner is too tight. They move the route to the next intersection. Nix calls the depot and confirms the revised stop. A driver reports the printed detour still shows the underpass closed, so the crew covers the obsolete arrows with temporary signs.

SCENE 3. EXT. EASTERN BRIDGE, AFTERNOON

Miri drives a test bus across the bridge. Sedge watches the supports and checks the weight. The route is safe. A delivery truck reaches the bridge and follows the bus. Nix radios Miri to hold at the next pullout while Sedge redirects the truck to the industrial road.

Palla arrives in a command vehicle with a map mounted on its hood. He points at the bridge and says the convoy is proceeding. Miri tells him the truck is not a bus. He asks if the map can be updated to show the distinction. Sedge takes a marker and adds two symbols: BUS and TRUCK. Palla holds the map steady.

The truck turns away. The bus continues on the approved route. A passenger asks whether the underpass is safe. Miri says it is closed pending inspection and gives the posted detour time. No one is asked to remain calm for the cameras.

The bus reaches the temporary stop. A passenger using a cane asks where to cross. Miri radios the dispatcher, who directs her to the marked ramp. Palla asks if this needs a camera crew. Sedge says it needs the ramp kept clear. Palla moves the map table away from the curb. A second bus approaches. Nix reads the route number, checks the sign, and confirms it is on the detour. Sedge logs the time while Palla holds the marker as Miri checks the stop.

SCENE 4. INT. TOWN OPERATIONS ROOM, EVENING

The drain crew reports the underpass is clear, but the road needs a final surface check in the morning. Sedge keeps the closure in place overnight. Palla asks whether they can announce victory. Nix says they can announce the east bridge detour is operating and the underpass remains closed.

The team updates the public map with bus stops, truck limits, and the inspection time. Palla tries to add a glowing animated arrow. Nix says the printed map is already clear. He turns off the special effects. Miri signs the route log, and Sedge files the drain report.

The next morning, Sedge and a road crew inspect the underpass. The drain is clear, but one patch of pavement needs another day to cure. Nix updates the closure notice and bus display. Palla asks whether the team can issue a victory bulletin. Miri says they can announce that the detour remains active. He reads the bulletin without adding the words "against all odds."

FINAL IMAGE: Palla folds the oversized map and tucks it under the dispatch desk. Nix points to the small route card drivers actually use; Palla reads it before giving his next order.
