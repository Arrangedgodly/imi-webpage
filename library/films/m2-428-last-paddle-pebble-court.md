# The Last Paddle at Pebble Court

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sports underdog films that promise a championship comeback from an unlikely team through an overblown training montage*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A team of office monkeys enters a paddle tournament with no practice, then discovers the tournament's greatest obstacle is its own motivational montage.

Cast: Coach Rudo, a macaque; player Mina, a capuchin; player Sef, a gibbon; player Taro, a lemur; tournament director Pala, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. RECREATION HALL - MORNING

A banner reads PEBBLE COURT DOUBLES. Coach Rudo sets three paddles on a bench. Mina holds a registration card.

MINA: We thought the tournament was next week.

RUDO: The bracket starts in twenty minutes. This is our underdog moment.

SEF: We have not practiced together.

RUDO: That makes the comeback more inspiring.

TARO: I have played once, against a wall.

RUDO: The wall taught you resilience.

Pala approaches with the bracket and a whistle.

PALA: You are scheduled against the library staff. Games are to eleven, win by two. Keep the ball below the ceiling lights.

RUDO: We are ready.

A ball rolls off the bench. No one catches it. It taps the wall and bounces back.

SCENE 2 - INT. WARM-UP COURT - MOMENTS LATER

Rudo starts a montage, clapping a fast rhythm. Mina practices a serve, Sef practices a backhand, and Taro ties his shoes. The music rises. They all try to hit at once, and the ball ricochets between paddles, then drops into a paper cup.

RUDO: Precision! Again!

MINA: We need turns.

RUDO: The montage cannot pause for turns.

PALA: The warm-up court is reserved for the other team in three minutes.

Rudo unveils a chalkboard with arrows and a drawing of a trophy. He names each arrow after a famous victory. The team studies the board instead of watching the ball.

TARO: The ball is behind the chalkboard.

RUDO: That is a defensive feint.

Rudo hands out matching headbands. Mina's is too loose and slides over her eyes. Sef ties it around his wrist. Taro reads the back of the registration card.

TARO: It says teams must bring one ball for warm-up. We have four.

RUDO: Extra equipment shows commitment.

PALA: The court has a ball rack. Please return the extras before the match.

Rudo stacks the balls on top of the chalkboard. One rolls down the board and knocks over the trophy sketch. He catches the board with one hand and waves the team forward with the other.

MINA: We could use the court lines to practice instead of the drawing.

RUDO: The lines are a visual aid for the audience.

MINA: They are boundaries. That is useful to us too.

SCENE 3 - INT. TOURNAMENT COURT - LATER

The library pair serves. Mina returns the ball cleanly. Sef steps forward, calling for it. Taro also steps forward. They bump paddles and miss the bounce.

PALA: Point, library.

RUDO: We need a captain's speech.

MINA: We need to call the ball.

RUDO: The crowd expects a turning point.

SEF: The point is to keep the ball in play.

The next serve clips the net and lands short. Mina reaches it, bumps it upward, and Sef returns it. The library pair misses. The score is tied.

TARO: We made a rally.

RUDO: The comeback begins now.

PALA: The match began ten minutes ago.

SCENE 4 - INT. TOURNAMENT COURT - CONTINUOUS

Rudo starts another motivational speech, but the ball is served while he talks. It hits the chalkboard, which has been propped near the sideline. The board tips. The trophy drawing lands face down over the real scoreboard.

MINA: Coach, move the board.

RUDO: The symbol of our dream is under attack.

SEF: It is covering the score.

Taro moves the chalkboard behind the bench. The rally continues. Mina and Sef call each shot aloud; Taro stays back and retrieves stray balls. They win a point, then lose the next. The match reaches ten-all.

RUDO: This is the final act. Give them your hearts!

MINA: Give us the ball.

Rudo tosses a spare ball instead of the live one. Pala blows the whistle and awards a replay. Rudo stops speaking and hands Mina the correct ball.

SCENE 5 - INT. TOURNAMENT COURT - LATER

The final rally is long. Mina returns the serve, Sef sends the ball deep, and the library pair returns it low. Taro calls "mine" and lets Mina take the shot. She lands it inside the line. The team wins by two.

PALA: Match to Pebble Court. Eleven-twelve? No, twelve-ten.

RUDO: Our historic comeback!

MINA: Our first match together.

SEF: We should sign up for practice before entering another tournament.

RUDO: Can I still use the montage music?

PALA: During warm-up, at a low volume.

Taro lifts the chalkboard. The trophy drawing has a paddle for a crown. He flips it around to reveal that he wrote the score backwards.

FINAL IMAGE: The team poses with a modest ribbon while Coach Rudo erases the word CHAMPIONSHIP from the chalkboard and writes PRACTICE, the music fading before its grand key change.
