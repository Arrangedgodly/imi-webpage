# The Mountain Cable Car Stops Twice

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: high-altitude disaster adventures with a stranded gondola, a ticking weather window, and a rescue plan*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: RANA, a gibbon cable operator; OTTO, a baboon mountain guide; VIV, a macaque climber; PEL, an orangutan mechanic; ELSI, a capuchin tourist; CAPTAIN RUM, a mandrill rescue chief.

EXT. MOUNTAIN STATION - AFTERNOON

A red cable car moves toward the summit. Snow starts to fall. At the lower station, Pel checks a dashboard. One warning light blinks.

PEL: The wind sensor is reading high.

RANA: The wind is calm.

Pel checks the second gauge. Its needle trembles. On the mountain-facing camera, loose snow skates across the ridge. Rana watches a flag at the roofline, which hangs still until a gust snaps it straight out.

RANA: The sensor is reading the gust before we feel it.

PEL: That is what it is for.

Captain Rum asks the radio operator to hold all summit departures. The operator repeats the closure to a group of climbers at the gate. One climber argues that the sky looks clear. Rum points to a cloud forming behind the ridge.

RUM: A clear sky at the station does not mean a clear cable.

A gust shakes the station window. The cable car stops above the valley. Inside, Otto, Viv, and Elsi look down through the glass floor.

ELSI: The floor was not in the brochure.

OTTO: It is not a floor. It is a window.

VIV: It is beneath us.

At the station, Rana tries the control panel. The motor is off. Captain Rum arrives with a rescue kit.

RUM: No one moves until we know whether the brake has held.

PEL: The brake is holding. The sensor has stopped the line.

ACT ONE

INT. CABLE CAR - CONTINUOUS

The passengers stand away from the doors. Otto asks Viv to clip each safety line to the central rail. Elsi holds a paper map that flaps in the draft.

OTTO: Put the map away.

ELSI: It is the emergency map.

VIV: It is a menu.

Elsi turns it over. The other side shows the trail map. They find the evacuation diagram underneath the fold. Viv reads the instructions.

VIV: Stay inside while the rescue line attaches. Then one person at a time.

A second gust hits. The cable car swings. Otto grabs a rail. Elsi's paper cup floats to the ceiling vent and stays there.

ELSI: My cocoa has reached the summit.

At the station, Pel checks the wind sensor and finds ice packed into the housing. Rana climbs a service ladder to brush it off, but the wind forces her back down.

RANA: I need the sensor clear to restart the line.

RUM: We need a rescue line before we restart anything.

Captain Rum launches a small inspection trolley along the cable. It stops short of the gondola. The rescue rope is too short by one meter. Viv sees a loose handrail bolt inside the cabin.

VIV: That bolt can hold the rope if we secure it.

OTTO: It is a handrail.

The cable car swings again. The passengers stop talking. Otto checks each clip and asks Elsi to keep both feet flat. Elsi puts the paper map between her shoes, where it cannot blow toward the door.

ELSI: I have secured the menu and the emergency plan.

OTTO: Good. Keep the drinks upright.

A small orange light flashes above the cabin door. Rana speaks through the public-address system, telling them the rescue trolley is approaching. The passengers can see its lamp moving along the cable, but it stops just beyond the gondola. The rope does not reach.

VIV: It is attached to the frame.

Elsi reaches for the bolt, but Otto stops her. He tests the rail, then helps Viv loop the rescue strap around the frame rather than the bolt.

INT. LOWER STATION - MOMENTS LATER

Rana and Pel use a long brush on the sensor from the sheltered side. Ice falls away. The wind gauge drops but remains above the safe restart line.

PEL: The sensor is clear. The wind is still too strong.

RUM: Then we use the trolley.

The inspection trolley returns with a longer rescue line. Captain Rum secures it to the gondola's emergency bracket. Otto and Viv clip in first. Elsi follows, carrying the emergency map flat under one arm.

ACT TWO

The rescue team guides each passenger along the cable to the lower station. The gondola remains locked until empty. Captain Rum crosses last and checks the bracket before unhooking the line.

Rana closes the summit route and puts a sign at the entrance. The rescued passengers wait inside the lower station while Pel replaces the frozen sensor cover.

RANA: The cable car stopped twice last week, and everyone kept calling it a glitch.

PEL: A warning is not a glitch.

Elsi pours the last of her cocoa into a paper cup. It is cold.

ELSI: The drink made the summit first.

Otto returns the menu map to the rack, unfolded to the emergency diagram. Viv points out that the evacuation route should be printed on both sides.

VIV: So nobody has to guess which side is the menu.

Captain Rum signs the closure notice. The gondola stays still as snow thickens outside.

FINAL IMAGE: The red cable car hangs safely at the station while the paper cup rests upside down beneath the emergency map.
