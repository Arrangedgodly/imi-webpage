# The Crown Is in the Pantry

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: historical costume epics, where a royal succession crisis turns on a crown stored beside the lentils*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: On the eve of a coronation, a missing crown sends the palace into a sweeping search that leads to the pantry.

Cast: Regent Mava, macaque; herald Tovi, gibbon; cook Sela, baboon; page Piri, capuchin.

SCENE 1. INT. PALACE HALL, DAWN

A velvet cushion sits empty beneath a painted portrait. The regent stands in a robe several sizes too long.

MAVA: The crown has vanished.

TOVI: We have searched the throne room.

MAVA: Search again, but with more urgency.

PIRI: I looked under the throne. There was a dust ball.

MAVA: Was it royal?

PIRI: It had a small thread of velvet.

The herald calls the court into a formal assembly. The cook enters carrying a pot.

SELA: The lentils are ready.

Mava tells Tovi to keep the court gathered while she searches the kitchens herself. Tovi asks whether the regent should be seen in a pantry.

MAVA: A ruler must know where the crown is.

SELA: Then come in. The floor is clean.

Mava lifts a sack of lentils. The crown is not beneath it. She lifts another and sneezes from flour dust.

MAVA: The pantry has attacked the regent.

PIRI: It has flour.

SELA: The crown will be easy to spot.

TOVI: Unless it is wearing a lentil.

TOVI: The coronation is not.

MAVA: Without the crown, there can be no coronation.

SELA: Without the lentils, there can be no lunch.

Mava commands that all doors be guarded. The pantry door remains open.

TOVI: Should I guard that door?

MAVA: It is a pantry.

SELA: That is where I keep the food.

MAVA: Guard it.

SCENE 2. INT. ARMORY, MORNING

The page brings an inventory scroll. It lists the crown, three helmets, four brass bowls, and one ceremonial ladle.

PIRI: The crown was last seen during yesterday's portrait sitting.

MAVA: I wore it.

TOVI: Then you took it off.

MAVA: I would remember taking off the crown.

PIRI: You took off the robe too.

Mava looks down at the long robe trailing across the floor.

MAVA: That is different.

The herald orders a search of the armory. The brass bowls are stacked in a corner. One has a ring of purple cloth inside.

TOVI: A clue.

PIRI: It is the lining from the crown box.

They examine it. The box is empty. The cook arrives to ask whether she can retrieve pepper from the pantry.

MAVA: Not until we discover the crown.

SELA: The pepper is in the pantry.

MAVA: Is the crown in the pantry?

SELA: I don't know. I haven't looked.

MAVA: Then you may look.

SCENE 3. INT. PANTRY, CONTINUOUS

Sela opens the pantry. The crown is on a shelf, beside a sack of lentils.

PIRI: It has been found.

TOVI: The crown is among the provisions.

MAVA: How did it get there?

SELA: You brought it here yesterday. You used it to press down the lid on the flour tin.

MAVA: I would never use the crown as a weight.

PIRI: The flour tin is still dented.

Mava studies the crown. A thin layer of flour dusts the gems.

MAVA: It has endured a humble task.

SELA: It kept the lid from popping off.

TOVI: The regalia has performed a service to the kitchen.

MAVA: We shall record the event as a royal inspection of provisions.

SCENE 4. INT. PALACE HALL, LATER

The crown is polished. The coronation begins. The herald reads a long formal address while the cook waits with the pot.

TOVI: Before the crown is placed, the ruler shall pledge to protect the kingdom's harvest, paths, and-

SELA: Lunch is cooling.

MAVA: Let the pledge be brief.

Tovi shortens the scroll. Piri places the crown on the regent's head. It slides sideways because flour remains in the lining.

PIRI: It is slipping.

MAVA: The crown has returned from its duties.

Sela brings a clean cloth and tucks it inside. The crown sits straight.

SELA: It needed a better fit.

TOVI: The kingdom is secure.

MAVA: The kingdom is hungry.

SCENE 5. INT. PANTRY, EVENING

After the ceremony, the crown rests in its case. Mava opens the pantry and inspects the flour tin. The lid is secure.

MAVA: No one may move the crown again.

SELA: Then who will weigh down the tin?

PIRI: The crown has already served its term.

TOVI: We can use a stone.

Mava considers this, then puts the crown case on a high shelf. Sela places a small stone on the flour tin.

MAVA: Let it be known the crown is no longer responsible for household duties.

SELA: The lentils still need a lid.

MAVA: The kingdom will provide one.

[SFX: PANTRY DOOR CLOSES, POT SIMMERS.]
