# The Royal Cook Loses the Ladle

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: royal kitchen dramas that turn a missing utensil into a race against the banquet bell*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A palace chef prepares soup for a royal banquet, but the missing ceremonial ladle sends the staff through increasingly theatrical searches before anyone checks the sink.

Cast: head cook Rena, an orangutan; apprentice Tavi, a macaque; steward Volo, a gibbon; dishwasher Pemi, a lemur; royal taster Sori, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - INT. PALACE KITCHEN - AFTERNOON

A huge pot simmers over a low burner. Rena tastes the soup. Tavi opens a drawer and finds forks, whisks and one wooden spoon, but no ladle. The banquet bell will ring in twenty minutes.

RENA: The soup is ready. The ceremonial ladle is missing.

TAVI: We have three serving spoons.

RENA: The royal table expects the silver one.

Volo enters with a checklist. He says the ladle was polished and placed beside the sink. The sink is empty. Sori arrives for a tasting and asks whether the soup is ready.

SCENE 2 - INT. PANTRY - LATER

Tavi searches the pantry. Rena searches the stove area. Volo interrogates the kitchen staff as if conducting a royal inquiry. Pemi says she washed the ladle and put it on the drying rack. The rack is empty. Rena opens the cupboard and finds a stack of plates bearing a silver ladle-shaped shadow.

TAVI: It was here.

RENA: That is the reflection from the serving tray.

Sori tastes the soup with the wooden spoon and confirms it is seasoned correctly. Volo asks for the ceremonial utensil before the guests arrive. Rena tells him to stop calling it a royal crisis.

Volo gathers the kitchen staff and asks each person to state when they last saw the ladle. Pemi says it was in the rinse water. Tavi says he saw a gleam near the sink. Rena says the silver polish left the counter at two. Volo repeats each detail in a solemn voice, then writes them on the menu board. The soup special is replaced by a list of witnesses. Sori arrives with a tasting cup and asks to be entered as "the last person to taste the soup."

SCENE 3 - INT. KITCHEN PASSAGE - CONTINUOUS

The staff search a laundry basket, a flour bin and a tower of clean bowls. Tavi lifts each bowl like a detective revealing evidence. Pemi checks the dishwasher and finds a rack of plates. The ladle is not there. Volo asks whether someone took it through the service door. Rena checks the sign-out board; no utensils have left the kitchen.

Pemi notices a wet trail from the sink to the dish rack. It ends at a folded towel. She lifts the towel and sees a metal handle underneath. Tavi reaches for it, but Rena stops him because the ladle is still hot from the rinse.

Rena asks Tavi to ladle one bowl with the wooden spoon as a test. It cannot reach the bottom of the pot, and Tavi splashes soup onto the edge of the silver tray. Sori tastes the sample and asks for a smaller portion. Volo attempts to carry the tray with both hands, but the checklist is still tucked under his arm. Rena takes the checklist and points him toward the dining room. He walks off without his royal inquiry notes, then returns to collect them.

The staff hear the banquet bell from the hall. Volo raises the checklist like a royal warrant and asks whether the ladle is the last missing item. Rena points to the bowls already stacked for service. He crosses soup service off the list and circles the bell time.

SCENE 4 - INT. PALACE KITCHEN - MOMENTS LATER

The banquet bell rings. Pemi pulls the towel back with oven mitts. The ladle is stuck inside a stack of wet pans, handle hidden beneath the top pan. Rena frees it. Volo announces its recovery as if declaring a treaty. Sori takes the ladle and serves the soup. The first bowl is too full; she pours some back into the pot.

SORI: The soup is excellent. The portion is a royal flood.

Tavi turns down the burner and wipes the counter. Rena asks Volo to carry the silver ladle to the dining room. He starts a speech about the recovered object. The banquet bell rings again, and he walks faster.

SCENE 5 - INT. ROYAL DINING ROOM - EVENING

The guests receive their soup. Rena and Tavi stand by the kitchen doors. Volo places the ladle on a silver tray. Pemi brings the cleaned pans back to the rack. Rena adds a hook beside the sink and hangs the ladle there after service.

FINAL IMAGE: The ceremonial ladle hangs above the sink on its new hook, while the wooden spoon remains in the drawer as the kitchen's actual daily tool.
