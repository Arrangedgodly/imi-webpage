# The Longest Close-Up

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: art-house slow cinema, long takes, symbolic close-ups, and audiences who wait for a mundane action to reveal its meaning*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
ELI, a capuchin bus rider
SORA, a ring-tailed lemur station cleaner
BIM, a gibbon driver
THE WAITING CROWD, macaques and tamarins
THE WORKER, a marmoset on the branch-clearing crew

SCENE 1. EXT. BUS STOP - DAWN

A bus stop stands beside an empty road. Eli waits. A paper cup sits on the bench. The camera holds on the cup for a full minute as wind moves the lid.

SORA (O.S.): Is this yours?

ELI: No.

Sora picks up the cup and places it in a bin. The camera stays on the empty bench.

SCENE 2. EXT. BUS STOP - LATER

The road remains empty. A row of people forms behind Eli. No one speaks. The camera studies their shoes: polished, muddy, wet, and one pair with different laces.

A posted schedule says the bus arrives at 7:05. The clock reads 7:18. The waiting crowd shifts. A leaf rolls through the frame and comes to rest beside a painted line.

ELI: The bus is late.

SORA: It is the only bus.

SCENE 2A. EXT. BUS STOP - MORNING

The bus does not arrive. The waiting crowd grows. One passenger shares a scarf with the person in short sleeves. Another offers an apple. The camera lingers on the apple as it passes from hand to hand, each person taking one slice and leaving the stem.

ELI: We have been waiting an hour.

SORA: The schedule says the bus should have come twice.

BIM (O.S.): The road is blocked by a fallen branch.

The crowd looks toward the road but cannot see the obstruction. Sora sweeps a small path in the snow around the bench. The leaf rolls into the cleared space and stops.

SCENE 3. EXT. BUS STOP - CONTINUOUS

The camera holds on the timetable. It does not change. Sora sweeps the sidewalk in careful strokes. Each stroke moves the leaf farther from the curb and closer to Eli's shoe.

Eli lifts his foot. The leaf stops. He lowers it. The leaf moves again. Sora notices the sweep has started a tiny breeze that nudges the leaf along.

SORA: It wants to cross.

ELI: It is a leaf.

Sora stops sweeping. The leaf stops moving.

SCENE 3B. EXT. BUS STOP - CONTINUOUS

The passengers continue waiting. The camera holds on the paper timetable as a shadow crosses it. A small tear in the corner lifts, then settles. Eli folds his arms. Sora sweeps the road dust into a neat line.

SORA: The bus is a place to go. The stop is a place to stay.

ELI: I can do both.

BIM (O.S.): The utility crew is here. They have lowered the wire and secured it.

The worker cuts the branch into sections. Each piece lands on the cart. The camera follows one log as it rolls, stops, and remains still.

WORKER: The road is clear. We can open it now.

SORA: Then the bus can come through.

A distant engine grows louder. The waiting crowd turns toward the road as one. No one rushes. The bus appears at the bend and brakes beside the marked line.

SCENE 4. EXT. BUS STOP - AFTERNOON

Bim opens the doors. The passengers step toward them. Eli stays beside the leaf. The camera settles into a close-up of his finger hovering above it. Bim leans out of the cab.

BIM: Are you coming?

ELI: I was about to move this across the line.

BIM: The door is also across a line. You can cross that one.

The finger remains still. Sora lowers her broom into frame. Eli looks toward her without turning his head. The camera refuses to widen. Only the broom bristles and one corner of his shoe are visible.

SORA: Shall I sweep it?

ELI: That would change the shot.

BIM: It would end the shot.

Sora withdraws the broom. Bim closes the doors halfway. The passengers already inside stare through the glass. One points at an empty wrist, where a watch would have made the gesture less theatrical.

Eli pushes the leaf. It catches on a crack in the paint. He pushes again. It turns sideways. The camera stays close enough that the waiting bus is only a warm reflection on his fingernail.

BIM: The fare is the same if the leaf comes with you.

Eli picks it up. The camera abruptly loses its subject and holds on bare pavement. Eli boards with the leaf. Bim shuts the doors before anyone can propose a second take.

SCENE 5. INT. BUS - MOVING - EVENING

Eli sets the leaf on the empty seat beside him. The camera holds on it while the road crew loads the branch outside the window. A passenger reaches for the seat.

ELI: It's occupied.

PASSENGER: By autumn?

The passenger sits. Eli catches the leaf and transfers it to his knee. The bus brakes. The leaf slides into the aisle. Everyone looks down; nobody moves. Bim watches in the mirror.

BIM: If it reaches the front, it needs a ticket.

Eli retrieves it before it crosses another painted line. He holds it flat between his palms for the rest of the journey. At his stop he stands, waits for the camera to settle on the empty seat, and steps out.

The camera keeps studying the upholstery. Outside, Eli taps the window with his leaf, trying to restore the final symbol to the shot. Bim drives away. The tapping disappears; the seat remains.

FINAL IMAGE: A new passenger sits directly in front of the lens. The frame fills with the back of a coat. The bus engine continues for twelve seconds before the cut.
