# The Reboot That Forgot Its Hero

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: franchise reboots that rename familiar characters, repeat origin scenes and advertise nostalgia while ignoring continuity*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A studio reboots a beloved monkey adventurer with a new name and origin, only to discover the original hero is still working in the prop room.

Cast: Koro, the original adventurer, a macaque; Tessa, the new director, a capuchin; Rell, a young actor, a lemur; producer Mavi, a gibbon; archivist Dena, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. STUDIO SOUNDSTAGE - MORNING

A painted jungle backdrop is rolled into place. Rell wears a new leather vest and holds a wooden compass. Mavi reads from a press sheet.

MAVI: We are introducing Koro as Kairo, the fearless explorer with no past.

TESSA: His name is Koro. The old films are in the archive.

MAVI: The reboot needs a clean slate.

RELL: Do I know where the compass came from?

MAVI: It was found in a cave during your first adventure.

TESSA: In the original, it was a gift from his aunt.

MAVI: We are not discussing the original.

Koro enters carrying a crate of old props. He sees the compass.

KORO: That is mine. I returned it after the last film.

SCENE 2 - INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - LATER

Dena opens a storage drawer. Inside are four identical wooden compasses, each tagged with a film number. Mavi points to the newest prop.

MAVI: This one is the reboot compass.

DENA: It is the same compass from the third film. We replaced its needle.

RELL: Does it still point north?

DENA: Its magnetic needle points north unless nearby steel deflects it.

Tessa tests it. The needle swings toward a metal shelf.

TESSA: It points to props.

Koro takes the compass and turns it in his paw.

KORO: In the old story, the compass was a symbol. He did not need it to find his way.

MAVI: We can add a line about legacy.

SCENE 3 - INT. SOUNDSTAGE - AFTERNOON

Tessa directs a new origin scene. Rell stands before a cardboard cave. A silent crew member drops a foam rock from above. It misses him and lands in the jungle backdrop.

TESSA: Cut. The old origin used a real stone prop, but the new one has foam.

MAVI: Make it feel heavier with music.

KORO: The music cannot make foam heavier.

RELL: I can pick it up and discover the compass inside.

TESSA: The first film had his aunt give it to him. This cave changes the origin.

MAVI: The audience likes familiar moments.

DENA: The audience also knows this prop. It has a scratch from the second film, where Koro dropped it below the bridge.

Rell turns the compass. The scratch is visible on camera.

SCENE 3A - INT. SOUNDSTAGE - CONTINUOUS

The crew tries a second take. Mavi asks Rell to look surprised when he sees the compass. Rell does so, but Koro immediately points to the old scratch.

KORO: That mark is from the waterfall scene.

RELL: Which one?

KORO: The second film. We dropped the compass and found it below the bridge.

MAVI: The new film has no waterfall.

DENA: The prop log says it was repaired after that shoot.

Rell lowers the compass. He looks toward the fake cave, then toward the archive door.

RELL: Maybe my character begins by asking what happened before.

TESSA: That gives us a scene with Koro instead of another origin.

SCENE 4 - INT. PRODUCER'S OFFICE - EVENING

Mavi reviews the footage. The new scene shows Rell discovering the compass in a cave; the archive cut shows Koro receiving it from his aunt beside a river. The handoff is repeated, but the origin has changed.

MAVI: We can call it an intentional echo.

TESSA: You have recycled the object and changed the story around it.

KORO: I can show Rell how to use the compass without pretending it is new.

RELL: I would like to meet the character before I play him.

MAVI: The publicity cards already say Kairo.

DENA: Change them. The name in the archive is Koro.

Mavi looks at the old posters on the wall. He takes down the new title card and replaces it with a sheet reading THE NEXT KORO ADVENTURE.

SCENE 5 - INT. SOUNDSTAGE - NEXT DAY

Rell and Koro rehearse a scene in which the young adventurer asks how to find a trail. Koro points out the compass needle is stuck against a steel crate. Rell moves the crate; the needle settles north.

RELL: So it is useful, but not magical.

KORO: It is a tool. You still have to look around.

Tessa frames the shot with the old jungle backdrop and a new trail marker. Mavi watches from the monitor, holding a stack of corrected press cards.

MAVI: We can sell this as a new beginning.

TESSA: It is a continuation.

MAVI: A fresh continuation.

Koro hands the compass to Rell. The new actor straps it on and checks the direction. The compass points away from camera toward the stage exit.

FINAL IMAGE: The old and new actors walk off the set together, following the compass past a sign that reads ARCHIVE, not into the cardboard cave.
