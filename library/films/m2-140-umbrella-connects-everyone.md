# The Umbrella Connects Everyone

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: ensemble dramas that force unrelated characters together through a chain of coincidences and a shared symbolic object*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A red umbrella passes through a crowded station, while a director keeps insisting every stranger's errand is part of one emotional web.

Cast: Rena, a macaque director; Odi, a lemur commuter; Garo, a gorilla station cleaner; Puck, a capuchin courier; and Tavi, a tamarin musician.

SCENE 1. INT. CENTRAL STATION, MORNING

Rain streaks the windows. Odi leaves a red umbrella beside a bench while checking the train board. Puck picks it up, thinking it belongs to a delivery. Rena films from the platform and whispers that the object has begun its journey.

ODI: I will be right back.

PUCK: I have to deliver this parcel.

GARO: That umbrella is not in the lost property bin.

Rena asks Garo to hold the umbrella until Odi returns. Garo says he is cleaning the platform, not collecting props. Puck places it by the desk and leaves. Odi returns, sees the bench is empty, and asks Garo if he has seen it. Rena holds her camera on the empty bench for a meaningful pause. Garo rolls his cleaning cart through the shot and wipes the bench. Odi waits while the floor dries.

RENA: Can we do that again without the cart?

GARO: The cart is why the floor is clean.

Odi retrieves the umbrella from the desk and opens it to check the fabric. A small hole lets rain through. Garo points to the desk.

SCENE 2. INT. STATION CAFE, LATER

Rena follows Odi into the cafe. He takes the umbrella to the table next to Tavi, who is tuning a small instrument. Rena asks Tavi to play something that suggests a hidden connection. Tavi plays a scale. Odi asks if he is waiting for the train.

TAVI: I am waiting for my rehearsal room to open.

ODI: I am going to visit my sister.

Rena writes "two journeys, one shelter" in her notebook. The umbrella leans against the table. A waiter moves it away from a puddle, then returns it to Odi. Rena asks for another angle that shows the object bringing them together.

ODI: We are sitting at separate tables.

Tavi asks if Odi knows where the practice rooms are. Odi does; he used to rehearse nearby. They talk about the station's old piano. Rena nods, delighted, and asks if the conversation can mention the umbrella. Tavi says it is raining. Odi says he needs to catch his train. Rena asks him to look back at Tavi as if they have known each other for years. Odi looks back to make sure Tavi has not left his instrument case in the aisle. Tavi waves and points at the train board. The train time has changed.

TAVI: Your platform is on the other side.

Odi thanks him and moves toward the stairs. Rena tries to follow with her camera, but the umbrella catches in the cafe door.

SCENE 3. INT. PLATFORM, AFTERNOON

Puck returns with an empty parcel bag. He sees the umbrella and recognizes its red handle. He asks Odi if the umbrella is part of a delivery. Odi says it is his. Puck says he nearly carried it across town. Garo overhears and asks if the umbrella belongs in lost property after all.

Rena positions all three beneath the station clock. She asks them to stand closer so the frame suggests an old friendship. They step closer; the umbrella closes between them and drips onto the floor.

GARO: I just mopped this platform.

Rena tells them to share the umbrella. Tavi arrives with his instrument case, which is too wide to fit beneath it. Puck offers to hold the case. Odi says they do not have to pose. The train arrives. The people split naturally toward different doors. Rena asks them to pause for a wide shot. The doors close before anyone reaches them. Garo presses the service button. They wait while the doors reopen. Odi boards; Tavi goes down the stairs; Puck heads back to the lobby; Garo pushes the mop cart away.

RENA: The composition has separated.

GARO: The train schedule did that.

SCENE 4. INT. STATION LOBBY, EVENING

Rena reviews her footage on a laptop. Garo mops around her chair. She opens a video call. Odi answers from his house, Tavi from a rehearsal room, and Puck from beside a parcel locker. Their locations appear in three separate boxes.

RENA: We need one scene where all the journeys meet.

ODI: Mine met my sister's front door. The umbrella is drying in her hall.

TAVI: Mine meets a conductor in thirty seconds.

PUCK: Mine is already late. The locker won't wait for your final act.

Rena draws a red line on her station map between the three call windows. Garo wipes up another umbrella puddle on the floor.

RENA: Could you all give the same meaningful sigh? I can cut between your faces.

ODI: I can sneeze. The umbrella has a hole.

TAVI: I can tune this string.

PUCK: I can enter a collection code.

Garo sets his mop beside the laptop.

GARO: I can close the station. That is my ending.

Three different sounds overlap: a sneeze, a twanging string, and the parcel lock beeping. Rena reaches for the volume controls.

RENA: Hold those feelings. We can find a common note.

The callers end their connections. Three windows disappear. Garo wheels his cart into the service corridor. Rena's red lines now connect three blank boxes and the laptop's low-battery warning.

FINAL IMAGE: The umbrella dries by Odi's door. Rena's unfinished map lies beside her closed laptop; its only remaining connection runs to the charging socket.
