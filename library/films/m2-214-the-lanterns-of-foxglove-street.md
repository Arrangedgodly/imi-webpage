# The Lanterns of Foxglove Street

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: period romantic melodramas, elaborate letters, yearning balcony scenes, and a mistaken inheritance that is actually a bequest of garden tools*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
ELIAN, a gibbon letter carrier
ROSA, a capuchin gardener
MADAM VALE, a macaque estate owner
SIR BRAM, a ring-tailed lemur neighbor

SCENE 1. EXT. FOXGLOVE STREET - MORNING

Elian rides a penny-farthing down the cobbled street with a bundle of sealed letters. One envelope has a red wax seal and a dramatic black ribbon. A gust lifts it from his satchel and carries it over the garden wall. Rosa catches it in the branches of a foxglove trellis.

ROSA: A letter for me?

ELIAN: I cannot say. The seal is closed.

ROSA: Then why does it have my name on it?

Elian checks the address: MADAM VALE, GARDEN HOUSE. Rosa lives in the garden house. The two exchange a long look. A violinist across the street begins a sentimental phrase and gets interrupted by a cart wheel.

SCENE 2. INT. MADAM VALE'S PARLOR - AFTERNOON

Madam Vale reads the letter aloud. It announces that she is to receive a legacy from an unknown relative. Sir Bram arrives, wearing a waistcoat covered in pockets and carrying a shovel.

BRAM: I have come to assist with the inheritance.

VALE: You were not invited.

BRAM: I sensed a shovel might be required.

The letter's final page is missing. Elian returns with the envelope's ribbon caught around his bicycle bell. He finds the page in the garden, where Rosa has used it to label a seed tray.

ELIAN: This is the missing part.

ROSA: It says "the tools in the shed." That is not the fortune I expected.

VALE: It is a fortune in tools. My aunt was precise.

Bram produces a second shovel from his waistcoat. The pocket sags. He insists it is a family heirloom, but Vale recognizes the garden shed's brand stamped on the handle.

SCENE 3. EXT. BALCONY - DUSK

Rosa stands on the balcony, reading an old gardening manual by lantern light. Elian waits below, rehearsing a grand speech. Sir Bram peers through a hedge with a looking glass.

ELIAN: Rosa, under the moon's pale-

A lantern sputters. Rosa leans over the rail.

ROSA: Can you hold this ladder while I change the wick?

Elian catches the ladder. His speech papers flutter into the street. Bram chases them and collides with the violinist's music stand. The violinist changes to a brisk tune while Bram marches after the pages.

ROSA: Is this part of your speech?

ELIAN: It is now the only part I can remember.

He offers the gardening manual instead. Rosa accepts it, and the pair spend the rest of the evening reading the diagrams upside down while Bram retrieves the last page from a puddle.

SCENE 4. INT. GARDEN SHED - NIGHT

Vale unlocks the inherited shed. Inside are old tools, seed jars, a broken wheelbarrow, and a portrait of the unknown aunt. A note explains that the tools are to be shared by anyone willing to maintain the public garden.

VALE: She left a legacy for the whole street.

BRAM: I brought a shovel.

ROSA: At last, your dramatic entrance has a purpose.

Elian discovers a small brass key in the tool chest. It opens a locked cabinet containing an old lantern with a working reflector. Rosa realizes the street can use the lanterns for a community planting night. The wheelbarrow has one square wheel and makes a slow, mournful clank as Bram pushes it.

SCENE 5. EXT. FOXGLOVE STREET - NEXT EVENING

Neighbors gather to plant bulbs. Vale distributes tools. Bram digs a hole much too large, then insists it is a "future pond." Elian tries again to deliver his speech to Rosa, but the lanterns glow, the neighbors begin singing, and the street becomes busier than he expected.

ELIAN: I had planned to say something about moonlight.

ROSA: You can say it while we plant.

He hands her a trowel. They plant a row of bulbs together. A single seed packet falls from the inherited shed and opens in the breeze, scattering seeds across Bram's oversized hole. He starts to shovel them out, then sees a garden bed forming and carefully pats the soil down.

SCENE 6. EXT. FOXGLOVE STREET - LATER

The violinist plays a gentle tune that this time survives the cart traffic. Elian and Rosa hang the last lantern together. Vale gives Bram a watering can and takes away both shovels until he promises to dig only where the map says.

BRAM: The map has no pond.

VALE: Exactly.

FINAL IMAGE: The lanterns swing above the street as the neighbors fill Bram's hole with bulbs, leaving him standing in the middle holding the last shovel like a ceremonial staff.
