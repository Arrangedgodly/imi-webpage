# First Contact at the Lost-and-Found Desk

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: alien first-contact films, cryptic signals, world-saving translations, and officials who mistake lost property for an invasion*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
DR. NARI, an orangutan linguist
CAPTAIN VEX, a macaque
PIP, a capuchin radio operator
LUMA, a lemur museum attendant
THE VISITOR, a small moon traveler

SCENE 1. INT. SPACE STATION - NIGHT

A repeating signal arrives from a tiny moon. The message is three beeps, a pause, and two beeps. Captain Vex orders an emergency meeting. Dr. Nari asks the station computer to slow the signal. Pip plays it backward, and it sounds like a kettle.

VEX: The visitor is demanding a response.

NARI: We do not know that it is a visitor.

A small craft docks at the station. A hatch opens. A traveler steps out holding one glove.

SCENE 2. INT. ARRIVAL BAY - MOMENTS LATER

The crew offers a welcome sign. The traveler points to the missing glove and repeats the beeps. Nari examines the craft. There is no damage. Luma recognizes the glove from the station's lost-and-found shelf; it was carried into the bay by a cleaning cart.

LUMA: The glove belongs to the station.

PIP: The signal may be asking for it back.

Vex asks Nari to translate. She tests sounds, gestures, and picture cards. The traveler points to a diagram of a hand, then to the glove. The meaning is clear, but Vex insists the exchange needs a formal first-contact greeting.

SCENE 2A. INT. ARRIVAL BAY - LATER

Vex brings out the station's first-contact protocol. It has a page for greeting, a page for shared symbols, and a page for emergency evacuation. Pip puts the glove on the welcome table. The visitor looks at it, then at its empty hand. Nari asks Luma to move the glove back to the station shelf so the traveler can indicate its own meaning. It points at the glove, then at the charge station.

PIP: Maybe it wants the glove charged.

LUMA: The adapter goes in the ship.

Vex begins a welcome speech. The visitor's antenna bends as the station airlock opens. Nari closes the inner door to stop the draft. The visitor points at the hatch and makes the same pattern of beeps. Pip checks the door control; the signal repeats whenever the pressure equalizes. They realize the beeps are a docking chime, not a message.

SCENE 3. INT. TRANSLATION LAB - LATER

Nari plays the beeps through different speakers. The pattern changes when the craft's antenna turns. Pip finds that the beeps are an automatic docking alert, not speech. The traveler has been pressing a button to ask the station to open the bay door. Vex asks if the door can be called a diplomatic threshold.

NARI: It is a door.

VEX: A door between worlds.

The visitor points at a charge station. Its battery is nearly empty. Luma finds a matching adapter in the lost-and-found drawer. Pip checks the power label; it fits. The traveler plugs in the adapter and the craft's lights brighten.

SCENE 3A. INT. TRANSLATION LAB - NIGHT

Pip logs the three-beep pause two-beep pattern as the station's automatic docking chime. Nari records that the visitor's repeated button press was a request to open the bay door; it was not a spoken warning. Vex asks whether they may still call the meeting first contact.

NARI: Call it what you like after you return the borrowed items.

LUMA: The glove is tagged for the visitor. The adapter is already powering the craft.

The crew places the glove beside the hatch and clears the walkway. Pip tests the door from the station side. It opens on the chime and closes after the craft is safely docked.

SCENE 4. INT. ARRIVAL BAY - DAWN

The traveler takes the glove and unplugs the adapter from the charging port. It gives Nari a smooth stone as thanks. Vex asks whether the stone is a treaty token. Nari says it may be a gift. The visitor's ship prepares to depart. Pip asks if they should send a message. The visitor taps three beeps, pauses, and two more. The bay door opens immediately.

LUMA: That was a request.

VEX: It was also a greeting.

SCENE 5. INT. STATION CORRIDOR - MORNING

Nari logs the encounter as a docking alert resolved by returning borrowed equipment. Vex asks to rename the report "Contact at the Edge of the Known." Luma says the lost-and-found drawer has a label already. Pip updates the station's inventory and places the adapter back on its shelf.

The crew writes a simple welcome card with pictures and instructions. Vex adds one decorative star, then removes it when it covers the bay door diagram.

FINAL IMAGE: The visitor's ship blinks two lights as it leaves orbit, and the station answers with the same two flashes.
