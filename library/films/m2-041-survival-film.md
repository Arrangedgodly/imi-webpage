# The Mountain Has a Gift Shop

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: survival adventure cinema, ordeal montages, and wilderness-hero resourcefulness*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A cautious hiking group follows its survival instructor toward a summit, where the greatest danger is his belief that every safe choice ruins the story.

Cast: Toma, a cautious capuchin; Leni, a macaque trail guide; Professor Brisk, a peacock survival instructor; Sedge, a turtle park ranger; and a mountain goat with a bell.

SCENE 1. EXT. MOUNTAIN TRAIL, MORNING

A sign points to the summit, the ranger cabin, and a gift shop. Brisk tears the map from Toma's hands.

BRISK: A true survivor trusts instinct.

TOMA: My instinct says read the sign.

Leni points out that the bridge is closed. Brisk calls the closure a test. Sedge, the ranger, says it is a repair closure with orange cones. Brisk steps over a cone and finds the bridge missing.

BRISK: A test with excellent production value.

Toma checks the weather and sees a storm warning. Brisk says the clouds are part of the challenge. Leni redirects the group to the ranger cabin.

SCENE 2. EXT. RIDGE, AFTERNOON

Rain starts. Brisk demonstrates shelter building. He takes every tarp and ties them into one enormous cape. Wind fills it like a sail and lifts him off his feet. Toma builds a small shelter from a single tarp while Leni and Sedge hold the corners. Brisk lands in a puddle beside it.

BRISK: The shelter needs cinematic scale.

TOMA: It needs to keep rain off.

Brisk crawls under the tarp. The five hikers and the goat fit, though Brisk's tail sticks out. The goat rings its bell whenever water drips on the tarp. Brisk asks if it is signaling rescue. Sedge says it is objecting to the rain.

SCENE 3. INT. RANGER CABIN, EVENING

The group reaches the cabin. Brisk refuses the wood stove, calling it a shortcut. Toma places a pot on the stove and heats soup. Leni checks the trail notice: the bridge repair will finish in the morning. Sedge contacts the ranger station.

BRISK: We must keep a night watch for the unknown.

TOMA: The unknown is outside. The known is soup.

They eat. Brisk asks for seconds, then claims the extra bowl is part of his calorie-management plan. The goat noses open the door and eats a corner of the old map. Toma photographs the route before the rest disappears.

SCENE 4. INT. RANGER CABIN, LATER

Brisk sets up a survival montage in the cabin. He tries to start a fire with two sticks, but the sticks are damp. Toma points to a box of matches beside the stove. Brisk calls them a last resort. Sedge says they are a first resort when available. Brisk strikes one; it lights the stove, which heats the kettle. He presents the kettle to the group like a trophy.

BRISK: Fire is the foundation of civilization.

LENI: The stove is the foundation of this cabin.

He demonstrates a compass, then realizes it points toward the iron stove. Toma moves away from it. The needle settles north. Brisk says the mountain has revealed its direction. Toma says the compass has moved.

Brisk draws a new route on a paper napkin, with a dramatic dotted line over a cliff. Leni refuses to follow it. He says the shortcut will save an hour. Sedge points to the ranger map: the lower trail adds fifteen minutes and stays open.

BRISK: The audience expects danger.

LENI: We are not an audience.

The goat steps on Brisk's napkin, leaving a muddy hoofprint over the cliff route. Brisk calls it a warning from the mountain. Toma says it is a hoofprint. Brisk folds the napkin and puts it in his pocket as evidence.

SCENE 5. EXT. TRAILHEAD, MORNING

Before they leave, Sedge asks Brisk to sign the cabin guest book. Brisk writes that the group faced peril and mastered the wilderness. Toma adds that they read the posted closure, used the stove, and stayed on the marked trail. Brisk draws a mountain beside both accounts.

The bridge is still closed, so the group takes the lower trail. They arrive safely, damp but not injured. Brisk gives each hiker a bottle-cap medal. Sedge asks him to pick up the huge cape, which is tangled in a bush. The goat has already chewed the knot free.

At the gift shop, Brisk finds a display of identical bottle-cap medals. He asks the clerk whether they are earned or sold. The clerk says both: one comes with a postcard. Brisk buys a postcard and writes "I survived using the safe route."

The clerk offers Brisk a second medal for buying the postcard. He declines, then asks if it comes with another postcard.

FINAL IMAGE: The group poses beside the summit sign without climbing to the summit. Brisk holds up the postcard for the camera; the gift shop's price sticker still covers the word "survived."
