# The Bell at Briar Cove

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: maritime adventure films, storm warnings, heroic sea rescues, and a captain whose map is permanently damp*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
CAPTAIN RILL, a macaque ferry skipper
ORO, a capuchin deckhand
TESS, a ring-tailed lemur lighthouse keeper
MR. PELT, a gibbon harbor inspector

SCENE 1. EXT. BRIAR COVE PIER - MORNING

A foghorn moans over the cove. Captain Rill studies a map that has been soaked so many times its coastline resembles a potato. A small ferry waits beside the pier. Oro loads crates of oranges while Pelt inspects the ropes.

PELT: Your route ends at the bell buoy.

RILL: The route ends at the island. The buoy is on the way.

PELT: The map says the buoy is the destination.

ORO: The map also says the sea is dry.

Rill unfolds the map. Water drips onto Pelt's clipboard. The map tears down the middle, splitting the island from the harbor. Rill pins the halves under two crates and tries to match the torn edges. The torn line makes a new island.

SCENE 2. INT. LIGHTHOUSE - DAY

Tess sees the ferry depart and notices dark clouds forming beyond the headland. She rings the storm bell. A flock of gulls answers with their own noisy alarms. Pelt assumes the bell signals a formal inspection and rows out in a dinghy with his clipboard.

TESS: The storm is turning toward the channel.

PELT: I have an inspection scheduled for the channel.

TESS: Then inspect the storm from shore.

Pelt pushes away. His clipboard sails off the boat and lands in the water, where it floats like a small office door. Tess signals him to return. He tries to row and retrieve the clipboard at the same time, turning in circles. She lowers a long hook; he catches the hook instead of the board.

SCENE 3. EXT. OPEN WATER - AFTERNOON

Rill steers by the shape of the cliffs. The storm whips the ferry around. Oro secures the orange crates with a rope, but the knots slip because the fruit has rolled against them. One crate breaks open. Oranges race across the deck.

ORO: The cargo is moving to starboard.

RILL: It has no ticket.

A huge wave hides the lighthouse. Rill turns the ferry toward the bell buoy, which rings faintly through the fog. They hear the buoy before they see it. The ferry drifts close enough for Oro to hook the bell rope with a boat pole.

ORO: I can pull us around the buoy.

RILL: Not by the bell. That is the warning rope.

ORO: Is there a route rope?

Rill points to the mooring line. Oro takes it. The ferry swings clear of a reef as the buoy clangs loudly behind them. The sound scares the gulls off the mast, and the open view reveals the lighthouse beam through a break in the clouds.

SCENE 4. INT. LIGHTHOUSE - SAME TIME

Tess keeps the lamp turned toward the opening in the clouds. Pelt returns, soaked, his floating clipboard caught under one arm. He admits the dinghy cannot reach the ferry. Tess gives him a signal flag and shows him how to make the harbor pattern: three short flashes, then one long.

PELT: I thought the bell was the whole system.

TESS: The bell says danger. The light tells you where to go.

PELT: The form has a box for danger. It has no box for where to go.

TESS: Add one.

Pelt writes on the clipboard. The wet paper tears again, and the pencil drops into the sea. He writes with charcoal on the wooden board instead. Tess teaches him the signal twice; he waves it backward the first time, accidentally signaling the fog to return.

SCENE 5. EXT. HARBOR CHANNEL - EVENING

Rill hears the bell pattern and follows the light through the fog. Oro uses oranges as weights to keep the mooring rope from sliding. A final wave pushes the ferry toward the pier. Pelt and Tess throw a line; Rill catches it and ties the knot with a turn that holds.

PELT: Your route has changed.

RILL: The sea changed it.

PELT: The map will need a revision.

ORO: We can draw it on an orange crate.

The crew ties the ferry safely to the pier. The oranges have been gathered into a neat row against the railing. One rolls free and stops against Pelt's boot. He picks it up and places it on the map to mark the island, finally giving the route the correct shape.

SCENE 6. EXT. BRIAR COVE PIER - SUNSET

The storm clears. Pelt replaces his damaged inspection clipboard with a board from the harbor shed. Tess rings the bell once to mark the ferry's return. Rill gives her the last orange. Oro tests each rope twice before unloading.

RILL: Tomorrow we take the island route.

PELT: I will update the map.

TESS: Keep it out of the water.

FINAL IMAGE: The new map dries on the lighthouse wall while a single orange rolls across the pier and rings the bell buoy with its soft bump.
