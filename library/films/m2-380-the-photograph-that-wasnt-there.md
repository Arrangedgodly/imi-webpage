# The Photograph That Wasn't There

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: mystery thrillers, missing photographs, shadowy evidence boards, and investigators who pin up pictures before checking the camera*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
DETECTIVE ODO, a gibbon
ARCHIVIST NARI, an orangutan
PHOTOGRAPHER PIP, a capuchin
CURATOR LUMA, a lemur
VISITOR VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. INT. CITY ARCHIVE - NIGHT

A photograph is missing from an exhibit wall. Detective Odo enters, rain dripping from his hat. Archivist Nari says the frame is still there, but the picture is gone. Photographer Pip checks the camera footage. Curator Luma insists the picture was on the wall at closing.

ODO: Who saw it last?

LUMA: The visitors.

Vex holds an empty frame and says the disappearance is impossible. Nari asks him not to touch the glass.

SCENE 2. INT. ARCHIVE OFFICE - LATER

Odo builds a clue board with string, pins, and a flashlight. The board has six photos of the frame and no image of the photograph itself. Pip says the camera recorded the wall but not the image because the lens was facing the exit. Odo asks for a close-up. The camera shows a reflection of the archivist holding a stack of papers.

Nari checks the catalog. The photograph is titled "Morning Window." It was due to be replaced for a conservation review. A red tag on the frame indicates it was moved to the storage room. Luma says she removed the tag at closing, assuming it was old.

SCENE 2A. INT. ARCHIVE OFFICE - LATER

Nari compares the visitor log with the conservation schedule. The photograph was removed at four for an examination. Luma signed the form, then left the frame empty while waiting for a reproduction. Pip checks the printer queue and finds the image file was sent to a different machine. Vex asks whether a visitor could have removed the original between four and closing.

NARI: The frame stayed locked.

VEX: A lock is not a witness.

Odo checks the seal and finds it intact. The glass has no fingerprints. Luma locates the storage receipt and confirms the original was moved to the conservation room, not taken. Odo cancels the alert to the city guard and keeps the investigation inside the archive.

SCENE 3. INT. STORAGE ROOM - MIDNIGHT

The team searches the storage shelves. Vex holds the flashlight over his own shoulder, casting a shadow that looks like a second person. Odo turns the light around. Pip checks the intake logs and finds the photograph listed under "flat works." It is stored between two boards in a conservation sleeve.

Nari examines the sleeve. It is intact. The photo is there, but the colors appear faded. She explains it was removed from the wall to protect it from light. The empty frame was left as a placeholder, and the replacement image was not installed because the printer jammed.

SCENE 3A. INT. STORAGE ROOM - MIDNIGHT

Nari opens the flat storage box. She wears cotton gloves and lifts the photograph by its edges. The colors are intact, but the paper is slightly curled. She checks the humidity card, which has changed from blue to pink. The room is too damp. She moves the box to a dry cabinet and writes a conservation note.

Pip asks whether the photograph can return to the wall. Nari says it needs a day to acclimate. Luma asks how the exhibit will look without it. Odo suggests displaying the empty frame with a clear explanation and a reproduction once it prints. Vex starts to call this a haunting. Nari hands him the humidity card.

SCENE 4. INT. ARCHIVE OFFICE - DAWN

Pip repairs the printer. The replacement image prints slowly. Odo holds it up and says the case has a copy. Nari clarifies that it is a reproduction, not the original. Luma updates the exhibit label to say the original is under conservation. Vex asks if they can leave the empty frame as a mystery. Nari says the visitors deserve to know why it is empty.

They hang the reproduction with a clear label. Odo removes the clue board. The red string forms a shape like a crooked question mark. Pip rolls it up and puts it in the supply drawer.

SCENE 5. INT. CITY ARCHIVE - MORNING

Visitors arrive. Luma explains the conservation work and shows them the photograph's reproduction. Nari keeps the original in a flat storage box and checks the humidity again. Vex asks whether the mystery is solved. Odo says the picture was moved, the frame remained, and the record was unclear. He closes his notebook.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty frame hangs beside a small label reading ORIGINAL UNDER CARE, while the protected photograph rests flat in a drawer.
