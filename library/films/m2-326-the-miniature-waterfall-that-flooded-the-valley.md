# The Miniature Waterfall That Flooded the Valley

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: practical-effects departments building enormous landscapes on tiny stages, then staging catastrophe at miniature scale*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
EFFECTS SUPERVISOR ODO, a baboon
DIRECTOR TAVI, a gibbon
ACTOR NIMA, a lemur
MODEL MAKER PIP, a capuchin
PRODUCER VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. INT. MINIATURE STAGE - MORNING

A tabletop valley contains paper trees, a painted cliff and a tiny village. Effects supervisor Odo tests a small pump. Director Tavi wants a gentle stream for the heroes to cross. Producer Vex studies the model through a camera lens.

VEX: It needs to look like a great flood.

PIP: The pump is the size of a walnut.

VEX: Then the walnut must be very powerful.

Nima, in a tiny costume against the model, stands on a foam rock. The pump starts. Water trickles through the valley and stops at a paper dam. Odo asks for less pressure. Vex asks for more drama. Pip warns that the village roofs are made of tissue paper.

SCENE 2. INT. MINIATURE STAGE - LATER

Odo widens the hose. The stream becomes a wave that knocks over three trees. Vex cheers, but Tavi calls cut. The scene calls for the heroes to save the village, not flatten it. Pip rebuilds the trees and adds a wooden channel. Nima steps onto the rock, looking toward the miniature houses.

NIMA: The water is moving too fast.

TAVI: We can show the danger without soaking the set.

VEX: A dry flood?

Odo lays a clear acrylic sheet in front of the lens to create a false reflection. The water appears wider and deeper. Vex asks for a second camera angle. The second lens reveals the whole valley sitting on a workbench beside a lunch thermos. He wants the thermos removed from the shot. It belongs to the effects crew and is holding down the map.

SCENE 3. INT. MINIATURE STAGE - AFTERNOON

Pip builds a breakaway bridge from thin balsa. Nima guides a small cart of seed packets across it. Odo releases the water; the bridge bends, but holds. Vex asks for a collapse. Pip explains that a collapsing bridge would strand the cart. Tavi blocks a rescue beat: Nima can pull the cart to safety using a thread hidden behind the rocks.

VEX: Can the thread be invisible?

PIP: It is already behind the rock.

VEX: Then it is emotionally invisible.

Odo starts the pump. The thread catches on a tree root. Nima pulls; the tree, bridge and cart slide together across the wet surface. The model village begins to drift toward the table edge. Tavi calls cut. Pip catches the board with both hands; Odo closes the valve. Vex asks whether the drifting village looked cinematic.

PIP: It looked like a model falling off a table.

SCENE 4. INT. MINIATURE STAGE - EVENING

The crew resets the valley. Odo installs a smaller nozzle. Pip anchors the board with clamps. Tavi reframes through the acrylic, hiding the table edge. Nima pushes the seed cart across the bridge as a narrow stream flows beneath. The thread works, guiding the cart through the channel. A single paper tree leans; Nima catches it and props it upright against a rock.

VEX: Could the tree fall in the final take?

NIMA: It is holding the hillside.

Vex watches the water reach the paper village and stop at the planned channel. It looks large through the lens, while the roofs remain dry. He approves the shot, then asks Odo to drain the basin. The plug is under the model. Odo lifts one corner; the valley tilts, and the water surges toward Pip's shoes. Tavi catches the board before it tips.


Tavi asks for a close shot of the villagers. The camera reveals a tiny figure lying on its side. Pip restores it with tweezers, but Vex asks whether the village needs a heroic statue. Nima holds up the figure; it is the village baker, carrying a tray. Odo places the baker beside the bridge. During the next water pass, the tray floats away while the baker stays upright. The crew follows the tray with the lens, and the miniature stream suddenly feels like a river.

SCENE 5. INT. SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The finished flood sequence plays. The stream fills the frame, the heroes guide the cart across, and the miniature village survives. Vex whispers, "Enormous." Pip points to the paper houses, still standing on the bench. Odo rolls the model into storage. The basin sloshes once; Nima holds the paper tree upright until it settles.

VEX: Could we make a sequel with a tidal wave?

TAVI: We need a larger tray.

PIP: And a smaller walnut.

They pack the pump and the acrylic sheet. Vex takes the map off the thermos and folds it carefully, finally leaving the set dry. The model's tiny river reflects the screen's last light.

FINAL IMAGE: On the dark stage, the miniature valley sits safely on its shelf, while a single drop of water lands in Vex's shoe.
