# The Last Frame at the Cinema

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: cinema dramas where the last frame is treated as a priceless relic*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
PROJECTIONIST MARA, a macaque
FILMMAKER VEX, an orangutan
USHER PIP, a capuchin
ARCHIVIST ODO, a gibbon
PATRON NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. NEIGHBORHOOD CINEMA - NIGHT

The screen is blank. Vex stands at the front row with a velvet cloth in his arms.

VEX: The last frame has vanished.

MARA: The screening ended. The projector is off.

PIP: The audience left. No film is on the platter.

ODO: The print is in its can in the booth, complete through the final frame.

NARI: I saw the ending. The hero walked into a sunrise.

VEX: But the last image is nowhere on the screen.

MARA: The screen is blank because the show is over.

Vex spreads the cloth over the front row as though protecting an artifact. Pip moves it from the aisle so guests can exit.

PIP: The relic is blocking the stairs.

VEX: It is a memorial to the image.

SCENE 2. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - LATER

Mara opens the film can on the workbench. Odo checks the label and reel number. Vex peers through a magnifier but does not touch the film.

ODO: The reel ends with the sunrise frame listed on the inventory.

VEX: Then why can no one see it?

MARA: Because the projector is off and the screen is blank.

NARI: Is there a still photograph in the lobby display?

PIP: There is a poster, but it shows the title card, not the ending.

Vex asks Mara to run the final frame again. Mara explains that the film is already stored and the booth must be closed for the night. Odo places the can in its case and locks the cabinet.

VEX: The final moment deserves an encore.

MARA: We can schedule a still-frame demonstration tomorrow, using a duplicate image. We will not handle the original print again tonight.

ODO: The inventory is signed. The cabinet is locked.

SCENE 3. INT. CINEMA LOBBY - MORNING

Mara brings a printed still from the distributor's archive. The original film remains locked in the booth. Vex has set an empty frame on an easel and calls it the last frame's shrine.

MARA: This is a reproduction, not a piece of the film.

VEX: It carries the memory of the sunrise.

PIP: It is a paper printout. We can display it beside the poster.

NARI: The frame is too large for the table. It blocks the ticket window.

Vex wants to stand the frame across the lobby, so every patron must walk beneath it. Odo points to the wall hook designed for displays. They lift the easel together and hang the reproduction at eye level.

ODO: Secure on the hook. No one needs to walk under it.

VEX: The last image now watches the audience.

MARA: It is a still for the morning exhibit. The screening happens tonight.

Pip checks the wall label and finds Vex has written ORIGINAL LAST FRAME. He replaces it with ARCHIVE REPRODUCTION.

VEX: The wording diminishes the aura.

PIP: It prevents a false claim.

SCENE 3A. INT. CINEMA LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Vex asks Odo to place a small velvet rope around the reproduction. Odo points out that the rope would cross the ticket line. Pip offers a tabletop stand instead, but Nari notes that the wall hook already holds the frame securely.

VEX: The last image requires ceremonial distance.

MARA: The print is behind glass. Guests can look without touching.

NARI: A rope would make people queue through the exit lane.

PIP: We can add a caption at eye level. That's enough to explain it.

Vex studies the caption and suggests a grander title: THE SUNRISE THAT ENDED AN AGE.

ODO: The image shows the hero walking into morning. Keep the label factual.

Mara writes "Archive reproduction of the final image" on the label. Vex sighs, but does not change it. He stands beside the frame, leaving the ticket window clear.

Pip checks the wall hook once and steps back. Nari turns the lobby lights on so the reproduction is visible without projecting the original film again. The booth stays locked, and the morning exhibit remains separate from tonight's screening.

SCENE 4. INT. CINEMA LOBBY - DAY

Nari stops to look at the reproduction, then heads for the ticket window. The image shows a bright horizon with the hero's silhouette near the edge. Vex stands nearby, waiting for a reaction.

NARI: It is a lovely sunrise.

VEX: And the final frame?

NARI: The last frame of the story is in the locked film can, where it belongs.

MARA: Correct.

Odo clips the inventory slip to the exhibit label. The original remains stored; the copy remains on the wall.

TAG: Vex adds a small sign beneath the reproduction: PLEASE EXIT AFTER THE ENDING, NOT THROUGH THE PICTURE.
