# The Midnight Express Is Early

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: runaway-train disaster cinema, ticking-clock edits, and heroic control-room speeches*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A tourist train rolls downhill with its brakes in service mode, forcing a station crew to manage an emergency that moves at walking speed while the mayor tries to deliver a disaster speech before it stops.

Cast: Lila, a lemur station manager; Buck, a gorilla mechanic; Tern, a pigeon dispatcher; Mayor Plink, a squirrel passenger; and passengers with snacks, umbrellas, and more patience than the mayor.

SCENE 1. INT. STATION CONTROL ROOM, NIGHT

A red warning light blinks. Tern points to the track display. The Midnight Express is moving, one square at a time.

TERN: Train 9 is rolling west.

LILA: Speed?

BUCK: Four miles an hour.

MAYOR PLINK: Sound the alarm!

LILA: The alarm is for danger.

PLINK: This is dramatic.

Buck checks the maintenance notes. The train's brake lever is still in service mode. Lila calls the conductor; the radio crackles with a report that the brake can be reset from the exterior panel. Buck grabs his wrench. Plink puts on a reflective vest over his pajamas.

PLINK: I will coordinate the evacuation.

LILA: There are twelve passengers, and they are seated.

PLINK: Then I will coordinate their continued seating.

SCENE 2. INT. PASSENGER CAR, CONTINUOUS

Lila boards the slowly moving train. The passengers look up from a card game. A tea cart rolls down the aisle. Lila catches it before the cups slide.

PASSENGER ONE: Is this the emergency?

LILA: That's the tea cart.

PASSENGER TWO: It was going faster than the train.

Buck jogs outside beside the carriage, carrying the wrench. He has the wrench in the wrong hand and the panel is on the other side. He waves to Lila through the window; the passengers wave back, thinking he is part of the tour.

SCENE 3. INT. CONTROL ROOM, LATER

Tern calls the next station. A sleepy dispatcher answers.

TERN: Clear the platform. Train 9 is approaching.

DISPATCHER (RADIO): It's closed. We put out a welcome mat.

Tern asks for a red emergency flag. The dispatcher says the flag is in the office, under a stack of timetables. Plink prepares a speech titled NARROW ESCAPE. Lila reads it over his shoulder.

LILA: The train is going four miles an hour.

PLINK: The speech is going five.

The train passes a sign advertising the Midnight Express at four miles an hour. Plink asks Tern to turn the sign around. Tern refuses; it is bolted down.

SCENE 4. EXT. SMALL STATION PLATFORM, NIGHT

Buck runs to catch up with the train, but the station porter passes him while pushing a suitcase.

PORTER: Need a lift?

BUCK: I need the panel.

PORTER: Then you need to walk faster.

Buck takes the porter's luggage trolley, rides beside the carriage, and reaches the panel. Plink narrates over the radio as if announcing a chase.

PLINK (RADIO): He approaches the mechanism with courage.

LILA (RADIO): He approaches it with a trolley.

The trolley bumps over a track seam. Buck grabs the railing and keeps his wrench from falling. Tern tells the passengers to stay seated, though the only passenger standing is Plink, who is waving from the window.

Buck reaches the exterior brake panel. The label is hidden by a maintenance clipboard. He removes it. A bright sticker reads: RESET BRAKE BEFORE DEPARTURE. The conductor calls from the cab.

CONDUCTOR (RADIO): We did. The switch was left in service mode.

BUCK: Which switch?

CONDUCTOR (RADIO): The one labeled SERVICE MODE.

Buck looks at the switch, then at the mayor's speech notes stuck over the label. Plink has been carrying them all evening. He apologizes and moves aside. Buck turns the lever. The train stops with a gentle click. The welcome mat lands under its first door.

SCENE 5. INT. TRAIN, MOMENTS LATER

The conductor checks the brake indicator and confirms the train is secure. Buck parks the trolley. The porter asks for his trolley back; Buck returns it with a thank-you and the wrench still in hand.

Lila checks every passenger. The card game continues. The tea cart is secured to a seat. Plink steps aboard to announce the successful evacuation, but everyone is still sitting exactly where they were.

PLINK: We narrowly avoided disaster.

PASSENGER ONE: We avoided it by staying seated.

LILA: The service crew will reset the train before it continues.

Plink asks if the passengers can applaud for the report. They clap politely. The train bell rings. He flinches and grips Buck's arm; Buck points to the bell cord, which a child has pulled to signal the stop.

Lila gives the passengers the corrected timetable. It lists the actual arrival time, not the scheduled one.

FINAL IMAGE: At dawn, the train returns to the station. Plink poses beside it for the press. It rolls one inch as the brakes settle. He leaps into Buck's arms; the photographer catches the only genuinely fast movement of the night.
