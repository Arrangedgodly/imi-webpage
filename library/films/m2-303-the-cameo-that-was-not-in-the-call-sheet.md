# The Cameo That Was Not in the Call Sheet

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: surprise celebrity cameos, secrecy rules, and productions that protect a reveal by hiding it from the crew doing the scene*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR ODO, a baboon
FIRST ASSISTANT LUMA, a lemur
ACTOR NARI, an orangutan
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
CAMEO GUEST VEX, a macaque
COSTUME RUNNER TAVI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. STUDIO HALLWAY - DAWN

First assistant Luma tapes a call sheet to the door. A black rectangle covers one name. Director Odo checks the schedule.

ODO: Why is the hallway scene at six?

LUMA: The surprise guest is available at six.

ODO: Which guest?

LUMA: I am not authorized to know.

The covered name has been photocopied onto six extra pages. Actor Nari arrives in a bathrobe for her scene. Pip carries a fake key. Costume runner Tavi checks a rack of gray coats.

NARI: Who am I meeting?

LUMA: You meet a stranger in the hallway.

NARI: Is the stranger in the scene?

LUMA: The stranger is the surprise.

Odo asks Luma to read the call sheet aloud. She skips the covered line. Tavi wheels in a gray coat. Someone inside it coughs.

TAVI: The coat has a throat.

LUMA: Wardrobe has supplied a hanger.

SCENE 2. INT. STUDIO HALLWAY SET - MORNING

Nari rehearses her scene. She walks to the elevator, drops the fake key, and waits for a stranger to return it. Pip enters as the concierge but walks past the key. Odo stops rehearsal.

ODO: The guest returns the key.

PIP: I was told not to look at the guest.

LUMA: That is correct. The audience must recognize them before you do.

Pip tries again with his eyes closed. He bumps the elevator door. Nari picks up the key herself. Odo asks if the surprise guest will be playing concierge. Luma says the guest's role is confidential. A second black rectangle covers the assistant director's notes.

The gray coat steps into the set. Vex's face is hidden behind the collar.

VEX: Is this the call for the elevator?

ODO: Who are you?

VEX: I am the guest.

ODO: The guest is a secret.

VEX: Then I am someone else.

SCENE 3. INT. MAKEUP ROOM - LATER

Tavi tries to fit Vex with a false mustache, a hat and tinted glasses. Luma insists his face must be concealed until the camera turns. Nari sees the disguise and recognizes him immediately.

NARI: Vex?

LUMA: That name is confidential.

VEX: Is my name the surprise, or my entrance?

Odo arrives with a sealed script page. It contains only the line, "The doors open, and the familiar face appears." No character name is printed. Tavi asks whether Vex should enter from the elevator or the hallway. Luma says the scene has not been written that far because the reveal was being protected.

PIP: We have actors, a door and no entrance.

Odo gives Vex a simple cue: return the key, say hello, then hold for Nari's reaction. Vex practices with the false mustache. It sticks to the cup of water. Tavi removes it with a pair of tweezers. Vex asks to keep the mustache for the next shot. Nari asks which character it belongs to. Luma covers the character column with another black rectangle.

SCENE 4. INT. STUDIO HALLWAY SET - AFTERNOON

They shoot the scene. Nari drops the key. Vex steps out of the elevator and hands it back. Pip turns away so he will not reveal the guest. The elevator doors close on Pip's sleeve. He stays in the shot, tethered to the elevator.

PIP: I have become part of the entrance.

Odo calls cut. They open the doors and free Pip. Luma checks the camera view. The guest's face is still hidden by the mustache, though the audience can see the familiar eyes.

LUMA: We need one clean reveal.

NARI: You can introduce him by name.

LUMA: Then it is no longer a surprise.

Vex removes the false mustache and steps back into the elevator. Nari drops the key again. This time he catches it before it hits the floor.

VEX: You dropped this.

NARI: I did that last take.

ODO: Keep rolling.

SCENE 5. INT. SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The crew watches the final cut. The studio audience recognizes Vex before Nari does, because his name appears on his coat tag. Luma tries to blur it. Odo pauses the image.

ODO: The guest was hidden from the cast, the crew and the audience. The coat tag introduces him.

Tavi sews the tag inside the collar. They replay the scene. The doors open, Vex returns the key, and Nari smiles because she knows him. No one gasps on cue.

FINAL IMAGE: Luma peels the black rectangle off the call sheet; beneath it, Vex's name is written in ordinary ink.
