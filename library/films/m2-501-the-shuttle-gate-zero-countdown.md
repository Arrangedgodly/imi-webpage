# The Shuttle at Gate Zero

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space-launch thrillers that stretch a countdown over every small departure checklist*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A crew prepares for a dramatic lunar launch, but the shuttle is still parked outside the hangar while the countdown clock runs for a different vehicle.

Cast: commander Varo, a macaque; pilot Nemi, a gibbon; technician Oru, an orangutan; launch announcer Tavi, a lemur; intern Sela, a capuchin; cart driver Hali, a squirrel monkey; offstage ground-crew chorus, macaques.

SCENE 1 - INT. LAUNCH CONTROL ROOM - NIGHT

A red countdown display reads T-MINUS 10. The crew watches the screen. It shows an empty launch pad. Oru checks the flight board and sees the shuttle ID is LUNA-3. The display is counting down to a weather balloon test.

VARO: We have ten minutes to launch.

ORU: We have ten minutes to the balloon test.

NEMI: Where is our shuttle?

Sela points through the window. The shuttle is parked at the hangar's far gate. Varo asks why it is not on the pad. Oru says the pad reservation begins at midnight.

SCENE 2 - EXT. HANGAR - MOMENTS LATER

Nemi climbs into the shuttle. Varo walks around it, checking the hull. Tavi narrates over the public address system: "The crew prepares to depart for the moon." A ground cart blocks the path to the pad. Sela asks cart driver Hali to move it. Hali unplugs the cart and rolls it into the marked bay, then radios that the route is clear.

VARO: How long until the cart is ready?

ORU: The cart is not part of the countdown.

Tavi restarts the countdown from ten, hoping to keep the audience engaged. It reaches three while the shuttle is still beside the hangar. Hali has already parked the cart. The countdown reaches zero before the shuttle starts. Tavi stops the balloon clock.

SCENE 3 - INT. SHUTTLE - CONTINUOUS

The crew straps in. Nemi discovers the nav display is still in demonstration mode. Oru switches it to flight. The public address narration continues, describing the launch as imminent. Sela asks Tavi to stop the countdown until the shuttle is at the pad. Tavi says the audience expects a launch at zero.

NEMI: The shuttle is still on the taxiway.

TAVI: The story is on schedule.

The crew reaches the pad. Hali rolls the support cart beyond the painted boundary and radios the ground crew. Oru checks the tie-downs and disconnects the service cable. Varo asks if they are cleared for lift-off. Nemi points to the safety gate, still closed. Sela radios the ground crew. The gate opens only after the loading crew clears the area.

SCENE 3A - INT. LAUNCH CONTROL ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Sela turns off the weather balloon countdown and starts the shuttle checklist. Tavi announces a new countdown over the loudspeaker, but begins at ten again. Oru holds up a stop card. The room goes quiet. Nemi reads the fuel level and checks the hatch. Varo watches the display, waiting for a number that does not appear.

VARO: Is the clock broken?

ORU: We are not counting down yet.

Sela places the balloon timer in a drawer and labels it BALLOON ONLY. Tavi switches the microphone to standby. He looks disappointed when the room does not react to the silence. Nemi asks for the pad crew. The crew responds over radio and confirms that the shuttle can be moved.

SCENE 4 - EXT. LAUNCH PAD - LATER

The shuttle is lined up. Tavi reaches for the countdown switch. Oru stops him until the final checks are complete. The crew checks the hatch, communications and fuel display. Hali radios that all ground carts and crew are behind the line. Each check passes. Nemi verifies the hatch seal from the cockpit. Sela calls the ground team by station; each crew member answers once. Hali confirms the cart is behind the painted boundary and the taxiway is empty. Oru looks across the pad, then gives the clearance signal.

VARO: We have counted down three times.

TAVI: The audience likes the anticipation.

Nemi asks Tavi to use one countdown only after clearance. The public address goes quiet. Oru gives final clearance only after Sela repeats Hali's all-clear. Tavi starts one countdown. It reaches zero once. Varo starts the engine; the shuttle lifts cleanly. Hali watches from the marked bay and confirms the pad remains clear.



SCENE 5 - INT. LAUNCH CONTROL ROOM - DAWN

The lunar crew leaves orbit. Tavi records the departure time on the mission card. It differs from the first countdown by forty-two minutes. He writes that the delay was a dramatic pause. Sela corrects the report to say the shuttle waited for the pad reservation.

FINAL IMAGE: The countdown display stands at zero beside a parked weather balloon, while the shuttle appears as a small bright dot in the window.
