# Teaser After the Final Bell

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: franchise films that interrupt their endings for a post-credit teaser promising a sequel before the current problem is settled*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: After returning a stolen bell, a weary team is kept in the theater by a teaser for a threat that turns out to be the theater's overdue maintenance.

Cast: Rumi, a gorilla courier; Sava, a macaque mechanic; Tiko, a lemur archivist; and Bex, a capuchin projectionist.

SCENE 1. INT. OLD RAIL STATION, DUSK

Rumi sets a brass station bell on the counter. The bell was taken from the museum by a prankster troop and has been carried across the valley. Sava has repaired its cracked handle. Tiko checks the inventory card.

TIKO: One bell, returned intact.

RUMI: Handle repaired.

SAVA: By me, with the correct screw.

Bex photographs the group beneath the station clock. A producer asks them to hold the pose after the bell has rung. Rumi keeps one hand on the bell, Sava holds a wrench, and Tiko keeps his pen over the inventory card.

PRODUCER: Can everyone look toward the horizon?

TIKO: The horizon is outside the station.

PRODUCER: Think about what comes next.

RUMI: The train comes next.

The producer asks for a more mysterious expression. The team looks toward the platform as the train approaches. The clock hand drops loose and lands on the counter. The minute hand drops loose and lands on the counter. Everyone looks up at it.

BEX: That is not the bell.

The clock stops. Rumi asks if they should repair it. Sava says the clock belongs to the station, not the museum. Tiko adds it to the maintenance list. The bell rings once as the train arrives, and the team boards.

SCENE 2. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, NIGHT

The crew attends a screening of the filmed return. The movie shows Rumi delivering the bell, Sava tightening the handle, and Tiko signing the inventory. It ends with the team boarding the train. The audience applauds. Bex keeps the projector running.

RUMI: Is there another reel?

BEX: There is a special scene after the credits.

SAVA: We have to stay?

BEX: The studio says it is essential.

The credits crawl. Sava begins to leave. Bex tells her the next film depends on it. Rumi sits back down. Tiko reads the names aloud until he reaches "Additional Brass Consultant." He asks if the bell was consulted.

SCENE 3. INT. CINEMA, LATER

A dark screen appears. A single gear turns in a shaft. A low rumble grows. A shadow crosses a map. The teaser cuts to the station clock, its loose hand pointing down.

TIKO: That is the clock from the station.

SAVA: The gear is from the clock.

RUMI: Is it dangerous?

Bex plays a title card: "THE HAND THAT WILL STOP TIME." A second card promises a new adventure next summer. The audience murmurs. Sava stands and asks Bex to rewind.

BEX: It is only a teaser.

SAVA: That gear is scraping the housing. I heard it when we set down the bell.

The screen flashes a production logo, then goes black. Bex says the teaser was designed to leave a question unanswered. Sava says the loose screw answers it. Rumi asks whether the clock can be fixed before the next train. Bex says the studio wants to save the repair for the sequel.

RUMI: The station opens at six.

TIKO: The maintenance list is due today.

Bex looks at the audience. Several people are already in the aisle. She stops the projector and turns on the house lights. Rumi asks what the threat is supposed to do. Bex admits the film crew has not decided. They only filmed the close-up of the gear and added thunder.

SCENE 4. INT. STATION CLOCK ROOM, MORNING

The team returns to the station before opening. Sava removes the back panel. The minute hand slipped because a small screw worked loose. She tightens it, oils the gear, and resets the clock. The station manager asks if the crew can film one more dramatic close-up. Sava asks what is supposed to happen.

MANAGER: The gear turns. The clock works. Something looms.

SAVA: The only thing looming is the opening shift.

Rumi rings the bell to test it. The train conductor waves from the platform, then departs on schedule. Tiko changes the maintenance card from "possible threat" to "small screw." Tiko writes "repaired" on the maintenance card. Rumi waits for the bell to signal the next train.

A station manager asks whether they can leave the clock uncovered for a dramatic shot. Sava says no. Bex tries to frame the turning gears from the side, but the protective panel reflects her camera. She lowers the lens and watches the clock run.

SCENE 5. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, EVENING

Bex submits the sequel teaser to the station manager. Tiko asks to replace the ominous title with a repair notice. Rumi says the bell's return already had a beginning and an ending. Sava agrees, then hands Bex the loose screw from the clock.

FINAL IMAGE: The cinema screen shows a plain card: "The clock is fixed. Please exit through the lobby." The audience leaves before the credits finish.
