# The Intermission Eats the Musical

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: backstage musicals where theatrical momentum collapses under an extended intermission and a missing cast member*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A traveling musical's second act cannot begin because the cast and audience have drifted into separate snack lines during the break.

Cast: stage manager Rena, a lemur; star Javi, a macaque; chorus lead Miko, a gibbon; usher Pala, an orangutan; pianist Suri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - INT. SMALL THEATER - EVENING

The first act ends with Javi frozen on a painted ship deck. The curtain closes. Rena checks her watch and calls a fifteen-minute intermission. Pala opens the lobby doors. The audience exits toward a snack table. The cast slips out through the stage door toward a food cart.

RENA: Places in fifteen.

SURI: The pianist stays here.

The pianist plays a short intermission tune. The lobby snack line bends past the ticket desk. Miko joins it wearing half a sailor costume and asks whether the show has another act.

SCENE 2 - INT. THEATER BACKSTAGE - FIFTEEN MINUTES LATER

Rena calls places. The stage is empty except for the ship deck. Javi returns carrying a paper cup. Miko is still in the lobby. Pala finds the cast at the food cart outside and leads them back. The audience has not returned; the snack line has become a second queue for coat check.

JAVI: The sea is ready.

RENA: The audience is not.

Suri plays the act-two opening. No one hears it through the lobby doors. Rena asks Pala to ring the handbell. The bell is inside the lobby, behind the snack table. Pala reaches for it, but the line blocks the table.

SCENE 3 - INT. LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Rena steps onto a chair and announces the second act. The audience turns, but the cast can't hear her. Javi sees the cue through the glass and begins singing silently from the stage. Miko enters the lobby, thinks the song is part of the show, and starts a dance around the queue. The line joins in, shuffling forward to the rhythm.

PALA: They are moving.

RENA: They are not going to their seats.

Suri changes the tune to a march. The snack line advances toward the auditorium doors. Pala opens them, and the audience files inside carrying popcorn and drinks. Rena counts the cast, then discovers the chorus has entered with the audience and is seated in row three.

Rena sees that the cast and audience are still separated by the snack counter. She asks Pala to move the queue toward the auditorium. The ushers turn the lobby ropes into a winding path, but the line now leads back to the coat-check desk. Miko starts the musical's opening step. The line follows, passing cups hand to hand so people can clap without spilling. Suri hears the rhythm from the stage and repeats it on the piano. Javi answers with the opening melody. The audience recognizes the tune and begins singing from the lobby.

SCENE 4 - INT. THEATER - MOMENTS LATER

Miko spots the stage and climbs over the seats, still dancing. Javi holds the ship's rope and starts the opening song. The cast follows the beat. Pala dims the lobby lights. The snack table is visible through the glass and appears to be part of the ship's galley.

JAVI: Welcome aboard.

Miko reaches the stage. The audience cheers. Rena gives up on the original blocking and signals Suri to keep playing. The cast performs from the aisles and stage, turning the late entrance into a choreographed arrival. A popcorn bucket rolls across the stage and stops at Javi's foot. He offers it to the chorus as a prop treasure chest.

Rena conducts the lobby chorus with a folded program. When the audience reaches the refrain, she lowers the program and points toward the auditorium. The line walks in on the final chord. Miko arrives last carrying three paper cups and takes the stage steps two at a time. Javi waits at the ship's wheel rather than beginning the next verse without him. Suri holds the chord until the entire cast is in place.

SCENE 5 - INT. THEATER LOBBY - LATER

The musical ends on time because Rena cuts the second encore. The cast bows in the lobby, where the snack line has finally disappeared. Pala returns the handbell to its hook. Miko removes the sailor sleeve and finds a ticket stub tucked inside.

MIKO: I missed the show and joined it by mistake.

RENA: You made your entrance on time.

Suri plays the intermission tune again, but the audience is already heading home.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty snack table sits under a sign reading INTERMISSION, while the popcorn bucket rests on the ship deck beside the final curtain.
