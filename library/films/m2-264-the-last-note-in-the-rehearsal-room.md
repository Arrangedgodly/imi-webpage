# The Last Note in the Rehearsal Room

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: backstage musicals, where a choir searches for a perfect final note while a cleaner tries to close the room*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A choir rehearses the finale past closing time, and the building's ventilation keeps supplying the note the singers cannot agree on.

Cast: Conductor Sava, gibbon; singer Piri, capuchin; pianist Odo, baboon; caretaker Nima, macaque.

SCENE 1. INT. REHEARSAL ROOM, EVENING

A choir stands in a half-circle. Sava raises both hands.

SAVA: One more time from the final chord.

PIRI: We have done the final chord eight times.

ODO: The piano is ready.

NIMA: The room closes in ten minutes.

SAVA: The last note must resolve.

PIRI: It resolves. You keep asking it to return.

The choir sings. The final note wavers. A ventilation fan hums beneath it.

[SFX: CHOIR, FAN HUM.]

SAVA: The room has added a note.

NIMA: The room is ventilated.

ODO: We can match the hum.

SAVA: No. We must overcome it.

SCENE 2. INT. STORAGE CORRIDOR, MOMENTS LATER

They move to the corridor to avoid the fan. The sound becomes even louder. Nima says the vent runs along the hall.

PIRI: The building is singing with us.

SAVA: The building is off key.

NIMA: The vent has not been tuned.

Odo plays the chord again. Piri sings the final note lower, matching the hum. Sava cuts her off.

SAVA: That is not the written note.

PIRI: It fits the room.

SAVA: The composition cannot change to accommodate a fan.

NIMA: The fan cannot change to accommodate a composition.

Sava asks the choir to try the note in another octave. The singers climb the scale, but one member's voice cracks.

PIRI: The high note has failed.

ODO: The low note is still available.

Sava separates the choir into two groups and asks one to hold the root note while the other tries the ending. Their voices overlap with the fan; the chord sounds fuller.

SAVA: The room is harmonizing.

NIMA: The room has one fan.

Piri asks to move the rehearsal to the lobby. The lobby is colder and the fan is louder there. Odo shuts the door between rooms, but the sound still carries through the vent.

ODO: We cannot outrun the ventilation.

SAVA: We can sing through it.

NIMA: You have four minutes before closing.

PIRI: Then we need one take, not another experiment. The singers climb the scale, but one member's voice cracks.

PIRI: The high note has failed.

ODO: The low note is still available.

SCENE 3. INT. REHEARSAL ROOM, LATER

Nima turns off the fan switch. The room gets quiet for a moment, then the ventilation starts again automatically.

[SFX: FAN CLICKS BACK ON.]

SAVA: It has returned.

NIMA: It is on a timer.

SAVA: We are also on a timer.

The choir rehearses the finale without the piano. They hold the chord while the fan hum continues. The note blends naturally, but Sava asks for one more attempt without the building.

PIRI: The building is part of the room.

SAVA: We can move the finale outside.

Nima points to the clock. The choir has one minute left in the room. Sava starts to propose another test, but the fan dips, then rises in a steady pitch. She signals the basses to enter on that pitch and the altos to follow.

PIRI: It has given us the note.

SAVA: Then we will give it the ending.


NIMA: It is raining.

ODO: The rain has a note too.

Sava looks at the clock. The room closes in two minutes. She raises her hands and asks the choir to sing the ending once, exactly as written.

SCENE 4. INT. REHEARSAL ROOM, CONTINUOUS

The singers begin. Odo accompanies them softly. The final chord lands, but the ventilation hum continues beneath it. Sava holds her hands up, waiting for the sound to fade.

PIRI: The chord is finished.

SAVA: I am waiting for silence.

NIMA: The fan does not stop.

SAVA: Then we will end while it is still sounding.

Sava lowers her hands. The choir stops together. The fan remains.

[SFX: FAN HUM ALONE.]

ODO: That is the final note.

SAVA: It is not in the score.

PIRI: It is in the room.

SCENE 5. INT. HALLWAY, NIGHT

The choir packs up. Nima turns off the lights. The fan shuts off with the room.

NIMA: Now it is quiet.

SAVA: We have found the true ending.

PIRI: The fan stopped.

SAVA: The building has finished its phrase.

Odo hums the ventilation note while carrying his music. The choir joins him in the hallway. Nima opens the door to let them out.

NIMA: Take the note with you.

SAVA: We will perform it tomorrow.

NIMA: Not here. This room closes at eight.

[SFX: DOOR LATCH, A FINAL HUM OUTSIDE.]
