# The Visitor Under the Mountain

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: first-contact science fiction, threat assessments, ominous signal decoding, and a greeting mistaken for a declaration of war*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
DR. MAVA, a gibbon geologist
RUK, a capuchin signal officer
TESSI, a macaque field scout
THE VISITOR, an unknown creature from a deep cave

SCENE 1. EXT. MOUNTAIN CAMP - NIGHT

A strange pulsing light shines from a cave. Dr. Mava studies the glow through a telescope. Ruk, a capuchin signal officer, records the flashes. Tessi, a macaque scout, packs fruit into a climbing pouch.

RUK: Three short, one long. It could be a warning.

MAVA: It could be a greeting.

RUK: The signal has no punctuation.

TESSI: Maybe it wants to borrow a banana.

A loud boom comes from the cave. The crew ducks. A rock rolls out and stops beside their campfire. Tessi notices the rock is warm and has a spiral scratched into it.

SCENE 2. INT. MOUNTAIN CAVE - LATER

The team enters with lamps. The walls glow with mineral veins. A small shape moves behind a curtain of mist. Ruk records another sequence of flashes and decodes it as "LEAVE NOW."

MAVA: That is a very firm greeting.

TESSI: I think it says "leave no fruit."

Ruk asks the signal device to translate again. It displays "LEAF NOW." The cave's glow pulses faster. Mava notices the light is reflected from a pool, not sent from a device.

SCENE 3. INT. UNDERGROUND POOL - CONTINUOUS

Tessi places a banana on a flat rock. The light stops flashing. A small creature emerges, takes the banana, and places a smooth mineral stone in its place. The team watches. Ruk raises his hands, palms out.

RUK: We come in peace.

The creature copies him, holding two hands up. One has the stone; the other has a banana peel. Ruk drops the communication device. It flashes "HOSTILE MOVEMENT."

SCENE 4. INT. CAVE CHAMBER - NIGHT

Mava identifies the creature's gestures as an exchange ritual: fruit for stone. The crew offers an apple. The visitor places three crystals on the ground, then taps them in a repeating rhythm. Ruk records the pattern. The device translates it as "THREE ROCKS."

TESSI: It is showing us the cave.

The visitor leads them to a narrow passage. The crystals mark a safe route around a deep shaft. Ruk's screen keeps labeling every gesture as a threat. Tessi turns it off.

SCENE 5. INT. DEEP CHAMBER - DAWN

The crew discovers a large mineral formation that glows in the same pattern as the visitor's signal. It is not a message sent to the sky; it is the creature's way of navigating its own home. Mava sketches the formation. Ruk offers the visitor a small hand mirror. The visitor touches the mirror, sees its reflection, and raises both hands again.

RUK: This time I think it means hello.

TESSI: Or it wants the mirror.

Ruk lets it keep the mirror. The creature places a crystal in his palm.

SCENE 6. EXT. MOUNTAIN CAMP - MORNING

The team returns to the surface. Ruk updates the contact report from "potential invasion" to "neighbor with cave." Mava leaves fruit near the entrance. Tessi takes one crystal, then places it back, deciding not to remove anything from the cave.

SCENE 7. INT. FIELD COMMUNICATIONS TENT - MORNING

Ruk sends the contact report to the research team. The distant analysts reply with a warning montage: maps, flashing lines, and a red arrow pointing at the cave. They ask whether the visitor has shown hostile intent.

RUK: It traded a crystal for fruit.

ANALYST, over radio: Could be a coded payment.

MAVA: It is a gift exchange.

The analysts demand another test. Tessi places a banana outside the cave and waits. The visitor leaves a smooth stone and a tiny drawing of the mountain. The radio team asks for a high-resolution scan of the drawing. Ruk turns off the screen and studies it with his eyes.

SCENE 8. INT. DEEP CHAMBER - AFTERNOON

The visitor returns the hand mirror and taps the mountain drawing. It adds a line leading to a second entrance near the camp. Mava checks the map and realizes the route avoids the unstable shaft.

MAVA: It is showing us a safe passage.

RUK: I will report the route, not the creature's face.

Tessi marks the passage with a small stone and leaves the visitor's crystals where they were. The team exits by the new route. At the camp, the analysts ask whether the contact was successful. Ruk says yes, then asks them to stop sending warning montages.

FINAL IMAGE: The visitor holds the hand mirror inside the cave; reflected mountain light answers the pulsing glow.
