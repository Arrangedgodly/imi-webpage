# The Poster with Too Many Stars

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: ensemble marketing posters that give every performer equal billing until the composition hides the story*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DESIGNER LUMA, a lemur
DIRECTOR VEX, a macaque
ACTOR ODO, a baboon
ACTOR NARI, an orangutan
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
PRINTER TAVI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. DESIGN STUDIO - MORNING

Designer Luma places a blank poster on an easel. Director Vex arrives with five headshots and a title card: THE LAST BRIDGE.

VEX: It is a story about one bridge and the team trying to cross it.

ODO: How many of us are on the poster?

VEX: Everyone.

NARI: Who is the lead?

VEX: The ensemble is the lead.

Pip stands beside the poster. Luma pins his picture at the center. Vex moves it to the edge so the title has room. Nari adds her portrait beside Pip. Odo insists his character is the bridge engineer. Vex gives him the same size as the other actors.

Tavi rolls in a printer. Luma asks for a draft. The printer produces a poster with headshots covering the bridge.

PIP: It is a story about faces.

VEX: The bridge is underneath them.

SCENE 2. INT. DESIGN STUDIO - LATER

Luma rearranges the portraits in a semicircle. Vex asks for all names to be equal size. The names spill beyond the frame. Tavi reduces the type. It becomes too small to read. Odo suggests putting the title at the bottom. Vex says the title must sit above the stars.

NARI: Then where does the bridge go?

VEX: Between the stars.

Nari moves her portrait upward. Pip's portrait is now partially behind the title. Odo steps to the poster and draws a line between all five headshots.

ODO: The bridge connects them.

Vex likes the idea but asks for a glow. Tavi adds a digital light effect. The bridge disappears in white. Luma removes the glow. Vex asks for a sunburst, then a reflection, then a flare. Every effect hides another face.

LUMA: The paper is full.

VEX: It needs more impact.

TAVI: The printer only has two sides.

SCENE 3. INT. PRINT ROOM - NOON

Vex requests a larger poster. Tavi prints it on two sheets and joins them with tape. The seam runs directly across Odo's eyes. Odo moves to the side; the poster moves with him because it is taped to his costume.

ODO: I have become the layout.

Pip asks whether the cast should pose with the poster. Luma places them in a line. Vex says the shot needs depth. Nari stands in front, Pip behind, Odo to the left. Their bodies block their names. Tavi adds name placards. The placards cover the bridge illustration.

VEX: We need everyone visible and the title readable.

NARI: You have asked the same sheet of paper to be six places.

Luma turns the poster sideways. All faces fit, but the title reads upward. Tavi rotates it. The tape seam opens. A small piece of the printed bridge falls onto the floor. Pip picks it up and holds it between the actor portraits. Luma asks the group to test the print from across the lobby. Nari can read the title, Vex can read the names, and Odo sees only the bridge. Pip walks closer; the perspective makes his face cover the words. They rotate the poster a few degrees and pin it at eye level. Tavi takes a test photograph. The flash turns all five portraits white, so she takes another without flash. This time the bridge line remains legible and Odo is no longer physically part of the seam.

SCENE 4. INT. DESIGN STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Luma asks each actor what the audience should remember. Odo says the bridge must be repaired before the crossing. Pip says the team has to decide who goes first. Nari says she plays the engineer who keeps the rope tight. Vex listens and removes three headshots from the draft.

ODO: Are we still in the movie?

VEX: Yes. But the poster is now about the bridge.

Luma places the bridge in the center. The actors' names go along the bottom in one clean line. Tavi prints a small proof. It has space for the title and the five faces. Vex asks if the movie looks too quiet. Pip points out the rope line crossing the poster, visibly taut.

PIP: It looks like something is at stake.

SCENE 5. INT. LOBBY - EVENING

The final poster hangs in the cinema lobby. Vex stands in front of it. The story title is readable; the cast names are legible. Odo's face is still larger than the others because he is holding the paper seam together.

FINAL IMAGE: Luma steps away from the poster, and Odo remains taped to the bridge at eye level.
