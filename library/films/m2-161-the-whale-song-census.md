# The Whale Song Census

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: wildlife documentaries and enumeration methods when animals refuse to hold still for the camera*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A field team tries to count a troop of monkeys by listening to their calls. Every recording produces a different total as the animals move, groom and answer from hidden branches.

Cast: Field lead Tavi, a macaque; acoustic scientist Seli, a gibbon; camera operator Odo, a baboon; and guide Kiri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. CANOPY EDGE, DAWN

Seli sets a microphone beneath a branch.

SELI: The troop calls at dawn. We can count the voices.

TAVI: We can count the monkeys we see.

ODO: The camera only sees this branch.

A call comes from behind them. Odo turns the camera. A monkey is already gone.

SCENE 2. INT. FIELD TENT, MORNING

The team listens to a recording. Three voices overlap.

SELI: I hear five calls.

TAVI: There were four monkeys.

KIRI: One called twice.

Seli labels the recording "Five."

TAVI: It is not five.

SELI: The sound file is five.

Kiri opens the tent flap. A monkey walks past carrying the microphone case.

SCENE 3. EXT. WATERING SITE, DAY

The crew sets up a camera to record the troop at the bowl. Two monkeys arrive, then three more. One grooms another while a sixth appears behind the trunk.

ODO: I have six in frame.

TAVI: One left.

Seli checks the audio. A seventh voice answers from above.

SELI: We have seven.

KIRI: That is the echo.

The call repeats from the other side of the tree. Seli moves the microphone. The echo stops.

SCENE 4. INT. FIELD TENT, AFTERNOON

The team compares three counts: visual, acoustic, and inferred.

TAVI: We saw six at the water bowl.

SELI: We recorded seven voices.

KIRI: I saw eight monkeys in the tree.

Odo prints a chart. It has three columns.

ODO: Which number goes in the report?

Seli studies the sound wave. A short call appears between two long calls.

SELI: The short call is the camera operator.

ODO: I did not call.

Kiri plays the recording. A monkey has copied Odo's camera beep.

SCENE 4A. INT. FIELD TENT, LATE AFTERNOON

Tavi draws the grove on a fresh page. Seli places symbols for recorded calls, and Kiri marks visible monkeys.

TAVI: We need a count the next team can reproduce.

SELI: We can record a ten-minute interval from one fixed point.

KIRI: And mark which voices overlap.

Odo tests the microphone from the tent entrance. The recorder captures the click of the camera and the rustle of the tent flap.

ODO: We need to separate equipment sounds.

Seli adds a channel for background noise.

SELI: If we count every sound as a call, the camera becomes an animal.

TAVI: It did copy the monkey.

KIRI: The monkey copied the camera first.

They listen again. This time they identify the repeating call and the single echo.

SELI: Six distinct voices, one echo, and one camera beep.

TAVI: That is the count for this recording.

KIRI: There may be others outside the interval.

Tavi writes the time and method beneath the number.

SCENE 4B. EXT. CANOPY EDGE, LATE AFTERNOON

The team compares the new count with their earlier notes.

TAVI: Six distinct voices in the recorded interval.

SELI: One echo from the trunk.

KIRI: The camera beep copied by a juvenile.

Odo writes each item in a different column.

ODO: We should not call the echo an extra monkey.

SELI: Or the beep.

Tavi looks through the canopy and counts visible animals. Two move behind leaves before she finishes.

TAVI: Eight visible across the full hour.

KIRI: That is a different measure.

Seli labels the page "Visual count, one hour." Tavi labels the sound file "Six calls, ten minutes."

TAVI: We can share both without pretending they match.

SCENE 5. EXT. CANOPY EDGE, SUNSET

The team returns the equipment. They find the troop in the same tree, but the branches are crowded.

TAVI: We can report a minimum count.

SELI: At least eight.

KIRI: There are nine.

A small monkey appears on Odo's shoulder.

ODO: We have nine.

The monkey leaps away. Kiri turns toward a rustle behind them.

KIRI: Ten.

TAVI: We cannot keep changing the count.

Seli closes the notebook.

SELI: We will report the time, location, and method.

TAVI: And the number?

SELI: The number we can support.

Odo lowers the camera and listens. The troop calls from several branches at once.

ODO: It sounds like all of them.

The team leaves the microphone in place and walks out of the grove. One last call comes from the microphone itself as the wind taps it against the stand.
