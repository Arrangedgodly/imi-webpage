# The Potion That Reversed Itself

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy potion quests, mysterious ingredients, transformation montages, and wizards who forget to label the bottles*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
APPRENTICE LUMA, a lemur
WIZARD ODO, a gibbon
ALCHEMIST NARI, an orangutan
COURIER VEX, a macaque
PIP, a capuchin

SCENE 1. INT. WIZARD'S WORKSHOP - MORNING

A potion bubbles in a copper pot. Apprentice Luma reads a recipe upside down. She adds a blue leaf and a pinch of salt. Wizard Odo says the potion should make a lost object visible. Courier Vex arrives carrying a sealed bottle labeled REVERSE.

ODO: That is not our ingredient.

LUMA: The label was facing down.

A cloud of green smoke fills the room. Pip's missing sock appears on the table, then disappears again.

SCENE 2. INT. WORKSHOP - LATER

Alchemist Nari checks the recipe. The potion is supposed to reveal lost objects for one minute. The reverse bottle is meant to undo the spell. Vex says the bottles were stored together because they were delivered in the same crate. The labels are handwritten and nearly identical.

NARI: We need to test one drop.

ODO: On what?

Pip places a lost button on a plate. Luma adds one drop. The button appears, disappears, then appears in Vex's pocket. Vex checks the pocket and finds three more buttons, none of which are his.

SCENE 2A. INT. WORKSHOP - MIDDAY

Nari places the bottles in separate trays and labels the shelf. Vex offers to move them to the storage room. Odo says the labels must face outward and each bottle must be returned to its own tray. Pip tries to help, but the charm on his sleeve snags the recipe card. The page flips over and reveals a small illustration showing the two bottles side by side with different colored caps.

LUMA: We have had the answer in the book.

ODO: The picture was upside down.

Nari compares the cap colors and confirms the ingredients are safe when used separately. She explains that the first test caused the reveal spell to cycle, while the reversed bottle shortened each cycle. The key kept appearing in its original place; Odo had picked it up while following the flashes.

SCENE 3. EXT. WORKSHOP GARDEN - AFTERNOON

The group searches for a lost key using the potion. The key appears in a potted plant, vanishes, and reappears on the gate. Odo follows it across the garden. Nari notices that the potion is not moving the objects; it is revealing their locations as the spell cycles on and off.

Vex pours one measured drop from the blue-capped bottle into a saucer. The key remains visible instead of blinking out. Nari writes the result beside the recipe so no one has to infer the bottle's purpose from its label again.

SCENE 3A. EXT. WORKSHOP GARDEN - AFTERNOON

Vex tries the spell on a lost garden trowel. It appears beside the compost bin, vanishes, and appears under a watering can. Pip has been moving the tools as he searches. Nari asks him to stop touching them while the test runs. They place the trowel in one spot and wait. The spell shows it clearly without moving it.

VEX: The trowel has chosen its place.

LUMA: You put it beside the compost bin.

Vex prepares a grand reveal for the camera, but Pip moves the watering can before the next pulse. Nari stops the test and has everyone hold still. The trowel appears in the same place twice. Odo writes the result on the lab card, and Luma pins it beside the recipe. The apprentice will use that card rather than guessing from memory.

SCENE 4. INT. WORKSHOP - EVENING

Nari uses a measuring spoon to prepare a small correction batch. Odo reads each ingredient aloud. Luma writes the steps on a new card. Pip holds the bowl steady. The spell shows the lost key in the potted plant and keeps it visible. Odo retrieves it. Vex turns the bottles so their labels face outward.

PIP: Will the potion find the missing sock too?

LUMA: We already found it.

The sock is still on the table, folded beside the recipe. Odo asks whether they need a second spell. Nari says they need a laundry basket. She checks the recipe card once more and files it beside the labeled bottles.

SCENE 5. INT. WORKSHOP - NIGHT

The team labels each bottle with a colored band and stores them in separate trays. Luma updates the recipe book. Vex delivers the crate to the correct shelf. Odo uses the potion to locate a missing spoon; it appears inside the sugar jar, where Pip had placed it during the test.

FINAL IMAGE: Two labeled bottles stand beside the recipe card, while a small blue leaf dries in the windowsill without glowing.
