# The Longest Day at Camp Canopy

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: summer-camp adventure films, secret initiation tests, heroic team challenges, and counselors who turn a short hike into an epic*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
COUNSELOR NARI, an orangutan
CAMPER PIP, a capuchin
CAMPER LUMA, a lemur
COUNSELOR VEX, a macaque
COOK ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. CAMP CLEARING - MORNING

The campers receive a map for a short hike to the lake. Counselor Vex calls it the Trial of the Ancient Pines. Counselor Nari explains it is a marked loop that takes forty minutes. Pip packs a lantern, a blanket, three snacks, and a compass. Luma brings a water bottle.

PIP: What if the trail disappears?

NARI: It is marked.

Vex hands them a whistle. It squeaks when he blows it and startles the camp dog.

SCENE 2. EXT. FOREST TRAIL - LATER

The group follows blue markers. Vex pauses at each one to recount a camp legend. Nari checks the time and asks him to keep walking. Pip reads the trail map upside down and leads them toward the picnic shelter. Luma turns it around. The lake is still ahead.

A branch falls across the trail. Nari checks it and finds it small enough to step over. Vex calls it the fallen guardian. Pip moves it to the side. The campers pass without needing the emergency rope.

SCENE 2A. EXT. FOREST TRAIL - LATE MORNING

A squirrel chatters from a branch. Vex tells the campers it is the trail guardian. Nari checks the guidebook and identifies it as a common tree squirrel. Pip offers a snack. Luma asks him to keep food packed until lunch so it does not attract wildlife. Pip zips the bag shut, then checks that the map is still in his pocket.

The path divides around a fallen tree. The blue marker is on the left branch, but Pip remembers seeing an arrow to the right. Nari asks him to look again. The arrow belongs to an old ski route, not the hiking loop. Vex steps onto the marked path and finds a small sign confirming the lake direction. He admits the marker was easier to follow than his legend.

SCENE 3. EXT. LAKE - MIDDAY

The group reaches the lake. Cook Odo has set out lunch. Pip opens his snacks, then realizes he carried enough for the whole camp. Vex asks for a team challenge before eating. Nari proposes a simple shoreline cleanup. The campers collect wrappers and return lost water bottles to the lost-and-found crate.

Luma finds a blue marker that has fallen off a tree. Nari reattaches it at eye level. Vex says the marker was a clue. Odo says it marks the route. Pip checks the map and confirms the group has reached the lake and can return along the same trail.

SCENE 3A. EXT. LAKE SHORE - MIDDAY

Odo lays out lunch away from the waterline. The campers sort bottles for reuse and wrappers for recycling. Pip finds a cap beside the bench and checks whether it belongs to a bottle in the lost-and-found crate. It fits. Luma fills her bottle at the pump, then asks the group to leave the shore clear for other visitors.

Vex wants a photograph of everyone standing on the dock. Nari checks the posted limit and sees that only four people may stand there. The group takes turns instead. The photo includes the lake, the map, and the camp lunch. Vex says the composition is less heroic but more accurate.

SCENE 4. EXT. CAMP CLEARING - AFTERNOON

The campers walk back. Vex starts a dramatic return montage. Nari tells him the group is walking, not escaping. They stop at the fallen branch and find it has rolled farther from the path. Pip places it beside the trail. The camp dog carries the squeaky whistle back to Vex.

The hike ends in thirty-eight minutes. Vex says the campers have completed an epic journey. He asks Nari where they will award the medal. Nari points to the shared pantry basket, where Pip has placed every spare snack. Vex pins a paper badge to the dog's collar; the dog trots off with it. Luma says they have learned the trail markers and cleaned the shoreline. Odo serves lunch again because Pip packed too many snacks and nobody ate the camp sandwiches.

SCENE 5. INT. CAMP DINING HALL - EVENING

The campers pin the corrected trail map to the wall. Vex writes a new description: "Lake loop, forty minutes, blue markers." Nari asks him to remove "Trial of the Ancient Pines." He does. Pip puts his extra snacks in the shared pantry. He returns the borrowed compass to its hook.

FINAL IMAGE: The camp map hangs level above the pantry, with a blue marker beside the easy loop and a snack basket below it.
