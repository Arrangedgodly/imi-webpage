# The Submarine Pool Test

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: submarine thrillers that treat a routine systems drill as a catastrophic descent into the deep*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A crew rehearses a submarine emergency in a shallow training pool, where the periscope keeps revealing the pool's lap lanes instead of enemy ships.

Cast: captain Tavi, a macaque; engineer Rema, a lemur; lookout Joro, a gibbon; instructor Pala, an orangutan; signal officer Venn, a capuchin; swimmer Niko, a young macaque; pool attendant Sera, a squirrel monkey.

SCENE 1 - INT. TRAINING POOL - DAY

A compact submarine mockup sits in a shallow pool. A raised wading ledge surrounds its hatch, above the four-foot pool floor. Tavi addresses the crew. Pala clips a checklist to the hatch.

TAVI: We are thirty fathoms below the enemy fleet.

PALA: You are in lane three.

The crew operates the periscope. Joro looks through it and sees a swimmer's kickboard. He reports an unidentified vessel. Venn sounds the alarm. Rema checks the depth gauge; it reads four feet. The hatch opens onto the raised ledge, where the water reaches only their ankles.

SCENE 2 - INT. SUBMARINE MOCKUP - MOMENTS LATER

The crew practices a power failure. Pala switches off the cabin lights. Emergency lamps glow red. Tavi orders silent running. The mockup's small pump continues humming. Rema turns it off. Water begins seeping into the hull from an open drain plug.

REMA: This is not part of the drill.

Pala opens the hatch. The crew climbs onto the raised wading ledge. The water is below their ankles. Tavi orders them to abandon ship in formation.

SCENE 3 - EXT. TRAINING POOL - CONTINUOUS

The crew gathers on the raised wading ledge beside the lane rope. Joro points at the kickboard still floating near the periscope. Venn says the enemy has escaped. Pala retrieves the board, which has Niko's name written on it. Niko waits at the pool edge beside Sera, the pool attendant.

TAVI: We have found the enemy's identification.

PALA: We have found a kickboard.

Sera confirms the pool lane is clear. Rema drains the mockup completely, then tightens the loose drain plug once. She checks the seal before the pump is restarted. Tavi asks for a second run with a deeper-sea backdrop. Pala turns on a projector showing blue water, but the image lands on the opposite wall.

SCENE 3A - EXT. TRAINING POOL - MOMENTS LATER

Pala calls one repeat drill without the alarm. Tavi wants to keep the red lights on for atmosphere. The crew checks each station while the emptied mockup remains above the waterline. Joro looks through the periscope again. This time he sees the pool attendant carrying a lane sign.

JORO: The hostile vessel is carrying a number three.

PALA: It is the pool's lane marker.

Venn uses the headset to call a surface check. The attendant answers that lane three is clear. Tavi reports the message to command in a deep voice. Pala asks him to use his normal voice so the crew can understand.

SCENE 4 - INT. SUBMARINE MOCKUP - LATER

The exercise resumes. Venn receives a signal through a headset. He announces a coded message from command. It is the pool attendant calling for lane four to clear. Tavi tells the crew to interpret the message as a warning. Rema recognizes the attendant's voice and asks the crew to surface.

The periscope rises. Joro sees the lifeguard chair. He reports a hostile tower. Pala asks him to identify the flag. It is a red towel drying on that same chair. The crew turns the periscope to follow the towel as the breeze lifts one corner; it stays on the chair.

SCENE 4A - INT. INSTRUCTOR BOOTH - LATER

Pala reviews the first attempt followed by the single repeat drill. The monitor shows the first leak, the emptied mockup, Rema tightening the plug once, and the successful repeat run. Tavi pauses on the frame where the periscope sees the lifeguard chair. Joro points out that the red towel was real, even if it was not an enemy flag.

JORO: We identified the object before sounding the alarm.

PALA: That is the useful part of the exercise.

Niko and Sera watch from the pool edge as Tavi adds one note to the report: distinguish the attendant's lane call from command signals. Venn checks that the headset is switched off. The lane marker remains with the attendant, and the mockup stays tied at the edge of the pool. Joro marks the lane number on the report, and Venn writes ATTENDANT beside it. Pala files the corrected report beside the pump switch.

SCENE 5 - INT. TRAINING POOL - AFTERNOON

The instructor signs the drill sheet. Tavi writes "survived deep-water emergency" in the comments. Pala changes it to "completed pool systems test." Pala returns Niko's kickboard to him, and Sera coils the lane rope. The submarine mockup remains safely tied to the pool edge.

FINAL IMAGE: Tavi salutes the periscope as it points toward the lifeguard chair, while the depth gauge rests at four feet.
