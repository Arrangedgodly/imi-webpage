# The Toys That Walked at Dawn

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: animated toy adventures, secret lives after bedtime, toy hierarchy, and a rescue plot launched when a child packs the wrong case*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
CAPTAIN BUTTON, a wind-up capuchin toy
PIPER, a plush ring-tailed lemur
RIVET, a tin macaque robot
MILO, a young gibbon child

SCENE 1. INT. MILO'S BEDROOM - NIGHT

The bedroom goes dark. The toys move. Captain Button winds his key and gathers everyone for the nightly check. Piper is missing from the shelf. Her scarf lies beside the open suitcase.

BUTTON: She's been packed for the school trip.

RIVET: The suitcase closes at dawn.

Piper is wedged between a boot and a lunchbox. She can't move without activating the boot's squeaker. The toys begin a rescue mission.

SCENE 1A. INT. BEDROOM - NIGHT

Button organizes a search party. A toy train rolls beneath the bed. Rivet proposes taking it to the suitcase, but the track ends at a pile of building blocks.

RIVET: The route is blocked.

BUTTON: We can move the blocks one at a time.

Piper calls from inside the suitcase, her voice muffled by a boot. The toys follow the sound, but each step on the floorboard creaks. They freeze whenever Milo shifts in bed. A toy rabbit covers the loudest board with its soft ear.

The team reaches the suitcase. Button assigns Rivet to hold the zipper, Piper to push the boot, and himself to turn the pull. The rescue is planned like a military operation, though the largest obstacle is a lunchbox.

SCENE 2. INT. BEDROOM - CONTINUOUS

Button leads the team across the floor. They travel by rolling under the bed, climbing a toy bridge, and crossing a patch of moonlight. Rivet's metal feet clank; Piper tells him to walk on the carpet.

RIVET: I am built for walking.

PIPER: Then walk quietly.

They reach the suitcase. The zipper is half closed. Button uses his winding key to turn the zipper pull, but his spring runs down. He must pause every few inches to wind himself again.

SCENE 3. INT. SUITCASE - LATER

The toys reach Piper. The boot's squeaker triggers whenever it shifts. Rivet tries to lift the boot; it squeaks. Button asks Piper to press the boot from underneath while he steadies the heel. The squeak becomes a rhythm. Milo stirs in bed.

The toys freeze. Milo opens one eye. Captain Button is caught mid-wind. Milo turns over and goes back to sleep.

Piper crawls free. The toys start to leave, but Rivet's legs are tangled in a charging cable. He pulls the suitcase toward the edge of the bed.

SCENE 3A. INT. SUITCASE - PRE-DAWN

The toys open the lunchbox. Inside are apple slices, a folded napkin, and a small flashlight. Piper uses the flashlight to find the zipper track. Rivet braces the boot; Button turns the zipper pull a fraction at a time.

PIPER: The suitcase is nearly closed.

BUTTON: The mission is nearly complete.

The zipper snags on the scarf. Piper pulls it free. The boot shifts and squeaks. Milo sits up in bed. The toys freeze. He looks at the suitcase, then at his shelf, where Piper's space is empty.

MILO: I forgot Piper.

He crosses the room and opens the case. He lifts Piper out and puts her on the shelf, then takes Button and Rivet along for the trip. The toys do not move until the door closes.

SCENE 4. INT. BEDROOM - PRE-DAWN

The suitcase begins to tip. Button winds himself to full speed and catches the handle. Piper braces it with her scarf. Rivet releases the cable and pushes from the other side. The suitcase rolls upright without a crash.

Milo wakes and finds his toys around the case. He assumes they are packed for the trip. He places Piper on top, closes the zipper, then changes his mind and reopens it.

MILO: Piper can stay home. She doesn't like the bus.

The toys remain still. Milo puts Piper back on the shelf, takes Rivet and Button in his backpack, and zips it partway.

SCENE 5. INT. SCHOOL BUS - MORNING

The bus starts. Inside Milo's backpack, the toys sway with every turn. Button's key slowly winds from the vibration. Piper watches through a small opening in the zipper.

BUTTON: The rescue is complete.

PIPER: We're on the bus.

RIVET: That is another mission.

MILO: I packed the right two this time.

The toys remain still as the bus crosses the bridge. Piper watches from the bedroom shelf in the window reflection, now safe at home. Button's key turns slightly from the road vibration, but he does not move.

FINAL IMAGE: Milo sits by the window. The toys ride safely in the open backpack, facing the road together as the sun rises over the school.
