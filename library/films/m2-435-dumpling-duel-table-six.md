# The Dumpling Duel at Table Six

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: cooking rivalry films that turn a friendly recipe contest into an escalating sabotage-heavy battle for a judge's approval*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: Two monkey chefs compete to make the best dumplings, but their attempts to outdo one another turn a quiet neighborhood cook-off into a flour-covered spectacle.

Cast: Chef Nilo, a macaque; Chef Pera, a capuchin; judge Oru, a gibbon; host Tava, a lemur; kitchen steward Beni, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - AFTERNOON

Six tables are set for a dumpling contest. Nilo arranges scallions in a perfect row. Pera measures dough. Host Tava stands between them.

TAVA: One hour to prepare twelve dumplings. The judge will taste each batch blind.

NILO: I have a family recipe.

PERA: Mine was taught to me by a neighbor.

ORU: I will judge the filling and the wrapper. Please keep the tables clean.

Nilo rolls his sleeves. Pera ties on an apron with a patch reading TABLE SIX. Nilo's table is number five.

NILO: My rival has a larger table.

PERA: It is one number farther away.

Before the contest begins, Oru lays out the tasting spoons and a bowl of plain sauce. Nilo has brought three jars of imported seasoning and a silver lid for each. Pera brings one small jar with a hand-written label.

ORU: The tasting is blind. Keep your names off the trays.

NILO: My plating style is recognizable.

ORU: I will cover the plates until the judging.

Pera writes a small number on each bamboo steamer. Nilo places his jars in a line, then turns every label toward the camera.

TAVA: This is a neighborhood contest, not a product launch.

NILO: The jars are part of my process.

ORU: The process is allowed. The tray labels are not.

SCENE 2 - INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - LATER

Nilo folds each wrapper into a crescent. Pera folds hers into small pleats. Nilo watches over the divider.

NILO: Your edges are uneven.

PERA: The dumplings are sealed.

NILO: Mine have identical corners.

Pera's steamer timer rings. Nilo tries to glance at the display. His elbow knocks a flour bowl onto the floor.

BENI: I will sweep that up. Step away from the spill.

NILO: It was a deliberate dusting.

PERA: You have dusted your shoes.

Nilo tries to wipe his soles, leaving flour prints toward table five. Beni places a wet-floor sign beside the spill. Nilo mistakes it for a contest marker and shifts the bowl around it.

SCENE 3 - INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

Pera pours broth into her steamer. Nilo uses a decorative bamboo fan to cool his tray. The airflow lifts three uncooked wrappers and sends them across the divider. One lands in Pera's bowl.

PERA: This is your wrapper.

NILO: It has crossed into your territory.

PERA: It has landed in my filling.

TAVA: The timer is still running. Can you keep cooking?

Nilo leans over the divider to retrieve the wrapper. His sleeve dips into the broth. Pera hands him a clean towel.

NILO: I will not accept help from my competitor.

PERA: Then accept a towel from a person with dry hands.

He takes it. Beni moves the fan away from the food and puts the stray wrappers in a clean tray.

SCENE 4 - INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - ONE HOUR LATER

The chefs present their dumplings. Nilo's tray is ornate, with a scallion spiral and a carved carrot garnish. Pera's tray is simple. Oru tastes both with plain soy sauce.

ORU: Nilo, your wrapper is excellent. The filling is cold in the center.

NILO: The fan must have cooled it.

ORU: Pera, your filling is warm and balanced. One dumpling is misshapen, but sealed.

PERA: That is the one Nilo's wrapper landed in.

NILO: It is a collaboration.

Oru checks the contest rules. The judge is required to taste twelve dumplings per chef. Pera's tray has eleven. Nilo's flying wrapper is among them.

ORU: I will judge the dishes as served. No points for garnish alone.

SCENE 5 - INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - MOMENTS LATER

Oru awards the ribbon to Pera. Nilo reaches for the decorative carrot spiral, which has rolled under the table. He crawls after it and finds the wet-floor sign, still in place.

NILO: The marker was not part of the contest.

BENI: It was part of keeping the floor safe.

Pera offers Nilo a warm dumpling from her extra tray. He tastes it and nods.

NILO: Your pleats held the steam. I was watching the table number instead of the timer.

PERA: The recipe is easy to share.

Tava asks them to lead the next community class together. Nilo starts to answer, but his flour-print trail ends beneath the sign.

FINAL IMAGE: Nilo and Pera stand behind the same clean table, folding dumplings side by side while Beni sweeps the last white footprint into a dustpan.
