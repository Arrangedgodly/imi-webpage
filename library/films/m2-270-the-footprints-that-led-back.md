# The Footprints That Led Back

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: noir crime films, where a detective follows suspicious footprints in a circle around the station*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A detective tracks a trail of muddy prints across town, then discovers the culprit never left the station.

Cast: Detective Seli, capuchin; partner Odo, baboon; witness Nara, macaque; station clerk Pava, gibbon.

SCENE 1. INT. STATION, NIGHT

A muddy print marks the floor near the door. Seli crouches over it.

SELI: One print in the entry. The case has begun.

ODO: It is raining outside.

SELI: Then the culprit came in from the rain.

PAVA: The floor was mopped ten minutes ago.

SELI: The culprit returned after the mop.

Nara enters carrying a basket of clean towels. She sees the print and stops.

NARA: That looks like my shoe.

SELI: You are a witness now.

NARA: I live upstairs.

ODO: You could be a suspect who lives upstairs.

Seli measures the print with a ruler. Odo finds a second print near the desk, facing the same direction.

SELI: The trail leads deeper into the station.

PAVA: It leads to the desk.

SCENE 2. INT. RECORDS HALL, MOMENTS LATER

A third print appears beside the records cabinet. Seli follows it. Another print marks the floor behind her.

ODO: The trail has doubled back.

SELI: The culprit is circling us.

NARA: The prints are all the same size.

PAVA: The mop bucket is beside the door.

Seli asks Nara to show her shoes. The soles are clean.

NARA: I changed shoes when I came upstairs.

SELI: You anticipated the investigation.

NARA: I came from the rain.

Odo checks the records cabinet. Mud has dripped from the edge of the mop bucket, which rolled slightly as someone opened the door.

PAVA: The bucket made the prints.

SELI: Buckets do not have feet.

PAVA: The mop head does.

The mop has fallen over, leaving a dark oval mark. Odo stands it upright. The trail appears to stop, then resumes near the desk.

SCENE 3. INT. STATION, CONTINUOUS

Seli lays string between the prints. The lines form a rough circle around the central desk.

SELI: The culprit is trapped in the pattern.

ODO: The culprit is a mop.

NARA: The prints start at the bucket and end at the bucket.

SELI: That is a closed route.

PAVA: It is a circle.

Seli photographs each mark before anyone touches it. She compares the spacing and notices each print is separated by the same length.

SELI: The culprit moved at a steady pace.

ODO: The bucket rolled at a steady speed.

NARA: The door nudged it each time.

Pava marks the door's arc with chalk. It ends exactly where the bucket stood.

SELI: We have a route and a motive.

PAVA: The motive was mopping.

Seli asks Odo to walk the path. His boots leave new marks, confusing the scene. Nara brings a clean sheet and covers the prints.

SELI: You have concealed evidence.

NARA: I have protected the floor.

Odo lifts one corner of the sheet. The original prints have blurred together.

ODO: The evidence is gone.

SELI: The case is harder now.

PAVA: The case is a wet floor.

SCENE 4. INT. STATION, LATER

Pava carries the bucket to the maintenance room. Seli circles its original place in chalk while Odo replaces the broken wheel lock. They return the bucket to its original position and test the door once. It swings inward but no longer nudges the bucket; the mop stays upright.

[SFX: DOOR CREAK, NO MOP THUMP.]

SELI: The trail has ended.

NARA: It began at the bucket.

ODO: The bucket has been detained.

PAVA: The floor is drying.

Seli photographs the locked wheel and draws a neat arrow from the door to the bucket. She labels the arrow cause, then labels the mop print evidence. Odo turns the report around; the arrow points directly at Seli's shoe.

ODO: The case has identified a second suspect.

SELI: My shoe is outside the circle.

PAVA: The report is upside down.

Seli rotates the paper. The arrow now points to the wheel lock. She files the report before the ink can run.


SCENE 5. INT. STATION, DAWN

Pava stores the mop upright. The wheel lock Odo repaired holds the bucket in place when the door opens. They leave the blurred muddy prints untouched for Seli's final photograph.

SELI: The trail ends where it started.

NARA: At the bucket.

SELI: A circular case.

ODO: A mopped floor.

Nara sets the towels by the stairs. Seli looks at one remaining muddy print beneath the desk. It is hers from the earlier test.

SELI: The culprit has left a fresh mark.

ODO: That is your shoe.

SELI: I was testing the scene.

PAVA: The floor was just mopped.

Seli examines her sole. It is muddy.

SELI: The case has turned inward.

NARA: It has turned into a cleaning job.

Seli takes the mop and begins cleaning the final print. The station door swings open; the bucket stays put.

[SFX: MOP SWISH, DOOR OPENS QUIETLY.]

ODO: The suspect is in custody.

SELI: The suspect is clean.

PAVA: The floor is finally dry.

[SFX: DAWN BIRDS, STATION CLOCK.]
