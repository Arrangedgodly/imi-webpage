# The Castle Has Electricity

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: historical dramas that favor grand costumes and speeches over basic period accuracy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A historical film's consultant tries to correct the set, but the director keeps restoring modern conveniences because they make the scene look grander.

Cast: Neri, a lemur history consultant; Badu, a macaque director; Soko, a gorilla actor playing the queen's steward; and Puck, a capuchin set decorator.

SCENE 1. INT. CASTLE HALL SET, MORNING

Soko stands in a velvet coat beneath a chandelier. Badu rehearses a royal announcement. Neri enters with a binder of notes and points to a plastic light switch beside the throne.

NERI: That is a modern switch.

BADU: It is outside the frame.

NERI: The camera is there.

Puck covers the switch with a painted wooden plate. It sticks out from the wall like a small shelf. Neri asks him to remove it. Badu says the hall needs more light. Puck turns on the chandelier. The bulbs glow white.

NERI: The candles were never electric.

BADU: The audience needs to see the faces.

Soko asks whether the queen should enter before or after the herald. Badu says after the trumpet. Puck cues a recording of a brass fanfare from a phone hidden in a velvet cushion. Neri hears a notification chime at the end.

SCENE 2. INT. THRONE ROOM SET, LATER

The scene begins. Soko announces that the queen has arrived. Badu asks him to bow lower. The coat catches on a modern zipper. Neri notices the zipper and points it out. Puck covers it with a sash.

SOKO: I cannot bow in this collar.

BADU: The collar shows power.

NERI: The collar is made of plastic.

Puck swaps it for a stiff fabric collar. Soko bows, and the collar bumps the throne. The throne rolls backward on hidden casters. Badu calls it a symbol of the court's instability. Neri blocks the wheels with a sandbag.

The queen's page enters holding a clipboard. Neri asks why there is a clipboard. Puck says it contains the revised lines. Neri hands him a wax tablet prop. The page tries to write on it with a ballpoint pen. The pen scratches a groove and snaps. Neri offers a reed stylus. The page presses too hard and leaves a hole through the wax.

NERI: The message is now visible from the other side.

BADU: That is a continuity problem.

NERI: It is a tablet problem.

Puck replaces the wax tablet with another prop. The page practices the line, but Badu asks him to look toward the chandelier for a more dramatic silhouette. The white bulbs turn his face blue.

SCENE 3. INT. CASTLE KITCHEN SET, AFTERNOON

Neri reviews the scene with Badu. The script calls for a courier to deliver a message by horse. The set has a printed banner announcing "ROYAL EXPRESS DELIVERY." Neri says the courier should hand over a sealed letter. Puck removes the banner and reveals an electrical outlet behind it.

BADU: Can we cover the outlet?

NERI: We have been covering it all day.

Soko enters with the letter. Badu asks him to make the delivery feel urgent. Soko runs across the room. His costume catches on the rolling throne, which begins moving again. The crew stops it. Neri asks for the wheels to be removed. Badu wants to keep them for a smooth camera move. The crew pushes the throne across the kitchen set. It rolls into a cabinet labeled "PRESENT-DAY ELECTRICAL." A modern kettle starts to whistle.

BADU: Is the kettle in the script?

PUCK: It is for tea between takes.

Neri removes the kettle and the throne wheels. The room becomes quiet. Soko walks with the letter and hands it to the queen's stand-in. The delivery takes three seconds. Puck unscrews them and finds a battery compartment underneath.

SOKO: What powers the throne?

PUCK: The dramatic entrance.

Neri asks for a plain wooden chair. Badu says the queen deserves a throne. They keep the throne but place it on blocks. It stays still. Soko delivers the letter at a walk, and the seal stays intact.

SCENE 4. INT. CASTLE HALL SET, EVENING

Badu watches the cut. The chandelier is dimmed, the modern switch is out of frame, the throne stays still, and the herald uses a real trumpet. Neri approves the sequence. Puck asks if he should leave the phone recording under the fanfare as a hidden joke. Badu considers it, then hears the notification chime in the cut. He asks Neri whether the sound belongs to the period.

NERI: It belongs to the phone.

BADU: Then remove the phone.

Puck searches the velvet cushion and finds it. The fanfare plays cleanly on the set trumpet.

Neri says the notification chime can stay only if the phone is shown. Badu declines. Soko puts the letter on a table and leaves the scene.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera pans across the castle hall and stops at a small sign reading "Set lights are controlled from the booth."
