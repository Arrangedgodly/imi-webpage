# The Heist of the Community Quilt

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: precision caper films where a crew stages an elaborate operation to borrow a blanket*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
PLANNER VEX, an orangutan
SEAMSTER MARA, a macaque
LOOKOUT PIP, a capuchin
LIBRARIAN ODO, a gibbon
NEIGHBOR NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. LIBRARY MEETING ROOM - NIGHT

A community quilt hangs behind glass. Vex studies it through a paper tube. Mara lays a floor plan across the table.

VEX: At midnight, we remove the quilt, cross the room without touching the bell, and return it before dawn.

MARA: We are borrowing it for tomorrow's outdoor reading. The library opens at nine, and the caretaker has the key.

PIP: I can be lookout from the lobby. The alarm bell is a desk bell that rings when the door opens.

ODO: The quilt is not under an alarm. It is behind a locked display case to protect the stitching.

VEX: Then our first obstacle is the lock.

NARI: Or we ask the librarian to unlock the case in the morning.

VEX: That's not a heist.

MARA: We need the quilt outside before the reading starts. The case key is checked out to Odo. We can carry it safely, with two people holding the edges.

SCENE 2. INT. LIBRARY - MORNING

Odo turns the key. The case opens. Vex produces black gloves and a small suction cup.

ODO: Why do you have a suction cup?

VEX: For the glass.

MARA: We're opening the case, not stealing the glass.

Vex puts the suction cup away. Mara and Nari lift the quilt by its reinforced border. Pip holds the storage sleeve open.

PIP: The sleeve is too narrow if we fold the corners inward.

NARI: The handling card says roll it around the wide tube. Don't fold the embroidered panels.

VEX: That adds an extra step.

MARA: It prevents damage. Hold the tube steady.

They roll the quilt evenly. Vex reaches for the edge, then stops when Mara points to the cotton gloves.

SCENE 2A. INT. LIBRARY GARDEN DOOR - MORNING

The team tests the route before carrying the quilt. Mara measures the doorway with the storage tube. It clears by two inches if held level.

PIP: We should turn the chairs sideways before we bring the quilt out. The aisle is narrow.

VEX: I had planned to glide through in one uninterrupted shot.

NARI: The camera can follow the quilt, but nobody should walk backward near the steps.

ODO: I'll open both doors and secure them with the proper stops. The handle doesn't latch by itself.

Odo wedges each door with rubber stops and checks that the threshold is dry.

MARA: Route clear. Two carriers at the tube, one person ahead to watch corners, and nobody touches the embroidered surface.

VEX: What is my role?

MARA: Carry the empty storage sleeve and keep the door from swinging.

Vex looks at the sleeve, then nods solemnly as if accepting command of a fleet.

SCENE 3. INT. LIBRARY - CONTINUOUS

The library bell rings. Everyone freezes. The door opens; a child enters with a book.

PIP: That was the front desk bell, not an alarm.

VEX: The authorities have arrived.

ODO: It's the library patron. We are allowed to be here.

NARI: Let's clear the doorway. The quilt is still supported on the tube.

Mara carries one end, Nari the other. They walk toward the exit, but the tube is too long to turn through the narrow door.

MARA: Stop. We need to angle it, not twist the quilt.

VEX: The diagram showed a straight path.

ODO: The diagram omitted the door frame.

PIP: Back up to the reading room and use the double doors by the garden.

The team reverses slowly. Vex holds the far end without touching the fabric. Odo opens the double doors. They pass through safely.

SCENE 4. EXT. LIBRARY GARDEN - MORNING

A table and four chairs wait beneath a tree. The quilt rests on the tube between two padded stands. Nari checks the wind. Mara clips the corners with soft fabric weights.

NARI: The wind is light. The quilt won't lift if we keep the edge weights on.

VEX: The operation is complete. No alarm sounded.

PIP: The desk bell rang when the patron came in.

ODO: The case is locked again, and the handling card is back in the drawer.

MARA: After the reading, we return the quilt the same way. No folding, no shortcuts.

A child arrives, sees the quilt, and sits on the grass instead of the fabric. Pip opens the book.

VEX: I planned a midnight extraction.

NARI: You planned to borrow a quilt from a library.

TAG: The reader begins. Vex holds the wind weights, convinced they are the security team.
