# The Flashback Remembers Differently

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: nested flashbacks that keep changing details while characters insist each version is more accurate*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A witness recounts a simple lost-key incident through three flashbacks, each one adding a new hat and a less reliable memory.

Cast: Pala, a macaque editor; Uru, a gorilla witness; Seli, a lemur detective; and Puck, a capuchin actor.

SCENE 1. INT. INTERVIEW ROOM, MORNING

Seli asks Uru how the community hall key went missing. Uru says he put it on the table after rehearsal. Pala edits the interview while Puck acts out the memory on a small set.

URU: I remember the table. I left the key beside the sign-in sheet.

PALA: Can we see the moment?

Puck walks in, places a key on the table, and leaves. Pala adds a soft dissolve. The dissolve lasts so long that the key appears on the table twice. She shortens it. Puck asks which key is the remembered one. Uru says there was only one. Pala circles the table image with a cursor, then deletes the circle. Seli asks what happened next. Uru says he went to the storage closet. Pala asks for a flashback to the closet. The film cuts to Puck opening a closet door.

SCENE 2. INT. STORAGE CLOSET SET, LATER

In the flashback, Puck reaches for a box. A second Puck appears in the doorway wearing a yellow hat. Uru says he did not own a yellow hat then. Pala says the hat helps the audience track time.

SELI: Which Puck is remembering?

PALA: The first one.

Puck in the hat says he remembers the key being in a coat pocket. The first Puck looks at Uru. Uru says there was no coat. Pala adds another flashback within the flashback. Puck, now wearing a blue hat, hangs a coat beside the door.

URU: I remember a coat now.

Seli asks if the memory has changed. Uru says the coat might have belonged to someone else. Pala rewinds to the interview room and adds a caption: "Earlier, maybe." The caption appears over the wrong Puck. Seli asks whether the hat is supposed to indicate a different day or a different memory. Pala says both. Uru points out he only went to the closet once.

URU: I did not have time for three days.

Pala adds a clock face, then removes it because the hands move backward.

SCENE 3. INT. EDITING ROOM, AFTERNOON

Pala builds the sequence. The table scene shows the key beside the sign-in sheet. The closet scene shows the coat. The nested flashback shows Puck putting the key in the coat pocket. Seli checks the prop log: the key was on a ring with a bright orange tag. In the flashback it has a blue tag.

SELI: These cannot be the same key.

PALA: It is a subjective recollection.

Uru watches the cut. He says he remembers hanging up a coat, but not putting the key in it. Pala adds a fourth flashback showing the blue tag turning orange. The shot becomes a close-up of a tag spinning in midair.

Puck asks if the audience will know which memory is true. Pala says the sequence lets viewers participate. Seli puts the orange key tag beside the interview footage. The color matches Uru's jacket. They replay the actual moment from the security camera: Uru pockets the key while carrying the sign-in sheet.

SELI: That is the whole mystery.

URU: I was holding the papers.

Puck asks if he should act surprised in the flashback. Seli says he can just return the key. Seli says they should find the actual key before deciding. Uru checks his jacket pocket and finds it on the orange-tagged ring. He had taken it home after rehearsal.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, EVENING

Uru returns the key to the hook by the entrance. Seli updates the sign-in sheet. Pala watches the footage and removes the coat flashback and the floating tag. She keeps the interview answer and a shot of the key being returned.

PALA: It is a shorter story.

URU: It is what happened.

Puck asks if they can still use the yellow hat. Seli says it belongs to the costume rack and is not evidence. Puck returns it. Pala checks the edit once more. A leftover yellow-hat close-up remains between the interview and the return. She removes it and saves the new cut. Uru signs the key back in.

FINAL IMAGE: The key hangs on the hall hook. The edit timeline contains one interview, one return, and no nested timelines. Seli asks Pala to keep the interview audio in the film. Pala labels the single take with the date and location, then removes the extra flashback tracks from the timeline.
