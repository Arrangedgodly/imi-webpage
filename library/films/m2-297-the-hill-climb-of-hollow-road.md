# The Hill Climb of Hollow Road

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: cycling underdog dramas, punishing training montages, team domestiques, and finish-line photo reviews*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A young lemur cyclist joins a team of older primates for the Hollow Road hill climb and discovers that the race is won by the rider who knows when to stop pulling.

Cast
- KET, a ring-tailed lemur rookie with an excellent balance and a too-small helmet.
- VEX, a macaque captain who takes every climb as a personal challenge.
- OMI, an orangutan mechanic and careful route planner.
- SERA, a capuchin sprinter who dislikes hills.
- RACE DIRECTOR PEL, a gibbon who runs the event from a rolling scooter.

## Scene 1: The Team

EXT. CYCLING TRACK - MORNING

The team rides in formation. Vex sets a fast pace. Ket falls behind, tail streaming in the wind. Omi checks the bikes at the finish.

VEX: Keep up with the team.

KET: I am behind the team.

SERA: That is not the same thing.

The team needs one more rider for the Hollow Road climb. Vex selects Ket because the course is narrow and she can balance through sharp turns. Ket asks whether the team expects her to win. Vex says she is expected to draft and carry the spare tire.

## Scene 2: The Training Montage

The riders train on flat paths, hills, and the steep lane behind the market. Ket can climb steadily but is slow on descents. Vex practices sprinting from every bend. Sera practices reaching the hilltop without looking at the slope.

OMI: We need a pace plan.

VEX: We ride faster.

SERA: That is not a plan. That is an instruction.

Omi marks water stops on the map. Vex removes two stops to improve the time. Ket notices the third stop is beside the only shade tree.

KET: We will need water there.

VEX: The finish is close.

KET: The finish is uphill.

At the next practice run, a practice cone rolls into the lane. Vex swerves around it, while Ket brakes and sets it upright. The delay costs her a few seconds, but the group avoids a collision.

VEX: A cone is not a rival.

KET: It was still in our way.

Omi adds a marker at the turn and asks the course crew to secure all loose objects. Vex complains that the route is becoming too safe to be exciting.

SERA: It is still a hill. You can be excited at the top.

## Scene 3: The Race

EXT. HOLLOW ROAD - RACE DAY

The riders start together. Vex accelerates immediately. Sera follows. Ket stays in the pack and watches the route. At the first turn, a pothole has opened. Ket signals the group to move left. Vex ignores her and jolts over it, dropping his water bottle.

PEL: Stay within the course markers!

VEX: I know the road.

KET: It changed overnight.

Sera stops to retrieve the bottle. Vex waves her on, then slows as the hill steepens. Ket catches up and offers her spare tire strap to secure the bottle cage.

## Scene 4: The Team Plan

At the shade tree, Omi checks the riders. Vex wants to push through. His rear tire is losing air. Ket offers the spare tire, but it must be fitted. Sera holds the bike while Omi repairs it. The rival team passes.

VEX: We are losing the race.

SERA: We are fixing the bike.

KET: There are three turns to the summit. I can set the pace on the narrow section. Vex, save your sprint for the last straight.

VEX: I lead this team.

KET: Then follow me for three turns.

Vex agrees. They ride in single file. Ket leads at a steady pace, using her balance to corner safely. Sera drafts behind her. Vex saves energy.

## Scene 5: The Finish

The rival team reaches the summit first, but one rider crosses the line outside the marked lane. Pel signals a penalty. Vex and Ket arrive together. Vex starts a sprint; Ket points to a loose gravel patch. He shifts left instead of accelerating. They cross the finish side by side.

PEL: Second place, by a wheel.

VEX: We could have won if we sprinted.

OMI: We could have fallen if we did.

SERA: Second is a finish.

Ket asks for the photo review. The image shows her front wheel ahead by a fraction. Pel awards the team first place. Vex looks at the photo, then at Ket.

VEX: You saw the gravel.

KET: I saw the whole road.

## Scene 6: The Team

The team lifts the trophy. Vex does not claim it alone. Omi begins checking the bikes for the next race. Sera asks for a flat course.

PEL: Next year is another hill.

SERA: I will train on a hill that has a chairlift.

## Final Image

The team rides back down Hollow Road together, stopping at the shade tree to refill their bottles before the descent.
