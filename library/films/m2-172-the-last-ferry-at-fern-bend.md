# The Last Ferry at Fern Bend

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster thrillers that stretch a minor ferry delay into a ticking-clock rescue*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A small troop waits for a hand-pulled ferry after a storm, while a documentarian tries to make the ordinary delay feel apocalyptic.

Cast: Pella, a macaque ferrypuller; Niko, a capuchin filmmaker; Daro, a slow loris traveler; and Ivi, a young squirrel monkey with a basket of guavas.

SCENE 1. EXT. FERN BEND, MORNING

A rope ferry rests on the near bank. The current is high from overnight rain. Pella checks both anchor knots. Niko sets a camera on a stump.

NIKO: How much time do we have?

PELLA: Until the water drops enough to cross.

NIKO: And if it does not?

PELLA: We wait.

Niko turns toward camera and lowers his voice.

NIKO: The crossing hangs in the balance.

Pella points to the ferry, which is tied securely to a tree.

PELLA: It is tied to the balance.

Daro settles beneath a fern. Ivi places her guavas in the shade and counts them twice.

SCENE 2. EXT. FERRY BANK, LATER

Pella tests the rope. It is intact, but a branch has lodged against the far anchor. She does not pull. Niko asks her to describe the danger for the film.

PELLA: The branch is pressing on the rope. We remove it from the bank when the water settles.

NIKO: Can you say, "The rope may snap"?

PELLA: It may not.

Ivi sees a guava roll toward the water and catches it with her foot. Niko swings the camera toward her.

NIKO: A race against the current.

IVI: I was saving lunch.

Daro peers across the stream and notices the branch shifting as the current ebbs. He points without leaving his fern.

DARO: It moved.

Pella watches the anchor line, then waits through another swell. The branch slides free on its own and floats downstream.

NIKO: The obstruction has escaped.

PELLA: The rope is clear.

SCENE 3. EXT. FERRY LANDING, NOON

The water drops, but the landing stones are still slick. Pella lays a wide plank over the first step. Ivi wants to board with her basket. Pella asks her to wait while she checks the ferry's balance.

NIKO: If we lose another hour, the light will change.

PELLA: The light changes every day.

Niko films his watch. Daro stands carefully and moves to the safe side of the plank. He tests it with one hand. Ivi copies him, then sets the basket down before climbing aboard.

Pella pulls the ferry a short distance, pauses, and checks the rope. The platform stays level. She brings it back, then invites the passengers aboard in pairs.

IVI: I thought this was the dangerous part.

PELLA: This is the part where we make it safe.

Niko films the loading. His camera strap slips off his shoulder and catches on a knot. Pella secures the ferry before helping him free it.

NIKO: The equipment nearly went over.

PELLA: It was tied to the bank.

SCENE 4. EXT. FERRY, AFTERNOON

The passengers cross slowly. Pella pulls in a steady rhythm while the others keep the basket centered. Niko asks everyone to look worried. Daro looks at the water, then at Niko.

DARO: Should we look worried about the ferry or your shot?

NIKO: Both, if possible.

The platform reaches the far bank. Pella secures the rope and steps off first. She checks the landing before helping Ivi down. The guavas remain intact. Niko asks for a second crossing to capture a more dramatic arrival.

PELLA: The ferry has completed its crossing.

NIKO: The film has not.

PELLA: The passengers have.

She begins untying the rope for the return trip. Niko reluctantly lifts his camera.

Pella asks Niko to film the return crossing from the bank, where he can keep the camera steady and stay out of the ferry lane. He does. Ivi hands each passenger one guava for the wait, and Daro folds the empty basket flat. The crossing is slower without a crew crowded around the lens. Pella checks the landing twice, then ties the ferry back to its tree.

SCENE 5. EXT. FAR BANK, SUNSET

Niko reviews the footage. The clearest shot is Pella checking the knot, then waiting for the branch to drift away. The passengers cross without incident. He exhales.

NIKO: We could call it "The Day the River Almost Changed." 

PELLA: It changed. It got lower.

Ivi offers him a guava. Niko accepts and turns off the camera. Daro points to the open trail.

DARO: We should take that path before dark.

Niko packs his gear. Behind them, Pella pulls the empty ferry back across, alone and unhurried.
