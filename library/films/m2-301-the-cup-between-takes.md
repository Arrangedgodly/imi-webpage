# The Cup Between Takes

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: continuity supervision, matched eyelines, and productions where a moved prop threatens an entire scene*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR PALLA, a gibbon
ACTOR ODO, a baboon
ACTOR NIMA, a macaque
SCRIPT SUPERVISOR VEX, a capuchin
PROP RUNNER TAVI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. TEA ROOM SET - MORNING

A painted window looks over a painted garden. Beside the table stands a full-height production mirror panel, framed in black flats to reflect the actors and cup while leaving the camera lens outside its angle. A real blue cup sits on the table. Script supervisor Vex measures the cup's distance from a saucer with a folded ruler. Director Palla watches the camera monitor. Odo and Nima wait in costume.

VEX: The cup is three inches from the saucer. The handle points toward the window.

PALLA: Does it look three inches?

VEX: It is three inches.

PALLA: That is a different question.

Tavi lifts the cup to clear a mark on the table. Vex raises his ruler.

VEX: Hold. The cup has moved.

TAVI: I moved it.

VEX: That is the condition I am reporting.

Nima leans toward the monitor. The cup appears to be centered under the window.

NIMA: If the cup is too far left, it will steal the frame.

ODO: It has no lines.

PALLA: It has a handle. That is practically a monologue.

Vex places a square of tape beside the cup. Palla asks for a rehearsal. Odo reaches for the cup on his dialogue cue. Nima is meant to notice and stop him. The cup's handle points toward her instead of the window. They stop. Vex resets it with his ruler and writes "handle to window" on his clipboard.

SCENE 2. INT. TEA ROOM SET - LATER

The first take begins. Odo reaches for the cup; Nima catches his wrist. The camera glides toward their faces. Behind them, Tavi moves the saucer to keep it out of the camera track. Vex calls cut.

VEX: The cup was right. The saucer was not.

TAVI: The track hit it.

PALLA: Can we make the track less visible?

TAVI: It is behind the table.

PALLA: Then the track is doing well.

The crew resets. Vex puts a second tape mark under the saucer and photographs both props. The next take reaches the argument. Nima lifts the cup in anger and sets it down. The handle now points at the camera.

VEX: Cut. It rotated.

NIMA: I turned it.

VEX: The scene says you are furious, not left-handed.

Nima turns it back, slowly, with an exasperated glare. Palla likes the gesture but asks if the cup can be set down without crossing the light reflection. Vex marks Nima's wrist angle. Odo's tail knocks the saucer. The crew freezes as the saucer spins to a stop exactly on its tape mark.

ODO: That was a good take.

VEX: The saucer was right. Your tail was not.

SCENE 3. INT. TEA ROOM SET - AFTERNOON

Palla asks for a wide shot. Vex says the cup marks are visible from the far camera. Tavi proposes using a matching duplicate. Vex inspects it: green, with a chipped rim.

VEX: The cup is blue and intact.

TAVI: This one is for the close shot.

NIMA: It is a different cup.

PALLA: It is an actor's double.

They place the blue cup outside the frame and the green cup on the table. Vex takes a continuity photo. Odo reaches for the green cup on cue. Nima stops him, but he grabs the handle anyway. The chipped rim faces the camera. Palla calls cut.

VEX: It was facing away.

TAVI: Before he picked it up.

Odo demonstrates the reach again, hitting the same mark. The green cup turns in his hand, showing the chip. Vex measures the grip and asks Tavi to sand the edge. Tavi says the cup is chipped, not rough. Palla suggests they shoot the wide scene with no cup. Vex protests that the cup motivates the argument. Nima says the argument is about Odo stealing her tea. Odo points out that he has not had tea. Palla tells him to drink from the prop.

SCENE 4. INT. CONTINUITY TABLE - AFTERNOON

Vex's photos cover a folding table. He tries to identify which cup appears in each frame. One photo shows the blue cup; its reflection makes it green. Another shows the duplicate, but the chipped edge is hidden by Odo's thumb. Tavi has written CUP A and CUP B on separate cards. Vex has put both cards beneath CUP A.

NIMA: Your labels are in the wrong columns.

VEX: I labeled the cups correctly.

NIMA: The labels are correct. Their positions are not.

Palla asks whether they can solve the scene without another close-up. Vex studies the continuity photos, then notices his ruler is reflected in every image. It makes a silver line that appears to run from the cup to the saucer, like a tiny crack. He shifts the ruler. The line disappears.

VEX: I have been correcting the reflection.

TAVI: You have been moving the props to match a ruler in the mirror.

Vex checks the glass. The production mirror reverses left and right, while the camera monitor shows the direct lens view without that reversal. Vex compares the two images and realizes he has been correcting the mirror reflection as if it were the camera frame. He takes down the extra tape marks and lets Tavi place the blue cup in the lens frame.

SCENE 5. INT. TEA ROOM SET - EVENING

They shoot the scene once. Odo reaches. Nima catches him. The cup stays where it is. Her hand turns the handle toward the window, then stops.

NIMA: That is not your tea.

ODO: It is a continuity error.

Palla keeps the camera rolling. Vex checks the monitor, then keeps his hands off the ruler. Tavi lifts the cup after the scene and replaces it on the blue tape square.

FINAL IMAGE: Vex photographs the untouched cup; in the picture, the ruler is outside the frame at last.
