# First Contact at Bus Stop Seven

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: first-contact science-fiction films that inflate an unfamiliar signal into a global mystery before trying ordinary conversation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: An apparent alien craft lands near a bus stop, but the visitor only wants directions to the planetarium before its public lecture begins.

Cast: Nima, a macaque transit worker; Dr. Soro, a capuchin astronomer; Vek, an extraterrestrial visitor; reporter Pala, a lemur; commander Oru, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - EXT. BUS STOP SEVEN - AFTERNOON

A silver pod settles in a vacant bus bay. Nima holds up a stop sign. A hatch opens, releasing a puff of harmless steam. Vek steps out carrying a folded star map.

NIMA: You cannot park in the bus lane.

VEK: I have traveled three systems to ask where I am.

NIMA: Bus Stop Seven, East Market. The planetarium is two stops north.

Pala arrives with a camera and a microphone.

PALA: Are you here to make contact with Earth?

VEK: I am trying to make contact with the planetarium.

PALA: What does your species want from us?

VEK: A lecture hall with a projector. My star chart is difficult to read outdoors.

SCENE 1A - EXT. BUS STOP SEVEN - MOMENTS LATER

Pala turns her microphone toward the pod. The microphone feeds a low hum into the camera. She flinches and drops her notepad.

PALA: The craft has answered.

NIMA: That is feedback from your microphone.

VEK: I can switch off my pod's signal if it interferes.

Nima points to the bus schedule. The signal light on the stop changes from red to green as the next bus approaches.

NIMA: The beacon is linked to the transit receiver. It is not a threat code.

Pala picks up her notepad. It has a smear of ink across the headline: VISITOR DECLARES WAR.

PALA: I will wait until I know what the visitor said.

VEK: I said I am late for a lecture.

SCENE 2 - EXT. BUS STOP - CONTINUOUS

Soro hurries from the planetarium carrying an event schedule. Oru, the city commander, arrives with a portable loudspeaker and a line of cones.

ORU: We have established a perimeter.

NIMA: The bus needs to stop here.

ORU: We will move the stop.

Soro hands Vek the schedule. The visitor examines it, then points at the planetarium address.

VEK: This is the correct building?

SORO: Yes. Your message asked for the public observatory and its lecture room.

PALA: The message sounded like a challenge.

SORO: It was an invitation. The words were translated from a radio pulse.

ORU: The pulse repeated every ninety seconds. We assumed a countdown.

NIMA: It matches the bus arrival signal.

SCENE 3 - INT. CITY COMMAND VAN - MOMENTS LATER

Oru studies the pulse on a monitor. It repeats as the bus route display flashes. The signal is a location beacon sent from Vek's pod.

ORU: We have prepared a defense for an address request.

PALA: I have already posted that the visitor may be hostile.

VEK: I came to share maps. I have brought a star chart and a small gift.

The gift is a smooth stone. Pala lifts it in a gloved hand.

PALA: Is this a device?

VEK: It is from my world. The surface is a good writing slate.

Nima taps it. The sound is ordinary stone.

NIMA: We have chalkboards at the planetarium.

ORU: Stand down the perimeter. Leave one cone so the buses can turn.

SCENE 4 - INT. PLANETARIUM LOBBY - LATER

Vek enters the lecture hall. The star projector lights the ceiling. Soro plugs the visitor's device into the presentation system. It displays a star map. The map's pulse syncs with the planetarium clock, revealing a route to Vek's home cluster.

PALA: The signal was a map all along.

VEK: And your room is ideal for showing it.

Oru removes the cones from the lobby entrance. Nima posts a temporary sign directing buses around the pod.

NIMA: The bus stop is moving one bay over until departure.

ORU: That is a more useful perimeter.

SCENE 5 - INT. PLANETARIUM - EVENING

Vek gives the lecture. The audience follows the star routes. Pala records a correction to her report. Oru sits in the front row, holding the stone slate. Nima arrives with a bus schedule.

NIMA: The last bus leaves in twelve minutes. The pod can remain in the unused bay overnight.

VEK: I will finish the questions and depart in the morning.

Soro invites Vek to return for a second lecture. The visitor agrees and places the stone slate beside the projector. A child writes a question on it with chalk.

CHILD: Does your world have buses?

VEK: We have a travel system. It is less punctual.

FINAL IMAGE: The silver pod rests beside the planetarium loading bay while the star projector paints its smooth roof with a map of Vek's home.
