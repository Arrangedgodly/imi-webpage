# The Acorn for Best Weather

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: awards-season prestige films, symbolic weather, and speeches that explain their own meaning*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A neighborhood filmmakers' collective enters its rain-gutter repair film in an awards festival and is asked to make the practical story feel more tragic.

Cast: Elin, a macaque director; Bo, a gorilla actor; Yara, a lemur editor; and Simi, a capuchin festival programmer.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, NIGHT

Elin screens her film, "The Gutter." It follows neighbors repairing a leaking roof before a storm. The audience applauds. Simi asks if there is a longer cut. Elin says the film is twelve minutes. Simi says the festival likes a personal journey.

BO: I carried the ladder.

SIMI: Did the ladder represent anything?

BO: It represented a ladder.

Simi suggests adding a childhood memory to give the roof emotional weight. Yara asks whether the roof has a childhood. Elin says the roof has a repair receipt.

Simi shows them a festival form asking for "a moment of irreversible personal change." Bo says the tenants moved their chairs away from the drip. Simi asks if the chairs can be shown in silhouette. Yara searches the footage and finds a shot of one tenant carrying a chair, then another, then a bucket. Elin says that is what happened. Simi suggests adding a thunderclap. The sound editor plays a recording of thunder; it covers the tenant saying, "The leak is over there." They remove the effect.

The festival requests a director statement. Elin writes that the short was made with six volunteers and donated screws. Simi says the statement needs a universal theme. Bo suggests that roofs keep rain out. Yara keeps the sentence. Simi makes a note to ask the jury what they consider universal.

SCENE 2. INT. EDITING ROOM, MORNING

The collective reviews the footage. Simi suggests a black-and-white flashback to the first rain. Elin says the camera was inside the house and no one was filming the clouds. Yara tries adding a slow-motion shot of a drop falling into a bucket. The drop lands outside the bucket.

Simi calls it a metaphor for missed opportunities. Bo asks why they do not just move the bucket. Elin adds a shot of him moving it. Simi requests a voiceover explaining the shot. Bo records: "The bucket was in the wrong place." Yara leaves it in.

They watch the film again. The storm scene is clear, but Simi says the ending needs an awards speech. Bo gives one, thanking the ladder, the bucket, and the person who bought screws. Elin says he is supposed to speak as the character, not the film. Bo asks what the character wants. Elin says a dry room for the tenants.

Bo performs the speech again, this time to the tenant group. They interrupt to ask if he can fix the downspout outside their actual building. Elin pauses the edit and asks if they have the right tools. One tenant has a ladder; another has a wrench. They check the roof from the ground, spot a blocked drain, and call the property caretaker before climbing. Simi films the neighbors scheduling a proper repair rather than staging a heroic leap.

SCENE 3. EXT. COMMUNITY ROOF, AFTERNOON

The festival requests a promotional photograph in dramatic rain. The crew waits under a canopy. Simi pours water from a watering can to create a symbolic downpour. It misses the roof and lands on the camera case. Yara moves the case. Bo checks the gutter and notices a new blockage caused by leaves.

The crew clears the leaves and tests the downspout. Water flows away from the building. Simi says this could be the new ending. Elin films the neighbors working together, then the tenants using the dry room. There is no dramatic storm, only an ordinary drizzle.

The building caretaker checks the repaired gutter and signs the work sheet. Bo asks if the signature can be a close-up. Elin films it, then films the tenants returning furniture to the room. Yara cuts between the bucket's old drip mark and the dry floor. Simi wants a title card explaining the metaphor. Elin says the shot already shows the result. He agrees, then asks whether the receipt can be shown after the credits.

SCENE 4. INT. FESTIVAL SCREENING ROOM, EVENING

The short screens in its original form. The audience laughs at the bucket line and asks how to volunteer for the next repair day. Simi says the ending lacks a tragic sacrifice. Elin points to the ladder, now back at the hardware shed. Bo tells Simi that the film is about fixing something before it gets worse.

The jury gives the film an award for practical storytelling. Simi tries to announce it as "Best Use of Weather." The presenter corrects him: the award is for community craft. Bo accepts and thanks the volunteers. He does not mention the ladder until it is safely stored.

FINAL IMAGE: The trophy is placed beside the repair receipt in the cinema lobby. A visitor reads the receipt, writes down the hardware store address, and walks out with a volunteer sign-up card.
