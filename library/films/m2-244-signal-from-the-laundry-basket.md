# Signal from the Laundry Basket

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: first-contact science fiction, where a crew mistakes ordinary laundry noises for an alien transmission*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A night crew decodes a mysterious signal from a laundry basket and discovers an unexpected message from the wash cycle.

Cast: Commander Yara, a macaque; analyst Pito, a gibbon; engineer Odo, a baboon; and intern Lemi, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. INT. LAUNDRY ROOM, NIGHT

A basket sits beside the washer. Three thumps sound from inside.

[SFX: THUMP. THUMP. THUMP.]

YARA: We have a signal.

PITO: It repeats every eleven seconds.

ODO: The dryer is off.

LEMI: The basket is full of towels.

YARA: Full of what?

LEMI: Towels.

Pito writes the thumps on a glass board: dot, dot, dot. Yara circles them and draws a star system.

YARA: The pattern points to a source.

ODO: It points to the loose wheel on the basket.

Another thump. The basket rolls against the washer.

PITO: It moved.

YARA: The signal is mobile.

Lemi checks the laundry tag. It says "do not tumble." Yara treats it as a warning from the unknown civilization.

SCENE 2. INT. CONTROL STATION, CONTINUOUS

Odo places the basket on a rubber mat. The thumps stop.

YARA: We have silenced it.

PITO: We have interrupted contact.

LEMI: Or stopped the wheel.

Yara tells Odo to remove the mat. The thumps return as the basket tilts. Pito records the rhythm again. A loose button bounces inside the basket and strikes the side.

[SFX: BUTTON TICKING.]

PITO: A secondary voice.

LEMI: It is a button.

YARA: What does it say?

LEMI: It says someone forgot to fasten it.

Pito taps back three times. The button rattles in reply.

YARA: The signal responds to us.

ODO: Your tapping made the basket move.

YARA: We have established communication.

LEMI: We have established gravity.

SCENE 3. INT. WASHER BAY, LATER

Lemi sorts the towels by color. A red sock is stuck to the washer door. When she peels it away, the seal releases with a soft pop.

[SFX: RUBBER POP.]

YARA: The chamber has opened.

ODO: The washer door was closed.

PITO: The sound matches the first pulse.

YARA: The message was inside the chamber.

LEMI: The message is a sock.

The sock has a small hole. Yara declares it a map of an inhabited world. Odo checks the wash schedule and says the machine has been waiting for the load to be sorted.

YARA: The alien machine has been waiting for us.

LEMI: The machine is a washing machine.

YARA: That is its cover.

Odo loads the towels, but the basket wheel catches on the cable. The basket jerks and gives three more thumps.

[SFX: THREE THUMPS.]

PITO: The signal is moving toward the washer.

ODO: The basket is caught on my foot.

SCENE 4. INT. LAUNDRY ROOM, DAWN

They untangle the cable. Lemi turns the washer on. It hums steadily. The basket stays still on the mat.

YARA: The signal has gone quiet.

Pito asks whether the crew should send a response before the cycle ends. They tap three times on the washer door. The machine thumps once in return.

[SFX: THREE TAPS, ONE THUMP.]

PITO: The reply is shorter.

LEMI: That is the rinse cycle starting.

Odo checks the panel and points out that the machine is balancing the load. Yara asks if the response means the towels have been accepted. Lemi says it means the load was uneven.

YARA: We must redistribute the crew.

PITO: We are not in the washer.

YARA: Then redistribute the towels.

They move the towels evenly. The washer quiets. The crew watches the water through the small window, waiting for another sign.

PITO: Perhaps it is waiting for a reply.

LEMI: The cycle takes forty minutes. We can wait.

Yara starts to record a message. Odo says they have no evidence of another civilization. Yara insists on a cautious greeting.

YARA: Unknown presence, we have received your pulses. We offer clean towels.

The washer begins its rinse cycle. A sock slides under the door and emerges wet.

[SFX: WATER SWISH, SOCK DROPS.]

PITO: It has sent a token.

LEMI: It has sent laundry.

Odo hands Yara the wet sock. She studies the hole.

YARA: A flag.

LEMI: A sock with a hole.

Yara puts it in the mending pile. The crew waits for the washer to finish. No new thumps arrive.

PITO: The signal has ceased.

ODO: The loose wheel is fixed.

YARA: Contact may still be possible.

Lemi tightens the basket wheel. It no longer rattles.

LEMI: Now it is definitely over.

YARA: We will monitor the basket.

[SFX: WASHER BEEPS. THREE EVEN BEEPS.]

PITO: Commander, it is transmitting again.

LEMI: That's the washer timer.

YARA: Log the message: "Cycle complete."

[SFX: BASKET ROLLS SMOOTHLY AWAY.]
