# The Lighthouse's Day Off

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: vacation films, reluctant substitutes, postcard montages, and guides who leave detailed instructions that nobody reads*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
LUMA, a lemur lighthouse keeper
PIP, a capuchin assistant
VEX, a macaque tourist guide
NARI, an orangutan harbor pilot
ODO, a gibbon ferry captain

SCENE 1. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE - MORNING

Luma posts a sign: CLOSED FOR ONE DAY. Pip is supposed to take over. A ferry approaches the harbor, but its light is off. Pip flips the switch; the beam shines inland. He rotates the lamp one turn too far and lights the roof.

LUMA: One full turn, not a parade.

PIP: I will keep it simple.

Luma leaves with a small bag. Vex arrives leading a tour group and asks where the lighthouse keeper is. Pip points to the sign. Vex asks if he can show visitors around anyway.

SCENE 2. INT. LIGHTHOUSE - LATER

Pip reads Luma's instruction sheet. It says check the weather, test the lamp, log the ferry schedule, and do not let visitors touch the glass. Vex turns the list into a scenic tour. He gives each instruction a dramatic title, including "The Secret of the Lens." He asks Pip to explain the history of the lamp. Pip reads the paragraph from the manual.

VEX: Make it sound ancient.

PIP: It was installed in 1982.

The tour group climbs the stairs. One visitor gets tired and sits on a step. Vex asks Pip to describe the climb as a test of endurance. Pip hands the visitor a folding stool and checks that the stair rail is secure. A child in the tour asks whether the lamp can see the whole island. Pip says it can reach the harbor, but not through the rock ridge. Vex tries to turn this into a speech about perspective. The child simply points toward the ferry, which has entered the beam safely. Vex calls it a contemplative pause. Pip gives the visitor water and checks the light. The beam now sweeps the harbor. Odo radios that the ferry can see it.

SCENE 3. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE BALCONY - AFTERNOON

A seagull lands on the railing and blocks the lens. Pip waves a cloth to shoo it away; the bird steals the cloth and drops it on the roof. Vex says the bird is part of the tour. Pip retrieves the cloth with a long pole. The visitors watch him balance along the railing while Vex narrates every step as a daring stunt.

Nari radios that a storm is approaching from the west. Pip checks the weather chart and sees the wind shifting. He has to adjust the lamp's shutters so the beam remains visible. Vex urges him to wait for the group photo. Pip asks the visitors to step inside for safety. Vex continues describing the sky until the first drops hit his hat.

SCENE 3A. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE - MOMENTS LATER

The wind shifts suddenly. Pip reads Luma's notes and adjusts the shutters by one notch. The beam still sweeps over the water, but the warning light for the harbor entrance flickers. Nari radios a change in visibility. Pip calls Odo on the ferry channel, then waits for the reply rather than guessing. Odo confirms he can see the main beacon but not the side marker. Pip resets the marker lamp from its control panel. Vex films this as a two-part rescue, first the beacon and then the marker. Pip asks him to hold the ladder steady.

SCENE 4. INT. LIGHTHOUSE - LATE AFTERNOON

The storm passes quickly. Pip tests the lamp and realizes the lens is cloudy. He uses the proper cloth and wipes it carefully, restoring the bright beam. A ferry enters the harbor safely. Odo sends a thank-you over the radio. Pip logs the weather and the lens cleaning. The list has a blank space for a visitor count; Vex counts the tour group twice because one guest went back to collect a hat.

VEX: We have had an eventful day.

PIP: We completed the list.

SCENE 5. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE - EVENING

Luma returns, rested. Pip reports that the lamp was turned inland once, blocked once, and then cleaned. He tells her the ferry reached the harbor. Vex gives Luma a postcard of the lighthouse from the tour, but it shows the light pointed at the roof. Luma takes it as a reminder to keep the instructions simple.

Pip asks if he can take a day off next week. Luma says yes, after he writes a clear guide for the next keeper. Vex offers to make the guide more exciting. Pip gives him a pencil and a blank page.

FINAL IMAGE: The lighthouse beam crosses the harbor and sweeps past the closed-for-one-day sign, which Pip has turned to read "OPEN TOMORROW."
