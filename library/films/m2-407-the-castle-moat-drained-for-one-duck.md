# The Castle Moat Drained for One Duck

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: medieval siege epics, royal emergency councils, and battles disrupted by a very small practical problem*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
QUEEN NARI, a lemur
CAPTAIN VEX, a gibbon
PIP, a capuchin engineer
MARA, a macaque gardener
THE DUCK, a silent performer

SCENE 1. EXT. CASTLE WALL - DAWN

A duck waddles through the castle moat. Queen Nari watches from the wall. Captain Vex calls for the archers.

VEX: The enemy has crossed the moat.

NARI: That is a duck.

PIP: It has no banner.

The duck paddles to a small island in the middle of the moat and sits on a stone. Mara says it is nesting. Vex says the island is strategically important.

VEX: We must retake the center.

MARA: The center belongs to the duck.

Nari asks whether the moat can be drained. Pip says the gate is jammed with leaves. Vex orders the drawbridge lowered. It drops, striking the island and making the duck flap its wings.

SCENE 2. INT. CASTLE COUNCIL ROOM - MORNING

The council studies a map. Vex draws arrows from the wall to the island. Mara erases them and marks a route for the gardener.

MARA: We can clear the drain.

VEX: Under enemy observation?

NARI: The enemy is a duck.

Pip opens the sluice lever. It does not move. He pulls harder. The lever comes off in his hand.

PIP: We need a wrench.

The duck quacks from outside. Vex interprets the call as a challenge. Nari tells him to stop answering. He bows toward the window.

NARI: Do not negotiate with it.

VEX: It has occupied the island.

The General draws a second arrow on the map. It ends at the duck's nest. Mara removes the map from the table and folds it so the arrow disappears.

MARA: We do not attack a nest.

VEX: We do not know what it contains.

PIP: It contains a duck.

The duck enters the council room through the open window and walks under the table. Vex stands on his chair. Nari reaches for the window latch, but the duck exits through the open door.

NARI: The enemy has left the room.

VEX: It may be scouting.

Mara gives Vex a bag of seed. He holds it like a diplomatic package and waits by the door.

SCENE 3. EXT. MOAT - NOON

Mara and Pip enter the moat in wading boots. The water reaches their knees. They clear branches from the drain. The duck swims in circles around them.

PIP: It's guarding the blockage.

MARA: It's avoiding us.

Vex lowers a basket from the wall. The duck climbs onto it. He pulls the rope. The duck rises above the water, flaps, and lands back on the island.

VEX: We have captured it.

NARI: It was never in the basket.

Pip frees the drain. Water begins to flow out of the moat. The island drops lower, and the duck steps onto the drawbridge. Vex orders the archers to stand down.

The current pulls a branch toward the open drain. Pip catches it with a rake. The duck follows the branch to the drawbridge, then turns back toward the island.

PIP: It is going back.

MARA: The nest is there.

Vex lowers a plank from the wall to make a path. The duck ignores the plank and waddles along the stones at the edge of the moat. Vex retracts the plank before it falls into the water.

VEX: The duck knows the route.

NARI: It has lived here longer than the wall.

SCENE 4. INT. CASTLE GATEHOUSE - AFTERNOON

The moat is nearly empty. The duck waddles through the gate. Mara follows it with a bowl of water. Vex sees the duck's nest beneath the bridge and stops the crew from raising it.

VEX: There are eggs.

NARI: Then it is not an invasion.

PIP: We should not drain the moat all the way.

Mara directs the last water through a side channel into the garden. The duck sits beside the bowl. Vex asks whether they should build a second moat around it.

MARA: No. A smaller pond.

SCENE 5. EXT. CASTLE GARDEN - SUNSET

The garden has a shallow pond. Mara places a reed mat beside it. The duck walks into the water and paddles. Nari tells the guards to leave the drawbridge raised until the nest is empty.

VEX: We have surrendered the moat.

NARI: We have maintained the castle.

Pip returns the broken lever to the council room. It no longer fits the wall mount. Mara uses it as a garden stake.

FINAL IMAGE: The duck stands beside the pond while Vex watches from the wall, still waiting for a formal signal.
