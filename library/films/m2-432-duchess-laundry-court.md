# The Duchess of the Laundry Court

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: costume romances that mistake formal etiquette, symbolic gifts and public misunderstandings for genuine courtship*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A duchess and a visiting cartographer are repeatedly mistaken for a couple after both reach for the same missing linen in a palace laundry.

Cast: Duchess Irena, a macaque; cartographer Lio, a gibbon; steward Varo, a lemur; maid Nessa, a capuchin; Queen Amara, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. PALACE LAUNDRY - MORNING

Irena enters carrying a map of the palace roof. Lio carries a sheet with a damp ink stain. They reach for the same clean linen on a table.

IRENA: That cloth is for the map room.

LIO: I was told it would cover my chart while it dries.

Nessa looks from one to the other. She smiles knowingly and carries away the rest of the linen.

NESSA: I will give you two a moment.

IRENA: We need a dry sheet, not a moment.

LIO: The steward sent me to collect the roof survey.

Varo arrives with a ribboned box.

VARO: A gift for the duke's proposed match.

IRENA: My brother is away.

VARO: The note says "the duchess's intended." I assumed.

LIO: I am an intended cartographer. I intend to finish the map.

SCENE 2 - INT. PALACE MAP ROOM - LATER

Irena hangs the chart on a board. Lio points to the tower roof. The damp ink has run into a blue river shape.

LIO: The ink spread where the laundry steam reached the paper.

IRENA: The map now shows a river through the west tower.

LIO: There is no river there.

Varo enters with the gift box and a pair of embroidered gloves.

VARO: The court expects a public announcement at noon.

IRENA: There is no announcement.

VARO: The guests have already gathered.

LIO: Perhaps they are here for the roof survey.

VARO: They are holding flowers.

Irena opens the box. Inside is a ceremonial handkerchief. She holds it over the map to blot the wet ink. The cloth stains blue.

IRENA: This is not a courtship token. It is a blotter now.

SCENE 3 - INT. PALACE GALLERY - NOON

Guests wait beneath a portrait of Queen Amara. The queen studies a program listing Irena and Lio as the betrothed pair. Nessa whispers to the steward.

QUEEN AMARA: I asked for the roof survey. Why does the program announce an engagement?

VARO: The invitation described a union between the duchess and the mapmaker.

LIO: A union of our notes. We combined the roof measurements.

IRENA: The west tower has two chimneys and one damaged gutter. It has no river.

The audience murmurs. Queen Amara looks at the handkerchief, now marked with blue ink.

QUEEN AMARA: That seems a practical gift.

IRENA: It was linen from the laundry.

Nessa presents a second dry cloth. She has embroidered a tiny roofline on it.

NESSA: I thought the map room might need a clean cover. I made this before I heard the announcement.

Lio offers it to Irena. Their hands do not touch; both are holding opposite corners.

SCENE 4 - INT. PALACE GALLERY - CONTINUOUS

Varo tries to amend the program. He reads the announcement from the page.

VARO: "The duchess and cartographer unite in a shared vision of the palace." That is technically correct.

IRENA: It is also why everyone thinks we are marrying.

LIO: We can correct it. The palace roof needs repair, not a wedding.

QUEEN AMARA: Then announce the repair. Guests can witness the plan.

Varo turns over the program sheet and writes: ROOF INSPECTION, WEST TOWER. Nessa hands him the map. He pins it beside the portrait.

SCENE 5 - EXT. PALACE ROOF - AFTERNOON

Irena, Lio and Nessa inspect the gutter from a secure platform. Varo holds the map at a safe distance from the edge. The queen watches from a balcony. The roof has no river; the blue mark is only ink.

LIO: The gutter is blocked by leaves. We can clear it before the next rain.

IRENA: The linen covered the map and protected the roof plan from smudging.

NESSA: I can embroider a label for the clean cloth: MAP COVER, NOT ENGAGEMENT GIFT.

Irena and Lio laugh. Varo begins to distribute the corrected programs. Several guests ask for roof copies.

QUEEN AMARA: The palace has been waiting for someone to read the map accurately.

LIO: The mapmaker and the duchess are not betrothed.

IRENA: We are co-authors of the repair plan.

Nessa folds the stained handkerchief into a small cover for the ink pot. Varo takes it downstairs for laundry.

FINAL IMAGE: The corrected roof map hangs beside the palace portrait while the stained ceremonial cloth dries on a line labeled OFFICE USE.
