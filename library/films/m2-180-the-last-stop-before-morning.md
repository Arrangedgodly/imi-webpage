# The Last Stop Before Morning

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: road movies where scenic detours turn a delivery into a journey of competing destinations*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A handcart crew tries to deliver fruit before dawn, but one passenger keeps demanding detours for the perfect view.

Cast: Kiri, a macaque driver; Nono, a capuchin navigator; Vesh, a gibbon passenger; and Luma, a lemur gatekeeper.

SCENE 1. EXT. GROVE PATH, NIGHT

Kiri pushes a low cart stacked with fruit past an old stump. Nono holds a map. Vesh rides in the back, looking up at the stars.

NONO: The direct lane gets us to the gate before sunrise.

VESH: The ridge road gives us a view of the moon.

KIRI: The fruit did not ask for a view.

Vesh points to a faded sign marked RIDGE SCENIC LOOP. Kiri takes the direct lane. A branch blocks it. She stops, sets the brake and checks the wheels before she and Nono lift the branch clear. One fig rolls into Vesh's lap.

VESH: The journey has given me a passenger.

KIRI: It is still part of the delivery.

SCENE 2. EXT. RIDGE TURN, LATER

The branch is gone, but Vesh says they should take the ridge to see the moon behind the valley. Nono warns that it is steep. Vesh promises not to move. Kiri reroutes. The cart climbs slowly. The view is good, but the cart tilts on loose stones. Kiri stops and tightens the woven net over the fruit.

VESH: We can see the whole grove.

KIRI: We can see the cart leaning.

Nono finds the correct trail on the map. Vesh asks for one minute to watch the clouds. A cloud covers the moon. Kiri waits while Vesh studies its shape.

VESH: It looks like a second cart.

KIRI: That cart is imaginary.

VESH: It has a better route.

Kiri pushes on before he can request a map.

SCENE 3. EXT. STREAM CROSSING, BEFORE DAWN

A shallow stream crosses the trail. Kiri tests the stones with a stick. Nono identifies a safe crossing downstream. Vesh spots a small wooden bridge up the bank and wants to take it for the silhouette.

KIRI: The bridge adds a loop.

VESH: It has rails.

NONO: The crossing has tested stones.

They take the stones. Kiri guides one wheel at a time. Vesh holds the cart steady, and Nono frees the net when it catches on a branch. The fruit stays in place. Vesh asks Kiri to pause for a reflection shot. She points to the current and continues.

SCENE 4. EXT. OLD STUMP, DAWN

The trail bends back to the old stump they passed before the ridge. Nono checks the map and realizes they took a loop. Vesh insists the stump looks different in morning light.

KIRI: It is the same stump.

VESH: We have changed.

Nono finds a shortcut through soft orchard ground. Kiri presses the ground with the wheel. It sinks. They refuse the shortcut. Vesh suggests they wait until the sun dries it. Kiri checks the cart and says the fruit is already ripe enough to bruise if they keep stopping.

NONO: The firm path bends north.

KIRI: Then we take the bend.

Vesh looks back at the stump, preparing a farewell speech. The cart rolls forward; he has to grab the side rail.

SCENE 5. EXT. GATE PATH, MORNING

The gate appears between two trees. Luma waits beside it with a ledger. Kiri stops the cart before the threshold and checks that every fruit remains under the net. Nono reads the map right-side up and gives Luma the delivery note.

LUMA: You are late for sunrise.

KIRI: We are on time for the delivery.

Vesh asks whether the gate could be opened toward the ridge for a final view. Luma swings it toward the grove. The gate bumps the cart's handle, turning the cart toward the ridge. Kiri catches it before it rolls.

LUMA: The gate opens this way.

VESH: Then the journey is not finished.

KIRI: The fruit is at its destination.

They unload once. Kiri holds out her hand until Vesh returns the fig from his lap. Luma counts the fruit and signs the note. Nono marks the direct route on the map for the return.

SCENE 6. EXT. GATE PATH, MORNING

The empty cart is turned toward home. Vesh asks for the ridge. Kiri starts along the marked lane. Nono folds the map correctly and puts it away.

VESH: We could take the scenic loop just once.

KIRI: We took it all night.

VESH: The first time was in the dark.

Nono reaches over and applies the brake. The cart stops at the fork. Vesh climbs down and reads the sign: RIDGE SCENIC LOOP, 40 MINUTES. He lifts a bent corner of the notice below it: VIEWING PLATFORM CLOSED FOR MAINTENANCE.

VESH: It is not open.

KIRI: Our fruit toured a closed viewing platform.

Vesh climbs back into the cart. They take the direct lane. Behind them, the sign swings in the breeze and points both ways.
