# The Origin of the Ordinary Bench

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prequels that explain every familiar object with an elaborate secret history*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A filmmaker traces the origin of a park bench through increasingly dramatic flashbacks, until the caretaker reveals it came from a standard catalog.

Cast: Miri, a macaque documentarian; Odo, a gorilla historian; Tessa, a lemur park caretaker; and Nib, a capuchin producer.

SCENE 1. EXT. PARK, DAWN

Miri films a wooden bench. Odo says it has witnessed generations of important events. Nib asks when it arrived. Tessa checks the maintenance ledger: three years ago, installed on a Tuesday.

ODO: Every legend begins somewhere.

TESSA: This one begins with a delivery truck.

Nib asks for a reenactment. Miri films the bench in slow motion as Tessa opens the ledger. The date is visible.

ODO: What happened before the truck?

TESSA: It was in a warehouse.

NIB: Can we film the warehouse?

Tessa turns the receipt around. It lists the warehouse number and model code. Odo asks if the number is a clue. Miri says it is a number. Nib asks Tessa to recreate the delivery. She rolls a utility cart past the bench while Odo narrates a storm that did not happen. A wheel squeaks. Tessa stops and tightens it.

SCENE 2. INT. PARK OFFICE, MORNING

Odo opens a box of old photographs. One shows a similar bench beside a fountain. He declares it the prototype. Tessa says it is a catalog image from the supplier. Nib asks whether the supplier has a mysterious backstory. Miri calls the number on the receipt.

The supplier confirms the bench model has been sold for twelve years. Odo asks if one of the earlier benches could have belonged to a royal garden. The supplier checks the records and says the park ordered four. One was installed at the pond, one at the playground, and two by the path.

Miri films each bench. Nib asks if the fourth one had a unique destiny. Tessa says it was placed in the shade because visitors requested it.

ODO: Who requested it?

TESSA: The park visitors.

NIB: Were they important?

TESSA: They were hot.

Miri films a sign-in card where three people asked for shade. Odo points at a smudge and calls it a coded mark. Tessa wipes it with a cloth. It is dirt from the cart. Nib asks whether the film has a twist. Miri says the shade is the twist.

SCENE 3. EXT. POND PATH, AFTERNOON

Odo asks Tessa to describe the bench's most historic moment. She tells him a family ate sandwiches there, a gardener tied a bootlace, and a visitor waited for a bus. Nib wants a dramatic score. Miri records the sounds: birds, a bicycle bell, and Tessa tightening a loose slat.

Odo finds a scratched initial on the armrest and calls it a clue. Tessa says the park crew will sand it down during maintenance. Odo asks if they can preserve it as an artifact. Tessa says the bench is for sitting, not a museum.

ODO: What if the scratch was left by a previous owner?

TESSA: It is a pencil mark from the maintenance crew.

Nib leans closer. The initials match the worker who replaced the slat. Odo asks for a dramatic reveal. Tessa picks up the sanding block. Nib stands guard over the camera, waiting for an emotional beat. The crew sands the splinter, wipes the seat, and tests the repair. A visitor sits down, checks a phone message, and leaves.

ODO: Was that the historic moment?

MIRI: It was a person using the bench. Nib suggests filming the repair as its origin story.

They remove the loose slat, replace it with a new one, and log the repair. Miri frames the work clearly. Odo tries to insert a flashback to the tree that produced the wood. Tessa asks for the lumber receipt. It lists a certified supplier and a batch number.

SCENE 4. INT. PARK OFFICE, EVENING

Miri screens the finished film: catalog order, delivery, installation, daily use, repair. Nib says there is no twist. Tessa says the bench is reliable. Odo wants one final reveal. Miri shows the credits, including the park crew and supplier.

The film plays at the community center. Visitors recognize the bench and ask where it is. Tessa points them to the shade. Odo waits for them to applaud the origin story. They sit down instead.

VISITOR: Where are the other benches?

TESSA: By the pond and playground.

Odo asks the audience to look for hidden connections among them. A child says they all have legs. Nib writes it on the discussion card. Miri packs the camera while Tessa checks the repaired slat one last time.

FINAL IMAGE: The bench holds three visitors and a bag of sandwiches. Odo's title card, "THE BENCH BEGINS," is folded into the recycling bin.
