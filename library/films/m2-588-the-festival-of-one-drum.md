# The Festival of One Drum

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: festival epics where a single prop is treated as the fate of a culture*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
FESTIVAL HOST VEX, an orangutan
MUSICIAN MARA, a macaque
DRUMMER PIP, a capuchin
STAGEKEEPER ODO, a gibbon
DANCER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - MORNING

A small hand drum rests on a padded stool. A painted sign reads ONE DRUM FESTIVAL. Vex addresses the gathering crowd.

VEX: Our people face silence.

MARA: The drum is here. The musicians are tuning.

PIP: The drum skin is intact. I have not struck it yet.

ODO: The festival starts at noon. The stool is stable.

NARI: The dance circle is marked in chalk. Please leave the center clear.

VEX: The drum must be guarded until the first beat.

MARA: It is a musical instrument, not a crown jewel.

Vex places a velvet rope around the stool. The rope blocks the musicians from reaching the drum. Odo lifts one end so Pip can pass, but Vex stops him.

VEX: The barrier preserves the legend.

ODO: It also blocks the performer.

SCENE 2. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - LATER

Mara removes the velvet rope and hangs it on a post. Pip checks the drum's strap while Odo keeps the walkway open. Vex has assembled a banner reading THE LAST RHYTHM.

VEX: The drum speaks for generations.

NARI: The drum was made last winter. My aunt painted it.

VEX: A recent legend is still a legend.

PIP: The strap is loose. If I carry it to the stage, it may slide.

MARA: Tighten it while it rests on the stool. Then lift it together, with Odo holding the other side.

Pip threads the strap through the buckle. Vex tries to add a ceremonial knot that would cover the adjustment. Nari unties it.

NARI: No decorative knot over the buckle. We need to see that it is secure.

VEX: The festival rejects pageantry.

MARA: It rejects a knot that hides the working strap.

SCENE 3. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - NOON

The crowd gathers. Vex steps between Pip and the drum to announce its historical importance. Mara moves him to the marked side of the stage. The drum remains on the stool until the performer is ready.

VEX: In an age before sound, the village waited for one beat.

PIP: There was sound. The birds were singing.

ODO: The audience can hear them now.

VEX: Then the drum must compete with nature.

NARI: It only needs to accompany the dance.

Mara signals. Pip lifts the drum with Odo, who carries the far side. They set it on the stage mat. The strap is secure. Nari begins a simple dance pattern; Mara taps the rhythm on her knees to help the children follow.

PIP: One beat for the opening step, two beats for the turn.

VEX: The first beat will determine the festival's fate.

Pip strikes the drum once. Its sound is clear but quiet. Vex turns to address the crowd, accidentally muffling the drum with his large banner.

MARA: Lower the banner.

Vex does so. Pip plays the rhythm again, and Nari completes the turn.

SCENE 3A. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

Vex tries to stop the crowd from clapping between beats, worried they will cover the drum. Odo shows him the dance cue card; applause is scheduled after the final turn, not between measures.

VEX: If they clap early, the rhythm may be lost.

NARI: They are following Pip's hands. The dancers know when to turn.

MARA: Leave the edge of the mat open. Your banner is covering the cue card.

Vex rolls the banner tighter, but the loose end unwinds and lands across the marked dance circle. Odo lifts it by the corner and passes it back without touching the drum.

ODO: The circle is clear again.

PIP: I can begin when Nari steps forward.

Nari gives the cue. Pip plays the opening pattern, and the dancers complete the turn together. This time Vex keeps quiet, though he mouths the word "destiny" to himself.

MARA: The music is doing the talking.

SCENE 3B. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - MOMENTS LATER

The dance ends on the cue. The audience claps after the final beat, just as the card indicated. Vex starts to announce a second, longer ending, but Pip holds the drumsticks at rest.

PIP: The piece is complete.

VEX: The festival's emotion remains unresolved.

NARI: The dancers are bowing. That's the ending.

MARA: Let them take their bow before you add another speech.

Vex steps aside. Odo carries the stool to the marked storage area while Pip keeps the drum strap in his hand. They place the instrument down together.

ODO: Drum is secure. The stage is clear.

VEX: The legend will remember this silence.

PIP: It was applause.

SCENE 4. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

The dance finishes. The audience claps. Odo lifts the drum back onto its padded stool only after Pip releases the strap.

VEX: The silence has been defeated.

PIP: The drum was never silent. We were waiting to play it.

NARI: The festival can continue with music and dancing.

Vex rolls up the banner. It is too long and catches around the stool legs. Mara helps him free it without touching the drum.

TAG: The sign painter changes THE LAST RHYTHM to THE FIRST DANCE. Vex asks if the paint has a sequel.
