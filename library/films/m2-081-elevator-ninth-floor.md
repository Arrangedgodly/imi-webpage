# Elevator to the Ninth Floor

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: confined-space thrillers, cryptic control panels, and heroes who refuse to read posted instructions*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A theater troupe gets stuck between floors in an old elevator and invents a conspiracy before noticing the posted emergency phone number.

Cast: Runa, a lemur director; Bok, a gorilla actor; Sedge, a macaque electrician; and Nib, a capuchin usher.

SCENE 1. INT. THEATER ELEVATOR, EVENING

The elevator stops between floors. The lights stay on. Runa looks at the ceiling vent and whispers that the building has trapped them. Bok strikes a heroic pose. Nib reads the panel, which says USE THE CALL BUTTON. He presses it.

RUNA: We need to conserve the battery.

NIB: It is a button.

Bok tries to pry the doors apart. Runa stops him and tells him to wait for the technician. He asks whether the technician is an ally. Sedge answers through the speaker that he is the electrician and can hear everything.

SCENE 2. INT. ELEVATOR, MINUTES LATER

Sedge asks them to confirm the floor number. Runa says they are between eight and nine. Bok claims the elevator is descending, though the indicator is dark. Nib notices the light over the call button is blinking in a regular pattern. Sedge says that is the call connection, not a coded message.

Runa asks if the walls are closing in. Bok stretches his arms and touches neither wall. The troupe begins rehearsing a speech to calm themselves. Nib asks them to sit on the floor so the doors are not blocked. They comply, though Bok says the floor is strategically low.

Sedge explains that a sensor stopped the car after a brief power interruption. He has restored power but must inspect the brake before moving it. Runa asks whether he can use the emergency release. Sedge says it is for trained staff only. Bok volunteers as trained; he once opened a jar.

Runa suggests that the group establish a speaking order so nobody panics over someone else's dialogue. Bok volunteers to go first and delivers a monologue about the elevator's betrayal. Nib points to the instruction card showing the alarm, phone, and door buttons. Runa says the card looks too ordinary to be important. Sedge reads the printed sequence over the speaker. Nib confirms that each light is on and that no alarm is sounding.

Bok asks if the silence could be a clue. A small chime sounds from the panel. Sedge explains that the car has reached the floor. Runa asks why the doors have not opened. Sedge says the car has not been cleared for movement yet. The troupe waits. Bok makes a second speech to fill the silence, but it ends before the safety check does.

SCENE 3. INT. SERVICE CORRIDOR, SAME

Sedge reaches the elevator machine room. A maintenance log says the car needs a reset after the outage. He follows the listed steps and radios each one to the passengers. Runa keeps interrupting to ask whether this is the twist. Sedge says the twist is that he is following a checklist.

The elevator speaker crackles. Bok interprets the noise as an unseen presence. Nib holds the call button while Sedge asks them to stop pressing it. Runa says the button may be the only connection to the outside. Sedge replies that the connection is working, which is why he is asking them not to wear it out.

Sedge checks the brake indicator and finds it safe. He tells them the car will move level with the eighth floor. The troupe braces as if a monster is approaching. The elevator drops one inch and stops. Bok yells his rehearsed line: "Nobody leaves until we know the truth." Runa reminds him they are waiting for a mechanic.

Sedge radios that he will test the doors from the outside. Nib asks the others to step back from the seam. Runa begins to explain the blocking, then notices she is no longer directing a scene. She moves behind the marked line with the rest. Sedge checks the door lock, confirms the landing position, and gives the all-clear. The dramatic pause belongs to the elevator; nobody needs to add one.

SCENE 4. INT. ELEVATOR, MOMENTS LATER

The car aligns with the floor. Sedge opens the doors from the control room after confirming the sill is level. The passengers step out one at a time. Runa asks if they should inspect the corridor for danger. Nib points to the illuminated EXIT sign and walks through it.

Sedge explains the sensor interruption came from a temporary building outage. The elevator will remain closed until he completes a full inspection. Bok asks if the troupe can ride again for a more suspenseful version. Sedge declines. Runa thanks him, then asks whether he has an ominous phrase for the poster. He says, "Scheduled inspection tomorrow."

The troupe returns to the lobby by stairs. Bok pauses on each landing to deliver a cliffhanger. Nib reaches the bottom first and holds the door open. Runa marks the elevator unavailable on the theater board, then crosses out her draft title: "The Shaft Knows."

FINAL IMAGE: Sedge tapes a clear service notice beside the panel. The troupe reads it carefully on the way past, taking the stairs without a single whispered theory.
