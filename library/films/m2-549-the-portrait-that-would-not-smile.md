# The Portrait That Would Not Smile

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prestige biopics that invent an emotional breakthrough for an unsmiling portrait*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
CURATOR MARA, a macaque
RESTORER PIP, a capuchin
DOCUMENTARIAN VEX, an orangutan
ARCHIVIST ODO, a gibbon
VISITOR NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. RESTORATION ROOM - MORNING

A portrait of a stern macaque rests on an easel. Pip adjusts the light. Vex films from behind a marked line.

VEX: The subject has carried a burden for years. Today, the portrait will finally smile.

MARA: It's a portrait. We cannot change the expression.

PIP: The varnish has yellowed. I am cleaning a test square to see the original colors.

ODO: The archive describes the subject as a serious school treasurer. It doesn't mention a tragic secret.

VEX: We need a backstory for the camera.

NARI: The plaque says the treasurer funded the library roof.

SCENE 2. INT. RESTORATION ROOM - LATER

Pip moves a cotton swab across a corner. A warmer color appears beneath the varnish. Vex leans toward the camera.

VEX: The smile is emerging.

PIP: That's a highlight on the cheek. The mouth is still straight.

MARA: Keep your hands on the table edge, Vex. Don't cross the line.

VEX: Perhaps the treasurer smiled only when alone.

ODO: There is a photograph of the original painting in the archive. I'll bring it.

He returns with a flat folder. Mara checks the gloves before opening it.

MARA: The portrait matches the photograph. Serious expression, same collar, same ribbon.

VEX: No hidden grin?

PIP: No. The colors were simply muted by old varnish.

SCENE 2A. INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - LATER

Odo lays out two photographs beside the painting. One shows the portrait before varnishing; the other shows a detail of the ribbon.

ODO: The old photograph is black and white. The catalog card names the blue ribbon, so the description did not come from the restored color.

PIP: The test square is even. I will stop cleaning at the edge of the frame and check the surface under magnification.

MARA: Don't go past the approved boundary. The remaining varnish is stable.

VEX: The subject's eyes look brighter now. Could that be the hidden smile?

NARI: The eyes are reflecting the work light. I'll lower the lamp and check again.

She lowers the lamp. The portrait's expression remains stern.

PIP: No change in the face. The highlight is part of the paint.

ODO: The archive also has a letter from the treasurer. It thanks the community for paying for the roof repair.

VEX: That could be our emotional climax.

MARA: It is part of the history. Read the letter as written; don't invent a last-minute confession.

Pip closes the folder and places the cleaned portrait beside it, keeping the surfaces apart.

SCENE 2B. INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Pip writes the cleaning date in the log. Mara checks the entry against the work order.

MARA: The cleaned square is recorded. The rest of the surface is untouched.

ODO: I'll return the letter and photograph to separate folders.

VEX: No hidden smile, no secret letter, no confession.

PIP: A careful restoration is still a story about how the colors came back.

NARI: The audience can see the ribbon and the ledger more clearly now.



PIP: I'll close the log and return the gloves to the clean cabinet.

MARA: The portrait stays covered until the gallery opens.
SCENE 3. INT. GALLERY - AFTERNOON

Nari reads the plaque to a small group. The restored portrait hangs behind a barrier. The colors are brighter, but the expression is unchanged.

NARI: The treasurer raised funds for the roof by keeping careful accounts and persuading neighbors to contribute.

VEX: We could say the smile is inside the eyes.

MARA: We can say the restored colors reveal details that were hard to see. That is already interesting.

PIP: The ribbon is blue, not black. The edge of the ledger is green.

ODO: The archive entry now records the cleaning date and the test area.

VEX: Can I ask the audience to imagine a smile?

NARI: You can ask them what detail they notice. Don't tell them what to feel.

SCENE 4. INT. GALLERY - CLOSING

Pip covers the portrait with a protective screen. Vex lowers the camera. Mara checks the gallery lights.

VEX: The film has no emotional transformation.

MARA: The portrait was never a film character. The restoration is the change.

NARI: The roof still stands. The accounts are in the archive.

ODO: And the portrait will remain behind the barrier.

Vex looks at the portrait as Pip turns off the work light. In the reflection, his own mouth is smiling.

TAG: Vex turns away, and the stern portrait remains stern.
