# The Last Broadcast from the Empty Planetarium

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: apocalyptic science-fiction broadcasts, heroic countdowns, and stations that keep transmitting after everyone has gone home*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
KETT, a macaque planetarium operator
PIP, a capuchin visitor
SERA, a gibbon astronomer
VEX, a lemur announcer
THE AUTOMATIC VOICE, a speaker

SCENE 1. INT. PLANETARIUM - NIGHT

The planetarium's dome fills with stars. A warning light blinks beside the projector. Kett says the last broadcast will begin at midnight.

VEX: The world is ending at twelve.

SERA: The show ends at twelve.

PIP: Those are different things.

The automatic voice announces, "Final program begins in five minutes." Vex steps to the microphone and reads a dramatic farewell. He says the stars will remember the audience. Kett turns on the projector. The first image is a large red planet.

VEX: It is approaching.

SERA: It is Mars.

The planetarium system runs an old disaster presentation. Vex asks Kett to stop it. Kett says the controls are behind the dome.

SCENE 2. INT. PROJECTION ROOM - LATER

Kett and Sera climb a short ladder. The projector's cable is tangled around a box of spare bulbs. Pip holds the ladder. Vex remains at the microphone, narrating their progress.

VEX: The final technicians ascend toward the engine.

KETT: We are six feet off the floor.

Pip touches the cable. The dome flashes. The audience seats light up one by one. The automatic voice announces that the facility is preparing to close.

PIP: It thinks the show is over.

SERA: The countdown is for the projector.

Vex continues his farewell. The speaker echoes him across the dome, repeating each line a second later.

VEX: We are alone among the stars.

AUTOMATIC VOICE: We are alone among the stars.

VEX: The echo is answering.

KETT: It is the sound system.

SCENE 3. INT. PLANETARIUM - MIDNIGHT

The clock reaches twelve. The projector shuts off. The room goes dark. A single emergency light shines over the stage.

VEX: The final signal has gone silent.

PIP: I can still hear you.

Vex says the broadcast must continue. He switches on a hand lamp and aims it at the ceiling. A round spot appears on the dome like a tiny moon. Sera asks him to hold it steady while Kett reconnects the projector cable.

The lamp shakes. The spot travels over the stars and lands on the emergency exit sign. Pip follows it to the door.

PIP: There is a way out.

VEX: That is the emergency exit.

Kett plugs in the cable. The stars return, but the dome displays a clock face. Sera says the projector is showing the maintenance menu. Vex reads the time as a countdown to the end of the world.

SERA: It is the lamp timer.

The automatic voice says the facility will close in one minute. Kett tries to cancel the shutdown. The menu asks for an operator code. She enters it. The display rejects the code because the system is still running the show.

Kett finds the code printed on a card behind the projector. The card is labeled "For the morning operator." Vex says the morning operator is the future. Sera says the card is for whoever opens tomorrow. Pip reaches for it, but the hand lamp throws his shadow across the dome like a giant figure.

PIP: Something has entered the theater.

VEX: It is coming from the aisle.

Sera moves the lamp. The shadow crosses the Earth and lands on the clock face. Kett reads the code aloud. The automatic voice repeats it as if it were the final coordinates.

AUTOMATIC VOICE: One, seven, four, two.

Kett enters the numbers. The screen asks whether to keep the current program or shut down.

SERA: We should let the last show finish.

VEX: The show is over.

Kett selects "complete program." The clock face disappears. The Earth image returns beneath the stars, and only the emergency sign remains lit.

SCENE 4. INT. FRONT DESK - MOMENTS LATER

Pip takes the microphone and announces that the planetarium is closing for maintenance. Vex objects that the broadcast needs a final message. Kett asks him to say what is actually happening.

VEX: We are closing for maintenance.

The dome shows one last image of Earth from space. Vex starts another speech. The automatic voice interrupts with "Please exit through the lobby." The audience leaves.

SCENE 5. EXT. PLANETARIUM - DAWN

Kett locks the doors. Sera rolls the projector cart to the repair bench. Vex asks if they can record the final broadcast again tomorrow. Pip points at the sign: "Closed for maintenance."

VEX: The world has another day.

SERA: The bulb does.

FINAL IMAGE: Inside the empty dome, one star remains lit. The automatic voice says "good night" to the projector.
