# The Moon Keeps the Receipt

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy adventure films in which magical artifacts have mundane check-out rules and heroes must return borrowed objects*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A young monkey borrows a glowing moonstone from a cave display, then must retrace the party's winding path before the keeper closes the library.

Cast: Paku, a squirrel monkey explorer; Deni, a gibbon guide; Sola, a slow loris cave keeper; and a troop of bats who use the cave roof as a roost.

SCENE 1. INT. CAVE LIBRARY, EVENING

A blue stone glows on a shelf. Paku reaches toward it. Sola hands him a paper ticket instead.

SOLA: You may borrow the light for one hour. Keep the receipt.

PAKU: Is it an ancient relic?

SOLA: It is an old stone, and the receipt is new.

Deni studies the cave map. The return path passes through three chambers. Paku places the stone in a pouch. Its glow leaks through the fabric.

SCENE 2. INT. ECHO CHAMBER, NIGHT

The group enters a chamber where each step echoes. Paku hears a second set of footsteps and turns. It is his own echo.

PAKU: Someone follows us.

DENI: We are the someone.

He sets the stone on a ledge to free both hands for climbing. A bat swoops past, stirring the receipt from his belt. Deni catches it against the wall.

DENI: The cave has requested paperwork.

Paku retrieves the stone. He checks the ticket, which now has a damp corner but remains readable.

SCENE 3. INT. ROOT GALLERY, LATER

A low passage is blocked by a fallen root. Deni says the safe route goes around the long side. Paku notices a narrow gap but tests it with a branch first. The branch fits, but the stone pouch snags. He backs out rather than pulling.

PAKU: The artifact is stuck.

DENI: The pouch is stuck. The stone is fine.

They lift the root together just enough to free the strap. The root settles back into place. They continue along the marked route. Paku keeps the ticket in his hand.

SCENE 4. INT. BAT ROOST, NEAR MIDNIGHT

A cluster of bats hangs above the path. Sola's note says to keep voices low. Paku whispers that the keeper must already be asleep. Deni points to the hourglass painted on the wall: one chamber remains before closing.

A bat drops a seed shell onto the ticket. Paku brushes it away, careful not to wake the roost. The printed time is still visible. They choose the side corridor to avoid passing beneath the bats.

SCENE 4A. INT. LOWER CAVE, LATE NIGHT

The side corridor ends at a shallow pool. Deni tests the edge with a long reed and finds a narrow dry ledge around it. Paku wants to hop across the wet stones, but the pouch would swing and strike the wall. They remove the stone, pass it hand to hand, and keep the receipt in Deni's dry sleeve. The bats remain asleep above them.

SCENE 4B. INT. TICKET ALCOVE, LATER

A ticket alcove has two hooks and a hand bell. Paku rings nothing. He checks the receipt against a faded diagram and matches its number to the shelf label. The stone's glow makes the ink readable without lighting the whole chamber. Deni replaces the receipt in Paku's hand.

DENI: The light is useful. So is the record.

PAKU: The record did not glow.

DENI: It got us back.

They follow the last arrow toward the library, keeping the borrowed stone level.

At the final turn, Paku compares the arrow on the wall with the cave map. One arrow has faded, but the shelf numbers are clear. Deni walks ahead to check the next chamber, then returns with a quiet hand signal. Paku covers the stone with the pouch so its glow will not disturb the bats. They move slowly through the last passage and reach the library door before the hourglass empties.

SCENE 5. INT. CAVE LIBRARY, MIDNIGHT

Sola waits by the shelf with a lantern. Paku returns the stone and presents the receipt. Sola stamps it. The glow fades as the stone meets its place.

PAKU: What happens if it is late?

SOLA: You renew it for another hour.

DENI: We crossed the cave to return it on time.

SOLA: And now you know the route.

Paku looks at the stamped ticket. The stamp is shaped like a tiny moon. He smiles and puts it in his pouch. A bat flutters down and lands on the receipt tray, then curls up beside the stone.

SOLA: That one has a library card.

The group leaves the light with its keeper and follows the ordinary lantern back to the entrance.
