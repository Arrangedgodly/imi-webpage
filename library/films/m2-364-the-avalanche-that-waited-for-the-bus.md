# The Avalanche That Waited for the Bus

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: alpine survival thrillers, blizzard countdowns, rescue helicopters, and hikers who keep forgetting the trail schedule*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
RANGER ODO, a capuchin
HIKER PIP, a squirrel monkey
GUIDE LUMA, a lemur
DRIVER VEX, a macaque
METEOROLOGIST NARI, an orangutan

SCENE 1. EXT. MOUNTAIN BUS STOP - AFTERNOON

A small group waits at a high mountain bus stop. The trail sign reads: LAST BUS 4:30. Hiker Pip checks his watch; it is 4:12. A distant rumble echoes from the slope. Guide Luma looks at the snowpack and tells everyone to move to the marked shelter.

PIP: Is that the avalanche?

NARI: It is a bus on the lower road.

The bus arrives early, but the driver refuses to move because the road crew has closed the pass. Ranger Odo checks his radio and confirms the upper slope is unstable.

SCENE 2. INT. MOUNTAIN SHELTER - LATER

The hikers sit inside the shelter while snow begins to fall. Vex asks if he should drive back down. Odo says the road is closed and tells him to stay put. Pip wants to retrieve a backpack left at the stop. Luma says it can wait until the slope is checked.

A loud crack sounds outside. Everyone looks at the door. A small avalanche slides across the empty bus stop and covers the bench. The shelter remains clear. Pip reaches for the door, but Luma stops him. Nari checks the radio forecast; another slide could follow if the wind changes.

SCENE 2A. INT. MOUNTAIN SHELTER - EVENING

Nari marks the road closure on the weather map. The first bulletin says the bus will be delayed; the second says it may be canceled. Vex asks which bulletin is the real one. Nari explains that the forecast is updated as the storm moves. Pip unfolds the shelter plan and finds a list of supplies. He checks off blankets and water, then discovers the emergency whistle is inside his own coat pocket.

PIP: I thought it was a trail marker.

LUMA: It can be both, if you use it correctly.

The group practices the shelter's exit route without leaving the building. Odo checks the door and makes sure snow has not blocked it. Vex insists on holding a dramatic lantern. The lantern is battery-powered and has a blinking mode. He turns it on by mistake, making the room look like a dance floor. Pip finds the steady setting.

SCENE 3. INT. SHELTER - NIGHT

The emergency radio repeats an alert with an exaggerated countdown. Vex turns the volume down. Odo checks the supplies: water, blankets, first aid, and two emergency flares. Pip uses a blanket to cover the draft beneath the door. Luma reads the map and confirms the shelter has a second exit on the uphill side.

VEX: Should we signal the helicopter?

NARI: It cannot fly in this wind.

Pip suggests using a flare. Odo says they should save it until visibility improves. The group takes turns listening to the radio and checking the shelter door. The countdown reaches zero; it is the time of the next scheduled weather bulletin, not a deadline.

SCENE 3A. INT. SHELTER - NIGHT

The bus radio crackles with a new update. The road crew needs daylight to inspect the slope. Vex asks whether they can take the maintenance track on foot. Luma points to the map and explains that it crosses the slide path. Pip puts his backpack down and checks the strap for wear. It is torn where the shovel caught it. Odo repairs the strap with a safety pin from the first-aid kit. The group marks the backpack's location on the shelter board so no one has to search outside in the dark.

SCENE 4. EXT. SHELTER - DAWN

The storm passes. Ranger Odo checks the slope from behind the barrier. Nari receives confirmation that the road crew has cleared the lower route. Luma leads the hikers to the bus stop by the marked path. The backpack is half buried, but it is still there. Pip retrieves it with a shovel instead of stepping onto the slope.

Vex tries to start the bus. The battery is cold. Odo warms it with a portable pack while Nari radios for the road crew. The bus starts on the second attempt. Nobody needs to be lifted by helicopter.

SCENE 5. INT. BUS - MORNING

The passengers board. Pip checks the schedule; the next bus is an hour later. He admits he missed the last bus because he followed an unmarked trail. Luma updates the trail sign with a clear arrow to the shelter. Vex asks whether the journey can be called a survival mission. Odo says it can be called a closed road and a safe shelter.

FINAL IMAGE: The bus descends the mountain as fresh snow covers the empty bench, leaving the shelter sign visible above it.
