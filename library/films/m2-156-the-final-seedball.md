# The Final Seedball

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: underdog sports films that turn a local team final into a montage of discipline, sacrifice and slow-motion fruit*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A seedball team reaches the final by practicing short passes. A documentary editor wants a dramatic rivalry, while the players focus on a cracked ball and the slippery field.

Cast: Coach Rena, a baboon; captain Tavi, a macaque; player Bolo, a gibbon; filmmaker Miri, a capuchin; and the Canopy Comets.

SCENE 1. EXT. FIELD, MORNING

Miri films Tavi tying a shoe.

MIRI: Tell me what you sacrificed for the final.

TAVI: I ate breakfast early.

MIRI: Could you make that sound harder?

TAVI: I was hungry during warmups.

Rena calls the team over. The ball has a crack.

RENA: We can use it for practice, not the match.

BOLO: The spare is in the equipment bag.

Miri zooms on the cracked ball.

SCENE 2. EXT. SIDELINE, LATER

The team practices passing. Bolo sends the ball to Tavi. Tavi returns it.

MIRI: We need a montage with more running.

RENA: The passing drill is the plan.

MIRI: Can you run through the drill?

The team repeats the same pass. Miri films from the other side. The ball rolls past her and stops at a bench.

TAVI: We need it back.

Miri keeps filming the bench as if it is a rival.

SCENE 3. EXT. FINAL FIELD, AFTERNOON

The Comets enter. A banner reads "THE LAST CHANCE." Rena checks the field, then moves a cone away from a wet patch.

MIRI: That changes the dramatic composition.

RENA: It changes the safe boundary.

The whistle blows. The Comets score first. Miri raises a card reading "DISASTER."

BOLO: It is one point.

The team passes carefully. Tavi scores. The crowd cheers.

MIRI: We have the comeback.

RENA: We have tied.

SCENE 4. EXT. FIELD, CONTINUOUS

The Comets score again. The team regroups. Miri asks Tavi to repeat a reaction.

MIRI: Tell the camera how you feel.

TAVI: I need the ball.

The Comets kick it toward the wet patch. Tavi waits until the referee moves the cone. Bolo intercepts the next pass, but the ball cracks.

RENA: Stop play.

The referee replaces the ball. The clock keeps running. Miri drops her card in the grass and tries to film the substitution.

SCENE 4A. EXT. SIDELINE, CONTINUOUS

The referee pauses for a water break. Miri uses the time to ask Rena for a dramatic story about the team's training.

MIRI: What did the team sacrifice to reach the final?

RENA: They practiced passing.

MIRI: Anything more personal?

RENA: They took turns filling the water bowl.

Miri turns to Tavi.

MIRI: What was the darkest moment?

TAVI: When the ball rolled under the bench.

MIRI: How did you overcome it?

TAVI: We lifted the bench.

Rena signals the team back to the field. Miri remains in the lane, backing up to keep filming.

RENA: Behind the line.

MIRI: I am getting the comeback angle.

The bench is behind Miri. She bumps it, and the spare ball rolls onto the field. The players stop to return it to the bag.

BOLO: The spare is out.

RENA: Put it away before play resumes.

Miri helps pick it up, then notices the timer.

MIRI: We lost part of the break.

RENA: We used it.

The whistle sounds. The team returns to position, and the match continues with the same score.Bolo checks the replacement ball before the whistle. The seam is intact.

BOLO: This one should hold.

TAVI: We should use the short pass.

Miri asks them to repeat it for a close shot, then notices Rena waiting with the team.

MIRI: We can get it during the match.

RENA: Good. We have to start.

The players take their positions. Miri backs behind the line and keeps the camera still.

SCENE 5. EXT. FIELD, FINAL MINUTE

The score is 2-1. The team makes three short passes. Tavi has a clear shot, but Bolo is open.

TAVI: Bolo!

He passes. Bolo scores. The score is 2-2. The whistle sounds before the next play. The match ends in a tie, and the teams share the trophy.

MIRI: Can we film a winning celebration?

RENA: We can film the teams carrying the trophy together.

The players lift it. Miri moves in for a close-up. The trophy tilts. Everyone steadies it.

BOLO: It is heavier than the ball.

TAVI: We should carry it with two hands.

Miri films the walk back to the equipment bag. The cracked ball is still there.

MIRI: That is the final image.

RENA: The final image is putting the gear away.

The players place the trophy on the bench. The cracked ball rolls toward it and stops against the base.
