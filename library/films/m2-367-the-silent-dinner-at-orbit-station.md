# The Silent Dinner at Orbit Station

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space-station dramas, oxygen alarms, elegant zero-gravity meals, and crews who treat a floating pea as a mission crisis*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
COMMANDER LUMA, a lemur
CHEF ODO, a capuchin
ENGINEER PIP, a squirrel monkey
DR. NARI, an orangutan
VISITOR VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. INT. ORBIT STATION GALLEY - EVENING

The crew prepares a formal dinner for a visiting scientist. A pea escapes its bowl and floats toward the air vent. Commander Luma orders a containment procedure. Chef Odo grabs a serving spoon and tries to catch it.

ODO: It is garnish.

PIP: It is approaching the filter.

The pea sticks to the filter. The station alarm sounds, then announces "vegetable detected." Dr. Nari checks the air system and confirms the filter is working.

SCENE 2. INT. DINING MODULE - LATER

The crew straps down the table and plates. Vex arrives in a stiff visiting uniform. The dinner is meant to celebrate a successful orbital survey. Odo serves a stew that remains in its bowl because of a thick broth. The bread floats away when Pip removes its cover. Luma catches it with a napkin.

NARI: The meal is safe.

VEX: It has left the table.

Odo says the bread is designed to be held. Vex asks whether it can be served in a cup. Odo finds a spare cup and places the bread inside. It sticks to the rim.

SCENE 2A. INT. STATION RECEPTION ROOM - EVENING

Nari draws a picture of the glove on a whiteboard. Vex draws a circle beside it. Vex interprets the circle as a planet, then as a greeting, then as a request for a new orbit. Luma turns the board around and finds a small inventory stamp on the back. The glove is station property. Vex points to the docking bay and plays the same three-beep signal from the console.

PIP: It came through with the cleaning cart.

LUMA: The cart brought it from the lost shelf.

The visitor removes a small tool from its belt and points to the glove's torn seam. Nari identifies a repair patch in the station kit. She asks permission with a gesture. The visitor offers the glove. Nari patches the seam and returns it. Vex puts it on, flexes the fingers, and taps the whiteboard twice. Vex starts to announce a treaty. Luma says the traveler has thanked Nari.

SCENE 3. INT. GALLEY - NIGHT

Pip checks the air filter. The escaped pea has triggered the station's foreign-object sensor, which will not reset until the filter is inspected. Luma asks the crew to put on gloves and follow the maintenance checklist. Odo wants to save the dinner before it cools. Vex asks if the problem is a security breach.

LUMA: It is a pea in a filter.

VEX: That is how breaches begin.

Nari removes the filter and finds the pea lodged beside a crumb of bread. The sensor clears. Pip installs a mesh cover over the vent. The dinner table is secured with magnetic clips, but the clips pull the plates too close together.

SCENE 3A. INT. AIRLOCK - NIGHT

Pip helps Vex check his shuttle adapter. The plug fits, but the cable is too short to reach the craft's charging port. Vex calls it an energy crisis. Pip finds an extension lead in the station's emergency kit. Nari checks the voltage, and Luma holds the cable away from the hatch. The craft begins charging. Its lights blink in a pattern that resembles the station's docking signal.

VEX: It is answering us.

NARI: It is showing the charge level.

Vex gestures toward the station's meal tray. Odo sends over a sealed cup of stew. Vex smells it, then returns the cup unopened. He accepts a piece of bread in a container instead. Odo notes the food preference on the visitor log rather than describing it as a cultural revelation.

SCENE 4. INT. DINING MODULE - NIGHT

The crew resumes dinner. Vex asks for a toast. The cups are magnetic and stick together when raised. Odo pours a little broth into a sealed cup for the toast. Luma asks everyone to wait until the cups are separated. Pip uses a spacer from the tool kit. The group raises their cups and toasts the survey.

Vex compliments the stew. The pea is placed in a small sample dish with a label: FIRST VEGETABLE IN ORBITAL FILTER. Nari removes the label and writes "pea, not a sample." Odo makes another batch of bread in smaller portions that fit each cup.

SCENE 5. INT. OBSERVATION WINDOW - LATER

Vex and the crew look at the planet through the station window. Luma explains the survey route and shows the recorded data. Vex says the dinner was memorable. Odo says the bread was designed for the table, not the vent. Pip checks the filter sensor one last time. It remains clear.

FINAL IMAGE: A single pea rests in a labeled dish beside the window, while the station's air vent hums quietly behind it.
