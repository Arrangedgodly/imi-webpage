# The Train with One Passenger Seat

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prestige rail journeys, rival passengers, and travel films that treat seating as a personal destiny*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
CONDUCTOR SERA, a gibbon
PIP, a capuchin passenger
VEX, a macaque passenger
NARI, a lemur porter
THE TRAIN, a silent performer

SCENE 1. INT. STATION PLATFORM - MORNING

A small train waits beside the platform. Inside it is one passenger seat. Conductor Sera punches tickets. Pip and Vex arrive at once. Nari rolls a folded luggage cart toward the next station.

PIP: I have a ticket.

VEX: So do I.

SERA: The train has one seat.

NARI: We could add another.

Sera says the train's designer placed the seat for optimal window viewing. Pip points to a bench on the platform. Vex says the bench is not in the train.

The station clock strikes. Sera calls for boarding. Pip steps onto the train. Vex follows and both sit. The seat folds flat under their combined weight.

VEX: The seat has rejected us.

SERA: It has made room for one.

SCENE 2. INT. TRAIN CAR - LATER

Nari lifts the seat back into place. Sera proposes a turn-taking schedule: Pip sits until the first bridge, Vex sits until the hill, then both stand.

VEX: The bridge is closer than the hill.

PIP: The hill is before the bridge.

Nari unfolds a route map. It is printed upside down. Sera rotates it. The stops are still out of order because the train is traveling backward.

SERA: The train's direction is not listed.

PIP: The engine is behind us.

VEX: Then we are moving forward toward the past.

The train starts. The only seat slides toward the rear wall. Pip tries to sit, but the seat moves with him. Nari wedges a bag beneath one leg. The seat stops.

Sera issues each passenger a turn card. Pip receives "window, first half." Vex receives "aisle, second half." They trade places as the train passes a row of trees, but the windows are on both sides.

VEX: Which one is the scenic side?

SERA: The side with scenery.

Pip leans toward one window. The seat wobbles as Vex shifts his weight. Nari asks them to stand until the track straightens. They both stand. The seat remains still.

PIP: The seat is calmer without passengers.

SERA: It was built for one.

Vex asks if the seat can be turned around so both passengers can see the view. Sera tries to rotate it. The leg is bolted to the floor and turns only a few inches.

SERA: One passenger at a time.

PIP: The train has two windows but one seat.

NARI: It has one seat and one rule.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIVER BRIDGE - NOON

The train reaches a bridge. Pip is scheduled to sit. Vex asks if he can sit until they cross, since he has the window side. Pip says the window is on both sides.

Sera signals the train to stop. The seat remains still. The passengers step outside to stretch. Nari unfolds the luggage cart beside them. Vex takes a photograph of Pip sitting in the seat. The train door closes behind them.

NARI: We are outside the train.

PIP: We have the seat.

VEX: We do not have the train.

Sera opens the sliding door from inside the train. Nari asks who should board first.

SERA: The person with the ticket.

Both raise their tickets. Sera punches them. The door closes before either can step in. Sera stays aboard as the train continues across the bridge, leaving the passengers beside the station-side walkway.

SCENE 4. EXT. NEXT STATION - AFTERNOON

The train arrives at the next station. The crew waves from the window. The single seat is visible and empty. Sera says it is reserved for the next passenger.

Pip and Vex follow the parallel walkway to the next station. Nari walks with them, pushing the platform's folded luggage cart. They arrive together and ask to board. Sera opens the door and says the seat remains available.

VEX: For one of us?

SERA: For whoever sits down first.

Pip and Vex gesture for the other to go first. The train begins to depart. They leap aboard together, stand in the aisle, and let the seat remain empty. Nari folds the cart and brings it inside.

Nari places the folded route map on it. The seat folds flat again.

PIP: It has a passenger now.

VEX: It's a map.

SERA: It has a ticket.

SCENE 5. INT. TRAIN CAR - SUNSET

The train moves through the valley. Pip and Vex stand by opposite windows. Nari holds the handcart folded under her arm. Sera checks the seat. The map has slipped into the gap.

SERA: We need to stop for the map.

PIP: It can ride.

VEX: It has the window seat.

Sera closes the door and signals the train onward. The map stays on the seat, open to the wrong direction.

FINAL IMAGE: The train rounds a bend. The route map points straight back to the station, while its single seat stays occupied.
