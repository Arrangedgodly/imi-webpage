# The Moonlit Mix-Up

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: screwball romantic comedies, mistaken identities, frantic doorways, and a grand gesture built around the wrong picnic basket*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
TAVI, a capuchin botanist
MIRA, a macaque night gardener
BENTO, a gibbon caterer
NELL, a ring-tailed lemur singer

SCENE 1. EXT. CONSERVATORY GARDEN - EVENING

Tavi carries a picnic basket toward the moon garden, where he hopes to meet Mira after their shift. At the gate, Bento stacks two identical baskets: one for the staff picnic and one for the conservatory's annual flower unveiling. Each has a blue ribbon.

BENTO: The savory basket goes to the singers. The sweet basket goes to the unveiling.

TAVI: Which is which?

BENTO: One has a blue ribbon.

TAVI: They both have blue ribbons.

BENTO: Then the system is working beautifully.

Tavi takes a basket and hurries inside. A bell rings. Nell enters carrying a bouquet and assumes he is delivering the unveiling centerpiece.

NELL: You're early. The flowers aren't ready to be admired.

TAVI: I was hoping to admire one person.

NELL: That's a strange way to deliver sandwiches.

SCENE 2. INT. CONSERVATORY HALL - CONTINUOUS

Mira arrives in the same moon garden through a side door. She finds a note on the bench: "Meet me where the night blooms." Bento wrote it for the flower unveiling speaker, not Tavi. Mira follows it to the staff picnic, where Tavi has placed the wrong basket on a table.

MIRA: I thought we were having dinner.

TAVI: We are. Apparently with a bouquet.

MIRA: I don't eat flowers.

Nell arrives and sees them together. She thinks Mira is the guest speaker and hands her a cue card. Tavi thinks Nell is Mira's supervisor and tries to sound professional.

TAVI: The moon lilies have been pollinated by moths.

MIRA: Thank you, Doctor Picnic.

A group of singers enters through the wrong door, carrying chairs. The garden fills with people who all believe they are waiting for something else. Bento rushes in with the second basket, stops at the sight of Tavi and Mira, and quietly switches the name cards instead.

SCENE 3. INT. FLOWER ROOM - NIGHT

Bento discovers both baskets have been switched. The sweet basket has sandwiches; the savory basket holds iced pastries. He attempts a quiet correction, but the staff have already begun eating. Tavi searches each table for the card he wrote for Mira.

BENTO: The card was tied to the basket.

TAVI: Which basket?

BENTO: The one with a blue ribbon.

TAVI: They both have blue ribbons.

MIRA: That's why you labeled the bottoms?

BENTO: The labels are under the baskets.

They lift one. A paper tag reads STAFF PICNIC. Under the other: FLOWER UNVEILING. The singers have put the second basket on a high shelf to keep the pastries cool. Nell climbs a ladder to retrieve it; the bouquet gets caught in a hanging vine. Tavi catches the ladder while Mira pulls the bouquet free. The entire room pauses, mistaking their teamwork for the grand romantic moment.

NELL: Don't clap. I'm still on the ladder.

SCENE 4. INT. MOON GARDEN - LATER

Tavi finds the card beneath the empty picnic basket. He reads it aloud: "Mira, meet me by the lilies. I want to tell you about the moths." Mira studies him.

MIRA: That's the most romantic sentence you've written?

TAVI: I also wrote one about chlorophyll, but it was too much.

The flower unveiling is announced. Nell needs a speaker to introduce the night blooms. She appoints Mira after believing the cue card makes her the expert. Mira improvises a clear explanation of how the moths pollinate the lilies. The guests listen, then applaud. Tavi holds the correct sweet basket while Bento runs in with the savory one.

BENTO: At last, the baskets are correctly identified.

MIRA: The pastries are already gone.

BENTO: Then the labels were right too late.

SCENE 5. EXT. CONSERVATORY GARDEN - NIGHT

The crowd leaves, carrying leftovers in paper napkins. Nell sings a short closing song beside the lilies. Tavi offers Mira the last pastry. She breaks it in half and hands him a piece.

TAVI: We could have had a quiet picnic.

MIRA: We could. But then you wouldn't have met the entire conservatory.

TAVI: I was aiming for one person.

MIRA: You managed to tell twenty people about moths.

They sit on the bench. Bento emerges with one final basket, now empty, and places it between them like a tiny table. He starts to explain the ribbon system, but Mira takes the blue ribbon off the basket and ties it around a nearby watering can.

BENTO: That was not its assigned use.

MIRA: It's a better label.

FINAL IMAGE: The two sit beneath the moon lilies, sharing one pastry while a moth lands on the blue ribbon and refuses to leave.
