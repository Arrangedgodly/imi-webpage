# The Clockwork Acorn

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: steampunk adventure films that build a grand machine to solve a problem better handled by patient observation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A colony builds an elaborate clockwork device to find a missing acorn, though the acorn is nearby and the machine keeps finding its own parts.

Cast: Miri, a squirrel monkey inventor; Odo, a macaque engineer; Fenn, a gibbon mechanic; and Sela, a capuchin seed keeper.

SCENE 1. INT. WORKSHOP, MORNING

Sela reports one acorn missing from the winter basket. Miri rolls out a brass machine with gears, bells and a rotating pointer.

MIRI: The finder will reveal the acorn's location.

ODO: Has anyone checked beneath the basket?

Miri turns a crank. The pointer swings toward Odo's pocket. Odo empties it: a washer, a twig and a folded note.

SCENE 2. INT. WORKSHOP, LATER

Fenn adjusts the gear train. The machine spins and rings a bell whenever someone walks past. Sela walks by carrying the seed ledger. The machine points at her, rings, and drops a small brass nut into the ledger.

SELA: It has found a nut.

MIRI: A clue.

Fenn retrieves the nut and inspects the axle. One nut is missing from the gear assembly. The machine's pointer swings toward the workbench, then slowly turns back toward the basket.

SCENE 3. EXT. GROVE, NOON

The team rolls the machine through the grove. Each bump makes it ring. A bird lands on the pointer; it points toward the bird. Odo notes that the bird is sitting on the machine. Miri attaches a small platform for it to perch safely. The device rolls on, now accompanied by the bird.

Sela checks the ledger. The missing acorn was last counted near the low shelf. Fenn suggests checking the shelf. Miri says the machine is already pointing there. It is, but only because the path slopes downward.

SCENE 4. INT. SEED ROOM, AFTERNOON

They guide the device into the seed room. Its wheel catches on the basket. The basket tips, and the acorn rolls under a cabinet. Sela sees it and reaches for the acorn. Miri asks her to wait until the machine completes its search. The gears grind. Odo shuts off the crank before the axle strains.

ODO: We can retrieve the acorn now.

MIRI: We need a conclusive reading.

Fenn slides a mirror under the cabinet. The reflection shows the acorn, the machine and all four monkeys leaning over it.

SELA: The reading is that we are blocking the light.

They move aside. Sela picks up the acorn and places it in the basket. Miri marks the machine's stopping point on a chart.

SCENE 4A. INT. SEED ROOM, LATE AFTERNOON

Sela checks the basket count against the ledger. The acorns are present, but the basket sits crooked because the cabinet hinge is loose. Fenn finds a spare peg in the tool tray and asks Odo to hold the door steady. They repair the hinge without taking the basket down. Miri turns the clockwork pointer toward the repaired cabinet. It stays still.

MIRI: The machine has confirmed the fix.

SELA: The hinge has confirmed it.

SCENE 4B. EXT. GROVE, DUSK

The team wheels the machine back to the workshop. A gentle slope starts it rolling faster. Odo grabs the handle and uses the brake, stopping it before the path bends. The bird hops off the pointer and flies to a branch. Miri checks each gear before they continue. Fenn ties the crank in place so it cannot turn by itself. They reach the workshop with all the parts, and the acorn remains in the seed room.

Fenn oils the axle and tests each wheel by hand. The machine rolls smoothly, but the pointer now faces the shelf label, not the basket. Miri writes down that the device is better as a demonstration than a locator. Sela places the stamped inventory card beside it for the next curious visitor.

SCENE 5. INT. WORKSHOP, EVENING

The team dismantles the machine and sorts its parts. Fenn uses the pointer as a label for the seed shelf. Odo puts the gears in a box. Miri presents the chart, which shows the machine found the basket after tipping it.

MIRI: A successful search.

SELA: A successful inventory.

The bird lands on the pointer and nudges it toward the parts box. Miri follows the point and finds the missing axle nut inside. Odo installs it on the shelf's loose hinge. The seed basket stays in place.

SCENE 6. INT. SEED ROOM, NIGHT

Sela checks the winter stock. Every acorn is counted. The clockwork machine sits quietly on a shelf, its bell disconnected. A small monkey turns the crank once. The pointer swings toward the acorn basket.

SELA: The basket is where we put it.

MIRI: The machine remembers.

ODO: The shelf remembers too. It has a label.
