# The Parade That Went Backward

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: civic pageant epics, aerial crowd shots, heroic floats, and a parade route planned from last year's map*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
MAYOR FENN, a capuchin
LIO, a macaque float builder
PENNY, a gibbon drum major
AUNT BESS, a ring-tailed lemur crossing guard

SCENE 1. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MORNING

The annual Canopy Day parade is about to begin. A banner reads "HONORING OUR ROOTS" but hangs backward. Mayor Fenn stands on a float shaped like a giant acorn. Lio, its builder, checks the wheels. Penny taps her drum major baton against the map.

PENNY: The route goes down the avenue, around the fountain, then back to the square.

FENN: The map says turn right at the fountain.

BESS: It says left. The map is upside down.

The band begins to march. The float rolls away before the parade has started, pulled by a team of eager tortoises.

SCENE 2. EXT. AVENUE - CONTINUOUS

The parade follows the acorn float. The band plays its opening march, then notices the float is moving backward. The crowd turns around to watch it. Fenn waves to the people behind him.

FENN: The view is better from the rear.

PENNY: We are facing the wrong way.

LIO: The float has no reverse gear.

Bess raises her stop sign to halt traffic. A flock of pigeons lands on the sign and turns it into a perch. Cars stop anyway because the pigeons have claimed the intersection.

SCENE 3. EXT. FOUNTAIN CROSSING - LATER

The route map has been used as a fan and now points in three directions. Penny asks the band to stop. The float does not stop; the tortoises reach the fountain and continue along the sidewalk.

BESS: They are not in the parade.

LIO: They are pulling the parade.

FENN: Can we ask them to turn?

Bess kneels and offers lettuce. The tortoises turn toward the snack, pulling the acorn float into a reverse loop around the fountain. The band rotates with it and plays the same opening march from the other end.

PENNY: That was a perfect transition.

FENN: It was the first time anyone has said that.

SCENE 4. EXT. MARKET STREET - AFTERNOON

The float reaches the market, where vendors have set up stalls across the planned route. Lio spots a gap between two awnings and directs the float through. The giant acorn scrapes one awning, releasing a shower of paper leaves. The crowd cheers as if the float has shed a layer.

AUNT BESS: The planned road is blocked. We can cut through the schoolyard.

PENNY: The map shows a schoolyard.

BESS: That is a picture of a schoolyard. The real schoolyard is in the opposite direction.

Fenn climbs down from the float to apologize to the vendors. The crowd follows, turning the pageant into an impromptu walking tour of market stalls. Each vendor gives the band a different snack to keep the tortoises moving.

SCENE 5. EXT. SCHOOLYARD - LATE AFTERNOON

The parade enters the schoolyard through the real gate. Children hold up drawings of the float from the wrong side. Fenn gives an unscheduled speech about changing the route when the map is wrong. He thanks the tortoises, who are now asleep beside the lettuce stand.

PENNY: We need a finale.

LIO: The float's battery is dead.

BESS: Then the band can finish on foot.

The musicians line up around the acorn float and play a quiet march. Fenn walks beside it, carrying the backward banner the right way up.

SCENE 6. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - SUNSET

The parade returns by a new route. The crowd cheers at the square. Lio marks the correct path on the map, which is now covered in lettuce stains and paper leaves. Fenn suggests the route become an annual tradition. Bess points out that next year the tortoises may not be available.

SCENE 7. INT. TOWN HALL - NEXT MORNING

Fenn presents the official parade report. It lists the planned route, the actual route, and the route followed by the tortoises. Penny brings in the spoons and lays them across the map to show the market detour. Bess pins the corrected route beside the old one.

FENN: Next year we use the corrected map.

BESS: Next year you ask the crossing guard first.

PENNY: And we bring our own spoons.

The mayor approves all three notes. Lio adds a small lettuce patch beside the schoolyard gate, in case the tortoises return.

SCENE 8. EXT. SCHOOLYARD - NEXT DAY

Penny tries the official march once more, facing the correct direction this time. The band stays still to admire her improvement. The tortoises return for lettuce but leave the float where it is.

FINAL IMAGE: A tortoise wakes up, nudges the map with its nose, and slowly turns it right side up.
