# The Great Ice Cream Heist

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: caper films, precision plans, elaborate vaults, double-crosses, and a getaway van that is a frozen dessert cart*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
MEL, a macaque planner
KIP, a capuchin lock picker
NORA, a gibbon getaway driver
UNCLE DOV, a ring-tailed lemur ice cream maker

SCENE 1. INT. ICE CREAM PARLOR - NIGHT

A neighborhood festival has locked its prize tub of moonberry ice cream inside a commercial freezer. The freezer's combination panel is broken. Mel studies a chalk map of the parlor as if planning a bank robbery.

MEL: We enter through the back door, avoid the alarm, open the freezer, and leave with the goods.

DOV: It is ice cream.

MEL: That is what they want us to think.

Kip examines the lock. Nora parks a dessert cart outside the alley, its bell wrapped in cloth.

SCENE 2. INT. PARLOR KITCHEN - LATER

The crew slips through the back door. Kip picks the freezer lock, but the door opens only a few inches. A tray of popsicles blocks it from inside. Mel signals a silent countdown. Nora pulls the tray out with a serving spoon, making each popsicle squeak against the rack.

DOV: The freezer has an alarm.

KIP: That is a popsicle.

The group freezes. A patrol guard looks in, sees them holding spoons, and asks if they are the midnight tasting crew. Mel nods. The guard offers a clipboard for comments.

SCENE 3. INT. WALK-IN FREEZER - NIGHT

Inside, rows of tubs sit under blue lights. The moonberry flavor is at the very back. Kip crawls across the shelf, avoiding a strip of frost that looks like a laser grid. It is a line of freezer icing.

MEL: Step only where I step.

KIP: You stepped on the floor.

Mel tiptoes along the shelf. Nora's dessert cart bell rings outside when a gust moves the cart. The guard hears it and investigates. The crew loads the tub into a cooler bag and escapes through the delivery window.

SCENE 4. EXT. ALLEY - CONTINUOUS

Nora starts the cart. Its small motor will not turn over. Kip tries the key, but it only opens the cash drawer. Dov notices the wheels are locked by a chain. The crew pushes the cart uphill, away from the festival.

MEL: The getaway is compromised.

NORA: It is a cart.

MEL: A very important cart.

The guard steps into the alley. Mel hands him the cooler bag, pretending it is a sample. The guard opens it, sees the moonberry tub, and asks whether they have brought spoons.

SCENE 5. EXT. FESTIVAL SQUARE - LATER

The crew reaches the festival on foot. Dov admits the prize tub was meant for the winner of the neighborhood ice cream contest. The heist has stolen everyone's dessert. Mel proposes returning it and entering the contest instead.

KIP: We have already broken in.

DOV: You have already tasted it.

Mel looks at the spoon in his hand. The crew returns the tub, but the ice cream has softened. Nora puts it into cups and serves it as a shared sample.

SCENE 6. INT. FESTIVAL OFFICE - NIGHT

The guard takes the crew to the festival office for a statement. A poster on the wall lists the moonberry tub as the grand prize. The festival organizer asks whether the freezer door was damaged. Kip says it was not, but the lock is now cleaner than before.

ORGANIZER: Did you steal the prize?

MEL: We borrowed it from the freezer.

DOV: You stole it.

Nora points out that the contest judges are still waiting for the tub. The guard reviews the security footage. It shows the crew entering the parlor, then spending several minutes trying to pass the stuck popsicles. The footage makes the caper look more like a kitchen lesson.

GUARD: Why did you not just ask for a taste?

DOV: We did. The freezer was locked.

The organizer releases them on the condition that they return the remaining tub before the contest begins. The crew agrees. The guard lends them a cooler and asks to be listed as the first taster. Mel asks whether the tasting counts as a successful operation. The organizer says it counts as dessert. Kip writes that on the chalk plan and draws a spoon where the vault should be.

SCENE 7. EXT. FESTIVAL TABLE - NIGHT

The tasting contest begins. The judges sample moonberry and vote for Dov. The guard receives the first bowl. Mel folds the chalk plan and writes "dessert service" at the top. Kip returns the freezer key. Nora parks the cart beside the table.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew stands around the empty tub, each holding a spoon; Mel quietly checks the lid for a hidden compartment.
