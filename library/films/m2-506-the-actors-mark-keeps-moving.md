# The Actor's Mark Keeps Moving

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prestige one-take dramas that prize uninterrupted camera movement over actors' practical blocking*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A director insists on one continuous shot through a crowded kitchen, but each actor's carefully placed mark is moved by the staff who actually work there.

Cast: director Pavi, a macaque; actor Rulo, a gibbon; camera operator Sema, a lemur; cook Bori, an orangutan; assistant Neri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - DAY

Pavi tapes a red X on the floor. Rulo rehearses a speech while Sema tests the camera rig. Bori carries a tray through the set and steps around the mark.

PAVI: The camera never cuts.

BORI: Then it should not block the oven.

Pavi moves the X. Neri marks a second position by the counter. Bori shifts a stool to reach the sink. Pavi puts a third X on the stool.

SCENE 2 - INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - LATER

The take begins. Sema circles Rulo, who walks to the first mark. Bori opens the oven behind him. The heat makes Rulo sweat. He wipes his brow and continues the speech. Neri moves the stool so Bori can pass. Rulo steps on the rolling stool and glides out of frame.

PAVI: Keep going.

Sema follows the stool into the pantry. Bori catches it before it hits the shelves. Rulo continues speaking to a rack of pots.

SCENE 2A - INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - MOMENTS LATER

Pavi asks Rulo to walk the marks without the camera. Bori moves the stool to reach the sink, and the new mark is covered. Neri picks up the red tape and puts it on the stool, thinking it is a prop label. The stool rolls when Rulo sits. Pavi tells everyone to freeze. Bori holds the tray steady, Rulo holds his speech pose, and Neri stands on one foot with the tape in hand.

PAVI: This is the most still the kitchen has been.

BORI: The soup is boiling over.

The crew breaks the pose. Bori saves the pot. Sema marks the counter instead, then discovers the counter is on wheels for the camera track. Pavi asks if the room has any fixed surface. Bori points to the floor.

SCENE 3 - INT. PANTRY - CONTINUOUS

Pavi calls a reset. He tapes the mark to the floor, then tells Bori not to move anything. Bori asks how to reach the ingredients. Pavi says the set must remain still. Bori reaches around the camera rig and retrieves a jar. The jar blocks Sema's lens, creating a full black frame.

Sema holds the shot. Rulo continues his monologue in darkness, believing it is part of the scene. Neri turns on the pantry light and accidentally reveals Pavi standing inside the refrigerator.

SCENE 4 - INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - LATER

Pavi tries again. The camera travels from the pantry to the counter, following Rulo's dialogue. Bori moves a pot to the sink. Neri moves the stool to the other side. Sema follows the action rather than the taped marks. The take remains continuous, but the red X has rolled beneath the refrigerator.

RULO: I have reached the place where the truth is kept.

BORI: The fridge.

Rulo opens it and finds his lunch. He takes the container, then returns it when Pavi points to the script.

SCENE 4A - INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

Pavi calls for the full take. Sema rolls the camera. Rulo reaches the refrigerator and delivers his speech to the open door. Bori carries the soup past him. Neri moves the stool; this time she carries it rather than rolling it. The camera follows the steaming pot through the pantry and back to the counter. Rulo stays in frame by stepping sideways around the camera rig. He reaches his final line as Bori sets the tray down.

Pavi calls cut. The camera never lost the action, though the red X disappeared beneath the refrigerator. Sema checks the footage. It shows a real kitchen working around the performance instead of a fixed mark. The camera follows the steam, not the red tape. Rulo asks whether the next take includes lunch. Bori keeps the camera rolling. Rulo steps back onto the kitchen mark.

SCENE 5 - INT. SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The crew watches the take. It includes the rolling stool, the black frame, the jar close-up and the refrigerator discovery. Pavi says it is not the intended blocking. Bori says it is the only take where the kitchen worked. Pavi asks Rulo to repeat the speech. The actor refuses until the lunch break.

FINAL IMAGE: The cut holds on Bori making soup in one continuous shot, while the red X remains under the refrigerator.
