# The Eight-Count Emergency

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: dance competition spectacle, judge theatrics, and manufactured rivalries*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A cautious lemur choreographer enters a dance contest to win a new floor for the community center, then must keep the judges from turning her crew's practical routine into a feud-filled spectacle.

Cast: Bea, a lemur choreographer; Flip, a capuchin who adds unnecessary spins; Maru, a young gibbon with a precise count; Judge Velvet, a peacock; Host Dash, a squirrel with a headset; and the rival team, The Talon Tappers.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY CENTER, DAY

Bea watches dancers rehearse on a floor with a soft spot. She puts a bucket over the wobble and marks it with tape. The contest winner gets a floor repair grant. Flip adds a spin to the team's routine and lands in the bucket.

BEA: Eight counts. Feet on the solid boards.

FLIP: I made it nine.

MARU: You fell on the ninth.

SCENE 2. INT. TV STAGE, NIGHT

Host Dash introduces the teams as bitter enemies. Bea and the Talon Tappers are friends from the same dance class. They wave across the stage. Dash asks them to glare. They try, but both start laughing. Judge Velvet writes "low intensity" on her card.

The teams perform. Bea's crew uses a simple step pattern that shows how to avoid the weak floorboard. Flip adds a spin, but Maru catches his sleeve and redirects him into the safe space. The audience cheers. The host calls it a near-tragedy.

SCENE 3. INT. GREEN ROOM, LATER

Dash offers Bea a scripted argument with the rival team's leader. The disagreement will continue after the commercial. Bea declines. Dash asks whether they can at least pretend to dislike each other's costumes. The rival leader says she likes Bea's shoes. Bea compliments her jacket.

The producer turns off the hallway camera. The two teams share snacks. Flip tries a new spin in the green room and collides with a prop trophy. It rolls into a laundry basket.

SCENE 4. INT. TV STAGE, FINAL ROUND

The judges demand a bigger finish. Bea asks for the floor grant rules. The contest producer reads them: the winner must demonstrate safe community choreography. Bea invites the rival team to join their routine. Together they perform the eight-count pattern, marking the weak board with a bright ribbon.

Velvet says the routine lacks a dramatic rivalry. The crowd chants for the dancers to continue. Maru adds a clean turn that avoids the bad board. Flip starts a spin, sees the bucket, and stops himself. The audience applauds louder than before.

SCENE 5. EXT. COMMUNITY CENTER, DAY

The repair crew installs a new floor. Dash arrives to film a victory interview. Bea and the rival leader are carrying chairs. He asks which team truly won. They point to the floor. Dash asks who hates whom. Both point to Flip, who has just spun into a stack of mats.

Judge Velvet presents the grant. She says the dancers earned it by cooperating, though she has requested a rematch to settle the rivalry that never happened. Bea declines until the building inspector approves the stage.

The production team films a confessional interview. Flip sits beneath dramatic blue light.

FLIP: Bea and I have never agreed on the spin.

The editor asks him to repeat the line with more pain. Flip says he is mildly disappointed. The editor adds a thunder sound and cuts to Bea looking at her shoe. In the next take, Bea is asked to say she cannot trust Flip. She says she trusts him to fall into a bucket. The editor nods and marks the line for the season finale.

Before the final round, the Talon Tappers suggest trading costumes for the performance. Dash calls this a betrayal. The teams explain they are cold in the studio. The costumes fit poorly, and one feather sticks to Flip's forehead. Judge Velvet says the wardrobe has introduced a new rivalry. The dancers trade back and continue rehearsing.

Dash asks Velvet to explain the trophy. She says it is awarded for excellence, courage, and compelling television. Bea asks which one paid for the floor. Velvet checks the envelope and admits the grant came from the building fund. Dash asks for a retake with more suspense. The dancers wait silently while he reads the fine print, then point to the line that says repairs are mandatory.

SCENE 6. INT. CONTROL BOOTH, NIGHT

DASH: Need a rivalry.

BEA: We are friends.

DASH: Friends can feud.

MARU: About what?

VELVET: Footwork.

FLIP: I have extra.

BEA: You have one spin.

FLIP: Two spins.

MARU: One fall.

DASH: That's usable.

BEA: No scripted fight.

DASH: A tense look?

BEA: No.

TALON LEADER: We can share tape.

DASH: Tape isn't dramatic.

VELVET: It holds the floor.

DASH: The floor is not a character.

BEA: It gets a grant.

MARU: It gets repaired.

FLIP: It gets no spin.

DASH: Can you spin safely?

FLIP: I can stop.

BEA: Show us.

FLIP: I have stopped.

MARU: Before starting.

DASH: That is a twist.

VELVET: Not a dance.

DASH: It can be edited.

BEA: We won't fight.

TALON LEADER: We'll dance.

DASH: Together?

BEA: Together.

VELVET: That scores well.

DASH: I'll call it betrayal.

MARU: By whom?

DASH: The format.

They all stare at Dash. He writes "surprise alliance" on his cue card and calls it original.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew tests the repaired floor. They mark the old weak-board pattern in colored tape as a dance step. Flip begins a spin, stops at the tape, and does a small, sensible sidestep. The host whispers, "A stunning upset."
