# The Greenhouse Monster Needs a Name

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: creature features with a growing plant monster, skeptical caretakers, and a town that mistakes horticulture for an invasion*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: DR. MORA, an orangutan botanist; JIP, a capuchin gardener; LEN, a baboon reporter; MAYA, a macaque student; CHIEF OLL, a gibbon ranger; THE VINE, a mandrill voice from the greenhouse speaker.

INT. CITY GREENHOUSE - NIGHT

A thick vine curls around a support post. A leaf taps the glass. Dr. Mora checks the growth chart.

MORA: It grew twelve inches overnight.

JIP: It has been getting extra light.

Dr. Mora measures the vine again. A tip curls past the mark while she holds the ruler. Jip moves the plant lamp to a lower setting. The vine follows the light, dragging a small potted fern off the shelf.

MAYA: The fern was in its path.

CHIEF OLL: The exit is behind it.

Mora tells the crew not to pull the vine. They move the fern to a safe table and mark a clear route to the service door. Len films the plant from behind the railing, but the vine turns toward the warm camera light.

LEN: It is looking at us.

JIP: It is growing toward the lamp.

A bell sounds. The vine has pushed a pot onto the floor. Len enters with a camera crew.

LEN: The plant is loose in the building.

MAYA: It is still in its pot.

The vine's tendril reaches toward a watering can. Jip moves the can away. Another tendril slides across the greenhouse door and blocks it.

CHIEF OLL: Nobody leaves until we clear the exit.

THE VINE, over the greenhouse speaker: I require a larger trellis.

MORA: That is the irrigation alarm.

LEN: The plant is speaking.

ACT ONE

INT. GREENHOUSE MAIN AISLE - CONTINUOUS

Chief Oll closes the outer gate. Mora checks the irrigation control and finds a loose wire connected to the speaker. The plant's movements press the wire against a contact plate.

MORA: The vine is triggering the voice system.

JIP: It is still growing through the aisle.

MAYA: We need to move the pot.

The irrigation system starts its evening cycle. Water spatters the floor, and the vine slides a few inches across the wet tile. The crew retreats behind the marked line. Chief Oll shuts the outer door and directs everyone to the dry path.

CHIEF OLL: Do not let the root ball cross the drain.

MORA: It needs a cart and a dry route.

Jip brings the handcart from the service bay. The wheels squeak. The vine curls around the cart handle, then tightens when Jip pushes.

JIP: It has a grip on the handle.

MAYA: It is holding the cart, not the building.

The crew tries to lift it. The pot is too heavy. Len films as they strain. A leaf swings down and covers the camera lens.

LEN: It has blinded the broadcast.

MAYA: The leaf is in front of the lens.

Jip brings a handcart. They wrap the root ball in burlap and slide it onto the cart. The vine tightens around a shelf, keeping the plant from moving.

JIP: It has hold of the seed trays.

MORA: Do not pull. Cut the loose tendril at the joint.

Chief Oll uses pruning shears to free the shelf. Maya catches the seed trays before they fall. One tray contains tiny seedlings, which she carries to a safe bench.

The greenhouse speaker crackles again.

THE VINE: Trellis request denied.

LEN: It has a grievance.

MORA: The speaker repeats the last alarm phrase.

Jip traces the wire. The control box sits behind the plant. He reaches for it, but the vine blocks his arm.

ACT TWO

Maya remembers that the greenhouse has a manual irrigation shutoff beside the east door. She turns the valve. The speaker stops. The vine slackens as the water pressure drops.

MORA: The plant is not attacking us. It is overwatered and has outgrown its frame.

CHIEF OLL: We can relocate it to the tall house.

The crew rolls the cart through the wide service door. The vine loosens from the shelf. They guide it onto a new steel trellis, then secure the base with soft ties. The plant's leaves settle under the grow lights.

Len lowers the camera and retakes the opening shot with the door clear.

LEN: The monster has been moved.

MAYA: The plant has been repotted.

JIP: It still needs a name.

Dr. Mora reads the label on the pot. It says NIGHT BLOOM, DEMONSTRATION SPECIMEN. The tag is crossed out, and Maya writes GREENHOUSE VINE, LOT 12.

The greenhouse speaker crackles once as Chief Oll restores power.

THE VINE: Larger trellis received.

MORA: The irrigation alert is fixed too.

Everyone looks at the plant. One leaf falls gently onto the tag without covering the door.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew leaves through the clear aisle while the vine climbs its new trellis under steady light.
