# The Painting That Wasn't There

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: art-heist mysteries with a locked gallery, multiple suspects, and a reveal hidden in the display setup*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: NARA, a baboon curator; VICO, a gibbon guard; PELL, a macaque painter; LUX, an orangutan critic; SABLE, a capuchin conservator; MARSHAL DANE, a mandrill.

INT. CITY GALLERY - NIGHT

A spotlight falls on an empty frame. A placard reads THE BLUE HOUR, BY PELL. Guests stare at the blank wall. Nara locks the gallery doors.

LUX: The painting has been stolen.

PELL: I painted a sky, not a key.

VICO: The frame was empty when I began the shift.

SABLE: The painting was never installed.

Nara asks each guest where they stood when the lights went out. No lights went out, but the guests all remember a brief shadow crossing the wall. Vico says he saw a rectangle pass the east corridor. Pell says the canvas was still wet when they left the studio.

MARSHAL DANE: Then why is the gallery locked?

NARA: Because someone called the police before checking the storage room.

The marshal checks the lock and finds no scratches. Sable uses a lamp to inspect the empty frame. Dust remains on the wall behind it, with no clean outline where a painting would have hung.

SABLE: This frame has never held the original.

LUX: It held a print.

PELL: The print was for the catalog.

Nara turns to Vico. He admits the catalog preview had been mounted briefly, then removed before opening. The event staff may have mistaken the empty frame for the finished display.

The guests turn toward Sable.

NARA: The opening was postponed because the paint was still drying.

LUX: The catalog said "on view tonight."

MARSHAL DANE: The room was locked. Who removed it?

Vico shows the security log. No one entered after closing. A small blue brushstroke appears on the floor beneath the frame.

ACT ONE

INT. GALLERY STORAGE - NIGHT

Nara leads the group to the storage room. Several canvases are stacked against a wall. The blue stroke on the floor points toward a covered object. Pell lifts the cloth. The painting is there, still wet.

PELL: It needs another hour before hanging.

Lux circles the canvas and sees that it has no signature.

LUX: This is the painting?

PELL: This is the painting.

Sable checks the back. A label says THE BLUE HOUR, but the canvas is facing the wall.

SABLE: Someone put it here after the catalog went to print.

Nara asks Vico to replay the corridor camera. On the monitor, Vico carries a large rectangle from one room to another.

VICO: That is the backdrop.

The monitor shows a blue painted flat with a sun cutout. It passed the gallery doors at 8:15.

LUX: It looks more finished than the empty frame.

PELL: It is a backdrop for the event photo.

Marshal Dane pauses the playback. A small corner of blue canvas appears beneath the flat. The painting traveled behind the scenery, not inside it.

ACT TWO

The group returns to the gallery. The empty frame is still on the wall. Nara removes it and finds that the hooks are too small for the real canvas. Someone had measured for a print but ordered the original size.

NARA: We have a missing painting without a theft.

Vico lifts the empty frame from the wall and turns it around. A thin layer of dust covers both hooks. The frame has not held a canvas during the gallery's last two openings.

VICO: The security log was right. Nothing left this room tonight.

Nara pulls up the catalog photograph on a tablet. It shows the blue painted backdrop behind the printed image of the painting. Lux compares the brushstrokes in the photo with the wet original.

LUX: We have been looking at a reproduction of a picture of a painting.

PELL: The gallery advertised an image, not a finished canvas.

SABLE: The placard did not say that.

Marshal Dane checks the timestamp on the photo. The image was taken in the studio before the paint had dried. The backdrop had been installed for the event while the real canvas stayed in storage.

VICO: No thief. Several bad labels.

NARA: And one empty frame.

VICO: That is a relief.

LUX: It is still an exhibit failure.

Pell proposes a projection of the painting until the wall hooks are replaced. Sable checks that the image will not touch the wet canvas. Nara hangs the projection screen over the empty frame. The gallery guests return to the room.

LUX: It is a beautiful image. The paint is not ready.

PELL: That is why we wait.

Marshal Dane closes the case. Vico turns off the corridor monitor. Nara changes the placard to "Opening tomorrow, after the paint dries." The critics leave. Pell carries the painting back to the drying rack, gripping only the dry stretcher bars.

SABLE: We should put the backdrop away before it becomes the next exhibit.

VICO: It is already on camera.

FINAL IMAGE: The projection of a blue dusk glows above the empty frame while the real painting dries face up in the locked studio.
