# The Evacuation of the Weather Dome

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster spectacles that use emergency briefings, countdown graphics, and skeptical officials before the heroes ignore the obvious safe option*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: When a leak appears in a glass weather dome, the mayor's cinematic response plan delays the simple evacuation.

Cast: Etta, a macaque weather technician; Rulo, a gorilla mayor; Nix, a lemur camera reporter; and Puck, a capuchin stage manager.

SCENE 1. INT. WEATHER DOME, MORNING

Rain taps the glass roof. Etta watches a drop form above the public exhibit. She puts a bucket beneath it and calls the mayor. Rulo arrives with a press team. Puck rolls in a digital countdown clock reading 10:00.

RULO: What are we counting down to?

PUCK: The dramatic danger.

ETTA: The roof is leaking over the cloud model.

NIX: Is the public in danger?

Etta points to the exit sign. The visitors begin walking toward it. Rulo asks them to wait for the official announcement. Puck starts the clock. Nix asks Etta to explain the leak to camera. Etta demonstrates with a cup of water and a model of the roof. The drop falls into the bucket.

RULO: Could it fall somewhere else?

ETTA: Yes. So we should clear the room.

Puck places the countdown in front of the exit. Etta moves it aside. The visitors file out. Puck asks them to walk back in for a reaction shot. Etta blocks the doorway with the bucket.

ETTA: They have already evacuated.

PUCK: We need to see them understand the scale.

NIX: They understood the exit sign.

Rulo tries to deliver a line about the dome being a symbol of civic resilience. The water drop hits the microphone stand. He steps away from the puddle and lets Etta move the stand.

SCENE 2. INT. DOME CONTROL ROOM, LATER

Rulo holds a press briefing beside a map. Puck has placed red arrows spiraling around the dome. Etta tries to point to the maintenance hatch. It is covered by a sticker labeled "FINAL REVEAL."

NIX: What is the danger level?

RULO: We are monitoring conditions.

ETTA: The leak is above the exhibit and the hatch is accessible from the service hall.

Puck cues thunder through a speaker. It sounds like a dropped metal tray. Rulo continues as if it were a distant storm. Nix asks why the mayor's team has not evacuated the dome. Rulo says they need to communicate the seriousness of the event.

Etta opens the service hall door. A dry corridor leads directly outside. Rulo asks if it has been inspected. Etta says she walked through it moments ago. Puck shines a red light through the doorway to make the route look dangerous. A custodian enters carrying a mop and exits through the same hall.

SCENE 3. INT. DOME FLOOR, AFTERNOON

The countdown reaches 01:00. Puck turns it around so the camera can see the numbers. The bucket is nearly full. Etta moves it away from an electrical display. Rulo's briefing is still underway.

ETTA: We need to shut down the model and clear the remaining staff.

RULO: We cannot show panic.

NIX: No one is panicking. They are waiting for you to finish talking.

Rulo looks at the room. Two staff members hold umbrellas indoors. He tells them to leave. Puck asks whether the order should come with a dramatic siren. Etta points to the fire alarm. Nix says not to touch it unless there is a fire. Puck lowers the siren cue sheet.

A maintenance worker opens the hatch. Water runs along the outside gutter and drops through a loose seal. He tightens the latch from the service ledge. The drip stops. The dome remains closed to visitors until the seal is tested.

SCENE 4. INT. DOME LOBBY, EVENING

Rulo gives a final update. The roof is safe, the exhibit is dry, and the public can return after inspection. Puck asks whether they can say "the crisis has been contained." Etta says it was a leaky seam.

NIX: We can report what happened.

RULO: Can we have a countdown to reopening?

ETTA: The inspector is walking through the hall now.

The inspector gives a thumbs-up from the doorway. Rulo begins an elaborate handshake, but the inspector holds up a clipboard showing the repair passed. They shake hands once. Puck asks them to repeat it in slow motion. Both decline and continue to the exit. Visitors reenter calmly. Rulo stands beside the bucket, now holding a small sign that says "Roof repair complete." Puck switches off the countdown clock.

FINAL IMAGE: Etta carries the bucket to the janitor's closet while the screen's red disaster map is replaced by a plain maintenance notice.
