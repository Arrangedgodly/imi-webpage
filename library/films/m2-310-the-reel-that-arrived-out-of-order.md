# The Reel That Arrived Out of Order

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: archival restoration, shuffled reels, prestige narration, and screenings where nobody knows which scene comes next*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
ARCHIVIST NARI, an orangutan
PROJECTIONIST ODO, a baboon
RESTORER LUMA, a lemur
NARRATOR VEX, a macaque
VIEWER PIP, a capuchin

SCENE 1. INT. SMALL CINEMA - MORNING

Archivist Nari opens a metal film can marked FINAL REEL. Projectionist Odo checks the projector. Restorer Luma sets a work light beside a strip of film. Narrator Vex rehearses solemn copy.

VEX: Rediscovered after a century, the final chapter returns...

ODO: This can is labeled Scene Two.

Vex lowers his page. Nari opens a second can marked SCENE TWO. Inside is a scene of a wedding. Luma checks the edge codes; the cans were stored out of order, but the film itself is intact.

NARI: We should inspect the reels before screening.

VEX: The program says final reel first.

PIP: The program was printed yesterday.

SCENE 2. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - LATER

Odo threads the reel. Nari asks him to verify the leader and splice. Luma checks the film for damage. The first image shows a bridge at dawn. Vex calls it the opening, but the character on screen says, "We have already crossed."

VEX: A bold beginning.

NARI: It may be the ending.

Pip reads a handwritten note on the can: "Bridge scene after storm." He lays the reels in order: storm, bridge, wedding, credits. Vex asks if the wedding could be a flashback. Odo says the film has no sound yet, so they should not invent dialogue.

Luma finds a second strip spliced into the wedding reel. It contains six frames of a title card, then darkness. She examines the splice under a magnifier. The frames are not damaged; they were joined backward.

SCENE 3. INT. RESTORATION ROOM - NOON

Luma trims the bad splice. Vex asks her to preserve every frame. She keeps the title card and reattaches it in the right direction. The reel now opens on the title, then the storm.

Nari views the frames on a light table. Pip takes notes on the plot. The characters build a bridge, carry a lantern across, and arrive at a wedding. Vex asks whether the lantern symbolizes the restoration. Nari says it is a lantern.

ODO: It has a handle.

VEX: A symbol with a handle.

Odo loads the corrected reels. He accidentally threads the wedding scene before the bridge again. The projector shows a couple exchanging rings, then a shot of the bridge under floodwater. Pip asks if the bride is waiting for a repair crew. Luma stops the projector before the film runs through the gate.

SCENE 4. INT. SMALL CINEMA - AFTERNOON

The first test screening begins. Vex introduces the film as a lost masterpiece. The title card appears; the letters are inverted. Luma realizes the second splice is still reversed. She stops the projector, rotates the leader and restarts. The audience watches the title correctly, then the storm and bridge.

Pip sits near the aisle. He calls out when the wedding appears, then turns to Nari.

PIP: This is the wedding.

NARI: That is what the frames suggest.

The bridge sequence plays. The restored frames show a character carrying a lantern across. The last shot is the wedding, followed by credits. Vex removes the prepared ending narration and lets the image finish.

Pip asks whether the wedding scene was meant to come after the bridge. Nari checks the handwritten scene card against the image: the same lantern appears at the banquet, now hanging beside the door. Luma inspects the reel edge and finds a penciled arrow pointing toward the credits. It confirms the order without adding a new scene. Odo rewinds to the storm once; this time the frame count matches the can label. Vex begins to narrate the lantern as a symbol, then notices the audience is watching the picture. He folds his speech and places it under the screening notes. Odo threads a short blank leader after the credits so the projector can stop cleanly. The audience stays seated until the screen goes white, then rises together; no one asks whether another reel is missing.

SCENE 5. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - EVENING

Odo returns the reels to labeled cans. Nari writes the scene order on a new card. Luma checks the corrected splice and signs the restoration note. Vex asks if the movie has been restored to its original order. Pip points at the can marked FINAL REEL, now containing credits.

PIP: It ended.

VEX: It ended in the right place.

FINAL IMAGE: Odo closes the film can marked FINAL REEL; the lid lands on top of the can labeled SCENE TWO.
