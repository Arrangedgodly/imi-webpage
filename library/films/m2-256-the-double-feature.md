# The Double Feature

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: movie-within-a-movie thrillers, where a prop chase is mistaken for a real emergency by the projection crew*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A film crew stages a chase with a fake key, but a missing prop makes every department believe the other movie is happening.

Cast: Director Nima, macaque; actor Voss, baboon; projectionist Seli, capuchin; prop master Kito, gibbon.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY THEATER, AFTERNOON

Nima stands before a painted castle door. Voss holds a brass key.

NIMA: In this scene, you escape with the key. You do not open the door.

VOSS: Why carry a key that opens nothing?

NIMA: It is a symbol of freedom.

KITO: It is a prop key. It does open the prop door.

NIMA: Not in the film.

Seli loads the reel in the projector booth.

SELI: The first feature is twenty minutes. The second is forty.

NIMA: This scene should take five.

VOSS: I can escape in one.

Kito checks the prop table. The brass key is gone.

KITO: The key is missing.

NIMA: We have a missing key in a film about a key.

VOSS: We can use the real one.

KITO: There is no real one.

SCENE 2. INT. BACKSTAGE, MOMENTS LATER

They search the prop table. A paper crown, a rubber apple, and a wooden spoon are on the floor.

NIMA: The key may have been taken by the villain.

VOSS: I am the villain.

NIMA: Then you have taken it.

VOSS: I have not.

Kito finds a key-shaped shadow on the curtain. It is made by a coat hanger.

[SFX: HANGER CLINK.]

KITO: A decoy.

NIMA: The thief left a clue.

Seli calls from the booth. The projector has started by itself; the audience is entering.

SELI: The house is open.

NIMA: We cannot film while they watch.

VOSS: We can perform live.

NIMA: That would be theater.

VOSS: We are in a theater.

SCENE 3. INT. STAGE, CONTINUOUS

The film begins on the screen, showing Voss on a painted ship deck. Nima asks him to improvise with the wooden spoon as a key. Voss holds it up.

VOSS: The key is not metal, but it opens the way.

KITO: It opens the kitchen drawer.

Nima says to continue. Voss approaches the castle door. The spoon does not fit the lock. He pushes the door and it swings open.

VOSS: Freedom is unlocked.

NIMA: The door was not locked.

The audience applauds. Seli says the film is now running behind the live action. On screen, a second Voss appears, holding the brass key.

KITO: Where did that come from?

SELI: It is in the movie.

NIMA: The movie was filmed yesterday.

They watch the screen. The key prop had been in Voss's sleeve in the first take. Kito checks the coat from that scene; it is on a mannequin.

SCENE 4. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH, LATER

Seli rewinds the reel to the scene where Voss puts the key in his sleeve. The key is visible for one frame. Kito reaches into the coat sleeve and finds it.

KITO: It was here the whole time.

NIMA: The key has returned from the past.

VOSS: I was carrying it when I escaped.

NIMA: Then the scene worked.

VOSS: I escaped through an open door with the key in my sleeve.

KITO: That is not a heist.

NIMA: It is a double feature.

Seli projects the frame on the wall. The key appears huge, covering the whole screen.

SELI: The close-up is dramatic.

VOSS: It looks like the key is escaping from me.

SCENE 5. INT. THEATER, EVENING

The screening ends. Nima announces the next film, but the projector jams on the enlarged key frame.

[SFX: PROJECTOR CLICKS, STOPS.]

NIMA: The key has shut down the cinema.

SELI: The reel is stuck.

Kito removes the film and frees it.

Nima asks to reshoot the ending while the audience is still seated. Seli says the projector is running again, but the castle door is backstage. Voss props it against the screen. It falls over and reveals the painted backside.

VOSS: The castle has no back wall.

NIMA: It has a budget.

KITO: It has a hinge. That is enough.

They rehearse the escape once more. Voss turns the key, opens the real backstage door, and walks out. The audience laughs at the accidental stage exit. Nima asks whether they can keep the laugh in the film.

SELI: The camera is off.

NIMA: Then we need to turn it on before the next joke.

VOSS: The joke was the door.

The crew resets the door, but now it is firmly locked. The key fits. The audience waits. Voss holds up the real prop key.

VOSS: This key opens the door.

KITO: It does now.

He unlocks the backstage door. Everyone exits through it.

NIMA: We have found the ending.

SELI: The movie is over.

VOSS: The door was open.

NIMA: That is the sequel.

[SFX: PROJECTOR WHIRS, BLACKOUT.]
