# The Great Umbrella Heist

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: caper films where elaborate plans unravel over a low-value object and a team member cannot resist improvising*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A capuchin crew plans to steal a bright umbrella from a market stall, then discovers the vendor is trying to give it away.

Cast: Sela, a capuchin planner; Oru, a macaque lookout; Mina, a gibbon climber; and Bex, a baboon market vendor.

SCENE 1. EXT. MARKET SQUARE, MORNING

A striped umbrella shades an empty fruit stand. Sela sketches the stall in the dust. Oru watches the entrance. Mina measures the distance from a low branch to the awning.

SELA: We enter unseen, lift the umbrella, and leave no trace.

ORU: Why do we need the umbrella?

SELA: It is beautiful.

MINA: It belongs to Bex.

Sela points to a hand-painted sign: FREE SHADE, TAKE UMBRELLA IF YOU NEED IT.

SELA: The sign may be a trap.

Oru checks the back. It reads: PLEASE RETURN THE STAND.

SCENE 2. EXT. MARKET ALLEY, LATER

The crew rehearses. Mina swings from the branch, lands beside the stand, and lifts the umbrella. The stand tips. Oru catches it. Sela silently gestures that the operation is compromised.

MINA: The umbrella is free.

SELA: Our approach is not.

Bex arrives carrying a box of pears. The crew hides behind the stand. Bex looks directly at them.

BEX: Are you waiting for the umbrella?

SELA: We are assessing its security.

BEX: It is tied to a chair.

The team sees a thin cord looped around the handle and chair leg. Oru unties it carefully. The chair slides away, rolling down the slight slope with the umbrella attached.

ORU: The target has moved.

BEX: That is the chair.

Mina races after it and catches it before it reaches the fountain. The umbrella canopy opens in the breeze, stopping the chair like a sail.

SCENE 3. EXT. FOUNTAIN EDGE, NOON

Sela takes command and orders a clean retreat. Bex asks why they are carrying the chair. Mina points out it belongs to the stall. Oru offers to return it, but the umbrella's open canopy blocks his view. He walks in a circle and ends where he began.

ORU: I have lost the alley.

BEX: You are at the fountain.

Sela unfolds her map. It is the reverse side of a market flyer and shows only pears. Bex offers to show them how the folding shade works. Sela suspects a second test, but lets Bex demonstrate. Bex closes the umbrella, removes the cord and folds the chair flat.

MINA: That was simpler than the plan.

SELA: The plan was protecting us from surprise.

BEX: The surprise was that I do not want the umbrella.

Bex explains that the stand is being moved indoors before rain. He does not have room for the umbrella and hopes someone will use it. Sela asks if they can take it without leaving a trace. Bex points to the empty stand.

SCENE 4. EXT. MARKET SQUARE, AFTERNOON

The crew attempts to carry the umbrella as a team. Sela holds the handle, Oru supports the shaft, and Mina walks backward to guide them. The canopy catches the breeze and turns them slowly toward the fountain. Bex sets the folded chair aside and calls directions.

BEX: Left.

SELA: Our left or the umbrella's?

BEX: The direction away from the fountain.

They turn. The umbrella bumps a hanging sign, which swings and taps Sela on the head. Oru catches the sign before it falls. Mina holds the canopy steady. They lower the umbrella, fold it and secure the strap.

SELA: We have acquired the object.

BEX: You have borrowed it. Bring it back when the weather clears.

SELA: A temporary heist.

BEX: A loan.

Before they leave the square, Sela asks Bex to show them how the stand folds for storage. He removes the canvas cover and nests the legs into a shallow slot. Mina holds the umbrella upright while Oru returns the chair. Sela writes the steps on the back of her map, including the part where no one should ride the chair downhill.

SCENE 5. EXT. MARKET PATH, EVENING

The crew walks under the umbrella. It shades all four, though Mina's long neck sticks beyond the edge. Sela carries the map, Oru carries the chair, and Mina keeps the canopy angled against the breeze.

MINA: We should have planned for rain.

SELA: We did. The map has a pear on it.

Bex calls from the stall.

BEX: Bring back the chair tomorrow!

Oru turns with the chair still in his hands.

ORU: We were returning it now.

The group pivots together, umbrella and all, and heads back. Sela updates the dust diagram: "Return chair first."
