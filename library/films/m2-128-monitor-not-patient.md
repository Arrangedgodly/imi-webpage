# The Monitor Is Not the Patient

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: medical dramas that turn routine examinations into emergencies with monitor beeps, shouted diagnoses, and miraculous single-clue cures*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A clinic's routine ear examination becomes a full-screen crisis when the film crew mistakes a squeaky examination stool for a failing monitor.

Cast: Sela, a lemur clinician; Boro, a gorilla patient; Vix, a macaque director; and Puck, a capuchin sound mixer.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY CLINIC, MORNING

Boro sits on an examination stool while Sela checks his ear. A film crew records a health segment. Puck adds a rapid electronic beep to the soundtrack whenever Sela leans toward the otoscope.

SELA: Does that hurt?

BORO: No. The stool squeaks when I move.

Puck adds a louder beep. Vix asks Sela to look alarmed. Sela lowers the otoscope and looks at the stool.

SELA: The patient is comfortable.

VIX: Can you say that with more urgency?

Sela asks Boro to turn his head. The stool squeaks again. Puck layers a siren beneath it. Boro sits perfectly still to stop the sound. The monitor behind them displays a normal pulse. Vix asks Puck to make the line flatten for a beat. Puck looks at Sela, then at the monitor.

PUCK: It will still be normal.

VIX: Just for the shot.

SELA: A monitor is not a prop warning light.

Boro leans forward, and the stool squeaks again. Puck's soundboard lights up with four alert effects. He accidentally triggers a foghorn. The room goes silent except for the little chirp.

SCENE 2. INT. EXAM ROOM, LATER

Vix stages a dramatic announcement. Sela explains that Boro has mild earwax and needs a routine cleaning. Vix asks whether there is a rare condition to make the scene more suspenseful. Sela says the ear is healthy.

Puck cues the beeping. Boro looks at the monitor.

BORO: That is the chair.

Puck stops the effect and asks Boro to repeat the squeak. Boro shifts. The stool chirps. Vix wants Sela to order a scan. Sela refuses; the exam does not call for one. She shows Boro the ear diagram and explains how to clean it safely. Vix asks for a close-up of Sela's hands, then asks Puck to add a heartbeat under the instructions.

SELA: I am explaining a cotton swab.

BORO: I was told not to put one in my ear.

SELA: Correct. Use a damp cloth on the outer ear only.

Puck removes the heartbeat. Vix asks whether the warning can be delivered as if time is running out. Sela points to the appointment clock. The visit is on schedule.

VIX: Could the diagram reveal a hidden clue?

SELA: It is a diagram of an ear.

Boro points to the little label showing the ear canal. Vix moves in for a close-up. Puck adds a thunderclap when Sela places the diagram on the desk. The paper flutters off the table.

SCENE 3. INT. CLINIC HALL, AFTERNOON

The crew follows Sela as she checks the supply cart. Vix asks her to hurry because the episode needs a crisis before the break. A delivery worker has put the cleaning supplies in the wrong cupboard. Sela looks at the cart label and moves the box to the correct shelf.

VIX: Is this the emergency?

SELA: It is an inventory mistake.

The squeaky stool rolls out of the exam room. Puck follows it with the boom microphone and records the chirp. The stool reaches a wall, turns, and rolls back toward Boro. He catches it before it hits a patient chair.

BORO: We should tighten the wheel.

Sela gets a wrench from maintenance. She tightens the wheel bolt and checks that the stool rolls quietly. Vix says the repair should be performed by the doctor, on camera. Sela hands the wrench to the custodian, who is qualified to fix furniture.

SCENE 4. INT. EXAM ROOM, EVENING

Boro finishes the ear cleaning. Sela writes down aftercare instructions. Boro reads them, asks one question about showering, and gets a clear answer. Vix signals Puck for an ominous sting.

BORO: I understand.

Puck plays a bright three-note chime. Boro nods and leaves. Vix asks if that can be a cliffhanger. Sela says the only unresolved issue is the stool's warranty, which the custodian has already filed. The monitor remains steady. Puck removes the siren, thunder, and artificial beeps from the edit. Vix asks if they can keep one suspenseful sound for the credits.

Puck plays the quiet stool. It makes no noise.

VIX: We need a hook for next week.

SELA: The next patient has an appointment.

Boro thanks Sela and leaves. The custodian wheels the repaired stool back to its place. Vix asks him to do it again for the camera; he says he has already moved it.

FINAL IMAGE: The clinic's episode title reads "Routine Ear Care." The monitor shows a normal pulse while Puck mutes the dramatic soundtrack.
