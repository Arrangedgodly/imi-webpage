# Cell Block Picnic

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prison escape films, elaborate tunnel schemes, and corrections bureaucracy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: Three mischievous macaques plan a daring escape from a low-security wildlife sanctuary, only to find the gate has been left open and the real obstacle is their elaborate schedule of approved activities.

Cast: Momo, a planner capuchin; Dots, a macaque with excellent knot skills; Grub, a sleepy baboon; Warden Finch, a small but strict bird; and Sedge, a turtle who runs the garden program.

SCENE 1. INT. SANCTUARY COMMON ROOM, NIGHT

Momo draws a tunnel on a napkin. Dots ties a spoon to a shoelace. Grub snores beside a bowl of soup.

MOMO: At midnight, we go under the east wall.

DOTS: The east wall is a hedge.

Momo studies the map. The sanctuary is surrounded by hedges, a pond, and one gate. The gate is open for evening cleaning. They decide the plan must be adjusted to preserve the drama.

SCENE 2. INT. LAUNDRY ROOM, NIGHT

The group ties bedsheets together. Dots tests the knots. The sheet reaches halfway to the floor. Grub falls asleep on the pile, adding unexpected weight. The knot holds, but the bedsheets are still attached to the laundry line.

Warden Finch enters, checking uniforms. The trio hides in a laundry cart. The cart rolls down a ramp and stops at the open gate. Finch wheels it back inside, unaware of the monkeys tucked under the sheets.

SCENE 2A. INT. SANCTUARY LIBRARY, NIGHT

Momo studies old escape stories. Every plan involves a tunnel, a disguise, or a daring leap. Dots points out that all the stories end with someone asking for a snack. Grub wakes at the word snack and asks if the library has soup.

They draw a new plan on the chalkboard. It includes the open gate, a guided walk, and a picnic. Momo labels it "Operation Outside." Dots suggests "Approved Walk." Momo keeps the first title because it sounds better.

SCENE 2B. EXT. POND, MORNING

The trio tests an improvised raft made from two garden trays. It floats for three seconds, then turns over. Grub remains dry because he is sitting on the bank. He says he has survived the escape attempt.

Sedge arrives and explains the pond is home to nesting frogs. He asks them to avoid disturbing the reeds. Momo apologizes and helps move the trays. Dots uses the rope from their sheet plan to secure the garden tools instead.

SCENE 3. EXT. GARDEN, MORNING

They dig a tunnel beneath a planter. Sedge the turtle arrives for gardening class and finds them. He gives them trowels and asks them to prepare the soil for carrots. Momo complains this is not part of the escape plan.

Sedge points to the open gate. Momo looks, then says they need to finish the tunnel for the plan's integrity. They dig until they reach a garden hose. Water sprays through the hole, soaking the napkin map.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMON ROOM, AFTERNOON

The monkeys present their escape plan to the warden, insisting they deserve credit for the idea. Finch asks what they want. Momo says a picnic outside the sanctuary fence. Finch checks the rules. The gate is open, but nobody is allowed beyond the marked trail without a guide.

Sedge volunteers to lead them. They pack sandwiches, the wet map, and one loose bedsheet. Grub brings his soup bowl.

SCENE 4A. INT. WARDEN'S OFFICE, AFTERNOON

Finch reviews the picnic request. The form asks for a destination, group size, and reason for travel. Momo wrote "escape" in the reason box. Finch says this is honest but unhelpful. They amend it to "supervised outdoor lunch."

The form also asks for emergency contact. Grub writes his own name, then falls asleep before signing. Dots nudges him awake. He signs with a soup stain. Finch accepts it because the form is now legible enough.

On the walk, Finch carries the napkin map. Momo carries the approved schedule. Dots carries the sandwiches. Grub carries only his bowl and says the division of labor seems unfair.

SCENE 5. EXT. HILL OUTSIDE SANCTUARY, SUNSET

The group walks the marked trail with Sedge. They eat lunch at a scenic hill. The sanctuary fence is visible below. Momo says this feels like freedom. Dots says it feels like walking. Grub says he likes the soup.

SCENE 6. EXT. SANCTUARY GATE, EVENING

MOMO: The gate is open.

DOTS: It has been open.

GRUB: I missed the opening.

SEDGE: We took a walk.

MOMO: It was an escape.

FINCH: It was scheduled.

DOTS: We had sandwiches.

GRUB: And soup.

FINCH: You had a bowl.

GRUB: The soup was in it.

MOMO: We crossed the boundary.

SEDGE: The trail marker.

DOTS: It felt daring.

FINCH: It was painted green.

GRUB: I saw a bird.

FINCH: That was me.

GRUB: A different bird.

FINCH: Where?

GRUB: I forgot.

MOMO: We need a sequel.

FINCH: For what?

MOMO: The return.

DOTS: We are home.

SEDGE: The return is complete.

GRUB: Can we return to dinner?

FINCH: It's in the common room.

MOMO: The gate is open.

DOTS: Let's go through.

FINCH: Use the path.

GRUB: The path is longer.

SEDGE: By six steps.

GRUB: An epic journey.

They take the path. Finch closes the gate behind them, then remembers the cleaning crew needs it open.

MOMO: Open gate?
FINCH: Open gate.
DOTS: Approved walk?
SEDGE: Approved walk.
GRUB: Is lunch approved?
FINCH: It is packed.

FINAL IMAGE: Back inside, the group celebrates the escape by hanging the bedsheet in the common room as a flag. Warden Finch asks them to take it down before laundry inspection. Momo asks if the flag can be entered on the schedule. Finch checks the form and says yes, but only on Wednesdays.
