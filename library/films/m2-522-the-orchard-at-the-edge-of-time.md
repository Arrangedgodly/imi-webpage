# The Orchard at the Edge of Time

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quests with a cursed boundary, a reluctant guide, and a magic object whose rules are oddly literal*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: FEN, a macaque mapmaker; TOR, a baboon guard; ELIAN, a gibbon orchard keeper; MOSS, an orangutan scholar; THE WARDEN, a capuchin spirit.

EXT. OLD ORCHARD - DAWN

A stone wall cuts through an orchard. On one side, apples are green. On the other, they are red and ripe. Fen holds a map whose ink shifts when the sunlight moves.

TOR: The path ends at the wall.

FEN: The map says it continues.

ELIAN: The map was drawn before the orchard grew.

A bell sounds from behind the trees. Moss steps through the wall as if it were a curtain, then returns holding a red apple.

MOSS: The orchard is only open for one hour each century.

TOR: How much time remains?

MOSS: Fifty-nine minutes.

The Warden appears in a tree hollow, holding a wooden key.

WARDEN: To cross, each traveler must leave something behind.

TOR: My pack?

WARDEN: Something you carry because you fear losing it.

Fen looks at the map. Tor grips a small medal. Elian holds pruning shears.

ACT ONE

EXT. STONE WALL - MOMENTS LATER

Fen approaches the wall and offers the map. The Warden shakes their head.

WARDEN: You carry it because you do not trust the road.

FEN: The road changes.

WARDEN: So does the map.

Fen folds the map and slips it into a belt. The wall remains solid. Tor offers the medal.

TOR: I carried this through the border patrol.

WARDEN: You carry it because you want someone to ask what it means.

Tor puts the medal on a stone. The wall opens a narrow gate. Elian asks if the shears must be left behind.

WARDEN: The shears are for work.

Elian passes through first. The others follow. Inside the orchard, the trees grow in circles. Each ring bears a different season. The bell sounds again.

MOSS: The center tree has the fruit we came for.

A red apple hangs above a dry stream. Fen consults the map, but its ink now shows the orchard wall behind them. Tor suggests going straight. Elian says the roots hide a spring.

They follow the roots. The ground shifts. One path loops them back to the same apple tree. The Warden's voice comes from the branches.

WARDEN: You are following the map.

FEN: I am following the roots.

WARDEN: You are following the map that shows the roots.

The orchard's rings begin to turn. Each row rotates like a slow wheel. Moss points to the same bell hanging from three different trees. It rings from one branch, then another, then a third.

MOSS: Which bell is real?

ELIAN: The bell is real. The sound is moving through the orchard.

Tor walks toward the nearest tree, but the path folds and returns him to the wall. He tries again without the medal, keeping his eyes on the branches. The loop still sends him back.

TOR: Leaving something was not the whole test.

WARDEN: You left what you wanted others to ask about. You still want the orchard to obey you.

Tor steps aside and lets Elian lead. Elian does not use the map. They listen for the bell and follow the sound to the quietest tree, where the bell is still. The trunk has a hollow full of fallen apples.

FEN: The sound came from the other trees.

MOSS: The fruit is here.

The Warden appears at the wall, holding the wooden key. They ask Fen to leave the map. Fen hesitates, then gives it to Tor instead of putting it away. Tor lays it beside the medal, with the map folded closed. The path opens.

Fen closes the map. They look at the orchard itself. A line of fallen apples leads downhill to the spring.

ACT TWO

The group reaches the spring. The red apple floats in the water. Tor steps toward it, but Elian stops him. A second apple is reflected beneath the surface.

ELIAN: The real fruit is in the tree. The water is showing the sky.

Moss climbs the tree and reaches for the lowest branch. The bell begins to ring continuously. The orchard's boundary starts closing.

FEN: The hour is ending.

MOSS: I have it.

Moss picks one apple and climbs down. The group hurries through the gate. Tor takes the medal from the stone, but the Warden holds out a hand.

WARDEN: The rule was to leave something behind.

Tor releases it.

TOR: I do not need the story to be carried for me.

The wall closes behind them. Fen opens the map. The old route is gone; a new path appears through the orchard, drawn in a different hand. Elian takes the apple and cuts it into four pieces. They share it beside the wall.

MOSS: It tastes like every season.

FEN: And not one of them is labeled.

FINAL IMAGE: The wooden key rests on the wall while a new green shoot grows where Tor left the medal.
