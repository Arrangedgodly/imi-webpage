# The Submarine Beneath the Tea Room

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: submarine thrillers that turn routine pressure checks and tight quarters into a command crisis*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A student film crew stages a submarine emergency beneath a tea room, then has to navigate actual plumbing, a missing prop hatch and a very calm plumber.

Cast: Commander Obo, a macaque; engineer Feli, a capuchin; navigator Rook, a gibbon; director Mera, a lemur; plumber Davi, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - DAY

A cardboard submarine console sits beneath a blue sheet. Mera frames the actors through a periscope made from a paper towel tube.

MERA: We are submerged beneath the North Sea. The pressure is rising.

FELI: We are under the tea room stage. The plumbing pipe is dripping.

OBO: Report, engineer.

FELI: The sink trap needs a new washer. It is not a submarine problem.

ROOK: The map says the sea is above us.

DAVI: The map is upside down. The sink is above you.

Mera lowers the sheet and sees water pooling near the cardboard console.

SCENE 2 - INT. BACKSTAGE TEA ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Feli places a bucket under the drip. Obo gives a dramatic order.

OBO: Seal compartment three.

FELI: There is one compartment. It is a storage bin.

Rook reads the script, searching for the emergency hatch prop.

ROOK: The hatch is missing.

MERA: We can improvise with a tray.

DAVI: Do not put a tray over the pipe. I need access to the joint.

OBO: What of the crew?

DAVI: Move the cardboard away from the leak.

They lift the console. One side folds. The periscope drops into the bucket. Rook fishes it out and finds a tiny toy fish caught in the tube.

SCENE 2A - INT. TEA ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Mera asks for a fog effect. Rook turns on a kettle, and steam drifts across the blue sheet. The sheet sags onto Obo's helmet.

OBO: Visibility is zero.

FELI: We are not in the water.

MERA: The steam is too warm for the submarine scene.

DAVI: It is also coming from the tea kettle. Turn it off before it boils dry.

Rook lifts the kettle. A tag on its handle reads PROP: EMPTY. It is full of water.

ROOK: The prop is not empty.

DAVI: That is a real kettle. The prop kettle is on the shelf.

Rook places it on the counter and fetches the empty wooden prop. Mera examines the two kettles, then marks one REAL and one SET DRESSING with tape.

MERA: The submarine captain can have an empty kettle.

OBO: I will drink after we surface.

FELI: We surface when the director says cut.

SCENE 3 - INT. PROP WORKSHOP - AFTERNOON

Mera builds a hatch from a cardboard circle. Feli tests a flashlight, which flickers. Obo practices speaking into a toy microphone.

OBO: This vessel has lost power.

FELI: The flashlight needs batteries.

MERA: We need tension. The audience should believe we are isolated.

ROOK: The door is open and the plumber is ten feet away.

DAVI: I can wait outside for the take, but I need to reach the pipe if it drips again.

MERA: We can record the sound of the leak as sonar.

DAVI: It sounds like water in a bucket. It is water in a bucket.

SCENE 4 - INT. TEA ROOM - LATER

Davi replaces the washer. The leak stops. The crew resets the set with the bucket removed and the console moved away from the sink. Mera calls for a take.

MERA: Roll camera. Keep the real room out of frame.

OBO: The vessel is sealed. We await orders.

FELI: Pressure is stable. No flooding.

ROOK: The chart shows clear passage to the teapot.

The paper hatch falls off the console and lands in front of the camera.

OBO: A breach!

MERA: Cut. The hatch is too light.

DAVI: The adhesive is on the wrong side. Flip it and press the rim.

Feli secures it. Mera adjusts the camera. The actors perform the scene again, now with the hatch attached.

SCENE 5 - INT. TEA ROOM - EVENING

The crew watches the finished shot on a small screen. The cardboard submarine rocks; the pipe is dry; the actors' voices carry the scene. Davi pours tea at the counter.

MERA: We have our emergency sequence.

OBO: The crew made it through.

FELI: The plumber fixed the actual leak.

ROOK: And the map now points down to the sink.

DAVI: It points to the pipe. The sink is above it.

Mera turns the map right side up. It shows an arrow labeled SINK and another labeled SUBMARINE SET.

FINAL IMAGE: The periscope stands upright in a teapot while a paper sign beside it reads: NOT A PLUMBING TOOL.
