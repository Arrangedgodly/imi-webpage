# The Great Confetti Avalanche

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster epics that turn a predictable logistical problem into a citywide countdown and heroic evacuation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A town's parade committee expects a confetti cannon to create a triumphant finale, but a crosswind traps the paper storm above the reviewing stand.

Cast: parade marshal Odo, a macaque; weather reader Fena, a lemur; cannon operator Jass, a capuchin; mayor Lilo, an orangutan; drummer Pex, a gibbon.

SCENE 1 - EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MORNING

Odo studies a map of the parade route. A bright cannon sits beside the reviewing stand. Jass fills its hopper with paper squares. Fena checks a windsock, which points sideways toward the mayor's tent.

ODO: We fire after the final band passes.

FENA: The wind is crossing the square.

JASS: The machine is aimed straight up.

LILO: The finale must cover the whole crowd.

Pex tests a drumroll. A gust carries the practice confetti onto the mayor's hat. Lilo brushes it off and asks Odo if the cannon can be tilted away from the reviewing stand. Jass says it has only a vertical setting.

SCENE 2 - EXT. PARADE ROUTE - LATER

The first band marches past. Pex leads a line of drummers. The second band follows with bright banners. Fena watches the windsock and calls out a shift. The flags snap toward the cannon.

FENA: Hold the launch.

ODO: The route is on schedule.

JASS: The safety pin is out.

Odo asks Jass to wait one minute. Lilo insists the television crew is framing the reviewing stand. She moves her chair to center the camera and sits directly under the falling confetti path. Fena points to a painted circle on the pavement showing the cannon's drift zone.

Before firing the full hopper, Jass demonstrates the wind gauge by tying a ribbon to the cannon. The ribbon snaps toward the mayor's tent. Lilo asks whether it is a warning or part of the decor. Odo circles the landing zone on the map. Fena asks him to mark a second safe area upwind. Odo draws a much smaller circle, which barely fits the bandstand. Pex moves his drum into the circle and declares it a solo platform.

SCENE 3 - EXT. TOWN SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

Jass fires a small test burst. The confetti shoots upward, catches the crosswind and forms a colorful cloud over the mayor. The crowd cheers, thinking it is the show. The cloud does not fall. It rolls over the roof of the bandstand and lodges in the gutters.

LILO: Where is the avalanche?

FENA: Above us.

Pex taps a thunderous drumroll. A clump of paper slides from the roof and lands in the mayor's lap. The television camera zooms in. Lilo raises both hands as if welcoming a tidal wave.

The paper cloud keeps sliding along the roof. Odo sends Pex to warn the final band, but the drumline is already playing. Pex tries a hand signal; each drummer copies it as choreography. Fena walks ahead and points to the confetti caught in the gutter. The television camera follows her, leaving the mayor out of frame. Lilo steps toward the camera and slips on one paper square. She catches herself on the reviewing stand and turns the near fall into a royal wave. The crowd cheers, mistaking the maneuver for a planned salute.

SCENE 4 - EXT. BANDSTAND - MOMENTS LATER

Odo orders the parade to pause. The marching bands stop in a crooked line. The crowd begins singing to keep the event alive. Jass climbs the bandstand stairs with a long broom. Fena warns him not to pull the paper down onto the electrical cables. He tests the gutter from the side; a sheet flutters loose and lands on Pex's drum.

Pex: The storm has struck the snare.

Fena reroutes the crowd away from the cannon's drift zone. Lilo finally moves her chair. Odo and Jass remove the hopper and carry it to the ground. With the power disconnected, Jass empties the remaining confetti into paper bags for the children to scatter by hand. The wind dies as the last band plays.

SCENE 5 - EXT. TOWN SQUARE - AFTERNOON

The parade resumes without the cannon. Children toss small handfuls of paper at the finish line. The mayor walks through the drift, picking confetti from her sleeve. The television crew asks for a reaction shot. Lilo looks up at the clean sky and says the town has survived its greatest paper emergency.

ODO: The finale was better without the cannon.

JASS: The cannon can be a planter.

Pex taps one celebratory beat. A last paper square falls from his drum and lands on the mayor's hat. She gives him the drumstick.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty cannon holds a small parade flag beside the reviewing stand, while one bright square of confetti remains pinned to the mayor's hat like a medal.
