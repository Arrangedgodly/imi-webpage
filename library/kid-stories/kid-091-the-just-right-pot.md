# The Just-Right Pot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Goldilocks and the Three Bears, with the temperature tests mixed together*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Kit and Pip and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Bo saw a cup of hot tea. He did not sip. He saw a cup of ice tea. He did not sip. He saw a pot. He put the hot tea and ice tea in it. Kit, Pip, and Dot ran in. Bo had tea for all. The pot was not hot and not ice. Bo had no cup now. He had to use the lid.
