# The royal bath

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal bath ceremony gives a toy the best seat*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Pip and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had a hat for his dip. Gus had a tub. Pip sat in it. Gus put hot tea in it. The hat hid the eye of Pip. Gus got it off. Pip put it on a toy. Gus did bow to the toy. "Sir, you can go in." Pip had to get out of the tub to bow too. The toy got the dry mat.
