# The Bog Costume

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Enchanted animal identities, with a soggy pretend eel*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Bo, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Bo sat by a bog in a red cap. "I am an eel." Dot did ask him to dip in. Bo did dip. His cap got wet. "I am not an eel!" Dot got his cap out. She put a pea in it. The cap sat on the bog. The pea had a tub now. Bo had no cap. Dot let him use her dry rag. It fit him.
