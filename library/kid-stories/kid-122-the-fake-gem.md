# The fake gem

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Gem heist trades one wax imitation for another*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Max and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a wax gem. Kit had a bag. Max got to the gem on a peg. He put the wax one on the peg and got the old one. Kit put the old gem in her bag. The sun was hot. The gem was goo by ten. Max did pat the one on the peg. It was goo too.
