# The compass lunch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Expedition guide follows a compass toward its own magnetic lunch tin*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Bo and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a pin in a cup to see the way. Gus had a nut tin. The pin did aim at Gus. Bo got by an oak. The pin did aim at Gus. Gus got the tin up. Bo got to it. Gus let him get a nut. Bo put the tin on the map. Now the way led to tea.
