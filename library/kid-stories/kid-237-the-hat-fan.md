# The hat fan

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A hero cools a bun but sends it to the wrong mouth*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Bo, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Kit can fan a big red hat. Dot had a hot bun. She may not eat it yet. Kit got in to aid her. The hat put air at the bun. Kit hit it as she ran by. Bo ate it out of the air. Dot put the hat on Bo. "You can fan my new bun."
