# The nut shield

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Invincible hero armor attracts snacks*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Bo, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Kit has a nut mat to bar a foe. Bo let a pea hit it. No pea got by. Bo saw a nut to eat. Kit let him eat it. Dot ate one too. The mat had a gap now. Bo let a pea fly at Kit. She ate it to win.
