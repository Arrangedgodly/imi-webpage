# The Bare Cap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Emperor's New Clothes, with an edible hat fastener*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Max, Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a hay cap. "No oaf can see it," he did say. Pip saw it. The air got it off Max. All did cry in joy. "It is in the oak," Pip did say. Max got it. Pip put a nut on it to pin it to Max. Max ate the nut. The cap was off now.
