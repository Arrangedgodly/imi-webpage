# The red gem

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective exposes fake treasure by dipping it in tea*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Gus, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned put a red nut in a box. "It is an odd gem!" Gus did not pay. He put it in his tea. The tea got red. Gus got the nut up. The red was jam. Ned had no odd gem now. Gus got a nut. Ned got a cup of jam tea, not a bag of pay.
