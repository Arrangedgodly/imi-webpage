# The best guess

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Counting contest consumes its own contents*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Fay and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a nut jar. Max had to say how big the nut sum was. Fay let him get it. Max ate a nut. He ate two. He did add as he ate. Max got it all and won the jar. Fay put one nut in it. "Try now," she did say.
