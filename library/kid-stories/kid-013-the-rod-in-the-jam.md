# The Rod in the Jam

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Sword in the Stone, with a warm jam jar deciding succession*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Max, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

A rod was in jam. Max did say, "Who can get it may be my son." Bo dug in the jar. The rod did not go. Bo set the jar in hot tea. He got the rod out. Max got him a cap. Bo put it on the jar. His law was to let no fly get at the jam.
