# The soft landing

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A pilot's heroic landing is actually a bed bounce*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Pip put his toy jet on a bed. Bo got in. Pip hit the bed. The jet got up and hit a cot. Bo got out. "I got to a new orb!" Pip did hop on the cot too. The new orb was fun, but Bo had to set his bed for a nap.
