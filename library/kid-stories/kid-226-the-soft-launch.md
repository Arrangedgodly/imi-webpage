# The soft launch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Rocket launch interrupted by a pillow dispute*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Bo has a jet in a box. Pip got in, a pad on his lap. Pip did not let Bo get on the pad. Bo got one end. Pip got the far end. The two did tug. The box did rip. Bo got a bit of a pad. Pip got a bit too. The big war got the two a bed.
