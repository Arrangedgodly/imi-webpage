# The sleeping cargo

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heist steals a bed without removing its demanding owner*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Ned and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Ned saw a cot in a hut. He got in to get it. Kit was on it in a nap. Ned got the cot to his den. Kit got up. "Tea," she did say. Ned got tea. "A bun too." Ned got a bun. Kit had her nap. Ned had a cot, but no way to use it.
