# The Full Flight

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Flying potions, with a recipe that makes lunch*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Max, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a hex in ink. It did say to add tea, fig, and egg to a pot. Dot did it. Max did say a hex to fly. The pot did not fly. Dot ate a bit. Max ate too. "We can fly in a bit," Dot did say. Max had to sit. His hex had fed him.
