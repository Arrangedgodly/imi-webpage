# The silent waterfall

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Expedition follows a roaring waterfall that is someone's bath*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Fay, Ned and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a din in her ear by a pit. Ned had a map. The two got in. Fay got wet. Ned had a cup out. The two saw a tap on a bar. Kit sat in a tub. "My dip!" she did say. Ned put a spa on the map. Kit put a nut cup out. Fay was wet, so she had to pay.
