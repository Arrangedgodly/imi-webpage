# The Paid Tin

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic mirrors, with a cleaner charging for each answer*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Fay, Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a tin to see her eye. She did ask who had the top hat. Kit got a rag. She got the fog off the tin. Fay saw her cap. She did ask who had the top hat now. Kit put a hat on the tin. Fay saw no one. Kit got a fig to get it up.
