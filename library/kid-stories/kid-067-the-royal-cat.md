# The Royal Cat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Dick Whittington's Cat, with peace achieved through naps*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit let Bo use her cat to get a rat. Bo put the cat in his den. It had a nap. The rat had a nap by it. Now no one ate his pie. Bo had a nap too. Kit got a tin of tea for her cat. The cat did not get up. It had a job to nap.
