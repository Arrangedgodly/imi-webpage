# The rescue queue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Action hero must wait his turn to perform a rescue*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Ned, Dot and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Ned ran to aid Dot in a big den. Dot had a tag. "Get one!" Ned got one. Gus had one too. Ned had to sit in a row by Gus. At the end of the day, Ned had to go in. Dot had got out to get tea. Ned got a cup. His tag was for tea, not Dot.
