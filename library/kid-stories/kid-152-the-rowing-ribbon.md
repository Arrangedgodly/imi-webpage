# The rowing ribbon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Rowing race winner tows away the finishing ribbon*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Bo and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had to row to a red tie to win. Fay was by the end. Bo got the tie off a peg and in his paw. Bo did row and row. Fay ran by him on the sod. "You won!" she did say. Bo did not see her. He got to his den. Now the end was in his tub.
