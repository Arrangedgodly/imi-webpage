# The Crowded Ark

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Noah's Ark, with the passengers packing their own furniture*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Bo let a cat in his ark. The cat had a mat. Bo let a dog in. The dog had a bed. He let a pig in. The pig had a tub. Now Bo did not fit. He put his own mat on the top. "I can see all of you!" The pig put its tub on top too. Bo had to fit in the tub.
