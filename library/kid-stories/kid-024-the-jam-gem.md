# The Jam Gem

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Alchemy examinations, with a snack mistaken for a gem*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Fay, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had to get a gem out of a nut. She hit it too far. It ran off her mat to a jar of jam. Max saw it. "A gem!" Fay did say it was a nut in jam. Max ate it. He got Fay a gem for her art. Fay put it by the jar. Now the gem was in jam too.
