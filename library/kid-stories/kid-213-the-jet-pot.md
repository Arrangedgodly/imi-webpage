# The jet pot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A stealth spacecraft carries a noisy alarm pot*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Bo, Gus, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Bo has a jet of tin. It has no din. Max has to get it by Gus. Bo put a pot in it. "Hit it to get aid." The jet hit a log. The pot hit Max. Gus got up. Bo put a rag in the pot. Now the jet can get by Gus.
