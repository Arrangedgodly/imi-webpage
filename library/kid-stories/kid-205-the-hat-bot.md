# The hat bot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Smart invention obeys a command too literally*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Bo has a bot to tip his hat. Kit is in his hut to see it. "Tip!" The bot can hit the hat. It is in a tub now. Bo has a new hat. "Tip!" It is in the tub too. Kit has a cup of tea for the bot. "Tip!" The tea is in a hat. Bo has tea to sip but no dry hat.
