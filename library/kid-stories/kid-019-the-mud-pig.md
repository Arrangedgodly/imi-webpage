# The Mud Pig

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Animal transformation tales, with a muddy mistaken identity*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Fay, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Fay got mud on her fur. Bo saw her in a tub. "A pig is in our den!" Fay did nod. Bo got the pig a pea. Fay did not eat it. She got a rag and the mud was off. Bo saw Fay. He got her a fig. Fay ate it. Fay put mud on her fur. "The pig may ask for pie!" Bo got the rag, not the pie.
