# The Soup Orb

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Crystal ball predictions, with the ball as a pot lid*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Gus, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Gus saw Fay in a big orb. He saw a rod too. "You may sip pea tea." Fay got the orb up. It was the lid of her pot. Gus saw a pea in it. "I see my lot too." He got a cup. Fay let him sip the tea. Gus put it on his cup. Now the orb hid the pea.
