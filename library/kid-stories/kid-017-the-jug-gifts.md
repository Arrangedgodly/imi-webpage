# The Jug Gifts

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Aladdin's wishes, with a genie who mishears the order*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Kit, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Kit was in a jug. Max got her out. "Ask me for a toy." Max did ask for a car. Kit got a jar. Max did ask for a gem. Kit got jam. Max did ask for pie. Kit got pie. Max ate. Kit ate too. Max put jam in his jar. He got no car and no gem, but his jar had jam. Kit got no jug, but she got pie.
