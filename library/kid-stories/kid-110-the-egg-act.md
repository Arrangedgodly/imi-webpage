# The egg act

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Chef show produces an accidental sandwich*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Pip and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Pip did lob an egg up for a pie cup. Fay had a pan. The egg hit an oak and got on his ear. Fay got a bun to mop it up. The ref ate the bun and egg. Pip won the cup. Fay got him a pan as a hat. Pip did lob an egg. It hit the pan, not his ear.
