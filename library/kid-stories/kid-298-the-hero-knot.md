# The hero knot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A grand entrance ends with the hero tied down by the work she interrupts*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had to tie a web to a cot. Kit ran in to aid her. "I am in!" she did cry. Kit got her paw in the web. Fay did tie the web to the cot. Kit did bow. The web did tug. Kit sat. Fay had Kit in a web on the cot. "I am in," Kit did say. "I am in a web!"
