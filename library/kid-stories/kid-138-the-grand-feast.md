# The grand feast

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Formal dinner relocates to the floor and then back up*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Kit and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Kit set a big box for tea. Ned had his top bow on. Kit did a jig. A nut hit the sod. Ned sat on the rug. Kit sat too. The two ate by the box. Kit put one nut on it. Ned got up on the box to eat it. Now the box had a pal too.
