# The missing getaway

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heist driver leaves the thief behind to obey the escape plan*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Gus and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had to get a box. Max had to run the van. Gus got in the den. "Go!" he did cry. Max ran the van off. Gus was yet in the den. He ran for Max. Max ran on. Gus had to run to his den. Gus got to Max at eve. It had a bus tag in it.
