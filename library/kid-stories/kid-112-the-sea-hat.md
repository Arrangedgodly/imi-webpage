# The sea hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate captain gives his hat authority and another pirate sits on it*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Bo and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a hat to use at sea. Fay had a sea tub. Bo put his hat on and got in. The air got his hat off. Fay got it on a rod. Bo did bow to the hat. "The hat is now the big guy," he did say. Fay got the hat off the rod. She sat on it. "Now I am on top."
