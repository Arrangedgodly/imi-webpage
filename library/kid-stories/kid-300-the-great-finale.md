# The great finale

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: An oversized victory flag is repurposed as a tea decoration*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Pip got to a red orb in his jet. Dot had a red rag on a pin for him. "You won!" Pip got the pin in his paw. It was too big for the jet. Dot put it in a cup and put tea in it. Pip got the cup. His big win was a cup of tea.
