# The mop in the mud

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sword in the stone chooses a ruler who assigns floor chores*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Ned, Gus and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Ned got a rod in mud. Gus did say the guy who got it out was to be the law. Ned did tug. Gus did tug. The two hit the sod. Dot had a tub of tea. She put tea in the mud. The mud got wet. Dot got the rod out. It was a mop. Gus did bow to Dot. Dot got him the mop. "The law has a rug job now."
