# The midnight stakeout

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Western night watch mistakes a snoring deputy for an intruder*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Kit and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had to spy on a den at eve. Fay had to spy too. Kit got a low din in her ear. She hid by a log. The din got big. Kit got her net. She did lob it at the log. Fay was by it in a big nap. Kit got her up. Fay saw the net. "Did you get my nap?"
