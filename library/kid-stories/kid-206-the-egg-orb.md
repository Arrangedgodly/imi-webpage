# The egg orb

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Planet landing turns into a kitchen discovery*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot and Pip got a toy jet to a big egg. Dot set a pin on it. The pin cut a gap. A goo ran out. "It has a sea!" Pip did say. Dot put a cup at the gap. Gus ran by, a pan on his arm. It was his egg. Dot put the cup in his paw. The new sea was an egg for tea.
