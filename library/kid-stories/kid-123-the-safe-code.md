# The safe code

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Safe cracking reveals a lunch box with a ticking toy*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Fay and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Fay hid a bun in a box. Gus had a key. He put his ear to the box. "Tic, tic!" he did say. Fay did ask why a bun did tic. Gus got the lid off. A toy was by the bun. Fay had put it in. Gus ate the bun. "Yum! I win the bun and its toy."
