# The Covered Law

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal proclamations, with a pie hiding the prohibition*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Max and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a law on a tag: No pie. Dot put a pie on the tag. It hid the No. Max saw pie. He ate it. Now he saw No pie. Dot saw no pie too. She got him a new tag: No tax. Max put pie on it at six. Dot ate the pie. Now she saw No tax. She put a nut in her bag.
