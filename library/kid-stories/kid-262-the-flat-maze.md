# The flat maze

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A spy escapes a maze by lifting the entire hay model*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Ned, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Ned put a key in a web of hay. Fay had to get it. She ran by a gap and hit a bad end. Ned did nod. Fay put her paw by the hay and got it up. The key was on a mat. "I won!" Ned had to get his web up now.
