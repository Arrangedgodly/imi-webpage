# The gum trap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Supervillain trap catches the wrong object*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned put gum on a mat to get Pip. Pip saw it and ran by. Ned ran to see how he did it. His fur got in the gum. Pip got his arm. The mat got up too. The two had to hop to the tub. Ned had to ask his foe for aid.
