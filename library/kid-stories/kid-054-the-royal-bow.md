# The Royal Bow

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Elaborate royal gifts, with the gift wrapper tied up instead*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Pip and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had to tie a bow on a box. He had a rag. He did tie one end to a paw. He did tie one end to an ear. Ned saw a bow, but no box. Pip did not nod. His ear had to tug his paw. Ned cut the rag and put it in the box. "A bow kit!" Pip did nod now.
