# The bowling bow

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Bowling champion refuses to topple beautifully dressed pins*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Pip and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had to hit a pin row. Kit put a bow on a pin. Pip had an orb in his paw. He did not lob it. Kit did ask why. Pip put the orb on the sod and sat by the pin row. "It is an act now," he did say. Kit got a bow for the orb too.
