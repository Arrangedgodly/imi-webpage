# The lost gold

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate treasure map leads to an endless loop of maps*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Kit and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a map to a gem. Gus had to dig by an oak. He dug a big pit. Kit saw a box in it. Gus got the box. It had a gem and a map. Gus saw the new map. It led to the oak too. "No," he did say. "One pit is my lot."
