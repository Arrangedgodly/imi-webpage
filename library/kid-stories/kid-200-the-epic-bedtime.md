# The epic bedtime

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Bedtime ritual is treated as a campaign that defeats the storyteller*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Dot and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had to put Pip to bed. Pip did ask for an ode. Dot did say one. Pip did ask for a hum. Dot did hum one. Pip did ask for a cup of tea. Dot got one. Pip did ask for a new ode. Dot sat by his bed. She had a nap. Pip put a rug on her. He did not get his ode.
