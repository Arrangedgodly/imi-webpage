# The Midnight Dye

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Twelve Dancing Princesses, with the royal investigator joining the jig*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Max and Fay and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Max saw mud on a rug. At ten, he hid by a bed. Fay and Dot did a jig. Max put red dye by the gap. It got on Fay and Dot. Max had to tap a paw to the jig. It got red too. At six, all had red fur. Max put a rug in his den for a jig at ten.
