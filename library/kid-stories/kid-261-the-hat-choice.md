# The hat choice

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A hero wardrobe decision takes longer than the emergency*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a red hat and a tan hat. Bo ran in. "My fig is too hot!" Kit got the red hat. It did not fit. She got the tan hat. It did fit. Kit ran out to aid Bo. He had put his fig in a tub. Kit had a hat but no job.
