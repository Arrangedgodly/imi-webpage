# The Hungry Hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Enchanted clothing, with a hat that also orders lunch*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Pip, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had a hat. It did cry all the way to a pie den. Gus saw Pip at the gap. He got him a bit of pie. The hat did cry. Gus put a bit in it. It did not cry now. Pip did ask for tea. Gus got a cup for Pip and a cup for his hat. The hat got a nap.
