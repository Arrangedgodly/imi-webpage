# The Court Picnic

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse, with royal bowing spoiling tea*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Gus and Bo and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had a den in an oak. Bo got him to a big den for tea. Max hit a pot. All had to bow. Gus sat to eat. Max hit the pot. All had to bow. Gus got his fig and ran to his oak. Bo ran too. Max hit his pot. No one did bow. Max ran to the oak for tea.
