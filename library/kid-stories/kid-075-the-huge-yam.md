# The Huge Yam

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Enormous Turnip, with tea doing the last part of the work*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Pip and Dot and Bo and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had a yam in the sod. He did tug. It did not get up. Dot did tug Pip. Bo did tug Dot. Ned put tea in the sod. It got wet. The yam got up. Ned had no tea. All had a yam. He put it in the pot. Now all had yam tea. Bo had a mug of mud.
