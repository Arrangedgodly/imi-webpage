# The car lift

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A hero rescues a runaway toy car by bundling it and its passenger into a rug*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Bo sat in a car on a log. The car ran off. Kit ran to aid him. She put a rug in its way. The car got on the rug. Kit got it up. Bo was in a bag of rug now. "I am not a fig!" Kit let him out. Bo got to his hut, his car in his lap.
