# The pie hat prize

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cooking contest awards a hat prize after a flying pie lands*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Kit and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Kit put his pie up on a box for the ref to see. The air got the pie to Dot. She put it on her ear as a hat. The ref saw her. He did say Kit had won the hat cup. Dot ate the rim of her hat. Now it did not fit.
