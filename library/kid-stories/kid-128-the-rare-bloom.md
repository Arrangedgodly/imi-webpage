# The rare bloom

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Expedition for a rare bloom finds a dressed twig*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Fay, Pip and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had to get a red bud. Pip had a map. The two saw red by a log. It did bob. Pip hid. Fay ran up. It was Dot in a hat. Dot let Fay get the red bow. Fay put it on the map in ink. Pip did say he had to get a bud yet. Dot put the bow on a rod. "Try now."
