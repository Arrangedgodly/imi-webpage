# The Cow Cap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal succession contests, with a crowned cow setting the job description*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Max, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Max hid a cap. Its new pal was to get his job. Bo saw it on a cow. He let the cow eat hay. Its cap got on the sod. Bo got it. He let Max use it. "Why not you?" Max did ask. "The cow ate hay in his cap. I may eat hay for my job." Max hid the cap in his pie tin.
