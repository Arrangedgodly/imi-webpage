# The pea jet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A space expedition ends in a cup of tea*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Dot set a pea jet on a pan. Kit was the top guy. "To the far rim!" Dot hit the pan. The pea hit a cup. Kit had to get it out. It was too far in. Dot put hot tea in it. Kit ate the pea. The big run was to tea.
