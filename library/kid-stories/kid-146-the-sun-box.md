# The sun box

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space launch ends in a painted destination and a useful fan*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Gus and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had a box to get to the sun. Max had a fig to eat on it. Gus hit a peg. A fan got his fur up. The box did not go. Max put a sun on a log in ink. He sat on it to eat. Gus had a fan to use. Max had a log. The sun had his fig on it.
