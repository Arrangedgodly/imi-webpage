# The time clock

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Time loop keeps demanding dinner until the traveler eats it cold*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Kit and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a toy to go to an old day. He hit a peg. Bo saw him. "We can eat pie now," Bo did say. Kit saw his pie. He had to get it hot. He hit the peg for a new go. Bo saw him. "We can eat pie now." Kit hid the toy. He ate the pie as it was to end the day.
