# The hidden snack signal

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spy's coded message is eaten by the messenger*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Pip, Bo and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Pip put jam on a bun to get Bo a spy tag. Gus had to get the bun to Bo. He ate a nut on it. He ate two. At the den, he ate the bun. Bo saw no tag. Gus did go, "URP!" Bo put it on a pad. Pip got a tag. It did say to get Bo a new tag to eat.
