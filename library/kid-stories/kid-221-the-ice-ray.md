# The ice ray

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Freeze ray solves a picnic problem*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Kit, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned has an ice ray. He can aim it at Pip. Pip has a cup of tea. Ned let the ray hit it. Now the tea is ice! Kit is by him to eat. She put a fig by the ray. Now it is ice too. Ned had to be the big guy. Now his job is to ice tea.
