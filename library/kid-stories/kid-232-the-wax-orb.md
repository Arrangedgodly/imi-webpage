# The wax orb

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A miniature submarine claims a floating wax bar as a planet*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Pip put his wee sub in a tub. Dot put a bar of wax in its way. The sub got on the bar. Dot let the tub go dry. "We got to a new orb!" Pip did cry. Dot put a dry rug by him. His new orb was for a tub. He had to let Dot use it at his dip.
