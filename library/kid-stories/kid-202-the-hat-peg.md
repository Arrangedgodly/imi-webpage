# The hat peg

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A flying hero gets stuck but accidentally provides shade*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a red hat. Dot was too hot. Kit ran to aid her. "I can fly!" The hat got on a peg. Kit had to sit on a box to get it off. Dot sat by Kit, out of the sun. "You did aid me," Dot did say. Kit sat all day.
