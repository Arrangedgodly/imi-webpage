# The Rug Landing

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Flying carpet quests, with a teapot acting as the landing brake*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Ned and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had a rug. It did fly. He had to get it to sit. He put a net on it. The net did fly too. Bo put a pot on it. The rug sat. Ned put tea in the pot. Now his rug was a mat for tea. Bo put a tag on it: Do not fly at tea.
