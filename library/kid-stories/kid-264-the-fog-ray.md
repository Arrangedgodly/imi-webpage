# The fog ray

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A cloaking device hides its own controls*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Ned, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max has a fog ray. He hit it. Fog ran out. Ned hid in it. Ned saw no Max. Max saw no ray. "How do I get it off?" Ned had the ray in his paw. "I can hit it for a nut." Max had to pay his foe to get his own ray out.
