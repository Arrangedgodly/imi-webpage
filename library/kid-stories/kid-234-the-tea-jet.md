# The tea jet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A space traveler discovers that snacks still fall*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Bo, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Bo has a nut on a web in his toy jet. "All can fly up in air!" Gus cut the web. The nut hit Bo. Gus put a bag of tea on the jet. It got cut. Bo had tea in his fur too. His jet was for tea, not for the sky.
