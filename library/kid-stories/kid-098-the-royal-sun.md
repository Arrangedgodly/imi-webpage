# The Royal Sun

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal astronomers and grand requests, with sun mistaken for son*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Max and Dot and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Max had no sun in his den. He did ask Dot for one. Dot got Ned, his son. "A son for you!" Ned was not hot. Max put sun on a tag in ink. Dot lit a wax rod. Now Max had a sun in a tin. Ned had tea for two.
