# The big cup

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Underdog sports final won by an accidental assist*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Pip and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had to get the cup. Kit had to hit a red orb. Pip saw a fig by the net. He ran for it. Kit hit the orb. It hit the paw of Pip and got in the net. The ref let Pip win. Pip put his fig in the cup. Kit did not get a cup. He got a wet rag to rub it.
