# The Bat Bed

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical summoning lessons, with the wrong word furnishing the pet's home*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Gus and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had to get a bat by a hex. He did say bed. A bed was in the den. Bo did ask him to try bat. Gus did say bat. A bat was in the den too. It had a nap on the bed. Bo put an A on a tag. Gus had got a pet and its cot.
