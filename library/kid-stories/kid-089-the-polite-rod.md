# The Polite Rod

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical tools that obey courtesy, with a wand awaiting permission*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Fay and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a rod. "Get the cup." It did not go. "Get the pot." It did not go. Dot did ask, "May I use you?" The rod did hop. It got the cup. Fay put a bow on the rod. "May I?" The rod did bow. It got the pot. Dot put tea in it. The rod got a cup too.
