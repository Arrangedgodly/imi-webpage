# The Seven Seats

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Snow White's crowded cottage, with a log everyone prefers*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Fay, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Fay saw a hut. Dot and her kin sat in it. Dot had a cup for Fay. Fay sat on a pot. It did hum. She sat on a box. It had jam in it. She sat on a log. Dot and her kin sat on it too. Fay got up and sat on a mat. Now the log had no gap, but Fay had all the mat.
