# The Sleep Race

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Tortoise and the Hare, with a victory earned in a teacup*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Dot and Ned ran to a bed up in an oak. Dot ran. Ned ate a fig. Dot got to the bed and had a nap. Ned got up to her. Dot had all the bed. Ned did say he won, for he did not nap yet. Dot did not get up. Ned had a nap in his cup. He won a bed of his own.
