# The Owl Post

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical owl mail, with a bird asleep through teatime*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Ned, Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had an owl for his job. It had a tag for Pip: tea at six. The owl had a nap. Pip got to Ned at six. "How did you see my tag?" Ned did ask. "We eat at six day by day." The owl got up at ten. It ate the tag. Ned had to pay it in pie now.
