# The old nut pod

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Expedition fossil is the explorer's forgotten lunch*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Ned and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Ned dug up an old nut pod. Fay put it to her ear. "So old!" she did say. Ned put it in a box by a tag. Fay got the tag. It did say ONE NUT. Ned had hid his tea in the pit one day ago. Fay put the pod up in a cup. "A nut one day old is old!"
