# The Net Champion

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroic contests, with a spectator winning the tug of war*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Pip and Dot and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had one end of a net. Dot had one end. A tug of war was on. Pip did tug. Dot did tug. Bo sat in the net. Now no one won. Pip and Dot put the net by a bed. Bo did not get up. He got his nap in the net. "I won," he did say.
