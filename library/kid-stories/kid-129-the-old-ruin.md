# The old ruin

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Treasure expedition misreads a map to a tea shop*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Ned and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Ned saw ink on a bar. Kit had a pad. He did say the ink was a map to ore. Ned dug and dug. Kit saw TEA on the pad. It did not say ORE. The ink led to an inn. Ned got tea. Kit got to fix the pit. Ned let him use his cup.
