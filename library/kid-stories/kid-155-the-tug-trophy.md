# The tug trophy

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Tug of war ends when both teams obey a helpful order*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Kit, Bo and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Kit and Bo had to tug a tie. A cup was by the end. Kit got low. Bo got low. Fay saw a fig on the tie. "Let go!" she did say. Kit let go. Bo let go. Fay got the fig. The tie lay on the sod. Kit put the cup by it. The tie had won by no act at all.
