# The Ant Rescue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Lion and the Mouse, with a monkey king misunderstanding the ant's preferred reward*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Max and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Max got a paw in a net. He did tug. It did not go. Dot did tug. It did not go. An ant bit a bit of net. The net let Max go. Max put a gem by the ant. It got a nut, not the gem. Max bit the gem. "Ah. I see." Dot got him a nut.
