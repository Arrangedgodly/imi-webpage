# The red eye

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective mistakes a toy lamp for an intruder*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Gus saw a red eye in his hut. "Who is in my hut?" It did not say who it was. Pip put a rug on it. The eye got dim. Gus did ask it to go. Pip got the rug off. It was a toy sun in a jar. Gus put a hat on it. "The eye has to nap now."
