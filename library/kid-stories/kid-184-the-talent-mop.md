# The talent mop

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic act hides the snack but leaves its muddy traces visible*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Pip and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had to do an act. Fay sat by him as the ref. Pip put a nut in a cup. He put a lid on it. He hid by a mat. Pip got the nut out. Fay did not see it. Pip did bow. Fay got him a mop. "You got the nut out, but all the mud is on my rug."
