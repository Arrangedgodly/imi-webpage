# The ice star

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Skating finale becomes an accidental cleaning job*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Dot and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had to win an ice cup. Gus had to mop the ice. Dot did try to hop. Her paw hit the mop. Dot sat on it. Gus did tug it by a tie. Dot did ski by the ref on the mop. She won the cup. Gus got tea in it as his fee.
