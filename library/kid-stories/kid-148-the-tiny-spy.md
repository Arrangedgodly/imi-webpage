# The tiny spy

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spy disguise turns its wearer into a fruit seller*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Fay and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Fay put on a wig to spy on Ned. Ned had a fig bag. He put it in her lap. Fay had to get the wig up to see. Ned got to his den. Fay let all get a fig for a nut. Ned got to the wig. "Did Fay go by?" he did ask. Fay had the nut bag. "No," she did say.
