# The mutiny nap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate mutiny succeeds and stops the voyage completely*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Max and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Max ran the sea tub. Ned had to row. "No job!" Ned did say. He put a tag on the rod. Max let him be the big guy. Ned sat. Max sat too. The tub did not go. The sun got low. Ned had to row to get his tea. Max got a tag too. "No job for me!"
