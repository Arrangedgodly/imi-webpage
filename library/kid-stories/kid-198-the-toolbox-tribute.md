# The toolbox tribute

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Grand storage project builds a home for its tools instead of the intended jar*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Gus and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had to cut oak for a box. Fay had a saw and a peg. Gus cut oak. Fay had it up. Gus put a peg in it. He got the box up. Gus put the saw in it. Fay put a peg in it too. Her big jar did not fit. Gus had got a box for his saw, not her jar.
