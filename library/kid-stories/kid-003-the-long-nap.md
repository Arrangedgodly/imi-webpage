# The Long Nap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sleeping Beauty, with a pie that beats a noisy rescue*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Fay, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had to nap for an age. Ned had to end her hex. He hit a tin. Fay did not get up. He hit a pan. Fay did not get up. Ned set a pie by her lip. Fay sat up and ate. "Is the hex at an end?" Ned did ask. Fay ate a bit. "Yes. But I may nap for an age if you get me a pie."
