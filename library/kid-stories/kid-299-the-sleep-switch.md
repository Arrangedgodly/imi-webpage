# The sleep switch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A sleep ray appears effective because its target is already napping*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Bo, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had a ray to get Bo to nap. Bo lay on a cot. He had a nap. Ned hit the ray. Bo did not go. Ned hit it a lot. Bo did not go. Ned put his ear by Bo. Bo had a nap. Ned sat. He had a nap too. Bo got up and put a rug on him. The ray had won a nap for Ned.
