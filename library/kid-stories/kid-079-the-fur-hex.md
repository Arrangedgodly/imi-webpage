# The Fur Hex

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Rapunzel and overpowered hair-growing spells, with an overcorrection*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Fay and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a hex for her fur. It got to her toe. She cut it. It got to her toe. She cut it. Dot did ask if she had to say the hex to end it. Fay did say it. Now she had no fur. Dot got her a rug. Fay put it on. "I may try a new hex."
