# The tan cap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective finds a missing cap that has changed color in dye*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Gus, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max had no red cap. Gus saw a red dot on a log. He ran by it to a cup. Max had put his cap in the cup. Gus got it up. It was tan. "A new cap!" Max saw his tan dye by the cup. Gus had got the cap and a way to dye it.
