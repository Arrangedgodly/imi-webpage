# The wet score pad

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports score disappears when the scorekeeper spills tea*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Max and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Max put a dot on a pad for a hit in the net. Ned had to win. Max got the pad wet by his cup of tea. Ned saw all the ink was off. "I can win now!" he did say. Max put one dot on a nut. Ned ate it. "I win now too."
