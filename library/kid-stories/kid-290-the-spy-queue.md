# The spy queue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A secret meeting is interrupted by a very public line*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Gus, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay put a spy tag by a fig hut. Pip was to get it. Ten sat in a row. Fay saw Pip at the end. She got him a tag. Pip did not go. "No! It is my go!" Gus did cry. Fay got a fig. She put the tag in a bag and got it to Pip as he ate.
