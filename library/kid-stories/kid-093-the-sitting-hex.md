# The Sitting Hex

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Perseus and Medusa, with the gaze making everyone sit instead of turning to stone*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Max and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a hex on his eye. If he saw you, you had to sit. Pip hid by a big tin. He put it up. Max saw his own eye in it. He had to sit. Pip ran by him. Max put a rag on his eye. Now no one had to sit. Pip let Max put a paw on him. Max got to his den.
