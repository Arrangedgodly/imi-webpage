# The hero lift

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A heroic lifting feat turns the hero into a picnic table*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had a big log on his lap. Kit got it up. Ned put a cup on it. Kit had the log up. Ned put a pie by the cup. Kit did not let go. "You can eat now," he did say. Kit had to sit, the log on her lap. She was a top pal and a tea mat.
