# The Bee Adviser

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fairy-tale animal advisers, with a bee choosing lunch over government*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had a bee on her cap. She did ask how to do her job. It did hum. Ned put a pea in a pot. The bee did hum. Ned ate a bun. The bee got off. Dot put a bud on her cap. A new bee sat on it. Now Dot did not ask him how. She let it eat.
