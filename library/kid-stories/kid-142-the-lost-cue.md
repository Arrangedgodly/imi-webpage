# The lost cue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Film stunt helper catches the actor instead of performing*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Ned and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had to cry as Gus hit the sod off a log in an act. Gus got on the log. Ned got a mat for him. Gus got off. Ned got him in his paw. The guy who ran the act was mad. He had to do it. Ned did not get him. The guy sat on the mat for a nap.
