# The pit prize

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A superhero award is more useful as rescue equipment*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay was in a pit. Kit had won a big cup. She set it by the pit to aid Fay. Fay got in it. Kit got it up. Fay sat in the cup all day. "I am the top pal now!" Kit put it up on a box. Fay had to get out to go to bed.
