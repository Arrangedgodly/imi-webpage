# The lost fig

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Detective follows clues to an embarrassing culprit*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Gus, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had to get a fig. Ned saw jam on a rug. Gus got a gem to see a big dot of jam. Jam was on a cup too! Ned saw jam on the gem. Gus had cut his fig on it. The fig was in his hat. His job got him a fig.
