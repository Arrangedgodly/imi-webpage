# The sea lid jaw

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate sea monster is a lost dinner lid*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Dot and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Dot saw a big jaw in the sea. Bo hid in the tub. Dot got a rod to jab it. It did bob. Bo did cry. Dot got it up. It was a pan lid. Bo had put it out by the sea. Dot got it to him. Bo put it on his pot. Now the sea guy had to sit on a pot of tea.
