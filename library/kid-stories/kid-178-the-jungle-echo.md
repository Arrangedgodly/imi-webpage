# The jungle echo

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Expedition ruler loses an argument with an echo*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Dot and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Dot got to a pit. Max sat by an oak. "I win!" Dot did say. "I win!" the pit did say. Dot got a rod out. Max got in. "Dot is a wit," he did say. The pit did say it too. Dot let Max win the pit. He did ask it for tea. It did ask him for tea too.
