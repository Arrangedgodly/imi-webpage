# The spy pie

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret message is eaten by its recipient*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Ned, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay put a spy tag in a pie. Ned was to see it at six. He ate it at ten to six. Fay ran in to get his spy tag. Ned had no tag. Fay got him a new pie, a tag on top. It bid him get a pie. He had a pie all day.
