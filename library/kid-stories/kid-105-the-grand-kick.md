# The grand kick

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Penalty kick suspense derailed by cleanliness*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Pip and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had to hit an orb to win. Bo was in the net. Bo saw jam on the orb. He got a wet rag to rub it. Pip sat on the sod. Bo got the jam off. The ref got Bo a bib. Pip hit the orb. It got in the net. Bo got jam on the bib. It was on his paw all the day.
