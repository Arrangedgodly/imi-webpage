# The hero net

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A rescue net catches a hero's own lunch*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Dot was in a pit. Kit had a net to get her out. Kit put it by Dot. Dot got in. Kit did tug. A nut in her bag got in too. Dot ate it. Kit got her up. Dot got out. "Can I get in it?" she did say. Kit put the nut bag in a box now.
