# The decoy

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heist distraction wins a fake prize for a fake pie*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Bo, Ned and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Bo put a mud pie on a box to fox Ned. Ned got all to see it. Bo hid by an oak and ate a fig pie he had got off Kit. Ned won a cup for the mud pie. Bo did go to Ned. "It is my pie!" Ned let Bo get the cup. It was mud too.
