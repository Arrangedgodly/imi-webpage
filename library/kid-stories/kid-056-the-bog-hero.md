# The Bog Hero

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Grand bridge-building quests, with the goal brought to the hero*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Ned and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had to get tea on the far end of a bog. He did not opt to get wet. He put a log by the bog. It did not get to the far end. He put a net on it. It did not get to the far end. Bo had a new pot of tea. He put it by Ned. Ned sat to sip. Bo put pie in the net.
