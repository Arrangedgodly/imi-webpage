# The odd print

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective mistakes a hat worn on a toe for a giant footprint*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Bo, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Gus saw one big paw dot and one wee paw dot in the mud. Was a big guy out for a run? Bo sat on a log. He had one hat on his toe. Gus ran up. Bo got it off his toe. Gus saw a big dot in the mud. The big guy was a hat!
