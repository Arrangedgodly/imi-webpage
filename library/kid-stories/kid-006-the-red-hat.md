# The Red Hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Little Red Riding Hood, with the delivery eaten en route*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Kit, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a red hat and a big bag. In it was pie, jam, a cup, and a rug. Gus was to get the bag. Kit did not get it up. Gus ran to her. He ate pie. Kit ate jam. Kit and Gus lay on the rug for a nap. Kit got the bag up now. Gus got it. "I got a cup and a nap. Oh, my!"
