# The Fox Toll

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fairy-tale bridge beasts, with a costume paid off in hats*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Gus, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had fur and a red wig. "I am a fox. Pay me to get by!" Dot had no gem. She got him a cap. Gus put it on his wig. He did not see. Dot ran by. He put the cap up and saw a nut. Dot put it by him. Gus sat to eat. The fox was not so sly now.
