# The tip jar

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spy dead drop is confused with a cafe tip*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Gus, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay put a key in a jar at a tea hut. Gus was to get it. He put a nut in the jar. Fay got in. The key had a nut on it. Gus had put all his pay in a tip jar. Fay got the key and a pie. Her spy job had got pay for tea too.
