# The ice oracle

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fortune teller predicts the melting of her own crystal ball*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Fay and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had an ice orb to see a new day. Kit put a nut in her cup. Fay saw the wet orb. "A wet day," she did say. Kit got a mat to sit dry. The sun was hot. Fay had a wet rug, not an orb. Kit sat on her mat. "You got the wet day, not me!"
