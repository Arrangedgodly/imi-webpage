# The Hat Hut

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Invisibility garments, with a visible and occupied hat*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Dot, Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had a big hat. No one was to see her in it. Kit saw a paw. Dot hid it. Kit saw an ear. Dot hid it. Kit saw a dot. "Is it a bed?" He sat. Dot did cry. Kit got up. Dot got her hat off. Kit put it on a peg. It was a hut now. Dot put jam in it. Kit did not sit on the jam.
