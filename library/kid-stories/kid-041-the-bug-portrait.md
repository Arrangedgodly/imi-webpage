# The Bug Portrait

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal portraits, with a bug making the sitter pose twice*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Ned, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Ned sat for Dot to do his art. A bug sat on his ear. He hit it. Dot put an arm up in the air in her art. The bug ran to his jaw. He hit it. Dot put an arm by his jaw too. Ned saw a lot of him in the art. "Now I can hit a bug and a bug!" Dot put a bug by one arm and by one arm.
