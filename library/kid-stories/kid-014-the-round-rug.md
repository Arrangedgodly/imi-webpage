# The Round Rug

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Round Table, with a picnic seating dispute*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Kit, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a rug for her kin. All had to sit on it. Bo did not get a bit of rug. Kit put a fig in the pot by her bed. All ran to the pot. Bo had all the rug now. Kit cut the fig up. Bo got a bit too. He sat on the rug to eat it. The kin sat by him, not on the rug. Bo had all the rug now.
