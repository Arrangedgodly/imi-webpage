# The nut case

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective solves a theft by checking an unusual hiding place*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had a nut in a box. Now it was not in the box. Gus saw a dip in the lid. He put his paw to it. The nut hit his paw. It was in a gap in the lid! Dot had put the lid on it. She put her nut in a bag now.
