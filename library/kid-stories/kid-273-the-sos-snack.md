# The SOS snack

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A space distress call advertises a missing condiment*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Pip put SOS on his toy jet. Fay ran up to aid him. "Are you ill?" Pip did not nod. He had a fig and a cup. "I ask for jam." Fay got him a jam jar. Pip got SOS off the jet. Fay put it on her bag. Now she had no jam for her own fig.
