# The sad cook act

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cooking accident wins an acting prize*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Gus and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had to cut a yam. Fay sat by him. The yam hit his toe. He did cry. Fay put a mat by him. "Sit and say why you cry," she did say. Gus put the yam up. Fay did cry too. The ref saw the two. He got all a cup for the sad act. Gus did not cut any yam for tea.
