# The hero pose

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A hero speech gives a stranded friend a convenient landing platform*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Pip was up on a log. Kit ran to aid him. "I am the top guy!" Pip did ask for a web. Kit put one paw on her hip. "I am so big!" Pip got on her hat. Kit hit hay. Pip got out. "You did aid me," he did say.
