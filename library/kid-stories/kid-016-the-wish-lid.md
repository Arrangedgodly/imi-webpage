# The Wish Lid

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Fisherman and His Wife, with a practical small wish*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Ned, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Ned met an eel. It let him ask for a toy. Bo did ask him to ask for a big hut. Ned got a lid. The eel hid in the bog. Bo was sad. Ned let him see a bee in his tea. Bo put the lid on. Now the bee had a hut too. Ned got a sip of tea at six.
