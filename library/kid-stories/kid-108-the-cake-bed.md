# The cake bed

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Giant baking contest fails at serving time*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Max and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a pie as big as a bed. Ned had one as big as his paw. Max had to cut his pie for the ref. The saw got in it and did not get out. Ned got the ref his pie. Ned won. Max put a rug on his pie and had a nap. As he got up, the saw was yet in it. It had its own rug.
