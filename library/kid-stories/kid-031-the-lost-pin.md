# The Lost Pin

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroic retrieval quests, with a hat gem in a teacup*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Ned, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had no gem on his hat. Gus dug by a log. No gem. He dug by a box. No gem. Now he got tea. The gem was in his cup. It was a pin. Ned put it on his hat. Gus got the cup. It was a gem too, to his eye.
