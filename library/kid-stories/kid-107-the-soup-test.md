# The soup test

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret recipe exposed by the chef's fur*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Gus and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had a pot of pea tea. Fay had to say the mix to win a fig. She had a sip. She did not see it. A pea got off the fur of Gus. Fay saw it. She did say pea and won. Gus hid his fur in a hat.
