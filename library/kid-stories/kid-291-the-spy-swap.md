# The spy swap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret agents swap their picnic bags instead of mission equipment*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Bo, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a red bag. Bo had a tan bag. The two met by a log. Fay got the tan bag. Bo got the red bag. Fay saw no key in it. Bo saw no map in his. Her bag had tea and pie. His had pie too. No spy kit! The two sat by the log to eat.
