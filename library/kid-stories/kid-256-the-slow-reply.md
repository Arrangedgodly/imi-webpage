# The slow reply

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A spy waits for a response from a very literal gadget*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Fay, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max got Fay a spy bot. "Ask it for the key." Fay did ask. The bot put a pea in her paw. Fay did ask a lot. The bot put a pea on her wig. Max saw the pea. "It has a bad ear." Max put a key by it. The bot ate a pea. Fay got the key.
