# The tea monster

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Monster film gets tea service in place of fear*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Max and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Max hid in an oak for an act. Bo had to run as Max did cry. Max did go, "RAH!" Bo ran to him. He had tea in a cup. "Tea for a big cry," he did say. Max had a sip. Max did ask for tea in a low cry. Bo ran to get it. The act had a big run yet.
