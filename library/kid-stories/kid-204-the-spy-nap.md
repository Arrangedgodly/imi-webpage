# The spy nap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret agent disguise fools its wearer*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Fay, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had to get by Max to get a map. She put on a wig and lay in a cot. Max saw the wig and set a rug on her. "She can nap," he did say. Fay lay as if she was in a nap. At six, Max got her up for tea. The map was on a tin lid. Fay got it. She got a nap too.
