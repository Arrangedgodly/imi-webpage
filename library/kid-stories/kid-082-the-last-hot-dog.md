# The Last Hot Dog

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Three Wishes, with the wishes undoing the snack*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Fay and Pip and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Fay let Pip use a hex. He did ask for a hot dog. A hot dog was by him. Bo did ask for one on Pip. Now Pip had a hot dog on his lip. Pip did ask for no hot dog. It was not on his lip now. Bo had no hot dog. Pip had no hot dog. Fay had a hot dog of her own.
