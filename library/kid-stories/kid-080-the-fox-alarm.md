# The Fox Alarm

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Boy Who Cried Wolf, with a polite fox and a teatime alarm*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Kit did cry, "Fox!" Gus ran to see. He saw no fox. Kit did cry, "Fox!" Gus ran to see. He saw no fox. A fox sat by Kit. Kit did cry. Gus did not run. The fox put a fig by Kit. She ate it. Gus got a fig too. Kit did cry, "Tea!" Gus ran for a cup.
