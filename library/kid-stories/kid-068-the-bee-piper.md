# The Bee Piper

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Pied Piper, with one bee following the music to tea*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Ned and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had to get a bee out of the den. He did hum. The bee did hum. Ned ran out. The bee did fly out. Dot sat to sip her tea. Ned did hum to get in. The bee did hum too. Dot put a cup by the gap. "Tea for two!" Ned let the bee sip too.
