# The New Wag

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Court jesters, with the monarch accidentally getting more laughs*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Bo, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Bo was a wag in a wig. Fay had a cap. Bo put a fig up in the air. It got in her cap. All did cry in joy. Fay sat on a tin by her bed. It did hum. All did cry in joy too. Bo got her cap. Fay got his wig. Now the wag had to sit for the gig.
