# The hat in the fog

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A stealth captain mistakes his own hat for danger*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Ned and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had to lie low in his sea tub in fog. Pip had to be mum. Ned saw a rag in the fog. "Not one cry," he did say. The rag got to Ned. Ned did go, "Ah! Ah!" Pip got the rag. "The hat of Ned!" Ned hid his red ear.
