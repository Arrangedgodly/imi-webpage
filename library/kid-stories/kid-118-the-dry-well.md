# The dry well

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Frontier water crisis is a lid misunderstanding*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Pip and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Pip got to a pit to get a sip. Ned had a cup. "No tea in it," Ned did say. Pip got a rod. The rod hit a lid. Ned had put it on the pit. Pip got the lid off. Ned got a sip. "A new pit!" Ned did say. Pip put the lid on. "Now it is old."
