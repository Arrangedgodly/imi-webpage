# The Twin Mop

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical duplication spells, with the duplicate refusing the same chore*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had to mop a den. She did say a hex. A new Kit was by her. "Mop for me." "No, mop for me." Kit and Kit sat. Dot put a mop by one and a mop by one. Now Kit and Kit had to mop. Kit had a pal, but no nap. Dot put a tag on the new Kit so she saw who was who.
