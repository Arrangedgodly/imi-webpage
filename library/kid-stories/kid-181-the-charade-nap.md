# The charade nap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Charades actor falls asleep before the correct guess*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Kit and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had to act a log. She lay on the sod. Fay had to say it. "A bed?" Kit did not nod. "A rug?" Kit did not nod. She did ask Kit to get up. Kit had a nap. Fay put a rug on her. "A nap?" Fay won the cup.
