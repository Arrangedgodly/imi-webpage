# The plank game

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Snack stacking delays the walk along a pirate plank*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Max and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Max bid Dot go on a log by the sea. Dot put a nut on it. Max put a nut by it. Dot put a fig on it. Max put one too. The log did dip. Dot sat on dry sod. She got her fig off the log. Dot ate her dry fig. Max had to get his wet fig.
