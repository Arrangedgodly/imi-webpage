# The Fog Lesson

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wizard weather lessons, with the teacher joining the hidden naps*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Kit and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Bo did use a hex for fog. Kit hid in it. Gus hid in it. Bo saw no pal. He did use a hex for sun. Kit was on his cot. Gus was on his rug. Kit and Gus had a nap. Bo lay on the sod. He did use his fog hex. Bo hid his nap in the fog.
