# The slow beam

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Time slowing gadget makes a snack take forever*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Pip got a ray to fix a fig in the air. Max let a fig fly to him. Pip did use the ray. The fig was in the air. Pip had to sit to eat. Max ate and had a nap. Pip cut the ray off. The fig hit his lip. To eat, you may let a fig go.
