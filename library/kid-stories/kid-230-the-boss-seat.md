# The boss seat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space throne is revealed as a folding chair*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned sat on a tan cot at the top of a log. Dot had to bow to him. Her lip hit a cot leg. The cot did sag. Ned and his cot hit the mud. It was fun! Dot put him on the log for a nut. The guy had to pay to sit.
