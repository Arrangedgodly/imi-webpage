# The pie clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective obtains a confession by offering a replacement snack*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Gus, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had a pie. Ned had jam on his lip. "Did you eat my pie?" Ned did not say yes. Gus got a new pie. "It is for the guy who ate my old pie." Ned put his paw up. Gus put his old pie tin in it. "You can rub it." Ned got the tin, not the pie.
