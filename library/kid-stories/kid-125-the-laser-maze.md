# The laser maze

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Laser maze heist is actually a washing line*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Pip and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Pip saw a red tie in a den. Dot had to put it up. Pip had to get a gem on a box. He got low and did pad. The tie hit his ear. A wet rag hit his lip. Dot saw him. "My rag!" Pip put the gem on the box. He had to put the rag up. It was his own bib.
