# The vault job

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Disguised fig heist reaches a shop checkout*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Dot and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Dot saw a fig in a box. Pip had a map of the den. He put on a wig to fox the cop. Dot got low. She got the fig. The cop put out a cup. "One nut," he did say. Pip got his wig off. He hid his map in it. "Can I pay in fur?"
