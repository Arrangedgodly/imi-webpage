# The star queue

*Format: Children's story parody*

*Cast: Bo, Dot, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned sat at the way to a toy sun. Bo had to get by him. He got a rod of tin. Ned put up one paw. "Get a tag." Bo got one. He was two. Dot was one. She had a go on the sun toy. Bo put his rod low. A go in a row was fun. A war was not.
