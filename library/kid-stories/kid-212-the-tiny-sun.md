# The tiny sun

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Miniature science experiment becomes a bedtime lamp*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Ned, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay has a sun in a jar. Ned put his toy jet by it. The jet got hot. Fay put the jar on ice. The ice got wet. Ned put the jar by his bed. He can see his map now. At ten Fay put a lid on the jar. The sun got to nap too.
