# The Box Tower

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Tower of Babel, with builders making a toy village instead*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Ned and Pip and Dot and Bo and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had to get a hut up in the sky. Pip put a box on a box. Dot put a box in a box. Bo put a box by a box. No one saw a hut in the sky. Kit put a toy in one box. She put a mat in one box. Now all had a den for a toy. Ned put his toy on the top.
