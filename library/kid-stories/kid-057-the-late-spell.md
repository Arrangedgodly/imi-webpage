# The Late Spell

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical school spell books, with a bedtime spell during a test*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

At ten, Kit had to do a hex. Gus got her a tag to use. Kit did say the hex. Gus had a nap. Kit had a nap too. At six, Kit got up. Gus got up too. Kit put the tag by a bed. The hex was not for her try. It was for a nap.
