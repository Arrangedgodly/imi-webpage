# The Endless Cup

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Bottomless magical cups, with a tea bath ending the serving job*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Dot and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a cup. He put tea in it. He had all the tea in a sip. He did say a hex. Now tea did not end. He let Dot sip. He let Ned sip. Dot put the cup in a tub. She let tea run out. Bo sat in the tub. His tea had no end, but his job did.
