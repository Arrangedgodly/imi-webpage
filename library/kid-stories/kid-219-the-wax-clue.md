# The wax clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mystery witness is a wax figure*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had a hat he did not see. A wax toy sat on a box. Gus did ask it for aid. It did not say a bit. Gus sat by it all day. The sun got hot. The toy did bow. His hat got off it. Gus did bow to it too.
