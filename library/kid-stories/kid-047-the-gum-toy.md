# The Gum Toy

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Golden Goose, with a sticky toy sending everyone to a bath*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Gus, Pip, Dot, Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Gus put gum on a toy. Pip got a paw on it. The paw did not let go. Dot got her paw on Pip. Kit got her paw on Dot. All had to go to a tub. Gus got the gum off. Pip did ask, "Is it a fun toy?" "It got us all in one tub!" Kit hid the gum.
