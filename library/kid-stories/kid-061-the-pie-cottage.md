# The Pie Cottage

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Thumbelina meets edible fairy-tale houses, with a very small dinner guest*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Pip and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Pip was as big as a pea. Dot set a big pie by him. Pip ate a bit. He did not eat it all. He ate a gap for his den. Dot put a cup by it. "Now tea is by the den." Pip sat on a pea. "I may eat my bed too."
