# The Egg Password

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret palace passwords, with a guard who gives away the answer*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a job by a gap. All had to say egg to go in. Pip did not see how to get in. "How can I go in?" "Say egg," Bo did say. Pip did say egg. Bo let him by. Pip put a tag up. It had egg on it. All got in. Bo had to use fig.
