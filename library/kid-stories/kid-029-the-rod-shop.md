# The Rod Shop

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wizard wand selection, with a dog choosing the wand*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Kit, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit got a rod off Bo. She hit a cup. The cup did not do a jig. A dog ran in and got the rod. Bo let it go. Kit got a cup of jam. She put a pea in it. It was a gem now. She ate it. The dog had a rod. Kit had jam.
