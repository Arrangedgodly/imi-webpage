# The secret song

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A spy signal gets recruited for a dance*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Bo, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had to hum to get Bo to get a key. She hid by an oak and did hum. Bo had a pot. He hit it to her hum. Fay did hum a lot. Bo did jig. Fay got up to ask for the key. Bo put it in her paw. "We can hum and jig at tea!"
