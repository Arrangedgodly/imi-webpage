# The Tub Show

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal processions, with the ruler pushing the new passenger home*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Max, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Max sat in a tub. Gus did tug it on a rug. It got in mud and did not go. Max got out to tug. Gus sat in it. The tub did go. Gus was in the big gig now. Max ran in the mud. He put his cap on Gus. Gus now had to tug the tub to his hut.
