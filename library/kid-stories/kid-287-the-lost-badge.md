# The lost badge

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective finds his official name tag stuck to a pie*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had no tag on his bag. Dot saw a pin on a pie. Gus had set the pie by his tag. He got the pin up. The pie got up too. Dot put a cup by it. Gus got his tag off the pie. Now his tag had jam. He had to use a new tag.
