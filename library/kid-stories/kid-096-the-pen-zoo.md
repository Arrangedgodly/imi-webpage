# The Pen Zoo

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical conjuring homework, with a shopping list becoming pets to feed*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a pen for a hex. She put "fig" on a tag in ink. A fig was on her mat. She put "pie" on it. A pie was on her mat. She put "cat" on it. A cat was on her mat. It ate the pie. Kit put "dog" on it. A dog was on her mat too. Bo got the pen out of her paw. Kit had to get tea for a cat and a dog now.
