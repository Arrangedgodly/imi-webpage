# The toy bus chase

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A dramatic chase for a ride that goes nowhere*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Gus and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Gus ran to get a bus. Bo had a van. "Hop in!" he did say. The two ran to the bus. Gus got on. He sat by a tot. Bo saw it was a toy bus. It did one lap and got to Bo. "I got you to the bus," Bo did say. "Now you can get me to the den."
