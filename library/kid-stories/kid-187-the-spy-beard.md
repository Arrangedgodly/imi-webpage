# The spy beard

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spy disguise gets mistaken for laundry*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Fay and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a big wig to spy on Pip. Pip saw tan fur by the gap. He did not see Fay. He got a tub and did tug her wig. Fay got in by the wig. Pip put gel on it. Fay had to get it off. Pip got his mop. "It has a pal now!" Fay put it on the mop.
