# The Tea Pin

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Epic compass-guided journeys, with the pointer following a metal rod*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Ned, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had a pin to be his map. It did aim at a rod. Bo had it in his paw. Bo ran to a cot. Ned ran too. Bo ran to an oak. Ned ran too. Bo sat. Ned sat. Bo put the rod by a cup. Ned had tea. His pin had got him to tea at six. He had to get a map for the way to his hut.
