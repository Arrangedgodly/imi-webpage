# The oak key

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret mission discovers an ordinary welcome*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Gus, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a key to an oak hut. She hid it in her fur. Gus sat by the hut. Fay got by a log. Her key hit the mud. Gus ran out. "You can tap," he did say. Fay did tap. Gus let her in. Her spy hut had no use for a key.
