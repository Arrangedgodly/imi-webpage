# The mud bot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cleaning robot makes its own footprints*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Dot, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max has a bot to mop mud. Dot put a mop in its paw. It can mop a bit. Its fur put mud on it as it ran by. Dot got a rug for it to use. No mud! Max had to cry for joy. The bot did bow and got in the tub. Dot had to mop now.
