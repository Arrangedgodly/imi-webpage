# The tiny town

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A miniature city mistakes a rod for an invading spaceship*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Ned, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a wee hut in a pan. Ned put a rod in it. "A big jet!" Max did cry. He put a net on it. Ned got the rod up. The net hit a hut. Max had to fix it. Ned got a tea cup. "A new sea!" Max put a toy sub in it. Ned had no tea now.
