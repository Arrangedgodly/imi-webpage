# The slow race

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Racing drama rewards a sensible pit stop*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Fay and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a car. Ned had a van. One peg on his van was bad. Fay ran off to win. Ned got a new peg and put it in. Fay had no way to end her run. She hit the hay at the end. Ned won. He got Fay out of the hay. She had a nap in it as he got to his den. She did not get up to get her car.
