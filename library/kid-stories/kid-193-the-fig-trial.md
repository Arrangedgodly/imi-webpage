# The fig trial

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Courtroom evidence gets eaten before the case can be settled*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Bo, Max and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Bo did say Max had got his fig. Fay was the law. She put the fig in a pot. Bo did tap the pot. Max did tap it too. Fay had a bit to see who had it. She had a new bit. No fig was in the pot. "You can go," Fay did say. Bo and Max did ask who ate the fig.
