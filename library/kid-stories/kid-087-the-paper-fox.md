# The Paper Fox

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wizard paper-animal enchantments, with the animal succeeding as a placemat*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had to do a hex on a tag. She cut it to be a fox. Bo did ask it to run. It did not run. He did ask it to sit. It did not sit. Kit put a cup on it. "My fox has a job." Bo put an A on it. The fox was a mat for his tea.
