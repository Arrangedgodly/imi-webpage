# The dust jig

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Dance hall performer outsources his encore to a mop on a string*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Ned and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had a jig at the inn. Pip had a tin peg on his toe. Pip did a jig. Mud got off the rug. Ned had a fan by his eye. Pip did a new jig. Ned got Pip a mop as his cup. Pip put a peg on it. Ned got a tie on the mop and did tug. Pip had a nap to the din.
