# The great peak

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mountain expedition climbs a moving van roof*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Dot and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot and Gus got up a big top. Dot put a tie on a rod. Gus put up a rag. The big top was the top of a van. The van did go. The cab man got the two to the den. Dot put one nut in his paw. Gus ate his fig. "I was at the top," he did say. "Now I can sit in the van."
