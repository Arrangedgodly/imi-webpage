# The Silent Court

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal silence decrees, with the king supplying the noise*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Max and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a new law. No din in his den. Dot sat and did not hum. Max ate a fig. "Hic!" Dot put a lid on his cup. Max got a sip. "Hic!" Max cut his law up. Dot did hum. Max hit his cup on a pan. Now hic was in the gig.
