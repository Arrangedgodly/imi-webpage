# The wax villain

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Villain gets trapped by his own dramatic hairstyle*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Max and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Max put wax in his fur to be the bad guy. Dot had to run for an act. Max got a paw up. It was in the wax. Dot got oil and a rag. Max did say she had to run. Dot got the wax out. Max got his paw up at her. Dot put the rag on the sod. Dot had run off. His big bad arm was up at a rag.
