# The sun tax

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space emperor demands an absurd tribute*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Pip met Bo on a red orb. Bo had a tin hat and a tax jar. "Pay me for the sun," he bid. Pip put a pea in the jar. Bo got mad. "I am the big guy!" Pip put the jar on his hat. The pea hit his lip. Bo had to bow to get it. Pip got the sun at no fee.
