# The Pet Test

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical familiar inspections, with the hidden pet eating the grade*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had to get a pet for Bo to see. Her pet rat hid in a bag. Bo saw no rat. Kit put a pea by the bag. The pea was not by the bag now. Bo saw no rat. Kit put jam by the bag. The rat ran out. It had jam on its jaw. Bo put an A in the bag. The rat ate it too.
