# The royal pet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A space monarch mistakes a toy robot for an obedient subject*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Bo, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a bot on his red orb. "Bow!" The bot did bow. "Bow!" It did bow a lot. Bo sat on his cot. Max put a nut by the bot. The bot ate it. "It is my fan," Bo did say. Max put a nut by his own cot. The bot ran to Max. It was a nut fan.
