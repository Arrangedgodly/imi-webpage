# The sock comet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Astronomers mistake laundry for a celestial threat*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Bo, Dot, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Dot saw a red dot by her toy sun. Max put his eye to a gem. "A hot orb!" Dot hit a pot. Max put a net in its way. It got a rag. Bo ran up to get it. He had let it fly at a peg. His big orb was but a rag.
