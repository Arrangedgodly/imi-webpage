# The hat hunt

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Missing artifact turns out to be a nesting place*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Bo, Dot, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had a hat she did not see. Gus saw a bit of oak on a rug. He ran to an oak. Dot had put her hat on it. It had a nut, a fig, and fur in it. Bo lay by it. It was his tea bed. Dot got her hat. Bo had to use a pan.
