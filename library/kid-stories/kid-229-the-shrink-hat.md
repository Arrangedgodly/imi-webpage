# The shrink hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Shrinking machine affects only an accessory*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max has a box to get Kit wee. Kit got in. Max hit a red dot. Kit got out. Her hat was wee. Kit was not. Max did try to do it a lot. The hat got wee. Kit put it on a pea. Max did bow to the pea. It was a toy top guy.
