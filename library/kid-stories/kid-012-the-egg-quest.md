# The Egg Quest

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Epic treasure quests, with a hen claiming the ceremonial drum*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Pip, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had a map. Ned had a tin to hit. Pip led Ned by a log to a hen. It had an egg. Pip got it. Ned hit his tin. The hen sat in the tin. Pip saw an egg in it. Pip got an egg and Ned got an egg. Ned did not get his tin. He had to use a pot. The hen had a new bed. Pip had his tea.
