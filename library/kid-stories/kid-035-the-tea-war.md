# The Tea War

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroic shields and peace treaties, with a bee kept out of tea*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Gus, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had a tin for his job. Max had a cup. Gus did say his tin was to bar a foe. A bee got in the tea. Max put his cup on it. Gus put his tin on the pot. Now no bee got in. His tin had won a war. Max got the bee a bit of jam so it did not go to war.
