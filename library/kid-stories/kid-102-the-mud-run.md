# The mud run

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Training montage defeated by a mud puddle*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Bo and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Bo ran up an oak. Max sat in mud. At the big run, the ref put a tub at the end. Bo ran so far he did not see it. Max got up to get him. His paw hit the tub. Max won. He put mud in the cup. Bo got a mud pie.
