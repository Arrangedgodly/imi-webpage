# The safe seat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A spy refuge becomes an advertised cot with a mysterious tapping sound*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Ned, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay hid in a big box. Ned sat on its lid. Fay had to get out. She did rap. Ned did not get up. He put a rug on the lid. "A fun cot!" Fay did rap a lot. Ned got up. Fay ran out. Ned put ink on a tag. "Cot for you. It can rap."
