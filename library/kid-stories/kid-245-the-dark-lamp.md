# The dark lamp

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A light rescuer exposes a villain who has not prepared for battle*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned got his hut dim. Kit ran in, a jar of sun in her paw. No ray! Ned had to cry for joy. Kit got the cap off the jar. The hut lit up. Ned was in a rug. Kit let him use his hat. A foe has to get up to put on his hat for a war.
