# The spy scent

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: An invisible spy reveals her location by answering a question about tea*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay has a kit you may not see. She got in the hut of Pip to get a spy tag. "Is it hot tea?" Pip did ask. Fay had a cup. "Yes," she did say. "You are by my bed!" Pip put a tag by the cup. Fay got it. Her tea was bad spy kit.
