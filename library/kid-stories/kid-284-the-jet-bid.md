# The jet bid

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A space auction mistakes a friendly greeting for a bid*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned was to let a guy buy a jet. Pip ran in to see him. Pip put his paw up to say hi. "You won!" Ned put the jet by him. Pip had to pay a nut. Pip put his paw up to say bye. "A new jet!" Ned did cry. Pip hid his paw in his bag now.
