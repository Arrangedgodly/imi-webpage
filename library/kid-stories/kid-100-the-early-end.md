# The Early End

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fairy-tale endings and overpowered narrative spells, with dinner ending too early*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Bo and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a hex for end. She put it on a tag. Bo had to dig. Kit put the tag by him. He sat. Ned had to sew. Kit put the tag by him. He sat. Kit had to eat. Kit put the tag by her pie. She did not get a bit. "Not yet!" She cut the tag up. All got up. Kit got her pie.
