# The vine cafe

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Jungle crossing becomes a fruit delivery*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Gus and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Gus saw a tie by a gap. Bo got on it. He did hop to an oak. Gus did not go. Bo fed him a fig. Gus ate it. "I am to get by the gap," Gus did say. Bo fed him two. Gus ate the two too. "No," Gus did say. "I can sit by the gap and eat."
