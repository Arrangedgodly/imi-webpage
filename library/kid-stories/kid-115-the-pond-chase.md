# The pond chase

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate pursuit ends at a floating picnic*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Gus and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Gus saw Kit row off in a sea tub. Kit had his box. Gus ran to get a tub too. He got to Kit by eve. "My box!" he did say. Kit put it in his lap. It had a bun for Gus and a bun for Kit. Gus ate his bun. Kit ate her bun. "Do we row now?" Gus did ask. Kit put on her nap cap.
