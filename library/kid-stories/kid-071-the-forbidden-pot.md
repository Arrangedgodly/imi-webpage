# The Forbidden Pot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Forbidden fairy-tale chambers, with the hidden occupant giving itself away*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a pot. "Do not get its lid up." Kit did not get its lid up. She sat by it. The pot did mew. Bo ran in. "Did you see my cat?" "No. It did mew." Bo got the lid up. The cat got out. Kit put the lid on his top. "Mew for me!" Bo did mew. The cat had a nap.
