# The golf caddy

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Golf helper retrieves the ball before the player can score*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Dot and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot hit an orb to a pit. Gus had to get it for her. He got it out of the pit. Dot hit it for a new go. Gus got it for a new go. Dot did ask Gus to sit. Gus put the orb in a cup by her. "Now it is in a pit by a paw," he did say. Dot let him win.
