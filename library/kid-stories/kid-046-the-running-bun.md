# The Running Bun

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Gingerbread Man, with a bite stopping a rolling bun*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Kit, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a hot bun. It ran off a pan and by a cot. Max ran to get it. He put a tin by the cot. The bun ran in. Kit put an eye on it in jam. It had to see Max eat a bit. It did not run now. Max put jam on its new lip.
