# The space toll

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A galactic toll collector accepts an awkward form of payment*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned put a rim by a red orb. "Pay a nut to get by." Dot had no nut. She had an oak in a pot. Ned got it. Dot got by. Ned had to add dew. Dot ran up, a cup in her paw. "Can I get a nut?" Ned sat by his oak. It had no nut yet.
