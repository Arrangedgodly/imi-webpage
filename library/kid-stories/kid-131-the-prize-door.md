# The prize door

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Game show contestant hands chore prizes back to the host*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Bo and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a red box and a tan box. Bo had to get one to win a cup. Bo got red. It had a mop. He got tan. It had a wet rug. "No!" Bo did say. He put the mop and rug by the cup. "You can win it all now," he did say to Fay.
