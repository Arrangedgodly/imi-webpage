# The space seed

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A growth machine produces an inconvenient tree*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Dot, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max put a nut in his new box. He hit the red dot. An oak got up in it. Dot had to get low. The oak got Max by his hat. "Get me out!" Dot cut a bit of the box off. Max got out. His box was now a hut in an oak.
