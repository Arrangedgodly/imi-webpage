# The Flying Hut

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Castles in the clouds, with fog mistaken for flight*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Kit, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Kit saw fog by her hut. "Our hut can fly!" Fay got a cup to get dew in the sky. Kit put a net out to get fog. No fog got in it. The fog did go. The hut was in its oak. Fay put tea in the cup. Kit put a bun in the net. "We got sky tea!" Fay did say.
