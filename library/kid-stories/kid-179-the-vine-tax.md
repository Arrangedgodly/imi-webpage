# The vine tax

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Bridge helpers give the tree their fee after anchoring their ropes*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Pip and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had to get by a gap. Kit had a tie for him. Pip had a tie for Kit. The two sat. One had to tug as one got on the tie. Pip put his end on an oak. Kit got by the gap. She put her end on it too. The oak got a nut as its fee. It did not use it.
