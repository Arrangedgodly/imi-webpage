# The pea tea mix

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cooking contest becomes a dish inspection*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Pip and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had a pea pot. Max had a tea pot. Pip put oil in the tea. Max put jam in the pea pot. The ref saw the two. Pip and Max put it all in a big pot. The ref had a sip. "Odd," he did say. The two ate it all. The ref saw a dry pot. "Top job!" The ref let the two win.
