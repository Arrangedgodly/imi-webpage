# The Pea Test

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Princess and the Pea, with noisy snack testing*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

A pea was in her bed. Dot lay on a rug. Gus ate in his den. Dot did not nap. She did not see a pea. "Is a rat in my bed?" Gus got his tin. He put it by her ear. Dot sat up. "Oh, it is you!" Dot put a cap on his tin. Gus did not eat. Dot had her nap.
