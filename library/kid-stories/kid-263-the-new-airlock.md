# The new airlock

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A space gate traps a captain because he refuses to remove his hat*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Bo, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Bo has a jet in a box. Dot has to see if its gap is big. Bo got in. His big hat did not fit out. "I am the top guy!" Dot put a cup by him. "Tea is out by me." Bo got his hat off. The gap was not bad. The top guy was too big.
