# The quiz king

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Riddle expert loses the box he is sitting on*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Max and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Max had to win a cup. Pip sat by him. "No fur. A lid. You can sit on it," the ref did say. Max did not say it. Pip put a paw on the box Max sat on. Pip won. He got the box for his den. Max had to sit on the sod.
