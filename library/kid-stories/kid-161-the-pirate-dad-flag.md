# The pirate dad flag

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fearsome pirate branding accidentally invites a parent aboard*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Bo, Dot and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Bo put BAD on a rag for his sea tub. Dot got a pen. She put DAD on it. Gus saw the rag and ran to Bo. "My boy!" he did say. He put a bib on Bo and got him a bun. Dot hid her pen. Bo got the rag off. Gus put it on the bib.
