# The glow mask

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret identity is exposed by costume design*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Kit has a red hat. It can get lit. Fay got ill. Kit lit her hat to get Fay to her hut. At six Kit got to a fig hut. Her hat lit up. "Hi, Kit!" all did cry. Kit hid it in a bag. The bag lit up. So it was not a spy hat.
