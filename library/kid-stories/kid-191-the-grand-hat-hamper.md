# The grand hat hamper

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Shopping expedition uses a hat for groceries with sticky results*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Dot and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had to get a fig, a yam and a jam bun. Gus had all in his inn. Dot had no bag. She put her hat on a box. Gus put the fig, yam and bun in it. Dot put the hat on her fur. The jam ran to her ear. Gus got her a rag. She put it on as her new hat.
