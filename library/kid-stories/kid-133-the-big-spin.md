# The big spin

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Prize wheel winner risks the prize to spin again*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Kit and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a rim on a rod. He hit the rim to win a pie. It got off the rod and did zip to a pie inn. Dot ran to get it. Kit got the pie. Dot got the rim. Kit did ask for a new go. Dot put the rim on his pie. "Hit it now."
