# The Ice Hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical party outfits that do not last the dance*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Kit, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had an ice hat. Bo had a hay hat. Kit got a wet ear. Bo got an ant in his fur. At a big hop, all had to do a jig. Kit put her hat in a tub. Bo put his hat in too. The hay got wet. The ice was not a hat now. Kit and Bo did a jig. No hat got in the way.
