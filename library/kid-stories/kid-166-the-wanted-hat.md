# The wanted hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Western wanted poster identifies a hat instead of its owner*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Fay and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Fay put a hat on a peg. Kit put up a tag to get a bad guy. It had the hat on it. Fay saw the tag. She got Kit the hat. Kit put it in a pen. Fay had a new hat at the inn. Kit did nod. The old hat got a pot of tea. Fay got no tea.
