# The swapped prize pegs

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Game show prize buttons have the contestants swapped*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Dot and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had to hit a red peg to win. Bo had a tan peg. Dot hit red. The ref fed Bo a fig. Dot hit red for a new go. Bo got a new fig. Bo hit tan. The ref fed Dot a fig. Dot got Bo to hit tan for her. Bo got Dot to hit red. Now the two had tea.
