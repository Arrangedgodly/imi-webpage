# The Rescue Net

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Tower rescues, with an easier exit behind a rug*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Dot, Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Dot sat in a den up in an oak. Kit was on the sod. Dot let a net go. Kit got in it. Dot did tug him up. Kit had jam. Dot ate it. Kit saw a gap by a rug. A log led out of it to the sod. Kit got to the sod by it. Dot had the net for her jam pot.
