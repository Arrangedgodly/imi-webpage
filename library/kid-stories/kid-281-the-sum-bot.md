# The sum bot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A calculator answers a math question with a snack*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Dot, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max has a sum bot. Dot put a nut and a nut by it. "One and one?" The bot did not say two. It ate a nut. Dot did ask a lot. It ate the new nut. Max had to get the nut jar. The bot had a sum now. "No nut!" Dot had to nod.
