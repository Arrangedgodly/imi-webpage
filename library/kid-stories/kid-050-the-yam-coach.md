# The Yam Coach

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cinderella's carriage transformation, with the pumpkin becoming supper*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Dot, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Dot was to go to a hop. Fay hit a yam to get a car. It was yet a yam. She hit it to get a nag. It was yet a yam. Dot put it in a pot. It was tea now. Fay got jam for it. Dot and Fay ate. No car had to go to a hop in her own den.
