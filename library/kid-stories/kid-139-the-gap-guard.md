# The gap guard

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal guard protects one entrance while ignoring another*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Max and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Max was to be a cop by a gap. Fay did ask to go in. Max did ask for a key. Fay did not see it. She ran to a new gap in the hut. Max put a bar in it. Fay got in at the old gap. Max put his hat by it. Now his hat had to be a cop too.
