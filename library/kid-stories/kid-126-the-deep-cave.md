# The deep cave

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Expedition monster is a hungry companion*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Kit and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Kit and Max got in a pit. A low din was in it. Kit lit a log. Max hid. The din did not end. Kit saw Max had no bun. He fed Max a fig. The din did end. Kit put a bad guy on the map. Max put a fig by it. "It can eat the fig, not us."
