# The Bog Gem

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wishing on stars, with a snack seller's lamp reflection*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Dot, Pip, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot saw a gem in a bog. She did ask it for a bun. Pip did ask for jam. Gus had a jug of oil. Its ray was the gem. Gus had a bun and jam too. He let Dot and Pip eat. Dot put a nut by the jug. Now her gem got a tip.
