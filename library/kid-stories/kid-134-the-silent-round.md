# The silent round

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Silence contest prize gets eaten by the helpful loser*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Gus and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had to sit on a log to win a fig. Ned had to be mum. A fly sat on the lip of Gus. Gus did fan it off. The fly sat on Ned. Ned did go, "Ah!" Gus won the fig. The fly sat on it. Gus did not eat as the fly was on it. Ned got it off. He ate the fig for Gus.
