# The Laugh Toll

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fairy-tale bridge guardians, with a laughter toll paid by a bee*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Fay, Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Fay sat by a gap. Kit had to get her to cry in joy to go by. He put a fig on his lip. Fay did not cry in joy. He put it on his ear. She did not cry in joy. A bee sat on it. Kit got up and ran. The fig got in her cap. Fay did cry in joy. Kit ran by. The bee had no fee to pay.
