# The Royal Act

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal talent auditions, with two performers sharing one job*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Max and Ned and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Max had one job for an act. Ned did hum. Bo did a jig. Max had to pay for one. He put Ned and Bo on one rug. Ned did hum as Bo did a jig. Now it was one act. Max put a nut on the rug for pay. Ned ate a bit. Bo ate a bit. Max did a jig too. He got no nut.
