# The chase scene

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Action pursuit reverses when a snack buyer refuses to share*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Dot and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Dot ran for Fay. Fay had one bun. Dot did hop a log and get low by a van. Fay ran in an inn. Dot ran in too. Fay did nod at the row. Dot had to sit in it. The inn had new pie. Dot got two. Fay did ask for one. Dot ran off. Fay had to run now.
