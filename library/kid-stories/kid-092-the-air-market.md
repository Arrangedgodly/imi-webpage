# The Air Market

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Enchanted market bargains, with the customer selling a rival invisible product*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Max and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a jar of air. It had a tag: New air. Dot did pay a nut for it. Max got the lid up. Dot did not see her air. She put a new nut in a jar. "Nut air for you!" Max did pay a gem. Dot got her nut out. Max saw no nut air.
