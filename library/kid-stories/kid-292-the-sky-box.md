# The sky box

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A toy astronomer mistakes moving display objects for a disappearing sky*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Dot, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had a sun and an orb in her toy sky. Max put the orb in a box. "We had an orb!" He put the sun in the box too. Dot saw no sky. She put her paw in the box. It had the sun and the orb. "The sky is in my paw!" Max had to nod.
