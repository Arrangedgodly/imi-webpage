# The coin ship

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spacecraft runs out of arcade tokens*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Bo, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Bo put a fee in his toy jet. It had a hum. Fay had a sun hat. The two saw a red orb on its map. The jet was off. Bo had no fee. Fay put a nut in it. No hum. The two sat on a red rug. Now the rug was a red orb. It had no fee.
