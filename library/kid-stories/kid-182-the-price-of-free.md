# The price of free

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Game show bidders outbid one another for a free sample*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Ned, Max and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had to say the fee for a bun. "One!" he did cry. Max did cry, "Two!" Gus had the bun. Ned did cry, "Ten!" Gus put up his paw. "You do not pay for my bun." Ned hid his nut. Max hid his too. Gus ate the bun. Now no one had to pay at all.
