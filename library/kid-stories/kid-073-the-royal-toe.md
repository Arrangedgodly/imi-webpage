# The Royal Toe

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal seals, with a toe signing better than a whole paw*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Max and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Max had to put a paw in ink for a law. He put his paw on a tag. The tag was all red. The law was hid. Dot got him a new tag. Max put one toe in ink. It fit. Dot had his law. "My toe has a job," Max did say. "My paw has a nap."
