# The ice tag

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A spy message melts before the meeting*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Gus, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay cut a spy tag in ice. Gus was to get it at six. Fay put it in the sun by his hut. At six Gus got a wet mat. Fay saw no ice. "Did you get the tag?" Gus did nod. "Yes. I am to mop." He got a mop. Fay had to mop too.
