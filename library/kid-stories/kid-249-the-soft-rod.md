# The soft rod

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A chosen ruler uses a useless weapon to fetch a snack*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Pip got a rod out of a pot. Ned did bow. "You are our top guy!" Pip did try to cut a bit of oak. The rod did bow. It was gum. Pip did use it to get a fig off a peg. It hit his hat. Ned did bow a lot. A guy who can get tea is a fun guy.
