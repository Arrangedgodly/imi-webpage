# The dessert vault

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Baking show locks its own key inside the pie box*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Bo and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a pie for a cup. Kit had a key. Bo put the pie in a tin box so no one got to eat it yet. Kit hid the key in the pie. The ref did go to try it. Bo had no key. Kit had no pie. The ref put his cup in a box too. "Try to get my cup now!"
