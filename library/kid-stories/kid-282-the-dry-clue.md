# The dry clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective finds the stolen jam jar after the culprit washes off the evidence*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Bo, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had to see who ate his jam. Bo had jam on his paw. Gus ran to get his map. Bo put his paw in a tub. No jam! Gus saw no jam on Bo. But Gus saw his jam jar in the tub. "You can rub my jar now." Bo got a rag.
