# The bread timer

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cooking contestant saves the timer rather than the food*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Ned and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had a bun in a hot tin. Dot had a toy to go tic. Ned had to get the bun out as it did tic. The toy did go tic. Ned got it out of the sun. He put it in an ice box. Dot did ask why. "I got the tic toy out," he did say. The bun was now tar.
