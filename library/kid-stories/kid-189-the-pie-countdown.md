# The pie countdown

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Action countdown disables a lunch heater*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Bo and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Bo saw a box go tic. Max hid by a log. Bo had to hit a red peg to aid Max. He hit it. The box did not tic. Max got out. He got the lid off. It had an icy pie. It was a pie box, not a bad box. Bo did bow. Max ate icy pie.
