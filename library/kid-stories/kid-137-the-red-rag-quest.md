# The red rag quest

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Grand detective hunt finds the missing cloth being worn*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Dot and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had no red rag. Bo ran to aid her. The two dug in a box and in the jam pot. Bo put the box and pot on a map. Dot sat for a nap. Bo saw the rag on her leg. He put DOT on the map. Dot put BO by it. "I got you too!"
