# The Small Giant

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Jack and the Beanstalk, with a giant who needs smaller furniture*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Pip, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Pip put a pea in a pot. It got big. Pip got up to a den in the sky. Max sat on a bed as big as a hut. "I am a big guy," Max did say. Pip saw he was not big at all. He got a box for Max. Max sat in it. Pip got the big bed. Now he had a hut in the sky.
