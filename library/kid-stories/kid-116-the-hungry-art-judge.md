# The hungry art judge

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Western drawing duel is judged by a referee who expects a real pie*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Fay, Bo and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

At ten Fay met Bo by an oak. Bo had a pen. Fay had one too. "I can win," Bo did say. He put an oak on his pad in ink. Fay put a pie on her pad. Gus saw the art. He put an X by the pie. "No pie for me? So no cup for you!"
