# The echo foe

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space warrior battles his own echo*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Bo, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a rod in a den on a red orb. "Who is in the den?" he did cry. "Den!" Bo bid the foe go. The foe bid him go too. Ned put a rug up in the den. No foe! Bo put a nut on the rug as pay for its big act.
