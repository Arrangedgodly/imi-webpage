# The hero cape

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Flying hero gives away the cape and must borrow it back*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Pip and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had a big rag to fly in. Kit did cry for aid in an icy den. Pip got off a box. His rag got on a peg. Kit got him off. Pip got her the rag to use in the den. Kit got him a fig. Pip ran to his den. He was so icy he had to ask Kit for his rag.
