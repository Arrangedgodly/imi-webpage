# The Vanishing Grade

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wizard school invisible ink, with the pupils choosing their own grades*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Fay and Kit and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a pen. No one saw its ink. She put an A on a tag for Kit. Kit did not see it. Bo saw no ink. Kit did say she had an A. Bo did say he had an A too. Fay got a new pen. It had red ink. Kit and Bo had a new try. Now Bo had a B. Kit had a C. Bo did ask for the old pen.
