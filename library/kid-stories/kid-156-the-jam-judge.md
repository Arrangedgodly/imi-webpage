# The jam judge

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cooking judge cannot reach a secret sample under a sticky lid*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Fay and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a jam pot to win a cup. Gus had to try it. He got a paw on the lid. It did not go up. Fay got a paw on it too. The jam did not let go. Gus did say it was top jam. He had not had a bit. "It can get a ref to sit by it all day!"
