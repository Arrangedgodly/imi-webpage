# The new law

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sheriff violates his own mud rule*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Kit and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Kit was the law at the inn. "No mud on the rug!" he did say. Fay saw mud on his toe. Kit put a paw on the rug to let her see his pin. Fay hit a tin pot. Kit had to rub the rug. He got his pin off. He put it on the mop. Now it was the law.
