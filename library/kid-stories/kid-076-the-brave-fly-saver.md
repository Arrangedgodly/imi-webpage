# The Brave Fly Saver

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Brave Little Tailor, with a heroic reputation earned from one fly*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had to sew a cap. A fly sat in his jam. Bo got a rod to get it out. He put it on a rag. It did not fly. Bo got a fan. It did fly. Max saw a tag on Bo: I won. Max let Bo sit by his gap. Bo put jam by it. A fly was in his new den. "I may win all day!"
