# The Name Tag

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Rumpelstiltskin, with the secret name worn on a hat*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Fay and Gus, monkeys; Kit and Max mentioned as guesses*

## Story

A sly imp had a gem. Fay had to say who he was to get it. She did try Kit. The imp did say no. She did try Max. The imp did say no. He did bow. His hat got off. It had Gus on it in ink. Fay did say Gus. She got the gem. Gus hid his hat in a bag.
