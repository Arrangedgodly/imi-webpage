# The spy ray

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A laser security system accidentally becomes a limbo game*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Gus, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had to get by a red ray. Gus had a box to get it low. Fay got low. The ray got low too. Fay got too low to get up. Gus got it low too. Fay lay on a mat. The ray did too. Fay sat up. "You try!" Gus got low. Fay hit the red dot on his box. She got by Gus.
