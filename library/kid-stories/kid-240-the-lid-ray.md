# The lid ray

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: An opening ray spills ink and accidentally summons a tea guest*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max has a ray to get up a lid. Gus did use it on an ink jar. The ink hit his fur. Gus did use it on a tin box. The lid hit a pot. Dot ran up for tea. Max sat on a mat of ink. Dot put a cup by him. "Is it tea?"
