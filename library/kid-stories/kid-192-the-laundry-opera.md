# The laundry opera

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Dramatic performance accidentally dries the washing*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Kit and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a wet rag on a peg. Pip had to hum a sad act. Kit put the rag by his lip. Pip had a big hum. The air hit the rag. It did bob. Pip did hum for a new go. The rag got dry. Kit put a new rag up. Pip did ask why. "You can do my wet rag act too."
