# The Three Huts

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Three Little Pigs, with a fan as the wind tester*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Ned, Fay, Bo, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Ned had a hay hut. Fay had a log hut. Bo had a tin hut. Gus had a big fan. He let it run by the hay hut. The hut had a dip. He let it run by the log hut. A log hit the sod. The tin hut did hum. All sat in it. Gus set his fan to low. Now the hut was not hot. Ned put hay in it for a bed of hay.
