# The egg alarm

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A guarded treasure is an egg claimed by its hungry guard*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Gus has an egg in a tin box. Kit has to sit by it. At six, Gus hit a pot. Kit put a net by the hut. Gus ran in. Kit did not let him by. "I am to eat," he did say. Kit saw his tin rod to eat the egg. She got the egg. "The top guy is me!"
