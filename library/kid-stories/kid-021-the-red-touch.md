# The Red Touch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: King Midas, with washable red dye*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Max, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Max put his paw in red dye. He was Sir Max for a day. All he got was red. His cup was red. His hat was red. He got a fig. The fig was red too. Max did not eat it. Fay got a rag and a tub. Max put his paw in it. Now he ate a new fig. Fay put his red hat on the tub. It was Sir Tub now.
