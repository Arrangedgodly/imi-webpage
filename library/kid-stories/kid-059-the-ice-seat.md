# The Ice Seat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Snow Queen, with a throne that becomes a puddle*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a bed of ice. Dot ran in. She had hot tea. Bo set the cup on his bed. The ice got wet. The bed got low. Bo sat on the sod. Dot got him a box. Bo put a rug on it. Now he had a dry bed and a cup of tea on ice.
