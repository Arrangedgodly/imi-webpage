# The new planet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: An astronaut marks a discovery on an edible map*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had a map in her jet. A red pea was the new orb. Pip ate it. Dot saw no red pea. "We ate an orb!" Pip had to say he ate it. Dot put a fig on the map. Pip ate it too. Dot put a pin on the map. Pip got a fig to eat, not a map.
