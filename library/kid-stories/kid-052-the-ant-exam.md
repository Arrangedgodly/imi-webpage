# The Ant Exam

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wizard levitation exams, with an ant earning the grade*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had to get a lid up. She did hum a hex. The lid did not go up. She put a nut by it. An ant ran to it. The nut was at its rim. The ant got the lid up. Fay put an A on a tag for the ant. Kit got a B for the nut. The ant ate the tag.
