# The big arm

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A superhero fights a shadow that copies every move*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Kit saw a big arm on her hut. She put her arm up. The big arm got up too. She put it low. The big arm got low. Pip put a mat up to bar the sun. The arm was out. Kit got by the mat. It was up. "My foe is me!" Kit did bow to the sun.
