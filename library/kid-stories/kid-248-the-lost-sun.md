# The lost sun

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space mystery is solved by moving a hat*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Bo, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Gus saw the sun go dim in his gem. Bo hit a pot. All ran up. Gus saw a big orb in the sky. Kit got a hat off the gem. Bo had put it on to bar mud. The sun was out. Bo had to hit the pot to end the din.
