# The moon mop

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space conquest brings responsibility for muddy footprints*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Pip won a run to a tan orb. "I own it all!" His fur put mud on it. Fay put a mop in his paw. Pip had to mop his mud up. He was an orb guy who had to mop all day.
