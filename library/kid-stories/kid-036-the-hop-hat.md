# The Hop Hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Seven-league boots, with a hat that must be worn to work*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Bo, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a hex on his hat. It let him go far in a hop. Ned set a fig on a mat. Bo did hop to it. His hat got off. Bo had to hop to his hat. Now he was by the fig. Ned put the hat on the fig. It did not hop. Bo had to put it on to get the hex. He ate the fig.
