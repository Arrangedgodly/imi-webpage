# The grand nap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Doomsday countdown turns out to be a nap timer*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Pip and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Pip saw a toy go tic. Max lay on a mat. "The end of all is by us!" Pip did say. He hid a fig and had Max in his lap. The toy did go TIC. Max got up and did ask for tea. Pip got his box out. "Max can nap!" he did say. Max got on the mat. "So let me nap to the end."
