# The tiny gate

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Science portal connects two nearby picnic plates*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Dot, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Dot has a gap in a rim. Fay put a pea in it. The pea got on a pan by her. "A new orb!" Dot did cry. Fay put her paw in. It got by the pea. Dot put a fig in it. At six the two had tea but did not get off the mat.
