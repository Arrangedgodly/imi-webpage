# The odd egg

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective mistakes a dyed nut for his own missing egg*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had a tan egg in a box. Gus saw it. His egg was tan too. "Did you get my egg?" Dot had a pot of tan dye. Gus saw a rod by it. He got the egg up. It had a cut. Dot cut it up. It was a nut. Gus had to get his egg out of his bag.
