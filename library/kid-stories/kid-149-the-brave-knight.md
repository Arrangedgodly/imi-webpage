# The brave knight

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fantasy rescuer needs practical oil to free both sword and friend*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Bo and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a pot on his ear. Dot did cry for aid in a big den. His oak rod hit a bar and did not get out. Dot got him oil. He got the rod out. Dot had a jar on her paw. She put oil in it. Her paw got out. Bo put his pot on the jar. "I win!"
