# The red clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Detective mistakes a trail left by a tool*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had a red rag she did not see. Gus had a map to aid her. He saw a red dot on a pot. "A cue!" But his pen had no cap. It put the dot on the pot. Dot got her rag off his map. Gus put on the pen cap.
