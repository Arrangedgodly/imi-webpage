# The big roar

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Villain intimidation is defeated by a practical request*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Ned, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Ned hid in a den. "I am bad!" Fay ran in, a bag on her arm. Ned let out a big cry. Fay put a nut in his paw. "Do it a lot." Ned did. A fig hit the mud. Fay got it. Ned got pay to cry at a fig all day.
