# The hot dish

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heat contest prize solves the winning dish's problem*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Bo and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a pot too hot to eat. Dot had a fan. Bo did not let her use it. His pot had to be hot to win. The ref got a bit and ran for ice. Bo won a fan. Dot put it by her fan. Bo did not let her use it. He did not eat, but he had a cup and a fan.
