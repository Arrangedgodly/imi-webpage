# The giant pea

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A growth ray turns an escape plan into dinner*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max put a pea in his ray box. He hit the red dot. The pea was as big as his hut. Kit was by the gap. The pea hid it. "How do we get out?" Max got a rod. Kit got a cup. The two ate a way out. Max had no use for a big pea at tea.
