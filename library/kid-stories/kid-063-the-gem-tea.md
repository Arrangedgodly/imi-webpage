# The Gem Tea

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Stone Soup, with the stone receiving the credit for lunch*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Gus and Ned and Dot and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had a gem but no pie. He put it in a pot of hot tea. Ned put a fig in it. Dot put an egg in it. Bo put an oat in it. All ate. No one ate the gem. "My gem got me tea!" Gus did say.
