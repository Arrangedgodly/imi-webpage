# The paired lifts

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Action escape elevator is matched by the pursuer*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Gus and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Gus had to run off as Bo was the bad guy. Bo had a tie to get a box up. Gus got in. "Up!" he did cry. Bo did tug. The box got up. Gus got out. Bo was by him. Bo had a box too. "I can go up too!" Gus did hum. Bo had a nap. Gus got out.
