# The loud award

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A hero medal makes stealth impossible*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Gus, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Kit won a tin pot for her big act. Fay had to get by Gus. Kit ran to aid her. The pot hit a log. Fay hid in a box. Kit put the pot in it too. Now the box had a din. Fay got out and set it by Gus. Kit had to win a rug, not a pot.
