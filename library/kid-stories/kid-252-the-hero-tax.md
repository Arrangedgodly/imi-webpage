# The hero tax

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A superhero toll booth rewards the villain instead*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Kit sat by a gap in the way. "I can get you up!" Ned had a bag and a fig. Kit got him up in the air. He ate a bit of it on her paw. "Pay me a fig," Kit did say. Ned did not get off. Kit had to eat too. He let her buy a bit for a nut.
