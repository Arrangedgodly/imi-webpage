# The secret handshake

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spy passwords make every ordinary greeting suspicious*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Kit, Ned and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a spy tap to get Ned. She had to tap his paw and do one new tap. Ned had a paw out. Kit did tap it. Ned did tap her paw. Kit did tap his for a new go. Ned did too. The two did tap all day. Bo had tea. He did ask why. Kit had to see if Ned was a pal yet.
