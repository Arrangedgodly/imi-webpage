# The nut pet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A retrieval robot adopts a nut instead of following its command*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Ned, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max had a bot to get a pea. Ned got it a nut. The bot had the nut and did not go. Max got it a pea. It did not let go of the nut. Ned got the nut. The bot did cry. Max put it in its paw. Ned got the pea. Now the nut had a pet and a tin paw.
