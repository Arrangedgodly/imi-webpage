# The oil bot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: An opening robot oils its owner and is reassigned to a toy car*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max has a bot to get a lid up. Kit did ask to see it. The bot put oil by the lid. It hit a pot. Oil got on a mat. Max had oil on his paw. He hit the mat. Kit got him up. Max put the bot by his toy car. It can oil the car now.
