# The treasure plug

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate wealth is tied up in keeping the ship afloat*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Kit and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a gem in a box. Fay had a sea tub. A gap in the tub let sea in. Kit put the gem in the gap. The tub was dry. Fay had to buy a bun at the bay. Kit got the gem out to pay. The tub got wet. Fay put it in the gap. "The sea can get a gem. We get no bun."
