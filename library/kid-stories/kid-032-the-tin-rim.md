# The Tin Rim

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Epic enchanted ring journeys, with pie cravings that predate the ring*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Dot got a rim of tin. Bo did say it got him to ask for pie. Dot put it on her paw. She did not ask for pie. Bo had jam on his lip. "You ate pie. You did not see my rim yet!" Dot put a rag in it. Bo ate pie. Dot had a dry lap. Bo had pie. The rim had no hex at all.
