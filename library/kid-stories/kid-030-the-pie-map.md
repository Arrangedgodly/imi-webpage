# The Pie Sun

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical astronomy homework, with an edible sun setting in a belly*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Pip, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had to do art of the sun. Fay had to do it too. Pip got ink. Fay got pie. Pip put a dot in his art. Fay put a nut on her pie. At six, all had a sun. Fay ate her art. "Why did you eat the sun?" Pip did ask. "It set," Fay did say.
