# The space jam

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A heroic space launch is thwarted by sticky fuel*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Pip, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Bo put his toy jet on a log. Pip had a jar for the gas. Bo did ask for it. Pip put jam in the jet. Bo hit the red dot to go. The jet did not go. Pip ate jam off the rim. "I can eat the gas!" Bo got a cup to get jam out.
