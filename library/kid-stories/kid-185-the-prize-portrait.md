# The prize portrait

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Game show portrait catches the winner looking away*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Max and Kit, monkeys*

## Story

Max won a cup. Kit had put Max on a pad in ink. Max saw it. It had his fur and ear, but no eye. "No eye!" he did say. Kit got a new pad. "You did not see me." Max sat so Kit saw his ear. Kit put an ear on the new pad too.
