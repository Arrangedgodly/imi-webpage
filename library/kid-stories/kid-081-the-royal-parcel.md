# The Royal Parcel

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal couriers, with the recipient hiding inside his own parcel*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Pip and Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Bo hid in a box for fun. Pip did not see Bo. He put a tag on it: To Bo. Ned got it. "It is for Bo." "I am Bo!" Ned got the lid up. Bo got out. Ned put the box by Bo. "I got it to you. You got out of it. My job can end."
