# The tin wig

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A gadget disguise attracts the entire tea set*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Bo, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max got Bo a tin wig. It had a red gem in it. The gem can get tin to hug it. Bo put it on to be an old guy. A tin rod got on it. A tin pin got on it too. Bo ran to the tea hut. A cup got on his wig. Max got the wig off. Bo had no wig, but he had to rub the tea set.
