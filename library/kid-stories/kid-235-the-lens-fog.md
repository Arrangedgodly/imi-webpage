# The lens fog

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A detective mistakes a dirty viewing gem for mysterious fog*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Gus saw a fog in his gem. Pip saw tan sky. Gus did aim at a sea. Fog! He did aim at his toe. Fog! Pip got the gem and did rub it. Gus had put hot tea on it. The fog was out. Gus got a rag for his kit. Pip got the tea.
