# The boss bot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A robot emperor is defeated by a seating arrangement*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Dot, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Dot has a bot as top guy in her toy hut. "Sit!" it did say. Dot sat. "Sit!" Max sat too. "Sit!" Dot put the bot on a cot. "You too," she did say. It did not get up. Dot and Max ran out to hop. The top guy had to sit as the two ran and had fun.
