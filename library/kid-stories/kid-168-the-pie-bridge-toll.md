# The pie bridge toll

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Western bridge toll paid in increasingly impractical goods*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Dot and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Dot had a van to get by a gap. Gus had a log on it. "One nut," he did say. Dot had no nut. She had a big pie. Gus got the pie. Dot got her van by. Gus sat to eat it. The pie hid the gap. A new van got on the pie. Gus got one nut for a bit.
