# The chase sketch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Action chase recorder keeps stopping to sketch the pursued actor*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Kit and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Kit ran for Gus in an act. Gus had to put her on a pad in ink. He sat by an oak to do it. Kit sat too. Gus ran for a new go. He sat to do new art. Kit lay by the oak. Gus got her on his pad in a nap. He had a nap act, not a run act.
