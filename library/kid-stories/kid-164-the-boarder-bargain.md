# The boarder bargain

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate boarding raid is treated as a free food delivery*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Pip and Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Pip got a rod by the sea tub of Gus. He got up it. Gus put a box in his lap. "One nut," Gus did say. Pip put his hat up. "I am to get, not pay!" Gus did nod. "Get the box. It is my old pie." Pip got off the rod. Gus got a new pie to eat.
