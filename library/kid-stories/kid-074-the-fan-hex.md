# The Fan Hex

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical school wind spells, with a switch doing the real work*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Gus and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Gus put a tag on a peg. A fan let it fly. Gus did say a hex to get it low. It did not get low. Bo hit a pin on the fan. The fan did not run. The tag got low. Gus put the pin in his kit. "A hex in a pin!" Bo put the fan in the kit too.
