# The red carpet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Film star premiere turns its carpet muddy*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Fay and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had a red rug for Dot. Dot had her top hat on. She ran on the rug and did bow. Mud got off her paw. The rug was tan. Fay put it by the mud pit. Now all ran on it. Dot did bow to all. Her rug was not red, but her ear was.
