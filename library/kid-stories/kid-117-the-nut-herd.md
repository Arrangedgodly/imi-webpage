# The nut herd

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Western supply drive delivers goods but leaves a passenger behind*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Dot and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Dot put a nut bag in a van. She had to get it to the inn. Max had to sit on it. Max hit a peg. The van ran to the inn. Dot ran for it. The inn got its nut bag. Max sat on Dot. She had to get him to the den. He got to sit at the end.
