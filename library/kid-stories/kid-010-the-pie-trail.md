# The Pie Trail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Hansel and Gretel, with the trail eater hired as guide*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Gus, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Gus and Bo put pie by a log and by an oak. A jay ate the pie. Bo did not see how to go. Gus fed the jay a nut. It led him to an oak. Gus and Bo had a hut up in it. Bo put the jay on a map in ink. Gus put a nut by its lip. "We can pay our pal." No jay ate the map.
