# The safe delivery

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heist steals a locked safe and becomes its delivery service*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Kit and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Kit did not get a box lid off. Pip had a van. The two got the box in it. At a hut, a guy got up. "My box!" he did say. He got Kit a nut. Pip did ask to see in it. The guy got the lid off. It had a box in it too. Pip had to get the new box to a new hut.
