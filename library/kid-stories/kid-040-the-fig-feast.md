# The Fig Feast

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal banquets, with a tiny feast followed by edible abundance*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Fay, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had to eat a lot. Gus had one fig. He cut it up. Fay had one bit. Gus had one bit. It was not a lot. Fay got her bag. It had a big fig pie. Gus cut it up. Fay ate a lot now. Gus put his fig nub on a tin. It was art, not tea.
