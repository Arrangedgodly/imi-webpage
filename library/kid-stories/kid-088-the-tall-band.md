# The Tall Band

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Bremen Town Musicians, with the frightened host joining the stack*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Pip and Kit and Gus and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a hut for a gig. He did not let Pip, Kit, Gus, and Dot in. Pip sat on Kit. Kit sat on Gus. Dot sat on Pip. All did hum. Bo saw a big mob. He ran out. The mob got in. Pip put a rug by Bo. "We can hum for you." Bo ran in. He sat on top. He had to hum too.
