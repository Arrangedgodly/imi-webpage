# The mail chase

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Urgent courier delivers a reminder to its sender*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-001*
*Cast: Bo and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a tag to get to a pal. Pip put it in a bag and ran. He ran by a log and up an oak. He saw Bo on the tag. Pip ran to Bo. The tag did say to buy a fig. Pip put it in the bag of Bo. Bo did ask Pip to get the bag to his den too.
