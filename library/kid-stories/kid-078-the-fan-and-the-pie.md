# The Fan and the Pie

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The North Wind and the Sun, with pie persuasion beating a fan*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Dot and Pip, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had to get a cap off Pip. He got a fan. Pip put a paw on his cap. Dot got a hot pie. Pip put his cap by her to eat. Bo got his fan off. He ate pie too. Dot put the pie tin on her top. Now Dot had a cap too. No one did try to get it off.
