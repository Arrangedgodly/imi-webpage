# The sheriff badge swap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Western sheriff loses authority after lending a shiny badge*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Bo and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a pin as the law of the inn. Max had a bad toe. Bo got him the pin to use. Max put it on his fur. "No run!" he did say. Bo had to hop. Max sat. "No hop!" Bo sat too. The two sat to eve. Bo did ask for his pin. Max did say the law had to nap.
