# The spy bow

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A greeting code turns into an endless polite contest*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Bo, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay had to bow to Bo to get a key. She did bow. Bo did bow too. Fay got low. Bo got low too. Her hat hit his hat. The two sat in the mud. Fay got a key off his hat. Bo got her spy tag off her hat. The two had met, but no one had got up yet.
