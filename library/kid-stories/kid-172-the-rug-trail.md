# The rug trail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heist carries away a rug that leaves an obvious path behind*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Pip and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had to get a red rug out of a den. Dot was the cop. Pip put it in a bag. One end got out. The end was by the den. The bag was by Pip. Dot ran on the rug to him. Pip put a bow on it. "For you!" Dot put a tie on Pip. "For you too!"
