# The Jig Job

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Red Shoes and dancing enchantments, with the dance repurposed as cleaning*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Bo and Kit and Dot, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a hex for his paw. It did a jig. He had to sit. The paw did a jig. He had to eat. The paw did a jig. Kit put a mop in his paw. Now his jig got mud off the rug. Bo put a cap by his mat. Dot put a nut in it. His jig had a job for tea.
