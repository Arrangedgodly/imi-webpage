# The jewel audition

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Thief's disguise is mistaken for a job applicant*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk C*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-C-002*
*Cast: Bo and Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Bo had a wig to get a gem. Fay had the gem on a box. Bo did a jig so she did not see his paw. Fay got him a rag. "You can rub the gem for me." Bo had to rub it all day. Fay got him a nut as his pay. Bo had no gem. He had a job.
