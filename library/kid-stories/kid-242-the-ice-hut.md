# The ice hut

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A villain writes his plan on transparent walls*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Kit, Ned, monkeys*

## Story

Ned has a hut of ice. His aim is on the ice. Kit can see it. He is to get her in a pit. Kit put a log on it. Ned ran out to tip her in. He hit the log and sat low. Kit put a rug on his ice. His aim was hid now.
