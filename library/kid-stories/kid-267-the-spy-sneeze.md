# The spy sneeze

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: An undercover agent struggles with a scratchy wig and a loose fastener*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Kit, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay hid in a hat hut. Kit had a wig for her. Fay put it on. A bit of fur got on her lip. "Ah!" Fay did cry. Kit got it off. Fay put a nut in the wig to pin it on. The nut hit her toe. "Ah!" Fay had to be a spy in a cap.
