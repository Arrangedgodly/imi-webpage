# The new paw

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Robot hand mistakes a handshake for a command*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Max got Dot a tin paw. It can use a cup and a key. Dot got Pip in a hug. The tin paw did not let go. Max hit a red dot on it. It let Pip go and got Max. Dot sat. Her pal had to aid her. The paw did not let him go.
