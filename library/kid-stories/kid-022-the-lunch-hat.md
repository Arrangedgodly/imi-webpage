# The Lunch Hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical school sorting ceremonies, with a bird choosing snacks*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Pip, Kit, Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Pip had to put Kit and Bo in a row. He did use a hat. Kit put it on. It did say, "Nut!" Kit sat by a nut. Bo put it on. It did say, "Fig!" Bo sat by a fig. Pip saw a jay in it. It had a nut and a fig. Kit fed the jay. Bo ate the fig. All got a row now. It was a row for tea. Pip got a cap for the jay.
