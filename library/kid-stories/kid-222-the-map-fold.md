# The map fold

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A secret spy delivery turns out to be a picnic errand*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Bo, Dot, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay has a map in a can. Bo bid her cut it by the red ink. She did. Bo put the map in a box. He put a fig in it too. "Get it to Dot." Fay saw the way now. It was not far to a mat. Her spy job was to put a fig in a box.
