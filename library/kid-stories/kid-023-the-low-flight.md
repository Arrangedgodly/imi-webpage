# The Low Flight

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wizard flying lessons, with a mop better at ground work*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-001*
*Cast: Bo, Gus, monkeys*

## Story

Bo sat on a mop. "Fly!" The mop did not fly. Gus put it on a toy jet. The jet did fly. The mop got up a bit. It hit a rug. Bo got the mop wet. Now it did a job. He put the jet on a peg. A mop had to fly low to get mud off a rug.
