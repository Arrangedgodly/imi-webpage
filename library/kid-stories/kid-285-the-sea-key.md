# The sea key

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A spy drops a vital key into a shallow cup*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-002*
*Cast: Bo, Fay, monkeys*

## Story

Fay got a key off Bo. She hid it in her cup. Bo put tea in the cup. Fay saw no key. "It is in a sea!" Bo got her a rod. Fay got the key out. It was wet but in her paw. Fay put the key in a bag. Bo got his cup of tea now.
