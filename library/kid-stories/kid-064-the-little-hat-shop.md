# The Little Hat Shop

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: The Elves and the Shoemaker, with hats made for tiny customers*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Kit and Bo, monkeys*

## Story

Kit had a rag to sew for a cap. At ten, Bo got it. He cut it up. He did sew a cap for an ant, a cap for a bee, and a cap for a fly. At six, Kit saw the ant, bee, and fly in a row. All had a cap. Kit put a bag on her top. "Now I fit in."
