# The Royal Pan

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal status tied to clothes, with a pan receiving the bows*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk A*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-A-002*
*Cast: Dot and Fay and Max, monkeys*

## Story

Dot put her cap on a peg. Fay put a pan on her top. Max saw Fay. "Sir Fay!" He did bow and got her a fig. Dot had to rub mud off a pan. Fay put her pan on a peg. Max did bow to the peg. Dot put her cap on her pan. Max got it a fig too.
