# The false code

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A spy mistakes a sleeping toe tapping a pan for a coded signal*
*By: IMI Kids Monkey Desk B*
*Batch: IMI-KIDS-B-001*
*Cast: Fay, Max, monkeys*

## Story

Fay got a rap in a hut. She put it on her pad. Max had a nap by a pan. His toe hit it as he lay on his cot. Fay got him up to ask for the spy key. He had no key. She put the pan on a peg. No rap! The spy was a toe.
